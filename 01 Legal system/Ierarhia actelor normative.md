# Ierarhia actelor normative (hierarchy of normative acts)

The backbone of this vault. Moldova is a **civil-law** system: written normative acts, ranked by legal force, are the primary source of law. The categories and their correlation are set by **[[Legea 100-2017 — actele normative (text)|Legea nr. 100/2017]]** (art. 6–7) and, at the top, by the **[[Constituția RM (text)|Constitution]]**.

## Categories (Legea 100/2017, art. 6)
Moldovan legislation consists of:

a) the **Constitution** of the Republic of Moldova;
b) **laws and decisions (hotărâri) of Parliament**;
c) **decrees of the President**;
d) **decisions and ordinances (hotărâri și ordonanțe) of the Government**;
e) normative acts of **central public authorities of specialty** (ministries, e.g. energy, finance);
f) normative acts of **autonomous public authorities** (independent regulators, e.g. [[ANRE]]);
g) normative acts of authorities of the **autonomous territorial unit with special status** (Găgăuzia);
h) normative acts of **local public administration** authorities.

## How force is determined (art. 7)
> Legal force follows the **competence and status of the issuing authority** and the **category of the act**. A higher-force act can amend or repeal a lower-force act of the same issuer. Where two acts of *equal* force conflict, the **later** one prevails (subject to exceptions in art. 5(3)–(4)). → [[Legea 100-2017 — actele normative (text)#Articolul 7. Corelaţia actelor normative]]

## Working ranking (highest → lowest)
1. **Constituția** — supreme law; all acts must conform → [[Constituția RM (text)#Articolul 7. Constituţia, Lege Supremă]]
2. **Legi constituționale** — amend the Constitution (Titlul VI procedure)
3. **Legi organice** — governed by [[Constituția RM (text)#Articolul 72. Categorii de legi|art. 72]]; regulate the most important domains; adopted by majority of elected MPs
4. **Legi ordinare**
5. **Hotărârile Parlamentului**
6. **Decretele Președintelui**
7. **Hotărârile și ordonanțele Guvernului**
8. **Acte ale autorităților centrale de specialitate / autonome** (ministries, [[ANRE]], etc.)
9. **Acte ale autorităților teritoriale autonome / administrației publice locale**

> [!warning] Organic vs ordinary
> Whether a matter *must* be an organic law is fixed by [[Constituția RM (text)#Articolul 72. Categorii de legi|Constitution art. 72]]. Getting this wrong is a common source of constitutional challenges — worth an atomic note: [[Concept — Lege organică vs lege ordinară]].

## International treaties
Ratified international treaties bind the state; where a treaty on fundamental human rights conflicts with domestic law, the international norm prevails ([[Constituția RM (text)#Articolul 4. Drepturile şi libertăţile omului|art. 4]]). Post-2024, **EU constitutive treaties and binding EU acts have priority over contrary domestic law** once Moldova accedes → [[Constituția RM (text)#Articolul 140¹. Aderarea la tratatele constitutive şi la actele de revizuire a tratatelor constitutive ale Uniunii Europene|art. 140¹]] (new Titlul V¹).

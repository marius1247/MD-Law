# Monitorul Oficial al Republicii Moldova

The **official gazette**. Normative acts acquire legal effect through publication here. Every act in `10 Legislation` carries its MO reference in frontmatter (`mo_publicare`).

## Why it matters
- **Publicity condition:** as a rule, a normative act enters into force only after official publication (or on a later date it specifies). Unpublished normative acts do not produce effects.
- **Citation:** the canonical citation format is `MO nr. <n>-<n>/<data> art. <n>` — e.g. the Constitution's republication: *MO nr. 78 art. 140 din 29.03.2016*.
- **Versioning:** amendments are themselves published in the MO; [legis.md](https://www.legis.md) then produces **consolidated** versions. This is why version-tracking matters — see [[Conventions]].

## Practical
- Search & full texts: [legis.md](https://www.legis.md) (State Register, Ministry of Justice).
- Entry-into-force rules are set by [[Legea 100-2017 — actele normative (text)|Legea 100/2017]] (chapter on intrarea în vigoare).

# Procesul legislativ (the law-making process)

How a normative act comes into being, per [[Legea 100-2017 — actele normative (text)|Legea 100/2017]] (stages of *legiferare*).

## Skeleton (to expand from the text)
1. **Initiative** — who may initiate (subjects of legislative initiative, [[Constituția RM (text)#Articolul 73. Iniţiativa legislativă|Const. art. 73]]).
2. **Ex-ante analysis** — problem definition, options, **analiza impactului de reglementare** for acts affecting business (Legea 235/2006).
3. **Drafting** — structure & content requirements.
4. **Consultation & endorsement** (avizare, public consultation).
5. **Adoption** — Parliament (organic = majority of elected MPs; ordinary = majority of present).
6. **Promulgation** — by the President.
7. **Publication** — [[Monitorul Oficial]] → **entry into force**.
8. **Monitoring & re-examination** — ex-post.

# The Moldovan legal system — overview

A one-page orientation before diving into any domain.

## Family
Moldova is a **continental / civil-law** jurisdiction. Codified written acts are the primary source; case law is not formally binding precedent, though **Constitutional Court** decisions are binding and higher-court practice is persuasive. Legal reasoning runs from general codes to special laws (*lex specialis derogat legi generali*).

## The main pillars (codes)
- **[[Constituția RM — text|Constituția]]** (1994, republicată 2016; amended through 2024) — supreme law.
- **Codul civil** (nr. 1107/2002) — the private-law backbone; a major 2018–2019 modernisation absorbed several standalone laws.
- **Codul fiscal** (nr. 1163/1997) — taxation.
- **Codul muncii**, **Codul administrativ** (nr. 116/2018, incl. administrative procedure), **Codul penal**, **Codul de procedură civilă/penală**, **Codul vamal**, plus sectoral codes (funciar, silvic, apelor, subsolului, electoral, audiovizualului, educației).

## Key organising laws (non-code)
- **[[Legea 100-2017 — actele normative (text)|Legea 100/2017]]** — how normative acts are made and ranked.
- Company law: **Legea 1134/1997** (societăți pe acțiuni) and **Legea 135/2007** (SRL).
- **Legea contabilității și raportării financiare** (nr. 287/2017).
- Energy: **Legea 107/2016** (energia electrică), **Legea 108/2016** (gazele naturale), **Legea 174/2017** (energetica), renewables.
- **Legea 131/2015** (achizițiile publice).

## The state (who makes & applies law)
- **Parlamentul** — unicameral legislature (101 MPs); adopts laws.
- **Președintele** — head of state; promulgates laws, issues decrees.
- **Guvernul** — executive; hotărâri & ordonanțe; ministries issue sectoral acts.
- **Autonomous regulators** — e.g. **[[ANRE]]** (energy), BNM (central bank), CNPF (financial markets).
- **Judiciary** — courts of law; the **Curtea Constituțională** reviews constitutionality (outside the ordinary court hierarchy).

# HANRE nr. 112/2019 — racordarea la rețelele de gaze naturale — Analysis
**Raw text:** [[HANRE 112-2019 — racordarea la retelele de gaze (text)]] ✅ *complete* · **Gas law:** [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)]] · **Network code:** [[HANRE 420-2019 — Codul retelelor de gaze naturale (notă)]] · **Regulator:** [[ANRE]]
> [!note] Current gas connection layer
> Unlike [[HANRE 168-2019 — racordarea la retelele electrice (notă)]], this regulation remains in the live gas-law framework. The consolidation records HANRE 328/2025 amendments.

## 1. What it is
This regulation governs connection to natural gas networks and provision of transmission/distribution services.
It applies to utilisation installations, production installations, storage facilities, gas-network interconnection, modification of existing installations, connection permits, technical interconnection conditions, service contracts, disconnection, reconnection, suspension and limitation.

## 2. Legal basis
The decision was issued under art. 7(1)(k) of [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)|L108/2016]].
Read it with [[HANRE 420-2019 — Codul retelelor de gaze naturale (notă)]]: HANRE 112/2019 governs connection workflow; HANRE 420/2019 governs network operation, interoperability, capacity and balancing.

## 3. Connection stages
Point 5 lists the process:
1. application for connection permit;
2. issuance of the permit;
3. connection contract and payment of design/connection costs where applicable;
4. design of gas installations;
5. coordination of design documents;
6. execution, reception and putting under pressure;
7. proof of technical-service capacity or technical-service contract where required.
The last item was added by HANRE 328/2025 and matters for safety and maintenance compliance.

## 4. Application and permit
A permit is required for a new gas installation or modification of an existing installation, including debit/pressure changes, relocation or adding appliances.
No permit is needed for replacement with appliances of the same capacity by an authorised person under the project, but the consumer informs the operator and supplier.
For potential final consumers, the operator issues the permit within 10 working days after a complete filing. For gas producers and storage operators, the deadline is 30 calendar days.
The permit is free, valid for 12 months and must state the connection point, pressure, pipe diameter, metering requirements and works needed.

## 5. Refusal and complaint
Refusal must be written and technically/economically justified.
The operator must explain what measures are needed to remove the refusal grounds, including network-development measures and concrete timing.
The applicant may complain to [[ANRE]]. Depending on the act, later litigation may fall under [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]].

## 6. Design and pressurisation
Connection or reconnection enabling consumption or injection is performed exclusively by the system operator.
The applicant may ask the operator to design/execute the connection installation or hire another authorised person, subject to operator coordination.
Ordinary operator deadlines: 45 calendar days for household applicants and 60 calendar days for non-household applicants, subject to justified exceptions.

## Practitioner cautions
- Separate permit, contract, design coordination, reception and pressurisation.
- Check owner consent for networks only technically serviced by the operator.
- For autonomous heating in apartment blocks, verify thermal-energy rules.
- Pair supply start with [[HANRE 113-2019 — furnizarea gazelor naturale (notă)]].

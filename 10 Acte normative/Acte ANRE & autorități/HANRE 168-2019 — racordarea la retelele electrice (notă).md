# HANRE nr. 168/2019 — racordarea la rețelele electrice — Analysis
**Raw text:** [[HANRE 168-2019 — racordarea la retelele electrice (text)]] ✅ *complete* · **Replacement:** [[HANRE 311-2026 — racordarea la retelele electrice (notă)]] · **Concept:** [[Concept — Racordare la rețea]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]]
> [!danger] ABROGATED — historical only
> HANRE 168/2019 was abrogated by [[HANRE 311-2026 — racordarea la retelele electrice (notă)|HANRE 311/2026]], in force 07.05.2026. Use only for legacy facts, permits and disputes under [[Legea 107-2016 — energia electrica (text)|L107/2016]].

## 1. What it was
This was the 2019 regulation for electricity-network connection and transmission/distribution service.
It covered:
- connection of utilisation installations and power plants;
- connection permits (`avize de racordare`);
- connection contracts;
- design/execution and admission into operation;
- ownership boundaries and delimitation acts;
- transmission/distribution service contracts;
- suspension, interruption, limitation, disconnection, reconnection and metering.

## 2. Legal basis and repeal
The decision was issued under [[Legea 107-2016 — energia electrica (text)|L107/2016]].
That law has been replaced by [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)|L164/2025]], and this regulation has been replaced by [[HANRE 311-2026 — racordarea la retelele electrice (notă)]].
For new connection matters after 07.05.2026, start with HANRE 311/2026.

## 3. Structure
The annex followed a classic connection sequence:
1. scope and definitions;
2. connection stages;
3. application for connection permit;
4. issuance, extension and termination of the permit;
5. design/execution and admission into operation;
6. ownership boundaries;
7. network-service contracts;
8. disconnection/reconnection and metering.

## 4. Historically useful definitions
Useful terms for older permits and contracts:
- `aviz de racordare`;
- connection contract;
- delimitation act;
- interaction agreement;
- approved power and contracted power;
- emergency minimum and technological minimum power;
- disconnection, limitation and suspension of service;
- solution study for power-plant connection.

## 5. What changed
[[HANRE 311-2026 — racordarea la retelele electrice (notă)|HANRE 311/2026]] adds L164/2025 logic:
- storage as a connection object;
- active consumers and energy communities;
- flexible, conditional and available capacity;
- waiting lists and capacity auctions;
- financial guarantees against speculative queue blocking.

## 6. When to use
Use this act for:
- applications filed before repeal;
- legacy permits and connection contracts;
- disputes whose facts occurred before 07.05.2026;
- comparison with [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)]].
Do not use it as current law for new connection advice.

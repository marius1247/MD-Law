# HANRE nr. 534/2019 — Regulile pieței gazelor naturale — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 534-2019 — Regulile pietei gazelor naturale (text)]] ✅ *complete* · **Gas law:** [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)]] · **Network code:** [[HANRE 420-2019 — Codul retelelor de gaze naturale (notă)]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Domain:** [[Energetică — sinteza sectorului]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Operational rulebook for Moldova's wholesale natural gas market — entry-exit capacity booking, the Virtual Trading Point (PVT), operational balancing, imbalance cash-out, and system-user obligations.
> **Primary business risk:** Asymmetric imbalance cash-out spreads (10% penalty on short positions) and capacity overbooking penalties erode trading margins; TSO capacity allocation disputes can block cross-border reverse flows.

## 1. Statutory hierarchy & legal foundation

* **Primary legal basis:** Arts. 67–69 of [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (text)]] — third-party access, market organisation, balancing.
* **Procedural subordination:** Capacity and imbalance disputes are reviewable under [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]]; commercial billing objections have short challenge windows.
* **EU acquis alignment:** Transposes Regulation (EU) 715/2009 principles (CAM NC) via Energy Community adaptation; entry-exit tariff system harmonised under [[Energetică — metodologii tarifare (notă)]].
* **Neighbouring acts:** [[HANRE 420-2019 — Codul retelelor de gaze naturale (notă)]] (network operation) · [[HANRE 112-2019 — racordarea la retelele de gaze (notă)]] (connection) · [[HANRE 113-2019 — furnizarea gazelor naturale (notă)]] (retail supply).

## 2. Practical legal mechanics & key provisions

### A. Entry-exit system & Virtual Trading Point (PVT)

* **Architecture:** Transport capacity is booked separately at **entry points** (Grebenyky, Căușeni, Ungheni, Isaccea interconnections) and **exit points** (distribution off-takes, industrial connections, export).
* **PVT (Punctul Virtual de Tranzacționare):** National virtual hub operated by TSO (Vestmoldtransgaz). Title transfer occurs at PVT without booking physical capacity between internal points.
* **Trade notifications:** System users notify TSO by D-1 of commercial positions.

### B. Operational balancing & imbalance cash-out

* **Daily imbalance:** Inputs (entry bookings / PVT purchases) minus outputs (exit bookings / PVT sales).
* **Short position:** User pays TSO the **Marginal Buy Price** = Weighted Average Gas Price × **1.10**.
* **Long position:** TSO pays User the **Marginal Sell Price** = WAPP × **0.90**.
* **Tolerance bands:** Small percentage tolerances encourage self-balancing before cash-out.

### C. Capacity products & booking

* **Firm vs. interruptible** capacity at entry/exit points.
* **Secondary capacity trading** at PVT for reallocation.
* **Bank guarantees** required for capacity bookings — default triggers allocation invalidation.

## 3. Legal ambiguities, vulnerabilities & risk matrix

| Provision | Identified flaw / ambiguity | Practical risk | Recommended strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Invoice challenge window** | 10-day objection period creates presumption of acceptance if missed | Loss of right to dispute imbalance statements | Automate invoice review; file formal notice of disagreement within 10 calendar days |

## 4. Relevant jurisprudence & ANRE practice

* **ANRE dispute practice:** ANRE enforces non-discriminatory capacity allocation at interconnection points; TSOs cannot prefer transit over domestic off-take without documented congestion evidence.
* **Billing disputes:** Courts apply [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]] art. 118 (material errors in administrative/commercial billing) — timely challenge is admissibility prerequisite.
* **Supplier-switching interaction:** ANRE penalises DSOs that delay meter-reading transfers required for consumer supplier switches under [[HANRE 113-2019 — furnizarea gazelor naturale (notă)]].

## 5. Client action checklist / compliance roadmap

- [ ] Register as system user with Vestmoldtransgaz; execute PVT agreement.
- [ ] Post bank guarantees exceeding peak monthly bidding volumes before capacity auctions.
- [ ] Implement real-time flow monitoring and D-1 trade notifications to TSO.
- [ ] Review imbalance statements within **10 calendar days**; file formal objections with SCADA evidence.
- [ ] For cross-border capacity, pre-qualify on regional booking platforms (PRISMA / Transelectrica where applicable).
- [ ] Cross-check capacity bookings with supply contracts under [[HANRE 113-2019 — furnizarea gazelor naturale (notă)]].

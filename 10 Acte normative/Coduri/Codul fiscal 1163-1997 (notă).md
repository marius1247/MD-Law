# Codul fiscal nr. 1163/1997 — Master analysis

**Raw text index:** [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 (text) — Index]] ✅ *complete by Titlu* · **Hub:** [[MOC — Fiscalitate & Contabilitate]] · **Synthesis:** [[Fiscalitate — sinteza sistemului fiscal]] · **Administrator:** [[SFS]]

> [!warning] Rates, thresholds and exemptions are time-sensitive
> The Fiscal Code is amended every year through the fiscal-policy package, typically effective 1 January. The rates and thresholds mentioned here are a July 2026 orientation only. **Before quoting any number, re-check the current consolidation, [[SFS]] guidance and Ministry of Finance materials.**

> [!summary] The Code is ten statutes inside one code
> The useful way to read the Fiscal Code is by **Titlu**. Titlul I supplies definitions and principles; Titlul II/III/IV are the main substantive taxes; Titlul V is the procedural code for assessment, audit, appeal and enforcement; Titlurile VI-X are narrower tax regimes.

## 2. Titlul I — the interpretive key

[[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul I#Articolul 1. Relaţiile reglementate de prezentul cod|Titlul I art. 1]] identifies the relations governed by the Code. [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul I#Articolul 2. Sistemul fiscal al Republicii Moldova|Art. 2]] defines the fiscal system, while [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul I#Articolul 3. Legislaţia fiscală|art. 3]] identifies tax legislation.

[[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul I#Articolul 4. Tratatele internaţionale|Art. 4]] is the treaty hook. For withholding, residence, permanent establishment and cross-border income, never stop at the domestic code: check the relevant double tax treaty. → [[Concept — Rezident fiscal]]

[[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul I#Articolul 5. Noţiuni generale|Art. 5]] is the definitions article. It is large and should be treated as the first interpretive stop for taxpayer, resident/non-resident, source, tax liability, arrears and fiscal-administration concepts.

[[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul I#Articolul 6. Impozite şi taxe şi tipurile lor|Art. 6]] distinguishes taxes and fees and their types. [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul I#Articolul 7. Stabilirea, modificarea şi anularea|Art. 7]] governs establishment, amendment and cancellation. [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul I#Articolul 8. Drepturile şi obligaţiile contribuabilului|Art. 8]] is the taxpayer rights and obligations provision.

## 3. Titlul II — income tax is the main substantive title

[[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul II#Articolul 12. Noţiuni|Titlul II art. 12]] defines income-tax concepts: annuity, dividend, taxable income, deduction and related terms. [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul II#Articolul 12. ## 1|Art. 12^1]] sets the income-tax fiscal period, normally the calendar year, with rules for new, liquidating, reorganised and IT Park entities.

[[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul II#Articolul 13. Subiecţii impunerii|Art. 13]] defines taxpayers. The resident/non-resident distinction determines whether worldwide income or Moldovan-source income is taxed. This is the entry point for [[Concept — Rezident fiscal]].

[[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul II#Articolul 14. Obiectul impunerii|Art. 14]] defines the taxable object. For resident legal persons and entrepreneurs, it is income from Moldovan and foreign sources less allowable deductions and exemptions. For non-residents, the focus is Moldovan-source income and the special non-resident chapter.

[[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul II#Articolul 15. Cotele de impozit|Art. 15]] contains rates. Do not quote them without current verification. As of the July 2026 synthesis, the headline orientation is 12% standard income tax, with special regimes such as agriculture, small non-VAT SMEs and IT Park treated separately. → [[Concept — Impozit pe venit]]

[[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul II#Articolul 18. Sursele de venit impozabile|Art. 18]] and [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul II#Articolul 20. Sursele de venit neimpozabile|art. 20]] are the gross-income / excluded-income gate. [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul II#Articolul 24. Deducerea cheltuielilor aferente activităţii|Art. 24]] is the practical corporate-income-tax provision: ordinary and necessary business expenses are deductible, subject to limitations and specific denial rules.

The most common advisory move is not rate selection; it is reconciliation: accounting profit under [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (notă)]] is adjusted under Titlul II to taxable income. That is why [[Concept — Situații financiare]] matters for tax.

## 4. Titlul III — VAT is transaction architecture

[[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul III#Articolul 93. Noţiuni generale|Titlul III art. 93]] defines VAT concepts. [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul III#Articolul 94. Subiecţii impozabili|Art. 94]] defines taxable persons, and [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul III#Articolul 95. Obiectele impozabile|art. 95]] defines taxable objects.

[[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul III#Articolul 96. Cotele T.V.A|Art. 96]] contains VAT rates. They are especially amendment-sensitive. The July 2026 synthesis uses 20% standard, 8% reduced for specified categories, and 0% for exports and assimilated supplies; verify before use. → [[Concept — TVA]]

The core mechanics:

- taxable value of domestic supplies — [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul III#Articolul 97. Valoarea impozabilă a livrării impozabile|art. 97]];
- taxable value of imported goods — [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul III#Articolul 100. Valoarea impozabilă a mărfurilor|art. 100]];
- calculation and payment — [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul III#Articolul 101. Modul de calculare şi achitare a T.V.A|art. 101]];
- input VAT deduction — [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul III#Articolul 102. Deducerea sumei T.V.A. pentru mărfurile,|art. 102]];
- VAT exemptions — [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul III#Articolul 103. Scutirea de T.V.A|art. 103]];
- zero-rated / exempt supplies — [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul III#Articolul 104. Livrările scutite de T.V.A|art. 104]];
- place of supply for goods and services — [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul III#Articolul 110. Locul livrării mărfurilor|arts. 110]] and [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul III#Articolul 111. Locul livrării serviciilor|111]];
- VAT registration and deregistration — [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul III#Articolul 112. Înregistrarea subiectului impozabil|arts. 112]]-[[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul III#Articolul 113. Anularea înregistrării|113]];
- fiscal invoice — [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul III#Articolul 117. Factura fiscală|art. 117]].

VAT advice is usually about classification and documentation: taxable/exempt/zero-rated, place of supply, timing, invoice, deduction and refund.

## 5. Titlul IV — excise

[[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul IV#Articolul 119. Noţiuni|Titlul IV art. 119]] defines excise, excise certificate, fiscal warehouse and authorised warehousekeeper. [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul IV#Articolul 120. Subiecţii impunerii|Art. 120]] defines taxpayers: authorised warehousekeepers and legal/natural persons importing excisable goods.

[[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul IV#Articolul 121. Obiectele impunerii şi baza impozabilă|Art. 121]] defines objects and base. [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul IV#Articolul 122. Cotele accizelor şi momentul|Art. 122]] contains rates and the taxable moment, and [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul IV#Articolul 123. Modul de calculare a accizelor|art. 123]] contains calculation, including marking with excise stamps.

Excise is highly numeric and goods-specific. Treat the annexes and current classification as live verification items. → [[Concept — Accize]]

## 6. Titlul V — where tax disputes are won or lost

Titlul V is the procedural spine of the system. [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul V#Articolul 131. Organele cu atribuţii de administrare fiscală|Art. 131]] identifies bodies with fiscal-administration powers. [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul V#Articolul 132. Sarcina de bază a Serviciului Fiscal de Stat|Art. 132]] gives the basic task of [[SFS]], and [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul V#Articolul 133. Atribuţiile Serviciului Fiscal de Stat|art. 133]] lists its powers.

For a lawyer, the priority provisions are:

- taxpayer / SFS information powers — [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul V#Articolul 134. Drepturile Serviciului Fiscal de Stat|art. 134]];
- fiscal code and registration — [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul V#Articolul 161. Dispoziţii generale|arts. 161]]-[[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul V#Articolul 168. Anularea codului fiscal|168]];
- tax liability creation and extinguishment — [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul V#Articolul 169. Naşterea şi modificarea obligaţiei fiscale|arts. 169]]-[[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul V#Articolul 180. Modificarea termenului de stingere|180]];
- tax returns — [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul V#Articolul 187. Prezentarea dării de seamă fiscale|art. 187]] and corrected returns under [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul V#Articolul 188. Darea de seamă fiscală corectată|art. 188]];
- forced execution — [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul V#Articolul 193. Condiţiile declanşării executării silite|arts. 193]]-[[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul V#Articolul 207. Evidenţa acţiunilor de executare silită|207]];
- fiscal control — [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul V#Articolul 214. Principiile generale de efectuare|arts. 214]]-[[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul V#Articolul 226. Citaţia la Serviciul Fiscal de Stat, inclusiv|226-series]];
- late-payment penalty — [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul V#Articolul 228. Majorarea de întârziere (penalitatea)|art. 228]];
- account suspension and seizure — [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul V#Articolul 229. Suspendarea operaţiunilor la conturile|arts. 229]]-[[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul V#Articolul 230. Sechestrarea bunurilor|230]];
- sanctions — [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul V#Articolul 231. Noţiunea şi temeiurile tragerii la răspundere|arts. 231]]-[[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul V#Articolul 264. Termenul de prescripţie pentru|264]];
- contestation — [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul V#Articolul 267. Dreptul de a contesta decizia|art. 267]] and following provisions.

The practical sequence in disputes is: control act → SFS decision → administrative contestation → administrative court. Deadlines and suspension of enforcement must be handled immediately; good substantive arguments are useless if the contestation period is missed.

## 7. Titlurile VI-IX — local and asset/activity taxes

[[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul VI]] covers real-estate tax. Start with [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul VI#Articolul 276. Noţiuni|art. 276]] definitions, [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul VI#Articolul 277. Subiecţii impunerii|art. 277]] taxpayers, [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul VI#Articolul 278. Obiectele impunerii şi baza impozabilă|art. 278]] taxable objects/base, [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul VI#Articolul 280. Cotele impozitului|art. 280]] rates and [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul VI#Articolul 283. Scutirea de impozit|art. 283]] exemptions.

[[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul VII]] covers local taxes: definitions in [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul VII#Articolul 288. Noţiuni|art. 288]], local-tax objects/base in [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul VII#Articolul 291. Obiectele impunerii şi baza impozabilă|art. 291]], rates and terms in [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul VII#Articolul 292. Cotele şi termenele de plată a taxelor locale|art. 292]], and local-authority powers in [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul VII#Articolul 297. Atribuţiile autorităţii administraţiei|art. 297]].

[[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul VIII]] covers natural-resource taxes. It matters for extractive, water-use and resource-intensive businesses and links to reporting under [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 23. Raportul conducerii|L287 art. 23(9)]] on payments to the state.

[[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul IX]] covers road taxes: the system is in [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul IX#Articolul 335. Sistemul taxelor rutiere|art. 335]], with separate chapters for vehicle-use and road-related taxes.

## 8. Titlul X — other regimes and the IT Park single tax

[[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul X#Articolul 367. Noţiuni|Titlul X art. 367]] begins the special regime. [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul X#Articolul 368. Subiecţii impunerii|Art. 368]] identifies taxpayers, [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul X#Articolul 369. Obiectul impunerii|art. 369]] the object, [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul X#Articolul 370. Cota impozitului|art. 370]] the rate, and [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul X#Articolul 372. Componenţa impozitului unic|art. 372]] the composition of the single tax.

For technology-sector advice, Titlul X is often more important than Titlul II because the single tax replaces much of the ordinary burden for eligible IT Park residents. But it is status-dependent: loss or acquisition of IT Park status changes the fiscal period and regime, cross-linking back to [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul II#Articolul 12. ## 1|Titlul II art. 12^1]].

## 9. Customs interface

The Fiscal Code does not stand alone at the border. Import VAT and excise are collected through customs mechanics, and "drepturi de import" in customs law include customs duty, VAT and excise collected by the Customs Service. See [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (notă)]].

For imports, the workflow is:

1. classify the goods under customs tariff rules;
2. determine origin and customs value;
3. calculate customs duty;
4. calculate import VAT under [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul III#Articolul 100. Valoarea impozabilă a mărfurilor|Titlul III art. 100]];
5. calculate excise where the goods are excisable under [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul IV]];
6. verify exemptions, preferences and refund/remission routes.

## 10. Practical hierarchy for legal work

For most files, read in this order:

1. [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul I]] for definitions, taxpayer rights and treaty rule;
2. [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul II]], [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul III]] or [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul IV]] for the tax type;
3. [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (notă)]] for accounting base and documents;
4. [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul V]] for procedure, audit, enforcement and appeal;
5. [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (notă)]] if goods cross the border;
6. double tax treaty, SFS guidance and annual fiscal-policy law.

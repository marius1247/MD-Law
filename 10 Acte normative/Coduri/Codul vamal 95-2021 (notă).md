# Codul vamal nr. 95/2021 — Overview analysis

**Raw text:** [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)]] ✅ *complete* · **Hub:** [[MOC — Fiscalitate & Contabilitate]] · **Synthesis:** [[Fiscalitate — sinteza sistemului fiscal]] · **Tax bridge:** [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 (notă)]]

> [!summary] Customs is the border layer of tax and trade law
> The Customs Code governs declaration, classification, origin, valuation, customs debt, guarantees, release for free circulation, special regimes, export, enforcement and customs decisions. For commercial lawyers it is the bridge between import/export contracts, [[Concept — TVA]], [[Concept — Accize]] and customs duty.

> [!important] EU-aligned structure
> The act transposes multiple EU customs, relief, VAT/excise-at-import and IP-border-enforcement instruments. It is not identical to the EU Union Customs Code, but its architecture and vocabulary are EU-facing.

## 1. What the Code covers

[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 1. Domeniul de aplicare a legislației vamale|Art. 1]] defines the scope of customs legislation. Customs activity includes implementing customs policy, ensuring compliance when goods, vehicles and persons cross the Moldovan customs frontier, collecting import/export duties, customs clearance, control and supervision.

The law generally applies according to the legislation in force when the Customs Service accepts the customs declaration and documents, with specific exceptions. That timing rule matters whenever tariff, VAT, excise or prohibitions change around shipment.

[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 2. Organizarea și funcționarea Serviciului Vamal|Art. 2]] governs organisation and operation of the Customs Service. The authority profile still needs a dedicated vault note; for now, treat "Serviciul Vamal" as the border administrator parallel to [[SFS]] in domestic tax.

## 2. Core definitions

[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 5. Noțiuni principale|Art. 5]] is the critical definitions article. The most useful terms:

| Term | Function |
|---|---|
| **acordare a liberului de vamă** | Customs release of goods for the declared regime. |
| **declarație vamală** | The act by which a person indicates the customs regime for goods. |
| **declarant** | Person lodging a declaration in own name or in whose name it is lodged. |
| **datorie vamală** | Obligation to pay import or export duties. |
| **drepturi de import** | Customs duty plus VAT and excise collected by Customs. |
| **regim vamal** | Free circulation/import, special regimes or export. |
| **reprezentant vamal** | Person appointed to perform customs formalities for another. |
| **valoare în vamă** | Customs value used for ad valorem import duties. |

The definition of "drepturi de import" is the main tax bridge: border debt includes not only customs duty, but also import [[Concept — TVA]] and [[Concept — Accize]] where collection belongs to Customs.

## 3. Information, representation and customs decisions

The Code is heavily decision-based. [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 6. Modalități de schimb și de stocare|Art. 6]] moves information exchange and storage toward electronic methods.

[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 11. Furnizarea de informații Serviciului Vamal|Art. 11]] is a liability provision for declarations and applications: the person submitting a customs declaration or application is responsible for correctness and completeness of information, authenticity and validity of supporting documents, and compliance with obligations tied to the regime.

[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 12. Reprezentantul vamal|Art. 12]] governs customs representation. In import/export disputes, identify whether the broker acted in direct or indirect representation because it affects who is debtor and who bears responsibility for declaration errors.

[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 15. Decizii luate în urma depunerii unei cereri|Art. 15]] and following provisions regulate customs decisions on application: acceptance conditions, effective date, notification, suspension, annulment, revocation and modification. Customs work is administrative-law work with specialised rules.

## 4. Authorised economic operator and simplifications

The Code provides an AEO-style layer. [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 37. Simplificări și facilități|Art. 37]] links simplifications and facilities to compliant operators. [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 38. Criteriile pentru acordarea statutului|Arts. 38]]-[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 43. Standardele de securitate și siguranță|43]] set criteria: compliance, record management, financial solvency, practical competence and safety/security standards.

For exporters and importers with frequent flows, the commercial value is speed, lower friction and fewer guarantees. For lawyers, the risk is that one serious compliance issue can undermine the status and therefore the client's logistics model.

## 5. Appeals and suspension

[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 44. Dreptul la o cale de atac și controlul|Art. 44]] states the right to appeal and judicial control. [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 45. Calea de atac împotriva deciziei vamale|Art. 45]] governs appeals against customs decisions.

[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 48. Suspendarea executării|Art. 48]] is practically important: challenging a customs decision does not automatically remove the debt or release issue. Suspension must be tested separately, often with guarantee implications.

This mirrors tax disputes under [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul V]]: deadlines and enforcement posture often decide the case before substantive customs classification is litigated.

## 6. Tariff classification

[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 53. Tariful Vamal al Republicii Moldova|Art. 53]] says customs duty is based on the Customs Tariff of Moldova. The tariff includes the Combined Nomenclature, customs duties, preferential tariff measures from agreements, reliefs, favourable treatment and other tariff/non-tariff measures.

[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 54. Clasificarea tarifară a mărfurilor|Art. 54]] defines classification as identifying the relevant subheading or subdivision. Classification is the first domino:

- it determines customs duty rate;
- it may trigger non-tariff measures;
- it affects excise categories;
- it affects VAT exemptions or reduced treatment where linked to goods type;
- it affects origin rules and evidence.

Commercial contracts should allocate classification responsibility and post-clearance adjustment risk.

## 7. Origin — preferential and non-preferential

[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 55. Dispoziții generale|Art. 55]] defines origin for tariff and non-tariff measures. Origin can be non-preferential or preferential.

[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 56. Confirmarea originii mărfii|Art. 56]] identifies when origin must be confirmed: preferential treatment, quantitative restrictions or other foreign-trade measures, environmental/health/consumer/order/security rules or other vital areas.

[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 58. Originea preferențială a mărfurilor|Art. 58]] ties preferential origin to the free-trade agreement rules. This is where DCFTA and other FTA analysis enters: the Code supplies the domestic customs procedure, but the origin rule is often in the agreement.

[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 61. Mărfurile în a căror producere intervin|Art. 61]] uses the classic substantial-transformation test where several countries are involved. [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 61. ## 1|Art. 61^1]] prevents origin-conferring processing where the purpose is avoidance of measures. [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 62. Operațiuni minime|Art. 62]] excludes minimal operations from origin-conferring transformation.

Origin disputes are document-heavy. [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 65. Dovada de origine|Art. 65]] permits Customs to require proof and supporting elements where origin is declared and doubts exist.

## 8. Approved exporter

[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 69. Dispoziții generale|Art. 69]] allows approved-exporter status where a preferential regime permits origin declarations by approved exporters.

[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 69. ## 1|Art. 69^1]] sets conditions: regular preferential-origin exports, no repeated origin-law violations, proof that exported goods meet origin rules, competent persons responsible for preferential origin, Customs access to origin documents/accounting/IT systems/production processes, and no insolvency procedure.

[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 70. Drepturile și obligațiile exportatorului|Art. 70]] lets approved exporters document preferential origin on their own responsibility and imposes record-keeping and notification duties.

This is a high-value compliance status for exporters; losing it can slow export operations and reduce customer value.

## 9. Customs value

[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 71. Domeniul de aplicare|Art. 71]] introduces customs valuation. [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 72. Metoda de determinare a valorii|Art. 72]] sets transaction value as the initial basis: price paid or payable for goods sold for export to Moldova, adjusted as needed.

Transaction value is available only if conditions are met, including that buyer-seller relationship does not influence price. Related-party import pricing must therefore be analysed both for customs value and transfer-pricing / income-tax purposes.

[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 73. Neacceptarea valorilor de tranzacție|Art. 73]] permits Customs to request additional information where it has justified doubts. If doubts remain, Customs may decide value cannot be determined under transaction value.

[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 74. Elemente ale valorii de tranzacție|Art. 74]] lists additions: commissions/brokerage except buying commissions, containers, packing, assists, royalties/licence fees, resale proceeds returning to seller, transport/insurance/loading to the place of introduction. [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 75. Elemente care nu se includ în valoarea|Art. 75]] lists exclusions such as post-entry transport, installation after entry, financing charges under conditions and import duties.

Where transaction value fails, [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 80. Metode secundare de determinare|Art. 80]] applies secondary methods successively.

## 10. Customs debt, guarantees, payment and enforcement

[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 87. Datoria vamală la introducerea|Art. 87]] and following articles govern customs debt on import and irregular situations. [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 91. Datoria vamală la export|Art. 91]] covers export debt.

[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 99. Garanția obligatorie|Arts. 99]]-[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 107. Eliberarea garanției|107]] cover guarantees. Guarantees are not merely procedural: they can determine whether goods are released while classification, origin or valuation is disputed.

[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 109. Notificarea datoriei vamale|Art. 109]] governs notification of customs debt; [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 110. Prescrierea datoriei vamale,|art. 110]] limitation; [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 113. Termenele generale de plată|art. 113]] payment terms.

Enforcement begins under [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 127. Domeniul de aplicare și condițiile|art. 127]] and includes forced-execution measures under [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 128. Modalități de executare silită|arts. 128]]-[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 139. Evidența acțiunilor de executare|139]]. This mirrors the fiscal-enforcement logic of [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul V]] but within customs procedure.

## 11. Declaration and release

[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 160. Declararea mărfurilor în vamă|Art. 160]] and following articles govern declaration. [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 162. Declarația vamală standard|Art. 162]] covers standard declaration, [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 163. Documentele justificative|art. 163]] supporting documents, [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 164. Declarația vamală simplificată|art. 164]] simplified declaration and [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 166. Declarația vamală suplimentară|art. 166]] supplementary declaration.

[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 181. Verificarea unei declarații vamale|Art. 181]] covers verification, [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 182. Examinarea mărfurilor și prelevarea|art. 182]] examination and sampling, [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 186. Acordarea liberului de vamă|art. 186]] release, and [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 187. Liberul de vamă condiționat|art. 187]] conditional release.

For commercial practice, the declaration should be treated as a high-risk legal statement, not as a logistics form. Errors in description, classification, origin, value or documents can create debt, penalties, seizure and post-clearance audit exposure.

## 12. Tax interface at import

Import VAT uses the Fiscal Code but is collected through customs. Start with:

- customs value under [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 72. Metoda de determinare a valorii|Customs Code art. 72]];
- import VAT taxable value under [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul III#Articolul 100. Valoarea impozabilă a mărfurilor|Fiscal Code Titlul III art. 100]];
- excise object/base and rates under [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul IV]];
- customs debt and payment under [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 109. Notificarea datoriei vamale|arts. 109]]-[[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)#Articolul 113. Termenele generale de plată|113]].

This is why import files need both customs and tax review. A customs valuation adjustment can increase customs duty, import VAT and excise simultaneously.

## 13. Commercial drafting implications

Import/export contracts should allocate:

1. who is importer/exporter of record;
2. who determines tariff classification;
3. who bears origin-document risk;
4. who bears customs valuation adjustments;
5. who pays import VAT and whether it is recoverable;
6. who provides guarantees;
7. who controls post-clearance audits and appeals;
8. whether Incoterms align with customs-declaration responsibility.

Customs risk often sits outside the visible price clause. A cheap import can become expensive if value, origin or classification is wrong.

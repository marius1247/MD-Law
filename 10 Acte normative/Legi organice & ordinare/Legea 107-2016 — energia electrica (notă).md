# Legea nr. 107/2016 cu privire la energia electrică — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 107-2016 — energia electrica (text)]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Regulator:** [[ANRE]]

> [!danger] SUPERSEDED — this law was repealed on 19 August 2025
> [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)|Legea nr. 164/2025]] replaced it in full. Read that note instead for the law in force. This note is retained because L107/2016 still governs **legacy positions** — installations admitted into operation, and projects designed, before 19.08.2025.

> [!warning] Scope of this note
> The ingested text is **truncated at Art. 16** ([[Status ingestie — Energetica]]). Analysis below covers Chapters I–III only. Market, tariff, supply and TSO/DSO chapters are not yet in the vault — do not treat the absence of a provision here as evidence it doesn't exist.

## What it is
The sectoral organic law for electricity. It builds the general legal framework for organising, regulating and monitoring the electricity sector, and **transposes Directive 2009/72/EC** (EU internal electricity market) — the reason its architecture mirrors the EU third package.

## Structure (as ingested)
- **Cap. I — Dispoziţii generale**: purpose & scope (art. 1), definitions (art. 2), regulatory objectives (art. 3)
- **Cap. II — Competenţe administrative şi de reglementare**: Government (art. 4), local authorities (art. 5), **the regulator** (art. 6), ANRE's attributions (art. 7–8), transparency (art. 9)
- **Cap. III — Organizarea şi licenţierea activităţilor**: activities in the sector (art. 10), **public service obligations** (art. 11), licensing principles (art. 12), scope of licences & outsourcing (art. 13), conditions & procedure (art. 14), licence-holder duties (art. 15), amendment/suspension (art. 16)

## Provisions worth pinning
- **Art. 1(1)** — the objectives list is the interpretive key for the whole sector: accessibility, reliability, continuity, quality, transparency, free market access, security of supply, consumer rights → [[Legea 107-2016 — energia electrica (text)#Articolul 1. Scopul legii şi sfera de aplicare]]
- **Art. 6–7** — establishes [[ANRE]] and enumerates its powers. Every ANRE act in this vault cites an article here as its `temei legal`.
- **Art. 7(2)(a)** → tariff methodologies ([[HANRE 486-2017 — Metodologie tarife transport EE (text)|486/2017]], [[HANRE 64-2018 — Metodologie tarife distributie EE (text)|64/2018]])
- **Art. 7(3)(a)** → [[HANRE 283-2020 — Regulile pietei energiei electrice (text)|electricity market rules]]
- **Art. 53(4) & 96(8)** → [[HANRE 423-2019 — Codul retelelor electrice (text)|network code]] *(articles beyond the truncation point)*
- **Art. 4(1)(m)** → [[HG 820-2024 — situatii exceptionale electroenergetic (text)|HG 820/2024]] on emergency preparedness
- **Art. 11** — public service obligations: the hook through which social/security objectives are imposed on market participants.

## How it connects
- Sits under the framework law [[Legea 174-2017 — energetica (text)|Legea 174/2017]]; parallel structure to [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (text)|Legea 108/2016]] for gas.
- Delegates operational detail downward to [[ANRE]] — see the cascade in [[MOC — Energetică]].
- Renewables support sits in a separate statute ([[Legea 10-2016 — surse regenerabile (text)|Legea 10/2016]]) but feeds the same market.

## My conclusions / open questions
- The law is **deliberately a delegation statute**: it sets objectives and competences, then hands the binding operational rules to ANRE. Practical questions (what tariff applies, how to connect, who bears cost) are almost never answered here — they're answered in tier-3 ANRE acts. Budget analysis time accordingly.
- Licensing (arts. 12–16) is the main entry barrier for any commercial activity in the sector — the first thing to check for a client entering the market.
- **Open:** the unbundling regime and TSO certification sit in the chapters not yet ingested; also the supplier-of-last-resort and universal-service mechanism. Complete the text before advising on any of these.

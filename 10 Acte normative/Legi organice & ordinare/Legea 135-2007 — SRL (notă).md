# Legea nr. 135/2007 privind societățile cu răspundere limitată — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)]] ✅ *complete* · **Hub:** [[MOC — Societăți & Guvernanță corporativă]] · **Synthesis:** [[Societăți & guvernanță — sinteza]] · **Registry:** [[ASP]]

> [!success] Full text now in the vault
> The complete consolidated SRL statute — **93 counted articles** — was ingested on 2026-07-26 from legis.md doc_id `153674`. It is the default statute for Moldovan private companies and should now replace the placeholders in the concept layer: [[Concept — Capital social]], [[Concept — Administrator]], [[Concept — Adunarea generală]], [[Concept — Consiliul societății]], [[Concept — Acțiune vs parte socială]].

> [!tip] Practical thesis
> The SRL is a **closed, register-centred company form**. Its essential mechanics are: constitutive state registration, freely set capital, one participation per member, transfer through the [[ASP]] register, general meeting + administrator as the core organs, and creditor protection through capital-maintenance, distribution and insolvency triggers.

## 1. What it is

Legea 135/2007 governs the formation, operation, reorganisation and liquidation of the Moldovan limited liability company. The statutory definition is in [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 2]]: the company has legal personality, capital divided into *părți sociale*, and obligations secured only by the company's patrimony. Members bear loss risk only up to their participation, except where the law pierces that limit.

This is the statute most corporate work starts from. Unless a regulator or financing structure requires a joint-stock company, the SRL is the usual vehicle: cheaper governance, no securities-market infrastructure, no CNPF issuer layer, and highly adjustable charter economics.

## 3. Formation is contractual until registration, then corporate

- **Legal personality begins on state registration** — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 9]]. The company is not a person before registration; pre-registration actors remain jointly and unlimitedly liable unless the company later assumes the obligations — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 15]].
- **Founders become members on registration** — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 11]]. Ordinary SRLs no longer have a general statutory member cap; the special 200-member cap applies to crowdfunding investment vehicles.
- **The constitutive act is the statute** — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 12]]. It binds founders before registration like a formation contract, then binds members and organs as a corporate constitution.
- **Mandatory statute content** includes founders, name, object, capital, nominal participation values, contribution terms, organs, representation, branches and liquidation/reorganisation rules — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 13]].
- **Statute publicity matters**: provisions and amendments are opposable to third parties only from registration in the State Register — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 14]].
- **Single-member SRL** is expressly contemplated, implementing the single-member company directive logic — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 18]].

> [!warning] Registration is not a formality for third-party effects
> If a charter amendment, seat change, administrator change or participation transfer is sitting only in a signed minute, it is not the full legal fact for third parties. The SRL statute repeatedly sends the decisive act to the State Register. Cross-check against [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 16]] and [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 33]].

## 4. Capital: no floor, but real maintenance rules

The SRL has **no general statutory minimum share capital**. [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 21]] defines capital as the minimum value of assets, expressed in lei, that the company must hold, and says the amount is set in the statute and/or recorded in the State Register. The old MDL 5,400 floor is gone.

Important mechanics:

- **Contributions are presumed cash unless the statute says otherwise** — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 22]].
- **Full payment is due within 6 months of registration** — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 22]].
- **In-kind contributions** must be civil-circuit assets; valuation approval is dealt with in the statute/general meeting mechanics — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 23]].
- **No return of contributions during the company's life** — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 22]].
- **Capital increase** may use profits/reserves, additional contributions, or debt-to-equity conversion — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 33]].
- **Additional contribution increases** carry pre-emption rights unless unanimously adjusted or otherwise validly structured — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 33]].
- **Capital reduction** triggers creditor notice, publication, objection/security rights, and administrator liability for improper registration — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 36]].

The real creditor-protection provision is [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 35]]: if contributions are not paid, or net assets fall below registered capital after the relevant periods, the company must reduce capital, cover losses, or ultimately face liquidation. This is the SRL version of the net-assets trigger in [[Concept — Capital social]].

## 5. The *parte socială* is not an *acțiune*

[[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 24]] is the anchor for [[Concept — Acțiune vs parte socială]]:

- the participation is a fraction of capital;
- one member holds **one** participation, although its size may change;
- it carries the bundle of member rights and obligations;
- one leu in capital gives one vote unless the statute says otherwise;
- the part can be divisible unless the statute limits this.

That makes the SRL participation a register-and-charter participation, not a freely circulating security. Ownership diligence looks to [[ASP]] and the constitutive act, not to a securities registrar.

## 6. Transfers: pre-emption is the default closing risk

[[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 25]] is the key transfer article:

- transfers to affiliates, other members and the company are free by default, unless the statute provides otherwise;
- a member cannot transfer before fully paying the subscribed contribution, except succession;
- sales to outsiders trigger default pre-emption for other members, and then the company, unless the statute excludes or modifies it;
- the selling member must send a written offer to the administrator, who notifies the members within **3 working days**;
- members have **15 days** to accept; the company has a further 15-day window if members do not exercise;
- a third-party sale must not be below the offered price;
- contrary clauses are null for the mandatory core.

Registration is constitutive for ownership transfer: [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 30]] says the part is considered acquired by the buyer from the date the State Register records the transfer. A signed SPA alone is not the end of the transfer.

> [!warning] SPA drafting gotcha
> Build SRL transfers around the register. Collect pre-emption waivers or run the statutory process before completion; define completion as registration; and check for pledges, seizures or other notations before closing. A clean contract with an unregistered transfer is not clean title.

## 7. Profit distributions: capital + solvency tests

The dividend rules are unusually practical:

- annual and interim dividends are permitted — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 39]];
- interim dividends require interim financial statements showing sufficient distributable funds;
- profit is distributed pro rata unless the statute or unanimous member decision changes the method;
- dividends are payable in money within 30 days unless otherwise set;
- unclaimed dividends caused by member fault revert to the company after 3 years.

The hard stop is [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 40]]:

- no distribution before all contributions are paid;
- no distribution if net assets would fall below capital + reserve;
- no distribution if due bond obligations are unpaid;
- no payment if the company is insolvent or would become insolvent because of the payment;
- illegal dividends must be returned.

This connects directly to [[Concept — Insolvabilitate]] and [[Concept — Administrator]]: approving or paying distributions near insolvency is a liability event, not just a corporate-formality defect.

## 8. The governance model: meeting + administrator, with optional board

The **general meeting** is the supreme organ — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 48]]. It can be held physically, by correspondence, electronically, or in mixed form. This is a modernised SRL statute, not a paper-only regime.

Exclusive general-meeting competence under [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 49]] includes:

- statute amendments;
- capital changes;
- appointment/removal of board, censor, audit committee where relevant;
- financial statements and profit distribution;
- reorganisation and liquidation;
- gratuitous transfers of company property/rights;
- branches, subsidiaries and co-founder participations;
- bond issuance;
- corporate governance code for relevant public-capital SRLs.

Voting thresholds are concentrated in [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 58]]:

- unanimity for additional contributions, modified profit distribution, agenda change, transformation into unlimited-liability form, and some deep charter economics;
- two-thirds by default for charter amendments, liquidation, reorganisation, gratuitous transfers, branches, subsidiaries/co-founder participations and capital increase;
- majority of all votes for ordinary governance matters, unless the statute adjusts.

Meeting practice matters. The statute regulates notice, agenda, information, minutes, electronic participation and written resolutions — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 52]], [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 54]], [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 55]], [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 56]]. Defects in convening and agenda control remain the most likely attack vector. → [[Concept — Adunarea generală]]

## 9. Optional board, mandatory only above scale

The SRL board is optional unless the company has more than **50 members** — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 63]]. Its competence is charter-driven but may include appointment/removal of the administrator, approval of reports and business plans, internal regulations, and convening meetings — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 64]].

Board members owe the same diligence, loyalty and liability rules as the administrator — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 68]]. In ordinary private SRLs the board is often unnecessary; in joint ventures, public-capital companies and large member bases, it becomes the continuous supervisory organ described in [[Concept — Consiliul societății]].

## 10. Administrator: external authority, internal accountability

The administrator is mandatory and is the executive/representative organ — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 69]]. Appointment and removal are by the general meeting or board if the statute says so, and the data must be registered in the State Register — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 70]].

Key powers and duties:

- represent the company without power of attorney — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 71]];
- keep accounts and registers, inform members, attend meetings, execute decisions — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 72]];
- act with diligence and loyalty — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 72]];
- convene the general meeting if net assets fall below capital — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 72]];
- file for insolvency immediately, and in any event within one month, if insolvency indicators appear and members do not cover losses — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 72]].

The external/internal divide is explicit. Internal limits bind the administrator toward the company — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 73]]. But third parties are protected: the company is bound by administrator acts even outside the object unless the third party knew of the overreach, and internal limits on statutory representation are not opposable to third parties — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 74]].

> [!warning] Due diligence rule
> Verify the administrator in [[ASP]], not only in the minute book. Registered appointment protects third-party reliance; unregistered internal limitations usually do not.

## 11. Control organs: censor, auditor, audit committee

A censor is optional in small SRLs but mandatory when members exceed 15, subject to crowdfunding-vehicle exceptions — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 77]]. Public-interest entities must form an audit committee — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 77]]. The censor checks management, reviews annual financial matters and must convene the general meeting if illegality or harmful conduct is found — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 78]].

The control layer is not decorative in disputed companies: it is the statutory bridge between accounting evidence, administrator conduct and member remedies.

## 12. Reorganisation and liquidation

The SRL statute deliberately delegates reorganisation mechanics to the Civil Code: merger, division and transformation are governed by the Civil Code — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 80]]. Dissolution and liquidation follow this law, the Civil Code and other laws — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 81]]. In insolvency, [[Legea 149-2012 — insolvabilitate (text)]] displaces ordinary governance and the administrator/liquidator framework takes over.

This is the corporate-law tool for restructurings described in [[Societăți & guvernanță — sinteza]], including energy-sector unbundling and group simplification transactions.

## 13. EU alignment

The preamble states transposition of art. 2(1), art. 4 and art. 5(1) of **Directive 2009/102/EC** on single-member private limited liability companies. The visible effects are:

- express recognition of single-member SRLs;
- written decisions of the sole member;
- public/register transparency of essential facts.

Unlike the SA statute, this is not the main vehicle for Directive (EU) 2017/1132 or shareholder-rights transposition; it is a lighter, closed-company regime that borrows modern electronic and governance mechanics without becoming a securities issuer.

## Practical takeaways

1. **Default to SRL unless a statute or financing plan requires SA.** No general minimum capital, no issuer register, lower governance cost.
2. **Draft the statute carefully.** It can change transfer conditions, voting economics, profit distribution, board competence and written/electronic decision mechanics.
3. **Treat participation transfers as register closings.** The State Register is where ownership changes become legal.
4. **Do not ignore net assets.** Capital may be freely set, but net-assets and distribution rules are live director-liability triggers.
5. **In troubled companies, start with [[Legea 149-2012 — insolvabilitate (text)#Articolul 14]].** The SRL administrator's one-month filing trigger lines up with the insolvency statute's 30-day duty.

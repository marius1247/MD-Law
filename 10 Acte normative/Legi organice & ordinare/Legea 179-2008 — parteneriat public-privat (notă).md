# Public-Private Partnership Law — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 179-2008 — parteneriat public-privat (text)]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Achiziții publice & Statul]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Legal framework for PPP concessions: project selection, feasibility, tendering, risk allocation, and Government guarantee mechanisms for infrastructure projects.
> **Primary business risk:** See risk matrix below.

## 3. Legal ambiguities, vulnerabilities & risk matrix

| Provision | Flaw / ambiguity | Practical risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **State guarantee** | Sovereign guarantee delays | Funding gap | Secure Ministry of Finance approval early |
| **Termination** | Ambiguous compensation on early termination | Stranded asset risk | Model termination payment waterfall in concession agreement |

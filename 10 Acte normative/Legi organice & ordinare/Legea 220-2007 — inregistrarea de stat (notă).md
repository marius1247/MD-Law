# Legea nr. 220/2007 privind înregistrarea de stat — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)]] ✅ *complete* · **Hub:** [[MOC — Societăți & Guvernanță corporativă]] · **Synthesis:** [[Societăți & guvernanță — sinteza]] · **Authority:** [[ASP]]

> [!success] Full text now in the vault
> The complete consolidated registration statute — **44 counted articles** — was ingested on 2026-07-26 from legis.md doc_id `155438`. It is the procedural layer that makes the SRL and SA statutes work in practice.

> [!tip] Practical thesis
> The State Register does three jobs: **creates** legal personality, **publicises** facts for third-party reliance, and **controls** entry through a legality check. Do not confuse it with business licensing, tax registration, securities registration, or beneficial-ownership compliance, although the procedure now interoperates with those systems.

## 1. What it is

Legea 220/2007 regulates:

- state registration of legal persons and individual entrepreneurs;
- keeping the State Register;
- registration of amendments, reorganisations, dissolutions and radiations;
- the legal status of the registration authority and registrars.

The scope is stated in [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 1]]. The key definition is in [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 2]]: state registration certifies formation, reorganisation, liquidation, suspension/resumption and amendments, by entering data in the State Register, with the effect of acquiring or losing legal capacity.

## 3. Constitutive registration

For company law, the crucial point is constitutive effect:

- an SRL acquires legal personality on state registration — [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 9]];
- an SA is registered through the general framework and then its securities are registered separately — [[Legea 1134-1997 — societati pe actiuni (text)#Articolul 35]] and [[Legea 1134-1997 — societati pe actiuni (text)#Articolul 36]];
- under this act, the legal person is considered registered on the date of the registrar's decision — [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 11]].

The decision assigns an **IDNO**, which also operates as the fiscal code, and the registration procedure transmits data electronically for tax, statistics, medical and social registration — [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 11]].

> [!warning] Formation documents are not the company
> Signature of the statute or founding decision does not itself create legal personality. Until registration, the actors remain in the pre-company risk zone described in [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 15]] and the Civil Code.

## 4. Documents and the one-stop shop logic

For legal-person registration, [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 7]] requires:

- registration application;
- founding decision and constitutive documents;
- for SAs, information on the company registrar;
- beneficial-owner information under AML law;
- other documents or approvals required by special laws.

If a special law requires prior approval for formation in a regulated sector, ASP checks that approval through automated data exchange with the issuing authority — [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 7]]. This matters for banks, insurers and other regulated entities: registration is still not the same as the sector authorisation.

[[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 8]] is now strongly digital:

- documents may be submitted electronically with electronic signature, on paper at ASP, or by registered mail;
- electronic powers of representation can be verified through the relevant representation register;
- foreign legal-person documents need Romanian translation and apostille/supralegalisation unless a treaty applies;
- ASP must provide standard-form acts and applications online;
- ASP may not refuse to receive the application or request documents beyond those required by law.

The ordinary registration term is **24 hours** from the next working day after required documents are presented — [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 5]].

## 5. Name and seat are registered facts

The company name is registered and must be used exactly as entered in the State Register — [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 9]]. ASP checks availability, distinctiveness and linguistic correctness. A name can be reserved for up to 6 months.

The seat is also a registered fact. [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 10]] is strict:

- ASP records the seat stated in the constitutive documents and cannot require additional proof;
- the company bears responsibility for authenticity of the seat data;
- a seat change must be registered within 30 days;
- the seat change becomes opposable to third parties from registration.

This interacts with [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)#Articolul 4]] and [[Legea 1134-1997 — societati pe actiuni (text)#Articolul 7]].

## 6. The registrar's legality check and refusal grounds

The registrar checks whether applications and documents satisfy legal requirements, obtains inter-authority data electronically, and decides registration or reasoned refusal — [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 11]].

Refusal is limited. [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 13]] allows refusal for, among others:

- missing required documents;
- non-compliant constitutive or other documents;
- breach of legal procedure for formation/reorganisation/liquidation/suspension/resumption/amendments;
- late filing of amendments without justification;
- judicial/enforcement/authority registration prohibitions;
- pledge, seizure or other encumbrance over the participation where transfer is not permitted;
- founder tax-evasion criminal-record bar.

The registrar **cannot refuse for reasons of expediency** — [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 13]]. Refusal can be challenged in court.

## 7. Registration decisions and file integrity

The registration decision is issued electronically and on paper if requested — [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 14]]. It can be corrected for obvious errors but can be annulled only by court, subject to the special case where the court declares void the underlying documents.

[[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 15]] requires ASP to keep registration files for each legal person and branch, including documents submitted and register transcriptions. Files are also archived electronically in machine-readable/searchable format, including structured data. This is important for evidence: the register is not only a snapshot; ASP keeps the legal file history.

## 8. Amendments: 30-day filing and legal effect on registration

[[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 16]] is one of the most important provisions in corporate practice:

- amendments to constitutive acts and State Register data are registered through the same general procedure;
- the company must file within **30 days** of the amendment decision;
- special timing applies for in-kind capital increases, share-issue/tresury-share cancellation results and SA reorganisations;
- amendments have legal force from registration.

The documents are listed in [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 17]]: application, competent-organ resolution, new statute or addendum, and other required acts/approvals.

## 9. Administrator mandate registration and resignation

The register contains the administrator's identity and the term of mandate — [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 33]]. Publicly visible data include the administrator's name — [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 34]].

The administrator can also get out of the register. [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 17]] allows a person registered as administrator to resign and request deletion if:

- the mandate term expired;
- the office ended under law or constitutive documents;
- the person has the right to resign, asked the competent organ to act, and more than 30 days passed.

The administrator must convene the competent organ within that period, and the legal person must adopt the necessary decision and complete publicity formalities. This solves the common problem of a director trapped in the register because shareholders refuse to process resignation.

> [!tip] Counterparty authority check
> For third-party authority, ask first: who is the registered administrator at [[ASP]]? Internal minutes matter for internal liability, but public registration is the practical reliance point. See [[Concept — Administrator]].

## 10. Register notes and public information

[[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 19]] allows notes in the register without a registration decision, including:

- licences issued/suspended/withdrawn/annulled;
- annual financial situation data from the statistics authority;
- seizure of a participation or enterprise as a going concern;
- prohibitions from courts/enforcement authorities;
- passive or inactive status;
- bank accounts, account-operation suspensions and public-budget debt.

The substantive content of the State Register is extensive under [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 33]]: name, legal form, registration date and IDNO, seat, founders/members and administrator, administrator term, beneficial owners, share capital and quotas, annual financial data, insolvency/reorganisation/dissolution entries, licences, prohibitions, public-budget debt, liquidators, inactive/passive status, notations and SA registrar information.

## 11. Publicity and reliance

[[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 34]] states that data from the State Register and constitutive acts are public, subject to information-access, interoperability, state/commercial secrecy, personal-data and registry law limits. Information may be obtained by extract, copies, information from constitutive acts, certificates, or interoperability.

[[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 34]] makes public online access free for key data: name, legal form, IDNO, registration date, seat, administrator, founders/members and participation quotas, and status notes such as passive, inactive, reorganisation, radiation or suspension. The public online data is updated each working day and is official.

This is the statutory basis for the domain-synthesis point: the register **publicises** facts and supports third-party reliance.

## 12. Reorganisation and dissolution are also register procedures

Reorganisation starts with a register note. A legal person undergoing reorganisation must notify ASP within 30 days; ASP enters "în reorganizare" — [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 20]]. After the creditor-notice period, the registration file must include the merger/division project, decisions, constitutive acts, creditor-security/debt-payment evidence, authorisations where required and transfer act or allocation balance sheet — [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 21]].

Dissolution registration enters "în lichidare" — [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 23]]. From that moment the legal person in liquidation cannot be founder/member of another legal person. Unless another liquidator is appointed, the administrator becomes liquidator on dissolution registration.

Radiation is the legal end: a legal person is considered liquidated from the decision of deletion from the State Register — [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 25]].

## 13. Inactive and suspended companies

The statute contains administrative cleanup mechanisms:

- inactive legal persons meeting statutory conditions are radiated ex officio after notice and a 2-month claims window — [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 26]];
- a company suspended for 3 years and not resumed may be radiated by ASP if it has no public-budget debts — [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 26]];
- from suspension until radiation, the administrator may not conduct activity and is personally and solidarily liable for operations undertaken contrary to that rule — [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)#Articolul 26]].

This matters in acquisition work: a "quiet" company may be passive, inactive, in suspension, in reorganisation or already on an ex officio radiation path.

## Practical takeaways

1. **Registration creates the company.** Formation documents are not enough.
2. **Amendments count from registration.** Put 30-day filing deadlines in closing checklists.
3. **The administrator is a register fact.** Counterparties verify authority through [[ASP]].
4. **SA files need two layers.** ASP registers the legal person; [[CNPF]]/registrar infrastructure handles securities.
5. **Extracts are not the whole file.** For disputes, request underlying constitutive documents and decisions where available.
6. **Status notes are diligence signals.** Passive, inactive, public-budget debt, prohibition, pledge/seizure and insolvency notes can change the transaction.

# Legea nr. 244 din 20.10.2024 — revizuirea Constituției (EU integration)

The **constitutional law** that amended the Constitution following the 20 Oct 2024 referendum. Published MO457-458/05.11.24 art. 633; **in force 5 Nov 2024**.

## What it changed
- Preamble: added recitals on European identity / irreversibility of the European course and declared **EU integration a strategic objective**.
- Inserted **Titlul V¹ — Integrarea în Uniunea Europeană** and **art. 140¹**: accession to EU constitutive treaties by organic law; **priority of EU constitutive treaties and binding EU acts over contrary domestic law** post-accession.

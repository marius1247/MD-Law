# Legea nr. 325/2025 privind achizițiile publice — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 325-2025 — achizitii publice (text)]] ✅ *91 articles — annexes 1–14 not in upload PDF* · **Sectoral utilities:** [[Legea 74-2020 — achizitii sectoriale (notă)]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Achiziții publice & Statul]] · **Synthesis:** [[Achiziții publice — sinteza]]

> [!important] Effective 01 January 2027 — replaces L131/2015
> Published MO 76–79/12.02.2026 art. 39. **Art. 90 alin. (4)** abrogates **Legea nr. 131/2015** at entry. Ongoing procedures and executing contracts continue under the law in force at initiation (**art. 91**).

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** New general public procurement statute — thresholds, procedures, transparency, contract modification rules, social/reserved procurement, design contests, and institutional architecture (AAP monitoring, ANSC remedies).
> **Primary business risk:** Bidders and contracting authorities still operating on L131/2015 templates until 2027 must retool procedures, certification rules, and contest routes; wrong-law citations in live tenders after 01.01.2027 are a nullity risk.

---

## 1. Statutory hierarchy & legal foundation

* **Primary legal basis:** Organic law adopted by Parliament; implements EU **Directive 2014/24/EU** (partial transpose listed in preamble; annexes I–IX, XII, XIV).
* **Supersedes:** [[Legea 131-2015 — achizitii publice (text)|Legea nr. 131/2015]] — abrogated on entry (art. 90 alin. (4)).
* **Parallel regime:** Utilities/energy/water/transport/postal procurement remains under [[Legea 74-2020 — achizitii sectoriale (notă)|L74/2020]] — do not assume one law covers all state spending.
* **Administrative remedies:** Prior complaint and judicial review → [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]]; procurement-specific contestation → **ANSC** (arts. 82–84).
* **Civil contracts:** Performance disputes → [[Codul civil 1107-2002 (notă)]] Cartea III.

---

## 2. Practical legal mechanics & key provisions

### A. Scope and thresholds (art. 1)

* **Standard thresholds** (art. 1 alin. (1)) — e.g. works **1M MDL**; central-authority goods/services **600k**; local/public-law entities **800k**; defence goods (annex 1 list) **600k**; social/specific services (annex 2) **1M**.
* **Higher-threshold particularities** (art. 1 alin. (2)) — e.g. goods/services **2.3M**; works **90M**; social services **7M** — triggers stricter EU-aligned rules including **mandatory EU publication** where stated.
* **Below-threshold** and **small-value** regimes in later chapters — verify against live AAP thresholds for GPA/WTO values (art. 1 alin. (7)).

### B. Core actors (art. 2, cap. XIII)

* **Autoritate contractantă** → [[Concept — Autoritate contractantă]]
* **Operator economic** / bidder
* **AAP** — policy, monitoring, certification framework (art. 85–86)
* **ANSC** — contestation body (art. 82–84) → [[Concept — Contestare (ANSC)]]
* **Grup de lucru** — mandatory composition, certification for capital investment/design-build (arts. 87–88)

### C. Procedures (cap. IV–VI)

* Open, restricted, competitive negotiation, competitive dialogue, negotiation without prior publication — selection depends on value band and justification duties.
* **Publication** — national MTender ecosystem + EU OJEU forms where mandatory (annex references 4–12 — download full annex pack from legis.md).
* **Documentația de atribuire** → [[Concept — Documentația de atribuire]]
* **Procedură de achiziție** → [[Concept — Procedură de achiziție]]

### D. Contract performance & modification (cap. XI)

* Strict **contract modification** rules with cumulative value caps and reporting to AAP (art. 74) — critical for energy/works extensions.
* **Subcontracting** disclosure in award notice (art. 84).

### E. Transition (arts. 90–91)

* Entry **01.01.2027**; Government must align secondary legislation before entry (art. 90 alin. (3)).
* **Grandfathering:** procedures started and contracts executing at entry continue under prior law.

## 3. Legal ambiguities, vulnerabilities & risk matrix

| Provision | Identified flaw / ambiguity | Practical risk | Recommended strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Art. 91** grandfathering | Scope of “în desfășurare” vs “în curs de executare” | Wrong remedy route or procedure challenge | Document procedure initiation date; map to L131 vs L325 before Jan 2027 |
| **Annexes missing in vault PDF** | Formulare UE / CPV lists not in upload | Invalid notices if wrong form used | Download full consolidare doc_id `152974` from legis.md |
| **Dual procurement laws** | L325 general vs L74 sectoral | Entity misclassified — wrong law applied | Run sectoral-activity test under L74 before assuming L325 |
| **Certification (arts. 87–88)** | Mandatory certified specialist as secretary for design-build above art. 1(2) thresholds | Procedure voidable | Staff certification calendar before 2027 |
| **ANSC vs court** | Parallel tracks | Premature court action | Exhaust ANSC where required → [[Concept — Contestare (ANSC)]] |

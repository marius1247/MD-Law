# HANRE nr. 112/2019 — racordarea la rețelele de gaze naturale — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 112-2019 — racordarea la retelele de gaze (text)]] ✅ *complete + Anexa 5³ CTP noncasnic* · **Gas law:** [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)]] · **Network code:** [[HANRE 420-2019 — Codul retelelor de gaze naturale (notă)]] · **Supply:** [[HANRE 113-2019 — furnizarea gazelor naturale (notă)]] · **QoS:** [[HANRE 422-2019 — calitate servicii transport distributie gaze (notă)|422/2019]] · **Concept:** [[Concept — Racordare la rețea]] · [[Concept — Consum tehnologic gaze]] · **Regulator:** [[ANRE]]

> [!note] Current gas connection layer
> Unlike [[HANRE 168-2019 — racordarea la retelele electrice (notă)]] (abrogated by 311/2026), this regulation remains live under L108. Consolidation records [[HANRE 328-2025 — modificare Codul retelelor de gaze naturale (notă)|HANRE 328/2025]] amendments (e.g. permit deadline **15 calendar → 10 working days**; OSD advance-payment tools vs risky suppliers).

> [!warning] Naming trap
> Do **not** confuse this with electricity connection ([[HANRE 311-2026 — racordarea la retelele electrice (notă)|311/2026]]) or with the **Gas Network Code** ([[HANRE 420-2019 — Codul retelelor de gaze naturale (notă)|420/2019]]). 112 = connection workflow; 420 = network operation / capacity / balancing (including conditional capacity via 328/310).

## 1. What it is
Governs connection to natural gas networks and provision of transmission/distribution *connection* services: utilisation and production installations, storage, interconnection, modification of existing installations, permits, technical conditions, service contracts, disconnection, reconnection, suspension and limitation.

## 2. Legal basis
Issued under art. 7(1)(k) of [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)|L108/2016]].
Pair with [[HANRE 420-2019 — Codul retelelor de gaze naturale (notă)]]: 112 = connection workflow; 420 = operation, interoperability, capacity, balancing.

## 3. Connection stages (pt. 5)
1. application for connection permit;
2. issuance of the permit;
3. connection contract and payment of design/connection costs where applicable;
4. design of gas installations;
5. coordination of design documents;
6. execution, reception and putting under pressure;
7. proof of technical-service capacity or technical-service contract where required *(added by 328/2025 — safety/maintenance compliance)*.

## 4. Application and permit
Permit required for a new gas installation or modification (debit/pressure changes, relocation, adding appliances).
No permit for like-for-like appliance replacement by an authorised person under the project — consumer still informs operator and supplier.
Deadlines after complete filing: **10 working days** for potential final consumers *(328/2025)*; **30 calendar days** for producers and storage operators.
Permit is free, valid **12 months**, and must state connection point, pressure, pipe diameter, metering requirements and works needed.

## 5. Refusal and complaint
Refusal must be written and technically/economically justified, with measures (including network development) and concrete timing to remove the grounds.
Complaint → [[ANRE]]; later litigation may fall under [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]].

## 6. Design and pressurisation
Connection/reconnection enabling consumption or injection is performed exclusively by the system operator.
Applicant may ask the operator to design/execute or hire another authorised person, subject to operator coordination.
Ordinary operator deadlines: **45 calendar days** household / **60 calendar days** non-household, subject to justified exceptions.

## 7. Risk matrix

| Risk | Where it bites | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| Stale 15-calendar-day advice | Permit deadline post-328 | Client diary still uses pre-328 clock |
| Permit expiry (12 months) | Stages 3–6 | Works start after validity lapses → re-apply |
| Cost / owner consent | Contract & private land | Networks only *technically* serviced by operator |
| Confusing 112 with 420/310 | Capacity / conditional capacity | Transit corridor dispute wrongly framed as connection |
| Supply without connection | HANRE 113 | Contract signed, no pressurisation / no permit |
| OSD advance-payment vs risky supplier | 328 tweak to 112 | Supplier cash-flow / access blocked |

## 8. Client checklist

- [ ] Confirm act: gas connection **112/2019**, not electricity 311/2026.
- [ ] Use **10 WD** (final consumers) / **30 CD** (producers/storage) for permit clock.
- [ ] Diary 12-month permit validity before design/execution spend.
- [ ] Separate permit → contract → design coordination → reception → pressurisation.
- [ ] For capacity/balancing/corridor products → [[HANRE 420-2019 — Codul retelelor de gaze naturale (notă)]] + [[HANRE 310-2026 — modificare Codul retelelor de gaze naturale (notă)]].
- [ ] Pair supply start with [[HANRE 113-2019 — furnizarea gazelor naturale (notă)]].
- [ ] Refusal file: written grounds + development measures + ANRE complaint path.

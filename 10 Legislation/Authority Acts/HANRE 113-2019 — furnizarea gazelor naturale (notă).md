# HANRE nr. 113/2019 — furnizarea gazelor naturale — Analysis
**Raw text:** [[HANRE 113-2019 — furnizarea gazelor naturale (text)]] ✅ *complete* · **Gas law:** [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)]] · **Connection:** [[HANRE 112-2019 — racordarea la retelele de gaze (notă)]] · **Network code:** [[HANRE 420-2019 — Codul retelelor de gaze naturale (notă)]]
> [!note] Current supply regulation
> The consolidated text records HANRE 177/2026 amendments, including the terminology shift from `reziliere` to `rezoluțiune`, supplier offer rules, offer-comparison tooling and fixed-price contracts.

## 1. What it is
This is the retail gas-supply regulation for final consumers, suppliers and system operators.
It covers supply, including last-resort and public-service supply; contracting and billing; disconnection and reconnection; limitation and interruption; service quality; complaints; and comparison of supplier offers.
It is the customer-facing companion to [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)]].

## 2. Legal basis and neighbours
The decision cites arts. 85(10) and 114(22) of [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)|L108/2016]].
For physical connection and network service use [[HANRE 112-2019 — racordarea la retelele de gaze (notă)]].
For transmission/distribution network operation use [[HANRE 420-2019 — Codul retelelor de gaze naturale (notă)]].

## 3. Contracting
Supply is based on a supply contract between final consumer and supplier.
The consumer may choose any supplier. Price is negotiated except where the contract is with a public-service supplier and the price is approved by [[ANRE]].
Last-resort supply is temporary under [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)|L108/2016]]. Do not confuse it with ordinary public-service supply for protected categories.

## 4. Offers and fixed-price contracts
HANRE 177/2026 added public-offer and comparison-tool provisions.
Suppliers must publish offers and provide free commercial information so consumers can make informed choices.
Consumers may accept a standard offer or request negotiated terms.
The regulation now recognises fixed-price contracts for at least 12 months, with specific principles and requirements.

## 5. Billing and payment
The regulation defines erroneous invoices and distinguishes them from estimated-consumption invoices.
For a disputed invoice, preserve the invoice, meter-reading data, supplier/operator correspondence, payment or objection proof and any complaint filed under this regulation.

## 6. Disconnection and reconnection
The regulation sets notice, grounds and procedures for disconnection and reconnection.
Disconnection can be about debt, safety or network integrity, so identify whether the relevant actor is the supplier, the system operator or both.
For physical reconnection steps, cross-check [[HANRE 112-2019 — racordarea la retelele de gaze (notă)]].

## 7. Quality and complaints
The regulation includes supply-quality obligations and complaint procedures.
Complaints may concern billing, contract performance, quality, consumption data, disconnection, reconnection or refusal to contract.
Escalation may go to [[ANRE]] and then, depending on the act, to administrative litigation under [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]].

## Practitioner cautions
- Identify the consumer category before assuming regulated-price protection.
- Distinguish supplier obligations from operator obligations.
- Object promptly to disputed invoices before disconnection.
- Check fixed-price contracts for lawful adjustment clauses.
- Pair supply status with connection status under [[HANRE 112-2019 — racordarea la retelele de gaze (notă)]].

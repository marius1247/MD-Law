# HANRE nr. 113/2019 — furnizarea gazelor naturale — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 113-2019 — furnizarea gazelor naturale (text)]] ✅ *complete* · **Gas law:** [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)]] · **Connection:** [[HANRE 112-2019 — racordarea la retelele de gaze (notă)]] · **Network code:** [[HANRE 420-2019 — Codul retelelor de gaze naturale (notă)]] · **Omnibus amend:** [[HANRE 177-2026 — modificarea unor hotarari ANRE (notă)]] · **Concept:** [[Concept — Furnizor de ultimă opțiune]]

> [!note] Current supply regulation
> Consolidated text records [[HANRE 177-2026 — modificarea unor hotarari ANRE (notă)|HANRE 177/2026]]: *reziliere* → **rezoluțiune**, public offers, ANRE offer-comparison tool, fixed-price ≥12-month contracts, large-consumer imbalance pass-through, competitive push for mid/large non-households.

> [!warning] Numbering trap
> Same number, different year and subject: **113/2019 = gas supply**. Do not confuse with electricity supply ([[HANRE 169-2019 — furnizarea energiei electrice (notă)|169/2019]]) or with gas *connection* ([[HANRE 112-2019 — racordarea la retelele de gaze (notă)|112/2019]]). Switching procedure is HANRE **363/2020** (amended by 177; not yet a vault pair).

## 1. What it is
Retail gas-supply regulation for final consumers, suppliers and system operators: supply including last-resort and public-service supply; contracting and billing; disconnection/reconnection; limitation and interruption; service quality; complaints; comparison of supplier offers.
Customer-facing companion to [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)]].

## 2. Legal basis and neighbours
Cites arts. 85(10) and 114(22) of [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)|L108/2016]].
Physical connection → [[HANRE 112-2019 — racordarea la retelele de gaze (notă)]].
Network operation → [[HANRE 420-2019 — Codul retelelor de gaze naturale (notă)]].
177/2026 also amends supplier-switching procedure **363/2020** (not ingested).

## 3. Contracting
Supply contract between final consumer and supplier; free choice of supplier.
Price negotiated except public-service supplier with ANRE-approved price.
Last-resort supply is temporary under L108 — do not confuse with ordinary public-service supply for protected categories.
Post-177: standard offers vs negotiated terms; fixed-price ≥ **12 months** with precontractual disclosure ≥ **5 WD** and early-exit fee only if disclosed, proportionate, loss-linked.

## 4. Offers and comparison tool (177/2026)
Suppliers must publish offers; ANRE runs a free comparison instrument.
Suppliers with ≥1 household/small non-household contract in the prior month must feed **12-month fixed-price offers**; updates within **1 WD**.
Non-submission → contravention / financial risk.

## 5. Billing and payment
Defines erroneous invoices vs estimated-consumption invoices.
On dispute: preserve invoice, meter data, correspondence, payment/objection proof, complaint file.
Monthly SO reads with actual-vs-estimated flag; electronic invoices on request; dual price units (lei/kWh + lei/1000 m³).

## 6. Disconnection and reconnection
Notice, grounds and procedures for disconnection/reconnection.
Identify actor: supplier (debt/contract) vs system operator (safety/network) vs both.
Physical reconnection steps → [[HANRE 112-2019 — racordarea la retelele de gaze (notă)]].

## 7. Competitive transition & large consumers
Consumers losing regulated-price eligibility under Gas Law art. **89** must contract competitively; public-service suppliers notify resolution; SOs publish annual lists of mid/large non-households by **1 February**.
Non-households > **50 MWh/day**: imbalance-cost pass-through to supplier beyond ANRE daily tolerance — annex detail partly OCR-weak; verify in consolidated text / [[HANRE 177-2026 — modificarea unor hotarari ANRE (notă)]].

## 8. Quality and complaints
Supply-quality obligations and complaint procedures (billing, contract, quality, data, disconnection, refusal to contract).
Escalation → [[ANRE]] → [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]] where applicable.

## 10. Client checklist

- [ ] Disputed invoice: object before disconnection; keep meter evidence.
- [ ] Pair supply status with connection status under [[HANRE 112-2019 — racordarea la retelele de gaze (notă)]].

# HANRE nr. 138/2018 — dezvoltarea rețelelor de distribuție a gazelor naturale — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 138-2018 — dezvoltarea retelelor de distributie gaze (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Gas law:** [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)]] · **Connection:** [[HANRE 112-2019 — racordarea la retelele de gaze (notă)|112/2019]] · **Network code:** [[HANRE 420-2019 — Codul retelelor de gaze naturale (notă)|420/2019]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Gas **DSO network development** — planning, extension and reinforcement of distribution networks. Replaces the 2011 gas extension regulation (via HANRE 439/2011 patch/abrogation).
> **Primary business risk:** Connection refusal ([[HANRE 112-2019 — racordarea la retelele de gaze (notă)|112]]) without checking whether a **development obligation** exists under 138.

## 1. Legal basis

Issued under **L108/2016 art. 49(1)**. Abrogates the gas extension regulation embedded in HANRE 439/2011 and cleans cross-references in that omnibus decision.

| Risk | Mitigation |

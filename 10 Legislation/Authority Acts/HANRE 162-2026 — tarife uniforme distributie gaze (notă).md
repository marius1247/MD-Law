# HANRE nr. 162/2026 — uniform gas DSO tariffs + equalization — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 162-2026 — tarife uniforme distributie gaze (text)]] ✅ (dispositif + Anexa 1–2) · **Methodology:** [[HANRE 443-2020 — Metodologie tarife distributie gaze (notă)|443/2020]] ⚠️ annex gap · **Overlay:** [[HANRE 785-2025 — modificare Metodologie tarife distributie gaze 443-2020 (notă)|785/2025]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Tarife și metodologii ANRE]] · [[MOC — Energetică]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Sets the first applied **uniform gas distribution tariffs** by pressure level (final customers + inter-OSD metering points) and the **equalization compensatory payments** per OSD (Annex 1); **repeals** prior per-OSD tariff decisions (Annex 2).
> **Primary business risk:** Billing or advising 2026 DSO charges from pre-uniform per-OSD decisions after **IF 1 April 2026**.

# HANRE nr. 169/2019 — Regulament privind furnizarea energiei electrice — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 169-2019 — furnizarea energiei electrice (text)]] ⚠️ *truncates at pt. 146* · **Law in force:** [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Connection:** [[HANRE 311-2026 — racordarea la retelele electrice (notă)]] · **Market:** [[HANRE 283-2020 — Regulile pietei energiei electrice (notă)]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** The retail electricity supply rulebook — contracting, billing, disconnection, metering disputes, universal service and last-resort supply.
> **Primary business risk:** Issued under repealed [[Legea 107-2016 — energia electrica (text)|L107/2016]]. Several operative numbers are **stale under L164/2025** (last-resort duration, US perimeter, dynamic/fixed-price contracts, aggregation). Vault text also **cuts mid-pt. 146** — metering-dispute / annex material is incomplete.

> [!danger] Truncation
> Last complete point: **145**. Point **146** breaks mid-sentence. Annexes 1–4 referenced in the regulation are **not in the vault**. Do not invent them.

## 1. Statutory hierarchy & legal foundation

* ANRE regulation under old **L107 arts. 63(7), 96(23)**.
* **Current mandate:** L164 arts. **9(3)(b)** and **110** require ANRE to approve a supply regulation covering quality, mandatory clauses, US and last-resort terms. Expect re-adoption / deep amendment under L164.
* **Currency conflicts to apply L164 over this HANRE:**
  | Topic | HANRE 169 | L164/2025 |
  |---|---|---|
  | Last-resort duration | **90 days** (pts. 7, 67–73) | **≥ 6 months** (art. 115) |
  | Universal service | households + small companies | households + **micro and small** (art. 114) |
  | Multi-supplier / sharing | narrow pt. 34 | arts. 117(2)–(4) |
  | Dynamic / fixed-price contracts | absent | arts. 119, 110(f) |
  | Aggregation / switching aggregator | absent | arts. 120–121 |

## 3. Operative regimes by problem

### A. Contracting & switching
Supply only under contract (pt. 5). Free supplier choice except US/LR (pt. 7). Formation deadlines: **2 working days** (to-be-connected) / **7 calendar days** (already connected) — pt. 44. Anti-switching / termination-fee traps prohibited (pt. 27). Detailed switching timelines now sit in **L164 art. 121** (max 20 days, limited early-exit fees).

### B. Universal service & last resort
→ [[Concept — Furnizor de ultimă opțiune]]

US territorial obligation (pt. 36); cannot refuse eligible consumer except debt rules (pt. 38). LR takeover on supplier failure (pts. 67–73): deemed contract from physical supply; written contract in **8 days**; old **90-day** exit — **override with L164 art. 115 (≥6 months)**.

### C. Billing & payment
Monthly invoice default (pt. 80). Mandatory content (pt. 87). Penalties only if contractual, capped by BNM average credit rate (pt. 91). Erroneous invoices: consumer-favour / supplier-favour correction rules (pts. 92–94). Preventive payment regime (pts. 107–113). **Sistem paușal** for unregistered consumption (pts. 95–100, 138–145) — high litigation zone; annex calculation missing.

### D. Disconnection
Household grounds pt. 115; non-household pt. 117. Notice + weekday/time constraints pts. 116–120. Protections: listed grounds only (pt. 123); no disconnect while invoice contested with supplier/court (pt. 124); ANRE may stay/reconnect during complaint (pt. 125). Non-billing disputes do not suspend payment (pt. 126).

### E. Metering disputes
Pts. 132–145 complete in vault; **pt. 146 incomplete** (disconnection stay while unregistered-consumption decision challenged). Flat-rate recalculation requires proven method of unregistered use (pt. 140); 20-day examination (pt. 141).

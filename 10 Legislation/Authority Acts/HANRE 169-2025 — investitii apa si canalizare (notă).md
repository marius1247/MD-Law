# HANRE nr. 169/2025 — investment principles in water & sewerage — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 169-2025 — investitii apa si canalizare (text)]] ✅ · **Corrected by:** [[Rectificare ANRE 05-03-5694-2025 (notă)]] · **Parent law:** [[Legea 303-2013 — serviciul public apa (notă)]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Achiziții publice & Statul]] *(utilities/infrastructure)* · [[ANRE]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Binding rulebook for planning, approving, reporting and **tariff-recognising** investments by public water/sewerage operators. Replaces HANRE 357/2019. IF with publication (08.04.2025 decision).
> **Primary business risk:** Spending without prior plan approval — **unapproved investments are not accepted into the tariff base**. Efficiency must be quantified; cost overruns are capped.

> [!note] Domain note
> Outside the energy MOC’s core cascade, but ANRE-regulated. Wire here + procurement/utilities hubs; do not file under `10` by industry.

---

## 1. Foundation

* Enabling: Law **303/2013** art. 7(2)(h¹).
* Binds operators of potable water, technological water, sewerage/wastewater treatment, and production/transport for redistribution; plus local public authorities.
* Approver: **ANRE** for licensed operators; **local councils** for non-licensed.

---

## 2. Architecture

| Instrument | Horizon | Key deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Development plan | 3 years | Submit by **30 May** of last year of period; approve in **3 months**; amend ≤1×/year |
| Annual investment plan | 1 year | Submit by **1 November**; approve in **60 days**; publish in **5 days**; amend ≤2×/year (by 31 Oct) |
| Implementation reports | Annual | **1 March** |
| Tariff acceptance decision | After realisation | Separate ANRE/local decision — site checks possible |

**Classification gate:** annual projects must be **mandatory**, **necessary**, or **efficient** (avg annual economic effects over useful life > total investment). Categories **A–H** (new networks → intangibles/other).

**Tariff impact** must be shown: (amortisation + return) / forecast volumes. Overruns generally capped at approved value × CPI; A/B projects may justify more but capped at **15%** of planned value or CPI if higher. Non-A/B unrealised projects cannot roll > **2** consecutive years. Third-party damage works are **not** tariff investments.

## 3. Risk matrix

| Issue | Risk | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| Unapproved spend | Permanent tariff exclusion | No CAPEX without plan line |
| OCR formula at pt. 56 | Wrong efficiency math | Use corrected refs ([[Rectificare ANRE 05-03-5694-2025 (notă)]]); pull PDF for formula |
| Wrong approver | Void process | Licensed → ANRE; else local council |
| Section cross-refs pre-rectificare | Wrong procedure cited | Evaluation = **§7**; approval = **§9** |

## 4. Client checklist

- [ ] Confirm licence status → approver.
- [ ] Build 3-year development plan (local pre-approval) before annual plan.
- [ ] Tag every project A–H + mandatory/necessary/efficient with evidence.
- [ ] Submit annual plan by 1 Nov (signed paper + Excel).
- [ ] Publish detailed approved plan within 5 days of decision.
- [ ] File 1 March realisation reports with commissioning/inventory proof.
- [ ] Apply rectificare section numbers when citing.

# HANRE nr. 177/2026 — omnibus amendment (gas supply & switching) — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 177-2026 — modificarea unor hotarari ANRE (text)]] ✅ · **Parents:** [[HANRE 113-2019 — furnizarea gazelor naturale (text)]] · Regulation on gas supplier switching (ANRE **363/2020**, not yet a vault pair) · **Gas law:** [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Gas-retail modernisation package (IF **20.03.2026**): fixed-price ≥12-month products, ANRE offer-comparison tool, digital contracting, imbalance pass-through for large daily consumers, and competitive-market push for mid/large non-households leaving regulated prices.
> **Primary business risk:** Suppliers who do not publish/feed **12-month fixed-price offers** into ANRE’s comparison tool face sanctions; mid/large non-households who stay passive when regulated-price eligibility ends risk abrupt resolution notices.

> [!note] Naming trap
> Frontmatter historically tagged this under electricity. Substance is **gas** (113/2019 + 363/2020).

## 2. Substantive changes

### A. Contract products & transparency
* Civil-law term cleanup: *reziliere* → **rezoluțiune**.
* Public-service perimeter clarified: households, **small non-households**, essential social-service entities.
* New **instrument de comparare a ofertelor**; suppliers must publish clear commercial offers.
* **Fixed-price, fixed-term ≥ 12 months**: no unilateral price change; precontractual disclosure ≥ **5 WD** before signing; early-exit fee only if disclosed, proportionate, loss-linked (supplier bears proof).

### B. ANRE comparison tool (new §6¹)
Public free tool. Suppliers with ≥1 household/small non-household contract in prior month must submit **12-month fixed-price offers**; updates within **1 WD**. Ranking by final price MDL/m³ and MDL/kWh. Non-submission → contravention/financial risk.

### C. Large-consumer imbalance pass-through
Non-households > **50 MWh/day** must compensate supplier for portfolio imbalance costs beyond ANRE daily tolerance, proportional to contribution — annex detail partly OCR-weak; verify consolidated 113 text.

### D. Debt rescheduling & switching
Rescheduling agreement can cure debt blocks on contracting/switching. Operators must give last **2 years** monthly consumption history. Some switching procedural points abrogated.

### E. Competitive transition
Consumers losing regulated-price eligibility under Gas Law art. **89** must contract competitively; public-service suppliers notify resolution; SOs publish annual lists of mid/large non-households by **1 February**.

### F. Billing / service
Monthly SO reads (actual vs estimated flag); electronic invoices on request; dual price units lei/kWh + lei/1000 m³; expanded service-centre and website duties.

## 4. Client checklist

- [ ] **Suppliers:** build ≥12-month fixed-price product; wire ANRE tool feed; update templates to *rezoluțiune* + disclosure pack.
- [ ] **Mid/large non-households:** confirm art. 89 status; procure competitive supply before resolution notice.
- [ ] **>50 MWh/day sites:** negotiate imbalance allocation expressly.
- [ ] **Switching files:** use rescheduling agreements; demand 24-month history.
- [ ] Ingest / open HANRE **363/2020** when advising switching litigation.

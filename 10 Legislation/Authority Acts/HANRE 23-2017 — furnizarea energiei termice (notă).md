# HANRE nr. 23/2017 — thermal energy supply regulation — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 23-2017 — furnizarea energiei termice (text)]] ⚠️ *annex 1 contract only* · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Parent:** [[Legea 92-2014 — energia termica si cogenerarea (notă)|L92/2014]] · **Condominium bridge:** Legea 187/2022 · **Patch:** [[HANRE 8-2023 — modificare racordare gaze si masurare gaze (notă)|8/2023]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Annex 1 is the **mandatory-structure supply contract** for district heating: parties (including condominium association as service intermediary), delimitation point, quality/continuity duties, metering & billing, disconnection rules, suspension/termination, liability, ANRE dispute channel.
> **Primary business risk:** Advising season start/stop or connection procedure from the contract alone — those live in the **missing regulation body** (e.g. +10°C three-night rule discussed in practice notes).

## Contract architecture (what you can rely on now)

* **Parties:** Supplier ↔ household/non-household consumer **or** condominium association (*intermediar de servicii*) representing unit owners under L187/2022.
* **Object:** Thermal energy at the **delimitation point**; quality parameters attached.
* **Supplier hard duties:** continuous delivery, 24/7 phone, reconnect ≤24h after debt proof, planned-outage notice (≥2 weeks / ≥3 days for tie-in works), direct billing to units including disconnected/non-reading units.
* **Disconnection for non-payment:** generally after 15 days past due (**except apartment blocks**), with **≥1 month** prior notice in standard cases; reconnect same day after payment proof.
* **Economic withdrawal:** supplier may stop if uneconomic, with **≥6 months** notice before **15 October**.
* **Penalties:** negotiable, capped at NBM prior-year weighted average lei loan rate.
* **Dispute ladder:** negotiation → [[ANRE]] → courts.

# HANRE nr. 23/2017 — thermal energy supply regulation — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 23-2017 — furnizarea energiei termice (text)]] ⚠️ *form annexes missing* · **Parent:** [[Legea 92-2014 — energia termica si cogenerarea (notă)|L92/2014]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Amended:** HANRE 792/2023

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Binding regulation for **district-heat connection, supply contracts, metering, billing, interruption, complaints and producer–supplier relations** (~175 points).
> **Primary business risk:** Using wrong connection/billing forms — annexes 1–3 (models) are **placeholders only** in this PDF; annex 1 was rewritten by HANRE 792/2023.

---

## 1. Architecture (operative body present)

* **Connection:** aviz de racordare (15 days), delimitation act, supplier builds connection installation after conditions met.
* **Contract / supply:** supply before heating season starts under defined outdoor-temp rules; illegal tapping / tampering sanctions.
* **Metering & billing:** consumer responsible for meter integrity; interruption / quality / complaint duties on supplier.
* **B2B:** producer–supplier heat purchase contracts; seals on metering.
* **ANRE oversight:** information duties; consumer may escalate to ANRE then court.

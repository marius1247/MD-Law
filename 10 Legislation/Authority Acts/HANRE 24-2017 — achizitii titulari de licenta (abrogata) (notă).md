# HANRE nr. 24/2017 — licence-holder procurement regulation — Analysis *(abrogated)*

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 24-2017 — achizitii titulari de licenta (abrogata) (text)]] ⚠️ *body complete; annexes 1–12 named only* · **Repealed by:** [[HANRE 305-2021 — abrogare HANRE 24-2017 achizitii titulari (notă)]] IF **06.08.2021** · **Live law:** [[Legea 74-2020 — achizitii sectoriale (notă)]]

> [!danger] Not in force
> Do **not** use this act for live procurement advice. Kept for historical disputes, tariff-file archaeology, and to correct vault folklore that still treated it as pending ingestion of a live instrument.

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose (historic):** ANRE-imposed procurement procedures for licence-holders in electricity, heat, gas and water/sewerage — efficiency-at-least-cost discipline for regulated activities.
> **Primary business risk today:** Citing it as current law after L74/2020 + HANRE 305/2021.

---

## 1. Foundation (historic)

* Adopted 26.01.2017; MJ 1205/03.04.2017; MO 119–126 art. 760 / 14.04.2017.
* Enabling articles cited L108/2016, L107/2016, L303/2013 and heat/energy framework provisions then in force.
* Abrogated by [[HANRE 305-2021 — abrogare HANRE 24-2017 achizitii titulari (text)|HANRE 305/2021]] expressly on L74/2020 art. 89(3).

## 2. Architecture of the repealed regulation

| Block | Role |
|---|---|
| Cap. I | Scope, exclusions, definitions |
| Cap. II | Principles / integrity commitments |
| Cap. III | Value thresholds; anti-splitting |
| Cap. IV–V | Procedures, notices, documentation, evaluation, file retention |
| Sanctions | incl. pct. **215** — ANRE may exclude non-compliant procurement cost uplifts from regulated tariffs and/or apply statutory sanctions |
| Annexes 1–12 | Templates / notice content *(not in vault PDF body)* |

Scope covered licence-holders’ purchases of goods, works and services used in regulated activities. Classic public-procurement carve-outs and small-value paths lived in the thresholds chapter — verify against the text for any pre-2021 file.

## 3. Why the vault still keeps it

1. **Currency correction** — multiple MOCs/syntheses listed “HANRE 24 not yet ingested” as if it were still the live bridge to procurement. It was repealed in 2021.
2. **Legacy mandates** — awards, complaints or tariff disputes with facts before 06.08.2021.
3. **Transition narrative** — ANRE self-regulation → parliamentary sectoral statute (L74).

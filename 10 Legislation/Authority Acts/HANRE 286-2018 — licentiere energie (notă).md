# HANRE nr. 286/2018 — regulated tariff/price *application* procedure — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 286-2018 — licentiere energie (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Concepts:** [[Concept — Tarif reglementat]] · [[Concept — Devieri financiare]] · **Related methodologies:** [[HANRE 626-2023 — Metodologie tarife transport EE (notă)]] · [[HANRE 64-2018 — Metodologie tarife distributie EE (notă)]]

> [!danger] Filename is misleading
> Vault short name says *licentiere* (licensing). The act is **not** the energy licensing regulation. It is the **Regulation on procedures for submission and examination of licence-holders’ applications regarding base costs and regulated prices/tariffs** (ANRE decision of 17.10.2018). Licensing doctrine lives in [[Concept — Licență în energetică]] and the sectoral laws ([[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)|L164]], [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)|L108]]), not here.

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Uniform ANRE procedure for (a) approving **base costs**, (b) **annual** regulated price/tariff approval, and (c) **in-period adjustment** when uncontrollable cost factors drive deviations beyond methodology thresholds. Applies to electricity, heat and gas licence-holders; water/sewerage licence-holders insofar as sector law does not provide otherwise.
> **Primary business risk:** Missing the sectoral filing deadline (e.g. electricity/gas annual filing by **1 February**) or filing an incomplete file — ANRE may reject or decide only on the argued portion, and that outcome is **opposable** to the holder.

## 1. Statutory hierarchy & legal foundation

* ANRE secondary act implementing the tariff methodologies’ procedural layer.
* Substantive algorithms remain in the sector methodologies (transport 626/2023+261/2026, distribution 64/2018 annex missing, renewables 375/2017 annex missing, gas 535/2019 not ingested, heat methodologies, water under [[HANRE 169-2025 — investitii apa si canalizare (notă)|169/2025]] investment + tariff methodology).
* Individual ANRE tariff decisions are out of vault scope by design.

## 2. Architecture

| Section | Role |
|---|---|
| **1 — General** | Scope; definitions of annual approval vs adjustment vs base-cost approval |
| **2 — Filing** | Who files, when, how (registry / registered post / e-signed email) |
| **3 — Examination** | **≤180 calendar days** from complete file; info requests; rejection/partial exam |
| **4 — Transparency** | Web publication, 15 WD public comments, draft decision published 15 WD before Board, public Board hearings |

### Key definitions (Section 1)
- **Annual approval** — full tariff/price approval under the methodology.
- **Adjustment** — mid-period review of only those cost elements whose objective, uncontrollable change generates tariff deviations above the methodology’s preset thresholds → [[Concept — Devieri financiare]].
- **Base-cost approval** — multi-year cost base that subsequent annual tariffs build on.

---

## 3. Operative deadlines (Section 2)

**Base costs**
| Sector | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Electricity / gas | **60 days** from IF of the relevant methodology |
| Heat / licensed CHP electricity | **60 days** from licence grant **or 180 days** before end of approved base-cost period |
| Water/sewerage | **60 days** from licence grant or end of approved base-cost period |

**Annual prices/tariffs**
| Sector | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Electricity | by **1 February** |
| Gas | by **1 February** |
| Heat / CHP electricity | by **1 May** |
| Water/sewerage | by **1 June** |

Filing channels: ANRE registry, registered post, or e-mail with qualified electronic signature (Law 91/2014). Cover letter in Romanian, signed by the administrator.

---

## 4. Examination & transparency (Sections 3–4)

* Examination ≤ **180 calendar days** from registration **after** all required documents are in; clock pauses/extends while the holder cures erroneous data.
* ANRE may request any justifying information in writing; non-response → reject or decide only the argued part; holder **cannot later complain** about incompleteness it caused.
* Admitted applications (+ public materials) on ANRE website within **10 WD**; **15 WD** public comment window; optional public debates.
* Examination results + draft Board decision published **15 WD** before adoption; Board sessions on these applications are **public** (notice ≥ **3 WD**).

## 5. Risk matrix

| Provision | Flaw / ambiguity | Practical risk | Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Filename / short title** | Looks like licensing | Wrong doctrine cited in opinions | Always open the regulation text; cite as tariff-application procedure |
| **180-day clock** | Starts only when file is complete | Holder thinks clock runs from first filing | Diary deficiency letters; treat as incomplete until ANRE silent |
| **Partial examination** | ANRE may decide only argued portion | Under-recovery locked in | File complete cost books; never leave optional annexes empty |
| **1 Feb electricity/gas** | Hard annual gate | Missed window → delayed tariff | Internal 15 Jan freeze for board-ready pack |
| **Adjustment vs annual** | Wrong procedure chosen | Rejection / wrong cost set | Map methodology deviation thresholds before choosing track |
| **Public pack** | Confidential cost data vs publication duty | Trade-secret leakage or publication delay | Pre-redact; mark confidential schedules expressly |

## 6. Client checklist

- [ ] Confirm which track: base costs / annual / adjustment.
- [ ] Hit sector deadline (electricity/gas annual → **1 February**).
- [ ] Align numbers to the live methodology (626+261 for transmission; do not use abrogated 486).
- [ ] Prepare Romanian administrator-signed cover + e-signature path if filing by email.
- [ ] Track deficiency letters; do not assume the 180-day clock has started.
- [ ] Monitor ANRE website for the 15 WD comment window and draft decision.
- [ ] Challenge the individual tariff decision under [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]] — not this procedure regulation alone.

# HANRE nr. 297/2022 — măsurare gaze naturale comerciale — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 297-2022 — masurare gaze naturale comerciale (text)]] ✅ *OCR (~136 pts)* · **Amended by:** [[HANRE 8-2023 — modificare racordare gaze si masurare gaze (notă)|8/2023]] (pt. 116) · **Connection neighbour:** [[HANRE 112-2019 — racordarea la retelele de gaze (notă)|112/2019]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Gas law:** [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)|L108/2016]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Replaces HANRE 385/2010 — sets **commercial gas metering** rules: metering-point categories, technical minima, OS/producer/supplier/consumer duties, data collection for billing, dispute handling, and transitional rules for legacy meters.
> **Primary business risk:** Billing/challenge on industrial consumers where metering is **not at the delimitation point** — post-8/2023 **pt. 116** rewrite is operative; cross-read with [[HANRE 8-2023 — modificare racordare gaze si masurare gaze (notă)|8/2023]] and [[HANRE 112-2019 — racordarea la retelele de gaze (notă)|112/2019]] pt. 60¹ (techno-consumption/losses).

## 1. Statutory hierarchy

* **Enabling:** L108 art. 8(1)(g), art. 69(2).
* **Abrogates:** HANRE 385/2010 metering regulation.
* **Amended:** [[HANRE 8-2023 — modificare racordare gaze si masurare gaze (text)|8/2023]] — pt. 116 (metering not at delimitation).
* **Neighbours:** [[HANRE 112-2019 — racordarea la retelele de gaze (notă)|112/2019]] (connection + loss annexes) · [[HANRE 113-2019 — furnizarea gazelor naturale (notă)|113/2019]] (supply/billing) · [[HANRE 420-2019 — Codul retelelor de gaze naturale (notă)|420/2019]] (network code).

## 2. Operative mechanics

### A. Scope (Cap. I)

Applies to producers, OS, suppliers, final consumers — including connection permits, design, installation and operation of metering equipment at **commercial metering points**.

### B. Metering-point categories (pts. 3–4 area)

Defines categories A–E with differing technical requirements; legacy meters may continue for C/D/E under pt. 134 transitional rule.

### C. Billing disputes (pts. 132–133)

Supplier must examine metering complaints within legal deadlines; consumer may attend metrological expertise.

### D. Post-8/2023 overlay

For non-delimitation metering, use **consolidated 297 incl. 8/2023 pt. 116** — critical for industrial behind-OS-meter arrangements.

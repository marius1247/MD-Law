# HANRE nr. 305/2021 — repeal of licence-holder procurement regulation — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 305-2021 — abrogare HANRE 24-2017 achizitii titulari (text)]] ✅ · **Repealed act:** [[HANRE 24-2017 — achizitii titulari de licenta (abrogata) (notă)]] · **Live regime:** [[Legea 74-2020 — achizitii sectoriale (notă)]] · **Hubs:** [[MOC — Achiziții publice & Statul]] · [[MOC — Energetică]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Formally repeals ANRE’s special procurement regulation for energy/heat/gas/water licence-holders (**HANRE 24/2017**), because sectoral procurement moved under **L74/2020**.
> **Primary business risk:** Advising on HANRE 24 thresholds, procedures or ANRE tariff-exclusion sanctions as if still live — they are not (IF **06.08.2021**).

---

## 1. Foundation

* Decision HANRE 305/20.07.2021; MJ registration 1653/27.07.2021; MO 186–189 art. 968 / 06.08.2021; **IF on publication**.
* Enabling basis expressly cites **L74/2020 art. 89(3)** (plus L100/2017 art. 67(1) and ANRE organisational regulation).
* Operative content is two points only: (1) repeal of HANRE 24/2017; (2) IF on MO publication.

---

## 2. What changed commercially

| Before 06.08.2021 | After |
|---|---|
| Licence-holders in EE / heat / gas / water-sewerage ran ANRE’s own procurement regulation (HANRE 24) | Sectoral utilities procurement under [[Legea 74-2020 — achizitii sectoriale (notă)\|L74/2020]] (+ implementing acts) |
| ANRE could exclude non-compliant procurement uplifts from regulated tariffs (HANRE 24 pct. 215) | Tariff discipline continues via licence/tariff law, but **not** via HANRE 24’s procedure code |

Historic contracts / disputes that arose while HANRE 24 was in force may still need the abrogated text for *tempus regit actum* analysis — that is why the vault keeps [[HANRE 24-2017 — achizitii titulari de licenta (abrogata) (text)]].

---

## 3. Risk matrix

| Issue | Risk | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| Stale opinions citing HANRE 24 | Wrong procedure / thresholds | Remap every live procurement file to L74/2020 |
| “ANRE procurement regulation still applies” folklore | Client runs obsolete internal rules | Update compliance manuals; point to L74 + AAP/ANSC track |
| Confusion with general L131/2015 | Wrong statute for utilities | Energy/water/transport/postal → L74; classic public buyers → L131 *(still pending ingestion)* |

---

## 4. Client checklist

- [ ] Confirm entity is a **sectoral contracting entity** under L74 (not ordinary L131 buyer).
- [ ] Strip HANRE 24 from internal procurement manuals and tender templates.
- [ ] For legacy challenges (pre-06.08.2021 awards), retrieve HANRE 24 text + annexes from MO/source.
- [ ] Cross-check regulated-tariff files: non-compliant procurement adjustments now argued under L74 + tariff methodologies, not pct. 215 of HANRE 24.

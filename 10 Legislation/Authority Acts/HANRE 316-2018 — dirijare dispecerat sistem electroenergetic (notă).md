# HANRE nr. 316/2018 — dirijarea prin dispecerat a sistemului electroenergetic — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 316-2018 — dirijare dispecerat sistem electroenergetic (text)]] ✅ *full OCR (~286 pts + org annex)* · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **TSO:** Moldelectrica · **Network code:** [[HANRE 423-2019 — Codul retelelor electrice (notă)|423/2019]] · **Market rules:** [[HANRE 283-2020 — Regulile pietei energiei electrice (notă)|283/2020]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** The **operational bible** for SE dispatch — organisational rules, command staff qualifications, real-time operation, outages, emergencies, and confidentiality. Binds TSO/DSO dispatch centres, power plants, and large consumers' operational staff.
> **Primary business risk:** Curtailment / outage compensation disputes framed under supply QoS ([[HANRE 169-2019 — furnizarea energiei electrice (notă)|169/2019]]) when the operative duty sits here; REMIT/market interface errors on dispatch orders.

> [!danger] L107 currency + live transition
> Issued under **L107/2016**. Post-L164/2025 the dispatch framework must be checked for re-adoption. Entire pre-2025 electricity tier-3 layer lives on transitional provisions.

| Block | Content |

## 3. Risk matrix

| Scenario | Wrong frame | Correct frame |
|---|---|---|
| Generator curtailment without compensation | Supply contract only | Dispatch order + market rules + 316 operational duties |
| Outage duration dispute | 169 SAIDI/SAIFI | 316 emergency/outage procedure + 537 QoS (not yet in vault) |
| Staff qualification challenge | Employment law | 316 professional-formation requirements |
| Cross-border dispatch | 423 only | 316 + ENTSO-E coupling procedures (post-sync) |

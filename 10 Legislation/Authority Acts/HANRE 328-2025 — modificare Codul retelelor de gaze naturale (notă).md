# HANRE nr. 328/2025 — amending Gas Network Code (& related) — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 328-2025 — modificare Codul retelelor de gaze naturale (text)]] ✅ · **Parent Code:** [[HANRE 420-2019 — Codul retelelor de gaze naturale (notă)]] · **Later rewrite:** [[HANRE 310-2026 — modificare Codul retelelor de gaze naturale (notă)]] · **Concept:** [[Concept — Capacitate condiționată]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> Introduces **conditional capacity** on the Trans-Balkan / vertical corridor into the Gas Network Code (HANRE 420/2019) and tweaks connection (112/2019) and quality (422/2019) deadlines. Decision **20.06.2025**.
> **Primary business risk:** Using 328’s original product conditions after **HANRE 310/2026** — the conditional-capacity chapter was **recast**. Read 328 for amendment history + still-live non-superseded tweaks; use **310** for current capacity product rules.

---

## 1. Foundation

* Enabling: L108 arts. 7(1)(k), 7(1)(q¹), 72(3).
* Transposes CAM Reg. **2017/459** art. 32(1) (EnC-adapted).
* Also amends [[HANRE 112-2019 — racordarea la retelele de gaze (text)|HANRE 112/2019]] (e.g. 15 calendar → **10 working** days; OSD advance-payment tool vs risky suppliers) and quality reg. **422/2019** (same day-count tweak).

---

## 2. Conditional capacity (2025 version — largely superseded on product detail)

→ [[Concept — Capacitate condiționată]]

* Special capacity product linked to corridor use (Căușeni entry / Grebeniki exit in the 2025 drafting).
* ANRE-approved tariffs under gas transmission methodology HANRE **535/2019** *(not ingested)*.
* Quantities **outside** user’s balancing portfolio / daily imbalance calculation.
* Strict use: route restriction, equal injection/offtake, no PVT access (2025 framing), customs transit, priority behind firm and interruptible.
* OST may reduce mismatched nominations to the lower amount / interrupt for breach.
* **Do not carry 2025 numeric thresholds forward** without checking consolidated Code post-310.

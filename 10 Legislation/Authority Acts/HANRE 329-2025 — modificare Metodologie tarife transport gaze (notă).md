# HANRE nr. 329/2025 — gas TSO tariff methodology amendment (conditional capacity) — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 329-2025 — modificare Metodologie tarife transport gaze (text)]] ✅ · **Parent methodology:** [[HANRE 535-2019 — Metodologie tarife transport gaze (notă)|HANRE 535/2019]] *(decision ✅ · methodology annex ❌)* · **Network-code product:** [[Concept — Capacitate condiționată]] · [[HANRE 310-2026 — modificare Codul retelelor de gaze naturale (notă)]] · **Gas law:** [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Updates HANRE 535/2019 gas transmission tariff methodology — unit changes, labour-index rule, IP wording — and **adds Section 7²** calculating regulated tariffs for **conditional capacity** at interconnection points.
> **Primary business risk:** Pricing Trans-Balkan conditional-capacity products without the parent 535 **methodology annex**; or missing the **25.06.2025** OST filing duty for conditional-capacity tariffs.

> [!warning] Parent annex still incomplete
> The vault has the [[HANRE 535-2019 — Metodologie tarife transport gaze (text)|535/2019 approving decision]] and this amending act, but **not** the 535 methodology annex. Tariff advice that needs the base BAR / allowed-revenue formulae must still pull that annex from legis.md / ANRE.

## 2. Substantive changes

### A. Units & definitions (pct. 9, 12)
* Transport-tariff unit shifts from **lei/m³/zi (lei/kWh/zi)** to **lei/1000 m³/h (lei/MWh/h)** — aligns capacity products with hourly reservation logic used for conditional capacity.
* “Cheltuieli efective” / “costuri de bază” clarified as lei or mii lei; “coeficient de multiplicare” notion removed.
* Formula definitions (CTPn, PFFnᴵ) tightened (m³; lei/1000 m³).

### B. Labour cost index (pct. 30 rewrite)
Annual labour-cost update may use the **national minimum-wage growth coefficient** instead of CPI **only if** all three conditions hold: HG changed the minimum wage; the licence-holder actually applied it; and that coefficient **exceeds** CPI for the same year. Stops automatic wage-pass-through without real application.

### C. Interconnection wording (pct. 54³)
References rephrased to **neighbouring TSOs’ networks / cross-border IPs** — cleaner Trans-Balkan drafting.

### D. New Section 7² — conditional-capacity tariffs
New pct. **58¹²** sets the regulated tariff formula for conditional capacity at entry/exit IPs where the product is offered, in **lei/1000 m³/h (lei/MWh/h)**.

Cost building blocks (as drafted):
* **CIn** = CAIn + RTn + CLn (infrastructure-related)
* Capacity terms: **Ccondn** (total reserved conditional capacity) vs **Ctotaln** (total transport capacity excluding conditional)
* **CLn** — lease costs of transport networks
* **COPn** — operating costs for network portions held by entities outside the national public budget, needed for reliability/continuity when offering conditional capacity
* **ACn** — other direct costs of the conditional-capacity product

> OCR/layout in the PDF splits formula lines; verify algebraic arrangement against the official MO PDF before filing a tariff application.

### E. Immediate compliance duty (point 2)
Gas TSO must file with ANRE, by **25 June 2025**, the application to approve regulated transport tariffs for conditional capacity.

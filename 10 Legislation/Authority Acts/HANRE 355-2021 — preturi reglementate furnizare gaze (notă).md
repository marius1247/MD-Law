# HANRE nr. 355/2021 — prețuri reglementate furnizare gaze — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 355-2021 — preturi reglementate furnizare gaze (text)]] ✅ *methodology secs. 1–8* · **Amended by:** [[HANRE 540-2024 — modificare Metodologie preturi furnizare gaze (notă)|540/2024]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Tarife și metodologii ANRE]] · [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Gas law:** [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)|L108/2016]] · **Concept:** [[Concept — Furnizor de ultimă opțiune]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Methodology for **regulated gas supply prices** to protected final-consumer categories and for **supplier of last resort (FUO)** — the price layer for public-service obligations under L108 arts. 89–90. Prices are differentiated by **delivery point** (entry to TSO / exit from TSO / HP·MP·LP DSO exits).
> **Primary business risk:** Quoting FUO or PSO prices without consolidating [[HANRE 540-2024 — modificare Metodologie preturi furnizare gaze (notă)|540/2024]]; also missing the monthly FUO approval clock (art. 9(7) L108 — by the 25th, IF on the 1st).

---

## 1. What the methodology regulates

Prices covered (sec. 2 / pt. 4):
1. Regulated supply at **entry** to transmission
2. Regulated supply at **exit** from transmission
3. Regulated supply at **HP / MP / LP distribution exits**
4. Parallel **FUO** price stack at the same point types (formulas through ~(47); **no correction component** for FUO — pt. 56²)

## 3. Commercial mechanics

* **Pass-through logic:** regulated supply price builds from gas procurement + transport/distribution service costs + supply margin / return under methodology rules.
* **Base costs:** 5-year approval; tacit extension only for adjustment if next period not yet approved.
* **Correction component:** applies to ordinary regulated PSO prices; **explicitly excluded** for FUO (56²).
* **FUO cadence:** approve by **25th** of each month; IF on **1st** of next month (not before MO publication) — pt. 60¹ / L108 art. 9(7).
* **Mid-year adjust:** if objective factors move regulated revenue by **>1%**.

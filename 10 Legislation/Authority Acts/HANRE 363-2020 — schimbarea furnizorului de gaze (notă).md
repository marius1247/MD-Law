# HANRE nr. 363/2020 — gas supplier switching procedure — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 363-2020 — schimbarea furnizorului de gaze (text)]] ✅ · **Gas law:** [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)]] · **Supply:** [[HANRE 113-2019 — furnizarea gazelor naturale (notă)]] · **Omnibus amend:** [[HANRE 177-2026 — modificarea unor hotarari ANRE (notă)]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** End-to-end retail switching procedure for natural gas — ≤ **20 calendar days** from request, **no switching fee**, capacity follows the customer, new supplier takes balancing responsibility from switch date.
> **Primary business risk:** Debt (or missing rescheduling agreement) at the offtake **suspends** the switch (pct. 24–25, as amended by 177/2026). New suppliers without live transport/distribution contracts freeze the process until cured (pct. 11).

---

## 1. Foundation

* Enabling: L108 art. **88(1)**. Decision 25.09.2020; MO 279–284/30.10.2020 art. 1049; IF **30.11.2020**.
* Abrogates prior switching reg. HANRE **676/2014**.
* Companion retail rules: [[HANRE 113-2019 — furnizarea gazelor naturale (notă)]].
* Material 2026 amendments via [[HANRE 177-2026 — modificarea unor hotarari ANRE (notă)|HANRE 177/2026]] (tipar notes “Ordinul SFS nr.177” — same omnibus): pct. 8 list, abrogation of pct. 13–14 & 26, debt-rescheduling cure in pct. 25, SO data duties in pct. 16/27.

Does **not** apply to designation of last-resort supplier (pct. 3).

---

## 2. Architecture (stages — pct. 8)

1. New supply contract signed  
2. Switch request filed with **current** supplier  
3. Current supplier notifies new supplier + SO  
4. SO sends offtake data incl. **24-month** monthly consumption  
5. SO ↔ suppliers additional agreements (capacity / offtake list)  
6. Meter check & reading  
7. New supplier starts supply  
8. Final bill from current supplier (≤ 2 weeks after switch)  
9. Customer settles residual debt  

**Switch date** = day the meter-control act is signed (pct. 17).  
**Hard clock:** ≤ **20 CD** from request (pct. 21).

---

## 3. Operative gates

| Gate | Rule | Practical bite |
|---|---|---|
| Debt | Pay all issued invoices + contractual penalties within **7 days** of filing, **or** hold a rescheduling agreement (177) | Suspension until cured |
| New supplier readiness | Must already hold transport/distribution contracts | Procedure suspended if missing (pct. 11) |
| Capacity follows customer | SO amends contracted capacity / offtake lists | Incumbent cannot keep capacity to block exit (pct. 12) |
| Balancing | New supplier assumes BRP for switched offtakes from switch moment (pct. 10) | Contract must say so expressly |
| Fees | No switching / contracting fees; obstruction banned (pct. 18, 31) | ANRE complaint path |
| Channels | In person / registered post / e-signed email (pct. 22) | Use published email |

Customer may abort by informing the new supplier; new supplier notifies current + SO within **1 WD** (pct. 19).

## 4. Risk matrix

| Issue | Risk | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| Stale pre-177 text | Miss rescheduling cure / abrogated pct. 13–14/26 | Work from vault text + [[HANRE 177-2026 — modificarea unor hotarari ANRE (notă)]] |
| Debt suspension | Switch calendar blows past 20 CD | Clear arrears or sign reschedule **before** filing |
| New supplier without network contracts | Frozen procedure | Pre-check TSO/DSO contracts in onboarding |
| Meter / data mismatch | Delayed switch date | Diary SO 5-day data pack (pct. 27) + 1-WD challenge (pct. 28) |
| Confusing with FUO designation | Wrong procedure | FUO is outside 363 (pct. 3) → L108 + 113 |
| Electricity 126/2020 analogy | Wrong sector clocks | Gas-only; EE switching is HANRE 126/2020 *(not ingested)* |

## 5. Client checklist

- [ ] Confirm gas (363), not electricity switching.
- [ ] New supplier: live TSO/DSO contracts + balancing clause in supply contract.
- [ ] Clear debt or sign rescheduling before / within 7-day window.
- [ ] File request with mandatory annex fields; keep proof of filing channel.
- [ ] Diary 20-CD outer clock + SO 5-day / 7-day internal clocks.
- [ ] On obstruction (refusal to sign additional agreements) → document + [[ANRE]] complaint.
- [ ] Pair with [[HANRE 113-2019 — furnizarea gazelor naturale (notă)]] for contract / billing / disconnection interface.

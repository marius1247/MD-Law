# HANRE nr. 375/2017 — fixed tariffs / prices for renewable electricity — Analysis

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> Approves the methodology for **fixed tariffs and prices** for electricity from eligible renewable producers (Law 10/2016 art. 14(2)). Applies from L10 entry into force; abrogates HANRE 321/2009.
> **Annex missing** — no formulas, technology bands, or indexation can be taken from the vault text. Confirm whether the project’s support path is still administrative fixed tariff vs competitive tender under current L10 quotas.

3. Abrogates HANRE 321/2009.

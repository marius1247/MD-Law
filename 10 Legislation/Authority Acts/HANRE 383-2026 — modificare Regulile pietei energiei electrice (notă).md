# HANRE nr. 383/2026 — amending the Electricity Market Rules — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 383-2026 — modificare Regulile pietei energiei electrice (text)]] ✅ · **Parent:** [[HANRE 283-2020 — Regulile pietei energiei electrice (text)]] · [[HANRE 283-2020 — Regulile pietei energiei electrice (notă)|parent notă]] · **Law:** [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Deep rewrite of [[HANRE 283-2020 — Regulile pietei energiei electrice (text)|HANRE 283/2020]] to align wholesale market machinery with L164 actors and introduce **POCB** (organised bilateral contracts market). In force **01.07.2026**.
> **Primary business risk:** Trading without OPEE registration, PRE status, balancing contract and updated guarantees. Balancing/system-services detail now largely lives in **HANRE 853/2025** (not yet ingested) — 283 alone is no longer enough.

---

## 1. Foundation

* Amends Market Rules approved by ANRE **283/2020**.
* Published MO 284–287 art. 512 / 30.06.2026; **IF 01.07.2026** (legis_id `155151`).
* Terminology: PPZ → **PI** (intraday); balancing market → **PE**; PRE wording → *parte responsabilă pentru echilibrare*.

---

## 2. Substantive changes that matter

### A. Scope rewrite (amended pts. 1–3)
Rules expressly cover POCB, negotiated bilaterals, PZU, PI, balancing market, system-services market. OPEE operates PZU/PI/POCB and registers bilaterals; participants pay regulated market-operation tariff.

### B. New POCB architecture (pts. 32¹–32⁴¹)
→ [[Concept — Piața organizată a contractelor bilaterale]]

Voluntary organised auction market for physical bilateral contracts. Prerequisites: balancing contract with OST, OPEE registration, POCB framework contract. Portfolio-level offers; delivery ≥1 month; optional public-index price formula; quantity band max **25%** (or **100%** for RES producers). Price/period/quantity cannot be quietly rewritten later; unilateral termination clauses banned. Matching/closing-price rules + penalties for non-signing.

### C. Bilateral registration tightened
All bilaterals (incl. POCB) registered with OPEE within **5 working days** of signing and ≥ **3 working days** before delivery. Price/payment clauses omitted from the copy filed with OPEE.

### D. PZU gate
Pre-coupling: **09:30** D-1. Post single day-ahead coupling: **12:00** D-1. Offers up to 6 days ahead.

### E. Balancing / system services stripped + cross-referred
Many old balancing pts abrogated → OST regulations under **HANRE 853/2025** (BRP & BSP terms). Wholesale participants must be PRE and submit physical notifications; only balancing-contract holders trade wholesale.

### F. New participant categories
Aggregators, traders, storage operators, citizen energy communities, renewable energy communities — express market inclusion (L164 cast).

### G. Operator trading limit
SOs may use POCB/PZU/PI **only** for losses / technological consumption — not for profit.

### H. Transitional imbalance pricing (pt. 780)
Until ANRE finds sufficient PZU liquidity, OST uses weighted average price of energy bought by regulated-price suppliers for imbalance settlement (excl. supported RES and urban CHP).

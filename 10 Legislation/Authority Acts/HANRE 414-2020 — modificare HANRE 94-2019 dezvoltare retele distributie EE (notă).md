# HANRE nr. 414/2020 — amendare Regulament dezvoltare rețele electrice de distribuție — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 414-2020 — modificare HANRE 94-2019 dezvoltare retele distributie EE (text)]] ✅ · **Parent:** [[HANRE 94-2019 — dezvoltarea retelelor electrice de distributie (notă)|94/2019]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Targeted patch to **pts. 23 and 25** of the electricity DSO development regulation — sharper **housing-demand categories** for Category I projects (individual vs block housing).
> **Primary business risk:** Using pre-2020 demand templates in connection/refusal disputes → wrong forecast duty on DSO.

## 1. What changed

| Target | Change |
|---|---|
| **Pt. 23 sbp. 2** | Adds **„sau folosință”** after „proprietate” |
| **Pt. 23 sbp. 4 lit. a)** | Individual housing: completed + under construction (valid authorisations) + **total planned units in zone** |
| **Pt. 23 sbp. 4 lit. b)** | Block housing: blocks under construction, units per block, **completion grade** per block |
| **Pt. 25** | Redrafted — Category I for existing/new consumers and **re-supply** of existing installations |

IF **27.12.2020** per MO publication note.

## 2. Practice notes

- Always read **94 + 414 together** for Category I forecasting.
- Disputes on „insufficient network capacity” should reference the **amended pt. 25** re-supply wording.
- Enabling chain still L107-based at adoption — apply L164 currency check.

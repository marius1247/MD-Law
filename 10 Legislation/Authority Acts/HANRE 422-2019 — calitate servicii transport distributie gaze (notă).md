# HANRE nr. 422/2019 — QoS gas transport & distribution — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 422-2019 — calitate servicii transport distributie gaze (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · [[MOC — Tarife și metodologii ANRE]] · **EE analogue:** [[HANRE 537-2020 — calitate servicii transport distributie EE (notă)|537/2020]] · **Tariff hooks:** [[HANRE 535-2019 — Metodologie tarife transport gaze (notă)|535]] · [[HANRE 443-2020 — Metodologie tarife distributie gaze (notă)|443]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Binding **quality-of-service** regime for gas TSO/DSO — continuity indices (**SAIDI / SAIFI / CAIDI**, interruption duration/count), delivery quality/pressure regimes, and quality of operator–user relations. Breach → ANRE may cut transport/distribution tariffs by **up to 10%** and/or order **individual compensations**.
> **Primary business risk:** Underestimating QoS exposure when modelling regulated revenue under 535/443; multi-site consumers assessed **per place of consumption**.

## Mechanics

* Applies to relations with system users / final consumers / connection applicants.
* Indicators cover continuity, gas quality & delivery regimes, and commercial-service quality.
* Tariff-methodology clawbacks expressly reference this regulation (see 535 § final; 443 §8).

# HANRE nr. 443/2020 — gas distribution tariff methodology — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 443-2020 — Metodologie tarife distributie gaze (text)]] ⚠️ *methodology body ✅; approving decision missing; CTP annex tail possibly truncated* · **Hub:** [[MOC — Tarife și metodologii ANRE]] · [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Parent:** [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)|L108/2016]] · **Concept:** [[Concept — Tarif de distribuție]] · [[Concept — Consum tehnologic gaze]] · [[Concept — Baza activelor reglementate]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Methodology for **regulated gas distribution tariffs** by pressure level (HP / MP / LP), including the **uniform distribution tariff** mechanism with **equalisation compensatory payments** between DSOs (sec. 7¹), base costs, return, and CTP/normative losses annex.
> **Primary business risk:** Ignoring the equalisation / reconciliation-entity mechanics when advising multi-DSO portfolio clients — unpaid equalisation can trigger regulated-revenue cuts and financing-cost shifts.

## 2. Distinctive mechanics

* **Pressure differentiation:** tariffs track the pressure level of the consumer’s connection / commercial metering points with other DSOs.
* **Uniform tariff + equalisation (7¹):** DSOs settle compensatory equalisation payments via a reconciliation entity; late/non-payment creates a deficit recovered under detailed 55ˣ rules (including possible bank-interest pass-through and revenue haircuts for the defaulting DSO).
* **CTP:** normative losses from injected volumes × Agency-accepted %; allocated to HP/MP/LP by network-length and station/metering-point shares (annex formulas (2)–(4)).
* **Efficiency incentive:** if actual material+third-party costs beat the methodological allowance, shared-saving bands (90% / 80% thresholds) feed regulated revenue (pt. 61); same approach for labour.

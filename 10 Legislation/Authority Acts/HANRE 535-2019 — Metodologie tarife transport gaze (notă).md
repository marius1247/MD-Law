# HANRE 535/2019 — gas transmission tariff methodology — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 535-2019 — Metodologie tarife transport gaze (text)]] ✅ *methodology + CTP annex* · **Hub:** [[MOC — Tarife și metodologii ANRE]] · [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Parent:** [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)]] · **Amendment:** [[HANRE 329-2025 — modificare Metodologie tarife transport gaze (notă)|329/2025]] · **Concepts:** [[Concept — Tarif de transport]] · [[Concept — Consum tehnologic gaze]] · [[Concept — Capacitate condiționată]] · [[Concept — Baza activelor reglementate]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Full **entry/exit** gas transmission tariff methodology for TSOs — regulated costs, base costs (5-year), WACC/return, cost allocation to entry/exit points, reference prices, capacity products including **conditional capacity** (sec. 7²), and CTP/normative losses.
> **Primary business risk:** Modelling tariffs from the methodology without applying [[HANRE 329-2025 — modificare Metodologie tarife transport gaze (notă)|329/2025]] overlays and without the live capacity-product definitions in [[HANRE 420-2019 — Codul retelelor de gaze naturale (notă)|HANRE 420/2019]].

* **Enabling:** [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (text)]] arts. 7(2), 99(5).
* **Instrument:** ANRE CA 535/2019 (27.12.2019; MO 44-54/14.02.2020 art. 173).
* **Procedure:** tariff filings via [[HANRE 286-2018 — licentiere energie (notă)|HANRE 286/2018]]; network products via [[HANRE 420-2019 — Codul retelelor de gaze naturale (notă)|420/2019]].
* **QoS feedback:** tariff reduction possible under HANRE **422/2019** gas QoS regulation *(not yet ingested)*.

## 3. Practical mechanics

* **Entry/exit:** tariffs (including harmonised) allocated to entry/exit points; reference-price determination in methodology.
* **Base costs:** approved for **5 years**; annual update mainly via correction component for year *n−1*.
* **Conditional capacity (7²):** specific cost stack (`ACₙ` etc.) — read with [[Concept — Capacitate condiționată]] and 329/2025.
* **CTP:** planned `CTPₙ = (Vₜ × Nₚₙ)/100` excluding backhaul; factual-under-normative keeps efficiency; repair purging can add `Qₚᵣ` → [[Concept — Consum tehnologic gaze]].
* **Mid-year update:** objective factors (gas for losses, electricity, FX, new legal duties) if deviation **>5%** of annual transport cost.

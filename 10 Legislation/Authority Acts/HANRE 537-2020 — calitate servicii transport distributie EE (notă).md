# HANRE nr. 537/2020 — QoS electricity transport & distribution — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 537-2020 — calitate servicii transport distributie EE (text)]] ✅ *regulation + annexes 1–4* · **Amended by:** [[HANRE 833-2023 — modificarea unor hotarari ANRE regenerabile (notă)|833/2023]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Concept:** [[Concept — Indicatori de calitate SAIDI SAIFI]] · **Electricity law:** [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)|L164/2025]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Binding **quality-of-service** regime for electricity TSO/DSO: interruption classification, **SAIDI/SAIFI/CAIDI/MAIFI** continuity metrics, guaranteed individual continuity limits, voltage-quality handling, compensation / tariff-reduction consequences, and reporting.
> **Primary business risk:** Citing compensation amounts or SAIDI ceilings without consolidating [[HANRE 833-2023 — modificarea unor hotarari ANRE regenerabile (notă)|833/2023]] patches; also L107→L164 enabling-currency risk.

> [!danger] L107 currency
> Issued under **L107/2016**. Check L164 re-adoption / transitional survival before challenging validity.

## 2. Practical legal mechanics

### A. Scope & force majeure carve-out

* Applies TSO ↔ DSO ↔ system users / connection applicants; multi-site users assessed **per installation**.
* QoS breach consequences **do not apply** where caused by force majeure, **special meteorological conditions**, or exceptional market situations as classified in the regulation.

### B. Continuity metrics (DSO)

* **SAIDI / SAIFI / CAIDI** on long interruptions (>3 min), planned vs unplanned separately; **MAIFI** for short/transient.
* Guaranteed individual limits (duration of a long interruption; annual count of unplanned long interruptions) by voltage level and urban/rural zone — values in **Annex 3** (2021–2025 period; ANRE resets every 5 years by 31 Aug).
* Annual SAIDI for medium-voltage “other causes” must not exceed the prior 3-year average (with OST travel/manoeuvre time carve-outs).

### C. TSO duties & tariff reduction

* TSO continuity indicators + duty to support DSO manoeuvres (≤60 min crew presence; ≤15 min outage notifications).
* ANRE may **reduce the transmission tariff** for breaches of specified notification/presence duties (Annex 3 magnitudes; 95% compliance safe harbour where automated).

### D. Voltage quality & compensations

* Voltage complaints → DSO measurements → Annex 4 act; cure ≤**30 days**.
* If root cause is TSO voltage duty breach, DSO pays user compensations (Annex 3) and recovers from TSO within 30 days.

### E. Commercial / connection-adjacent QoS

* Failure of certain connection/information duties triggers individual compensations (Annex 3) under the art. 54 machinery.

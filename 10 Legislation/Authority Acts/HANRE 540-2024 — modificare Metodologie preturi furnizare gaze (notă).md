# HANRE nr. 540/2024 — amendare metodologie prețuri furnizare gaze — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 540-2024 — modificare Metodologie preturi furnizare gaze (text)]] ✅ · **Parent:** [[HANRE 355-2021 — preturi reglementate furnizare gaze (notă)|355/2021]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Expands HANRE 355/2021 scope to **regulated prices for specific final-consumer categories + supplier-of-last-resort (FUO)** under public-service obligations (L108 arts. 89–90). Redrafts title, pts. 1–2, 4 (price categories by network exit point / pressure level), and pt. 6 definitions.
> **Primary business risk:** Using pre-540 methodology wording for FUO pricing — always consolidate 355 + 540; parent **methodology annex still missing** from vault.

## 1. Key changes

| Target | Substance |
|---|---|
| **Title / scope** | Adds FUO + protected-category supply in PSO context |
| **Pt. 4** | Nine regulated price types (4.1–4.9) by TSO/DSO exit and pressure level |
| **Pt. 6** | FUO price definition tied to art. 90 L108 (lost supplier scenario) |

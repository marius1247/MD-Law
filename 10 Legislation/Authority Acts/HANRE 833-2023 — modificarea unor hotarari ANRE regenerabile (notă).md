# HANRE nr. 833/2023 — renewables omnibus (connection, supply, QoS) — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 833-2023 — modificarea unor hotarari ANRE regenerabile (text)]] ✅ · **Parents amended:** HANRE **168/2019** (connection — later abrogated by [[HANRE 311-2026 — racordarea la retelele electrice (notă)|311/2026]]), [[HANRE 169-2019 — furnizarea energiei electrice (notă)|169/2019]] (supply), HANRE **537/2020** (QoS — *not yet ingested*) · **Renewables law:** [[Legea 10-2016 — surse regenerabile (notă)]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Align ANRE secondary acts with **LP331/2023** renewables reform — especially **net metering / prosumer** mechanics, eligible-producer commissioning deadlines, and related connection/supply/QoS duties.
> **Primary business risk:** Reading 833’s connection amendments as if HANRE **168/2019** were still the live connection regulation — it was replaced by **HANRE 311/2026**. Supply-side (169) and QoS (537) amendments remain relevant until those parents are re-adopted/replaced.

## 3. Practical legal mechanics

### A. Net metering / prosumers
* Connection and supply rules distinguish **beneficiaries of net metering** and **prosumers**.
* Supply regulation amendments state that final customers lose net-metering beneficiary status when installed capacity / place-of-consumption conditions are breached (exact thresholds — verify in consolidated 169 + L10 as amended by LP331).
* Suppliers with net-metering contracts must operationalise settlement and information duties added/adjusted by 833.

### B. Eligible producers & commissioning
* Connection opinions and deadlines interact with **eligible-producer** status granted by Government or ANRE and with **art. 35 L10/2016** auctions.
* Failure to commission within the decision’s deadline (without extension) feeds aviz / status consequences — coordinate with [[Concept — Producător eligibil]].

### C. Currency layering (critical)
1. **833 (2024)** patches **168**.
2. **311 (2026)** **abrogates 168** and builds the L164 connection regime (flexible connection, capacity auctions, etc.).
3. Therefore: for **live connection files after 311 IF**, start from 311; use 833 only where 311 preserves equivalent mechanisms or for events during 168’s last life.
4. For **supply (169)** and **QoS (537)**, 833 amendments remain the working patch until those parents are replaced under L164.

## 5. Client checklist

- [ ] **Prosumers / net metering:** confirm installed capacity vs place of consumption; supplier settlement clauses match post-833 169.
- [ ] **Eligible-producer projects:** diary commissioning deadline in Gov/ANRE decision; link to connection aviz under **311** if post-abrogation.
- [ ] **DSOs:** update QoS reporting labels per 537 amendments (avize / refusals “în termen”).
- [ ] Do not file new connection requests on 168 templates.

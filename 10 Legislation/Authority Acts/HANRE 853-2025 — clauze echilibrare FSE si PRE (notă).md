# HANRE 853/2025 — balancing FSE & PRE terms and conditions — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 853-2025 — clauze echilibrare FSE si PRE (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · [[MOC — Piața de energie electrică]] · **Parent:** [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)|L164/2025]] · **Market rules:** [[HANRE 283-2020 — Regulile pietei energiei electrice (notă)|HANRE 283/2020]] · **Concepts:** [[Concept — Parte responsabilă de echilibrare]] · [[Concept — Furnizor de servicii de echilibrare]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** ANRE decision approving **two** operational regulations — (1) Terms & conditions for **balancing service providers (FSE)** and (2) Terms & conditions for **balance responsible parties (PRE)** — under L164 art. 39. **IF 1 July 2026**.
> **Primary business risk:** Treating market-rules (HANRE 283) as enough. From 1 July 2026, qualification, bidding, settlement, collateral and PRE registration live in these annexes; missing the OST procedures deadline (+6 months) will strand operational detail.

> [!info] Closes a named energy gap
> [[Status ingestie — Energetica]] listed **HANRE 853/2025** among still-missing parents — primary decision + both regulations now in vault.

## 2. Architecture (two regulations in one act)

### A. Decision shell
Points 1–2 approve the two regulations; point 4 sets IF.

### B. Regulation — FSE (balancing service providers)
Chapters cover: definitions; balancing energy market (PEE) operation; **technical qualification** of FSE; data exchange; monitoring; bidding / selection / committed transactions; settlement & invoicing; payment guarantees & late penalties; disputes; **balancing capacity** procurement & transfer; frequency-containment reserve (RSF) special rules; final provisions.

### C. Regulation — PRE (balance responsible parties)
Chapters cover: definitions; general principles; **obligation to assume balancing responsibility** (incl. suppliers of renewables prosumers); registration; **payment guarantees**; physical notifications; imbalance settlement; information systems; final provisions (OST IT system + procedures within **6 months** of approval).

## 3. Practical legal mechanics

| Topic | Where | Commercial bite |
|---|---|---|
| Who must be a PRE | PRE reg. §3.2 | Every wholesale participant in a perimeter; suppliers take prosumer imbalance |
| FSE qualification | FSE reg. Ch. III | Technical qualification per SOGL-style process before bidding |
| Capacity contracts | FSE reg. Ch. VII | OST procures RR / replacement reserves; transfer rules |
| Collateral | Both regs | Payment guarantees for PEE / PRE obligations — credit risk to market |
| Settlement & disputes | FSE Ch. V; PRE settlement chapters | Monthly notes, invoicing, contestation clocks |
| OST procedures | PRE final pts. 270–272 | IT imbalance settlement system; public procedures **+6 months** after approval |

| Issue | Risk | Mitigation |

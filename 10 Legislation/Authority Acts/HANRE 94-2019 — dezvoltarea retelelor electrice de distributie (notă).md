# HANRE nr. 94/2019 — dezvoltarea rețelelor electrice de distribuție — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HANRE 94-2019 — dezvoltarea retelelor electrice de distributie (text)]] ✅ *OCR from legis.md PDF* · **Amended by:** [[HANRE 414-2020 — modificare HANRE 94-2019 dezvoltare retele distributie EE (notă)|414/2020]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Electricity law:** [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** How DSOs plan, evaluate and execute **distribution-network development** — the grid-expansion layer upstream of [[HANRE 311-2026 — racordarea la retelele electrice (notă)|connection]] and downstream of policy/NTDP.
> **Primary business risk:** Treating a connection refusal as final when the DSO has a **development-plan obligation** under this regulation; or citing pre-414 wording on housing-demand categories.

> [!danger] L107 currency
> Parent enabling articles cite **L107/2016**. Validity now rests on L164 transitional rules until ANRE re-adopts — check [ANRE › Hotărâri](https://anre.md/acte-normative-3-18).

## 2. Operative mechanics

### A. Development plans (pts. 8–9 area)

DSOs must submit **distribution development plans** on a rolling cycle (initial 2020–2022 plan by 1 Sept 2019; later periods per pt. 9). Plans feed ANRE approval and investment recovery via tariff methodology.

### B. Project categories & demand forecasting (pts. 23–25 — amended by 414/2020)

414/2020 refined **Category I** demand data for:
- individual housing projects (completed + under construction + total planned units);
- block housing (blocks under construction, units per block, completion grade).

**Practical point:** connection-queue and refusal letters must use **post-414** category definitions when assessing whether network reinforcement is required.

### C. Abrogated predecessor

Repeals the 2011 distribution extension regulation (HANRE 439/2011 patch set).

## 3. Risk matrix

| Provision | Risk | Mitigation |

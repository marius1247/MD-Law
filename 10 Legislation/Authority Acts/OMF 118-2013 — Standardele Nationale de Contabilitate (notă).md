# OMF nr. 118/2013 — approval of National Accounting Standards (SNC) — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[OMF 118-2013 — Standardele Nationale de Contabilitate (text)]] ✅ *approving order* · **Parent law:** [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (notă)]] · **Amend touch:** [[OMF 73-2022 — modificare indicatii metodice contabilitate (notă)]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Fiscalitate & Contabilitate]] · [[Contabilitate & raportare financiară — synthesis]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** MF order that **approved** the post-2014 SNC suite and related methodological indications, set the 2014 optional / 2015 mandatory calendar, and repealed the old numbered SNC 1–62 family.
> **Primary business risk:** Treating this 5-page order as the standards themselves. Operative measurement/presentation rules live in the **individual SNC texts** published in MO 233–237/22.10.2013 art. 1534 (+ later MF amendments) — **not yet ingested as a corpus**.

> [!warning] Completeness
> Vault now has the **gateway order** + the list of standard titles. Book-vs-tax work still needs the actual SNC “Imobilizări…”, “Venituri”, “Cheltuieli”, etc.

## 2. What the order actually does

### A. Approves (IF for standards from 01.01.2014; mandatory 01.01.2015)
**SNC titles (topic-named, not numbered):**
Prezentarea situațiilor financiare · Politici contabile / estimări / erori / evenimente ulterioare · Imobilizări necorporale și corporale · Contracte de leasing · Deprecierea activelor · Creanțe și investiții financiare · Stocuri · Capital propriu și datorii · Venituri · Cheltuieli · Contracte de construcții · Costurile îndatorării · Diferențe de curs valutar și de sumă · Părți afiliate și contracte de societate civilă · Particularitățile contabilității în agricultură.

**Methodological indications:**
* production-cost accounting / costing
* accounting for natural persons carrying on entrepreneurial activity *(amended via OMF 73/2022)*

### B. Transition
* Recommendatory application from **1 Jan 2014**; **mandatory from 1 Jan 2015**.
* Early adopters in 2014 do not also apply the repealed old SNCs.

### C. Repeals from 1 Jan 2015
Entire legacy numbered SNC set (SNC 1–31, 62 etc.), application commentaries (C.S.N.C.), and the old conceptual framework orders listed in pct. 2–3.

# OMF nr. 73/2022 — amendments to MF accounting methodological indications — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[OMF 73-2022 — modificare indicatii metodice contabilitate (text)]] ✅ · **Parent gateway:** [[OMF 118-2013 — Standardele Nationale de Contabilitate (notă)]] · **Accounting law:** [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (notă)]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Fiscalitate & Contabilitate]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Small cleanup of normative annexes to three MF methodological packages — removes obsolete references and points justice-sector professionals to the current cash-register HG.
> **Primary business risk:** Low for corporate SNC filers; relevant for **sole entrepreneurs**, **justice-sector professionals**, and **individual family-doctor cabinets** still on these indicații.

---

## 1. Foundation

* OMF 73/10.06.2022; MO 187–193 art. 720 / 24.06.2022; IF on publication.
* Enabling: L287/2017 art. 8(1)(b).

---

## 2. Operative changes

| # | Parent package | Change |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Indicații metodice — natural-person entrepreneurs (**OMF 118/2013**, Annex 6) | **Deletes pct. 3** |
| 2 | Indicații metodice — justice-sector professionals (**OMF 216/2018**, Annex 4) | Deletes pct. 3; rewrites pct. 4 → **HG 141/27.02.2019** on cash-register equipment for settlements |
| 3 | Indicații metodice — individual family-doctor cabinets (**OMF 181/2019**, Annex 4) | **Deletes pct. 4** |

Parents **216/2018** and **181/2019** are not yet in the vault — only this amending order.

---

## 3. Risk matrix / checklist

| Issue | Strategy |
|---|---|
| Advising a lawyer/notary/bailiff on books | Pull OMF 216/2018 + apply OMF 73; cash-register path = HG 141/2019 |
| Sole entrepreneur on OMF 118 indicații | Confirm Annex 6 without deleted pct. 3 |
| Family-doctor cabinet | Pull OMF 181/2019 + OMF 73 |

- [ ] Do not treat this order as amending the SNC standards themselves — it touches **methodological indication annexes** only.
- [ ] Corporate double-entry SNC entities: usually irrelevant.

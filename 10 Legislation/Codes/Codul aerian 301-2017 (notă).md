# Air Code — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Codul aerian 301-2017 (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Drept comercial]] · **Framework:** [[Legea 1194-1997 — transporturi (notă)]] · **Challenge:** [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Civil aviation code: Ministry policy vs CAA (administrative authority) safety oversight, aircraft registration & airworthiness, flight operations (AOC + flight authorisation), aerodromes, ATM/ANS, SMS and aviation security.
> **Primary business risk:** Commercial air transport without valid **AOC + flight authorisation** (art. 27) or operating unregistered / unairworthy aircraft (arts. 19–20) → certificate suspension/revocation (arts. 14–15) and grounding.

---

## 1. Statutory hierarchy & legal foundation

* Modal specialis under [[Legea 1194-1997 — transporturi (notă)]] art. 2 / art. 18.
* Dual public administration: **organ central de specialitate** (policy — MIDR) vs **autoritate administrativă de implementare** (CAA — certification, continuous oversight) — arts. 6–8.
* Chicago Convention / ICAO SARPs alignment through CAA technical acts (art. 7).
* Challenges to CAA individual acts → [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]].

---

## 2. Practical legal mechanics

### AAC powers & oversight (arts. 6–18)
* Ministry: policy, consumer protection, airport/ANS tariff methodology to Government, search & rescue / technical investigation framing — [[Codul aerian 301-2017 (text)#Articolul 6. Misiunea, funcţiile de bază]].
* CAA: implement policy; certify; continuous surveillance; airspace organisation; irregular/regular flight permissions; safety directives — [[Codul aerian 301-2017 (text)#Articolul 7. Misiunea, funcţiile de bază]].
* Continuous oversight programme; non-compliance findings; aeronautical inspectors; certificate suspension/revocation; ramp inspections; responsibility transfer; exemptions — arts. 9–18.

### Registration & airworthiness (arts. 19–20)
* Moldovan Aircraft Register; registration only if not registered elsewhere, with valid type certificate and export/equivalent airworthiness — [[Codul aerian 301-2017 (text)#Articolul 19. Înmatricularea aeronavelor]]. Registration certificate mandatory on board (remote for UAVs). Registration ≠ ownership proof. Radiating grounds include destruction, disappearance, owner/operator request, missing CoA >3 months (repair exceptions up to 2 years).
* Essential airworthiness requirements (Annex 1); type certificates, continuing airworthiness — art. 20.

### Personnel & operations (arts. 22–34)
* Aeronautical personnel / flight crew / PIC powers — arts. 22–25.
* Civil operations classes: commercial air transport; non-commercial (GA); specialised commercial (aerial work) — art. 26.
* Commercial air transport requires **certificat de operator aerian** + **autorizație de zbor** from CAA — [[Codul aerian 301-2017 (text)#Articolul 27. Operaţiunile de transport aerian]]. Scheduled vs non-scheduled — arts. 28–29; authorisation of operations art. 30; non-commercial / specialised / other flights arts. 31–33.

### Aerodromes, ATM, safety & security (arts. 35–45)
* Aerodrome/airport/heliport certification & use — arts. 35–39.
* ATM / air navigation services — art. 40.
* National safety programme, SMS, aviation security, facilitation, search & rescue — arts. 41–45.

## 3. Risk matrix

| Provision | Flaw / ambiguity | Practical risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Art. 27** | AOC vs flight authorisation conflated | Ops without full pack | Diary both certificates before route launch |
| **Arts. 14–15** | Continuous oversight findings | Fleet grounding | SMS + corrective-action tracker |
| **Arts. 19–20** | Lease / dual registration | Radiating / invalid CoA | Single-registry check; continuing AW org |
| **Art. 19(9)(h)** | CoA gap >3 months | Automatic radiating risk | Maintenance planning vs 3-month clock |
| **Arts. 6–7** | Ministry vs CAA competence | Wrong addressee for filings | Policy/tariffs → Ministry; certificates → CAA |
| **Art. 30** | Irregular international permissions | Denied entry / fines | File permissions per CAA rules |

---

## 4. Client checklist

- [ ] Confirm operation class (art. 26) and obtain AOC + flight authorisation for CAT (art. 27).
- [ ] Register aircraft on Moldovan registry or valid foreign registration path (art. 19).
- [ ] Maintain type certificate / continuing airworthiness (art. 20).
- [ ] Crew licensing and PIC documentation (arts. 22–24).
- [ ] Aerodrome/ground-handling and slot/use agreements separate from AOC (arts. 35–37).
- [ ] SMS aligned with national safety programme (arts. 41–42).
- [ ] Challenge CAA acts via [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]].

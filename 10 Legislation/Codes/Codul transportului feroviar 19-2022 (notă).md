# Railway Transport Code — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Codul transportului feroviar 19-2022 (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Drept comercial]] · **Framework:** [[Legea 1194-1997 — transporturi (notă)]] · **Challenge:** [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Directive 2012/34-style railway code: institutional unbundling of **Administratorul infrastructurii** from railway undertakings, Authoritatea Feroviară as safety/authorisation body, capacity allocation via Network Statement, access charging, and market regulatory organ.
> **Primary business risk:** Operating without **autorizație de transport feroviar** (+ safety certificate) (arts. 21–22) or without contracted capacity (arts. 38–44) → suspension/withdrawal and blocked paths; IM independence failures (arts. 9–15) → foreclosure challenges.

---

## 1. Statutory hierarchy & legal foundation

* Modal specialis under [[Legea 1194-1997 — transporturi (notă)]] arts. 2, 16; replaces prior Law 309/2003 architecture.
* EU alignment: Directive 2012/34/EU access package; COTIF/CIM and SMGS for contracts of carriage (apply where referenced in later chapters).
* Note: prior stub linked Energy Community accession — **incorrect enabling path**; organic hook is transport framework + this Code.
* Ministry (policy/tariffs/PSO) vs Autoritatea Feroviară (safety, authorisations, control) vs market regulatory organ (arts. 50–51) — arts. 3–7.
* Challenges → [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]]; state control also via Law 131/2012 (art. 21(2)).

## 2. Practical legal mechanics

### Institutions (arts. 3–8)
* Ministry: policy, National Railway Infrastructure Programme, multiannual IM contract, access-charging methodology to Government, PSO contracts, consumer protection — [[Codul transportului feroviar 19-2022 (text)#Articolul 3. Misiunea, funcțiile de bază şi atribuțiile]].
* Autoritatea Feroviară: safety authority; issues transport authorisations, safety authorisations/certificates, locomotive driver licences; control — [[Codul transportului feroviar 19-2022 (text)#Articolul 4. Misiunea, funcțiile de bază şi atribuțiile]].
* Rights/principles/organisation of AF — arts. 5–7; duties of sector entities — art. 8.

### Infrastructure manager & unbundling (arts. 9–20)
* Independent infrastructure administration — art. 9; IM management duties — art. 10; railway undertakings administration — art. 11.
* **Financial separation** of IM vs transport accounts — art. 12.
* IM independence and essential functions (path allocation + charging decisions) — arts. 13–15; impartiality art. 15; revenue use art. 16; financing art. 17.
* Access conditions to infrastructure and service facilities; limitation of access rights — arts. 18–20.

### Railway undertakings (arts. 21–26)
* Fitness tests: no insolvency; manager criminal/labour/customs clean record; 12-month financial capacity declaration; competent ops control; civil-liability insurance (minima by Government); no material tax arrears — [[Codul transportului feroviar 19-2022 (text)#Articolul 21. Condiții pentru desfășurarea]].
* Authorisation issued by AF free under L160/2011 Annex 1; **30-day** examination — art. 22.
* Planned/unannounced controls; prescriptions; repeated breach → suspend/withdraw authorisation (insurance failure / restricted rolling stock → withdraw) — arts. 23–24.
* Registers and continuous supervision — arts. 25–26.

### Charging & capacity (arts. 27–49)
* Efficient capacity use; **Documentul de referință al rețelei** (Network Statement) — arts. 27–28.
* Charging framework, multiannual IM contract, charging rules/exceptions/discounts, performance scheme, reservation charges — arts. 29–36.
* Capacity rights; allocation/revocation; applicants; capacity contract; allocation calendar; applications; planning; coordination; congested infrastructure; ad-hoc; disruption measures — arts. 38–49.

### Market regulator (arts. 50–51)
* Organul de reglementare a pieței — independence and appeal functions over IM/charging/access discrimination (art. 50–51). Use for path/charging disputes before or alongside administrative court.

### Safety / maintenance / carriage
* Later chapters cover maintenance, construction, safety management, staff, and contracts of carriage for passengers/goods — plead those articles when the dispute is operational rather than access.

## 3. Risk matrix

| Provision | Flaw / ambiguity | Practical risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Arts. 9–15** | Legacy integrated CFM perimeter | Discriminatory access | Test essential-function independence; Network Statement |
| **Arts. 21–24** | Authorisation vs safety certificate | Ops without full pack | AF authorisation + safety certificate before commercial runs |
| **Arts. 28, 41–44** | Opaque path allocation | Market entry blocked | Diary Network Statement calendar; appeal to market organ |
| **Arts. 29–36** | Charging methodology shifts | Sudden access-cost spike | Model tariffs vs Government methodology |
| **Art. 23** | >6 months restart → unannounced control | Surprise suspension | Pre-notify AF before relaunch |
| **Cross-border gauge** | 1520/1435 interface (practice) | Transit delay | Dual COTIF/SMGS contract design |

## 4. Client checklist

- [ ] Obtain AF **autorizație de transport feroviar** (arts. 21–22) + applicable safety certificate/authorisation.
- [ ] Maintain civil-liability insurance at Government minima (art. 21(1)(6)).
- [ ] Read current Network Statement (art. 28); apply for paths on calendar (arts. 43–44).
- [ ] Separate access charges from haulage contracts; keep capacity contract (art. 42).
- [ ] Map IM essential functions / financial separation if advising IM or challenging foreclosure (arts. 12–15).
- [ ] Path/charging disputes → market regulatory organ (arts. 50–51) then [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]].
- [ ] Do not treat L117/2009 Energy Community as enabling act for this Code.

# Constituția Republicii Moldova — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Constituția RM (text)]]  ·  **Backbone:** [[Ierarhia actelor normative]]

## What it is
The supreme law (adopted 29 July 1994, in force 27 August 1994; republished 2016). Everything else in the vault sits beneath it. Art. 7 makes the point bluntly: any law or act contrary to the Constitution has no legal force → [[Constituția RM (text)#Articolul 7. Constituţia, Lege Supremă]].

## Structure at a glance
- **Titlul I** — General principles (state, sovereignty, rule of law, language, symbols)
- **Titlul II** — Fundamental rights, freedoms and duties (the bill of rights)
- **Titlul III** — Public authorities (Parliament, President, Government, public administration, judiciary)
- **Titlul IV** — National economy and public finances
- **Titlul V** — Constitutional Court
- **Titlul V¹** — *EU integration* (new, 2024)
- **Titlul VI** — Revision of the Constitution
- **Titlul VII** — Final and transitional provisions

## Provisions that anchor other domains
- **Art. 72** — which matters must be *organic* law (elections, citizenship, state of emergency, etc.) → [[Constituția RM (text)#Articolul 72. Categorii de legi]]. Central to [[Ierarhia actelor normative]].
- **Titlul IV** — property, economy, the budget and taxation base — feeds [[MOC — Fiscalitate & Contabilitate]] and property law.
- **Titlul III** — the whole [[00 - Harta instituțională|institutional map]].
- **Art. 126–127** — market economy, protection of property and competition — feeds commercial and [[MOC — Energetică|energy]] law.

## The 2024 EU amendment (important, current)
Adopted by referendum (20 Oct 2024), enacted by **[[Legea 244-2024 — revizuirea Constituției (UE)|Legea nr. 244/2024]]**, in force **5 Nov 2024**. It:
- added preamble recitals affirming the European identity of the people and the irreversibility of the European course, and declaring **EU integration a strategic objective**;
- inserted **Titlul V¹** and **art. 140¹**: accession to the EU constitutive treaties is decided by Parliament through organic law, and once acceded, **EU constitutive treaties and binding EU acts have priority over contrary domestic law** → [[Constituția RM (text)#Articolul 140¹. Aderarea la tratatele constitutive şi la actele de revizuire a tratatelor constitutive ale Uniunii Europene]].

## My conclusions / open questions
- The EU-priority clause (art. 140¹) is the constitutional hook that will, over time, pull large parts of the commercial/energy/procurement corpus toward EU acquis alignment — worth tracking per domain.
- Open: exact interaction of art. 140¹ priority with art. 4 (human-rights treaty priority) and ordinary *lex posterior* rules.

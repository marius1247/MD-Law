# HG nr. 10/2024 — energy & climate governance — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HG 10-2024 — guvernanta energetica si actiuni climatice (text)]] ✅ *full regulation (~112 pts, OCR)* · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Parents:** [[Legea 139-2018 — eficienta energetica (notă)|L139/2018]] · [[Legea 174-2017 — energetica (notă)|L174/2017]] · **EE agency:** [[CNED]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Approves the **Regulation on the energy and climate governance mechanism** — Moldova's framework for **PNIEC** (integrated national energy and climate plans), progress monitoring, public participation, and reporting on the five **Energy Union** dimensions. Partially transposes **Regulation (EU) 2018/1999** (EnC-adapted) and **Implementing Reg. (EU) 2022/2299**.
> **Primary business risk:** Missing PNIEC / NECP update cycles when advising on sector targets, renewable quotas, or efficiency obligations — this is the procedural spine linking policy documents to implementation.

## 1. Statutory hierarchy

* **Government regulation** under L139 + L174 — not an ANRE market rule.
* **Ministry of Energy** controls execution (dispositif pt. 2).
* **Neighbours:** [[Legea 139-2018 — eficienta energetica (notă)|L139/2018]] (EE targets, FEE) · [[Legea 10-2016 — surse regenerabile (notă)|L10/2016]] · [[Legea 174-2017 — energetica (notă)|L174/2017]] · [[CNED]] · crisis/exceptional regimes ([[HG 820-2024 — situatii exceptionale electroenergetic (notă)|HG 820/2024]]) — different instruments.

## 2. Operative mechanics (high level)

From regulation pts. 1–3 area:

1. **Governance mechanism** covers ten-year and long-term energy/climate objectives, including EnC climate-neutrality alignment.
2. **Five dimensions:** security, internal market, efficiency, decarbonisation, R&D/competitiveness — mutually reinforcing.
3. **Core outputs:** elaboration, notification, updating and evaluation of **PNIEC**; national progress reporting; stakeholder consultation architecture.

Downstream pts. (vault text) detail institutions, timelines, PNIEC content, monitoring indicators, and EnC reporting — use the `(text)` file for article-level navigation.

# HG nr. 1059/2023 — electricity security PSO on Energocom — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HG 1059-2023 — PSO securitate aprovizionare energie electrica (text)]] ✅ consolidated · **Amendments:** [[HG 156-2025 — modificare HG 1059-2023 PSO securitate EE (notă)|HG 156/2025]] · [[HG 801-2025 — modificare HG 1059-2023 PSO securitate EE (notă)|HG 801/2025]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Imposes a **public-service obligation** on SPA „Energocom” (licensed supplier) for **security of electricity supply**: buy on bilateral / organised markets and sell to universal / last-resort suppliers and system operators under a framework contract.
> **Primary business risk:** Advising that the PSO ended at 31.03.2025 or 31.12.2025. Consolidated calendar (via HG 801) runs **1.01.2024 – 31.12.2026**.

> [!tip] Currency
> Enabling clause remapped to **L164/2025** by HG 801 (was L107). Period end date: 31.03.2025 → 31.12.2025 (HG 156) → **31.12.2026** (HG 801).

---

## 1. Mechanics

| Point | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | PSO on Energocom for **1.01.2024 – 31.12.2026**, within territory authorised by system-operator licences |
| 2 | Energocom procures on bilateral and/or organised markets; sells to FSU / FUО / OS |
| 3 | Buyers sign **framework** EE sale-purchase contracts with Energocom |
| 4 | IF on publication (22.12.2023) |

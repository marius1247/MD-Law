# HG nr. 1060/2023 — CNED organisation & statute — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HG 1060-2023 — organizare functionare CNED (text)]] ⚠️ *annex 4 may be truncated* · **Authority:** [[CNED]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Parent law:** [[Legea 139-2018 — eficienta energetica (notă)|L139/2018]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Transforms the Energy Efficiency Agency into **IP Centrul Național pentru Energie Durabilă (CNED)** — statute, structure, organigram, staff ceiling; Ministry of Energy as founder; consequential renames across EE secondary acts.
> **Primary business risk:** Citing AEE as the live EE implementing body after 1.01.2024; or relying on incomplete annex-4 renames for auditor / programme acts.

> [!warning] Completeness
> Statute + structure present. Annex 4 (consequential HG amendments) appears to **cut off mid HG 676/2020**. Re-fetch before citing the full rename package. Amended by **HG 765/2025**.

| Point | Effect |

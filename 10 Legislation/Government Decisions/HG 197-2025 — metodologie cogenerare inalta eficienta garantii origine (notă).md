# HG nr. 197/2025 — HE CHP electricity quantities for guarantees of origin — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HG 197-2025 — metodologie cogenerare inalta eficienta garantii origine (text)]] ⚠️ *methodology annex missing* · **Parent:** [[Legea 92-2014 — energia termica si cogenerarea (notă)|L92/2014]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Concept:** [[Concept — Garanții de origine (cogenerare HE)]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Approves the methodology for quantifying **electricity from high-efficiency cogeneration** eligible for **guarantees of origin**; updates **harmonised efficiency reference values** in HG 297/2016 to reflect Delegated Regulation (EU) **2023/2104**; and patches the communal-services billing regulation (metering / recalculation formulas).
> **Primary business risk:** Issuing or challenging CHP GO certificates without the **methodology annex** (absent from this PDF) — the dispositif alone does not contain the calculation rules.

> [!warning] Annex missing
> PDF ends with placeholder „Metodologie”. Harmonised reference-value **tables** amending HG 297/2016 **are** present in the dispositif (pt. 2). Use those for fuel/reference updates; fetch the methodology annex before GO certification opinions.

---

## 1. Statutory hierarchy & legal foundation

* **Primary:** L92/2014 (thermal energy & cogeneration) — art. 7(1)(f)(h), 8(1)(b)(d), 14, 42(12).
* **Also:** Legea serviciilor publice de gospodărie comunală 1402/2002 art. 13(1)(b) (billing-regulation patches in pt. 3).
* **EU:** Transposes Delegated Reg. (EU) **2023/2104** amending (EU) **2015/2402** (harmonised efficiency reference values under Dir. 2012/27/EU).
* **ANRE follow-up (pt. 4):** ANRE must align its secondary acts for HE-CHP GO certification.

---

## 2. Practical legal mechanics

| Point | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Approves HE-CHP electricity quantification methodology for GOs *(annex missing)* |
| 2 | Amends HG 297/2016 — EU clause + **Table 1** (electricity reference values by fuel / construction year bands: before 2016 · 2016–2023 · from 2024) + heat reference table updates |
| 3 | Patches communal services regulation (heated area definition, metering, absence recalculation pt. 118, erroneous invoice recalculation pt. 134, annex 3 heat-allocation coefficients) |
| 4 | Mandates ANRE to amend its regulatory acts for HE-CHP GO certification |

Notable fuel/reference updates in pt. 2 include hydrogen (traded), e-gases, biogas, residual heat bands, and a **from-2024** construction-year column with higher values for several solid/liquid/gaseous fuels (e.g. superior coal / HFO / natural gas **53.0**).

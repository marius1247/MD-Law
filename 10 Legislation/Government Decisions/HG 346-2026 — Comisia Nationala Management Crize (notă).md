# HG nr. 346/2026 — National Crisis Management Commission — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HG 346-2026 — Comisia Nationala Management Crize (text)]] ✅ · **Amended by:** [[HG 379-2026 — modificare HG 346-2026 crize (notă)]] · **Parent statute:** Legea 248/2025 *(still not in vault)* · **Also:** [[Legea 150-2026 — modificare management situatii de criza (notă)]] · **Energy bridge:** [[HG 820-2024 — situatii exceptionale electroenergetic (notă)]] · [[Legea 101-2026 — consolidarea mecanismelor de interventie in situatii de criza (notă)]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Implements L248/2025 by constituting the **Comisia Națională de Management al Crizelor** (PM-chaired), approving national + local/regional regulations and operational coordination centres (COC), and repealing the old exceptional-situations commission architecture (HG 1340/2001, HG 803/2018).
> **Primary business risk:** Ignoring CNMC decisions during alert/emergency — they bind public authorities and can cascade to energy/petroleum operators via sector plans (Annex 3 touches [[HG 820-2024 — situatii exceptionale electroenergetic (text)|HG 820/2024]]). Parent L248 still missing for full hierarchy advice.

## 2. Practical legal mechanics

### A. Decision body (pct. 1–2 + Annex 1)

* CNMC constituted; PM = president.
* **Permanent members** include Ministers of Interior, Finance, Justice, **Energy**, Infrastructure, Economy/Digital, Health, Defence; SIS director; National Security Council secretary; CNMC Centre DG.
* Decisions by majority of members present; enter into force on adoption; non-compliance → legal liability (Annex 1 pts. 30–35 area).
* Secretariat / analytical support: **Centrul Național de Management al Crizelor**.

### B. Territorial layer (Annex 2)

* Regional/territorial and local crisis commissions + **COC** operational centres under LPA lead; activate on danger/crisis; coordinated with the National Centre.

### C. Consequential GD amendments (Annex 3)

* Amends several GDs, including contact/organisation tweaks and the **electroenergetic exceptional-situations action plan** under [[HG 820-2024 — situatii exceptionale electroenergetic (text)|HG 820/2024]] (e.g. protected-consumer wording: kindergartens → elderly asylums in one limb).

### D. Local implementation clocks (pct. 4–5)

* LPAs: **1 month** to set up commissions/COCs; central specialty authorities: **2 months** for related acts.

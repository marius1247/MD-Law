# HG nr. 365/2024 — obligația de stocare gaze naturale — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HG 365-2024 — obligatie stocare gaze naturale (text)]] ✅ *full (~22 pts)* · **Amended by:** [[HG 677-2024 — plan sezon incalzire 2024-2025 (notă)|677/2024]] (pt. 4 → 50 mcm EnC/EU) · **Related:** [[HG 364-2024 — modificare stocuri securitate gaze (notă)|364/2024]] (HG 668 patch) · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Designates **Energocom** as storage-obligation holder; sets **15% consumption** storage level, procurement mechanics, separate accounting, cost recovery, profit caps, and crisis-use rules under gas exceptional-situations regulation.
> **Primary business risk:** Conflating this **storage obligation** (HG 365) with **security stocks** under HG 668/364 (47.1 mcm) or seasonal plans (HG 677) — three linked but distinct instruments.

## 1. Operative highlights

| Topic | Rule (pts. 1–22 area) |
|---|---|
| **Holder** | S.A. Energocom (licensed supplier with storage experience) |
| **Volume** | 15% of national consumption (pt. 3) |
| **EU storage** | Pt. 4 (original): EnC/EU facilities — **replaced by HG 677** (50 mcm) |
| **Procurement** | Monthly tender rounds, supplier notifications (pts. 5–17) |
| **Costs** | Recovered via regulated mechanism; ANRE approval (pts. 8, 20) |
| **Crisis use** | Alert/emergency under gas exceptional-situations reg. (pt. 18 area) |

# HG nr. 379/2026 — early IF for CNMC core — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HG 379-2026 — modificare HG 346-2026 crize (text)]] ✅ · **Amends:** [[HG 346-2026 — Comisia Nationala Management Crize (text)]] pct. 6 · **Countersigned by:** Minister of Energy · Minister of Justice

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** One-point amendment: pulls **formation of the CNMC**, **approval of its regulation (pct. 2.1)** and **Annex 1** into force on **28 July 2026**, instead of waiting the general one-month clock in HG 346 pct. 6.
> **Primary business risk:** Assuming the national commission was inert until ~10 August 2026 — the strategic layer was live from **28 July 2026**.

---

## 1. Mechanics

* **Basis:** L100/2017 arts. 56(3¹), 62, 63.
* **Substance:** HG 346/2026 pct. 6 completed with exception text for pct. 1, pct. 2.1, Annex 1 → IF **28.07.2026**.
* **This HG’s IF:** on publication (28.07.2026).

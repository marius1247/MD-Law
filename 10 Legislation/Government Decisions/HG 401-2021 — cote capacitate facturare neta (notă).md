# HG nr. 401/2021 — net-billing capacity quotas — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HG 401-2021 — cote capacitate facturare neta (text)]] ⚠️ *annex 2 only* · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Successor track:** [[HG 599-2025 — limite capacitate regenerabile 2030 (notă)|HG 599/2025]] · **Concept:** [[Concept — Facturare netă]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Annex 2 sets the **100 MW** net-billing capacity envelope and **individual plant ceilings** for households (≤10 kW or consumption-based formula) and budget institutions (consumption/contracted-power formula), valid **until 31.12.2025**.
> **Primary business risk:** Advising 2026+ prosumer sizing from this annex after the 2030 package ([[HG 599-2025 — limite capacitate regenerabile 2030 (notă)|599/2025]]) — verify which envelope governs the connection date.

## Operative mechanics (annex 2)

* **Aggregate quota:** 100 MW for PV / wind / biogas net-billing installations.
* **Households:** choose ≤10 kW **or** `P = E_max / k` with k = 1200 (PV), 1600 (wind), 6100 (biogas) kWh/kW·year.
* **Budget institutions:** individual ceiling from max(3-year peak annual consumption, contracted power × OSD category average) / same k factors.

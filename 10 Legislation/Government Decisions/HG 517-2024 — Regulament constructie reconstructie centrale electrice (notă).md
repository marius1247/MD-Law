# HG nr. 517/2024 — regulation on construction/reconstruction of power plants — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HG 517-2024 — Regulament constructie reconstructie centrale electrice (text)]] ✅ · **Patched by:** [[HG 596-2025 — infrastructura transport EE autorizare centrale echilibrare (notă)|HG 596/2025]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)|L164/2025]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Government authorisation procedure for **new / reconstructed / extended power plants > 20 MW** (cumulative installed power after HG 596). Commission at ME; HG decision names applicant, capacity, technology; refusals contestable.
> **Primary business risk:** Filing under repealed L107 article cites, or omitting the **FDI/security investment pre-approval** added by HG 596 pt. 28.6.

---

## 1. Scope & exceptions (post-HG 596)

* Applies to new plants **> 20 MW**, reconstruction > 20 MW, extensions where **cumulative installed power > 20 MW**.
* **Exceptions:** winners of competitive/auction processes under L164; large eligible producers >20 MW under L10 auctions.
* Enabling clause remapped to **L164 art. 4(1)(g)**; internal cites arts. **105–106** (was L107 19–20).
* New checklist item: **Consiliul pentru Examinarea Investițiilor de Importanță pentru Securitatea Statului** pre-approval.
* Annex 2: abrogates legacy plant-specific HGs (1151/1999, 262/2000, 215/2001, 436/2004 regulation, 713/2004, 1440/2004).

---

## 2. Procedure sketch

1. File to ME / Authorisation Commission (technology, construction schedule, EIA gate, land title/superficie, etc.).
2. Commission decides accept / refuse; on accept, draft HG with applicant identity, capacity, technology.
3. HG authorisation published; refusal → administrative challenge path.
4. Timelines run from **MO publication** (HG 596 tweak to pt. 44).

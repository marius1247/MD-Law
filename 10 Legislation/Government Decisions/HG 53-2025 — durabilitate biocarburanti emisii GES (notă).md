> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Approves **sustainability criteria** and **GHG calculation methodology** for biofuels, bioliquids and biomass fuels — EnC/EU RED II transposition layer for transport renewable-energy accounting.
> **Primary business risk:** Import/sale of non-compliant biofuels without sustainability proof; missing link to [[HG 74-2025 — calcul consum energie regenerabila (notă)|74/2025]] consumption-statistics regulation once annex ingested.

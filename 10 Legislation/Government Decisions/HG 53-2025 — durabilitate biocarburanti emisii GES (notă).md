# HG nr. 53/2025 — biocarburanți / emisii GES — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HG 53-2025 — durabilitate biocarburanti emisii GES (text)]] ✅ *regulation + annexes 1–2; annex 3 still missing* · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Parent:** [[Legea 10-2016 — surse regenerabile (notă)|L10/2016]] · **Stats bridge:** [[HG 74-2025 — calcul consum energie regenerabila (notă)|74/2025]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Approves **sustainability criteria** and **GHG calculation methodology** for biofuels, bioliquids and biomass fuels — EnC/EU RED II transposition layer for transport renewable-energy accounting.
> **Primary business risk:** Import/sale of non-compliant biofuels without sustainability proof; mis-scoring advanced vs capped feedstocks when using Annex 2 double-counting rules.

## Annex layer (now in vault)

* **Annex 1 — Energy content table:** mass (MJ/kg) and volume (MJ/l) lower heating values for biomass-derived and renewable fuels (biopropane, FAME/FAEE, HVO, co-processed oils, FT liquids, DME, renewable H₂, ETBE/MTBE renewable shares, etc.).
* **Annex 2 — Feedstock types:**
  * *Section 1* — advanced / double-counted feedstocks (algae, biowaste, straw, manure, bagasse, lignocellulosic fractions, etc.).
  * *Section 2* — capped double-counted streams (used cooking oil; category 1–2 animal fats under L129/2019).
* **Annex 3** — still missing from the dump (placeholder only).

## Checklist

- [ ] Match each consignment’s feedstock to Annex 2 section before claiming advanced contribution.
- [ ] Use Annex 1 energy-content factors consistently with [[HG 74-2025 — calcul consum energie regenerabila (notă)|74/2025]] statistics once that annex is complete.
- [ ] Track Environment Agency voluntary-scheme certificates (regulation pts. 35–36) and CNED origin/feedstock disclosures.

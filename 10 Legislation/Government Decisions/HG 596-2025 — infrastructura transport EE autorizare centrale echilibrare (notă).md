# HG nr. 596/2025 — TSO corridor + plant authorisation + balancing-capacity PSO — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HG 596-2025 — infrastructura transport EE autorizare centrale echilibrare (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · [[MOC — Racordare și acces la rețele]] · **Patches:** HG 1037/2023 · [[HG 517-2024 — Regulament constructie reconstructie centrale electrice (notă)|HG 517/2024]] ✅ · [[HG 26-2025 — PSO acces retea producatori regenerabile pret fix (notă)|HG 26/2025]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Three-track Government patch — (1) clean-up of **Vulcănești–Chișinău** expropriation parcels/rules (HG 1037/2023); (2) **remap HG 517/2024** plant-construction regulation from L107 → **L164**, add FDI/security investment pre-approval; (3) extend [[HG 26-2025 — PSO acces retea producatori regenerabile pret fix (notă)|HG 26/2025]] PSO priority queue to **winners of balancing-capacity auctions** (storage / plants).
> **Primary business risk:** Ignoring balancing-auction winners in the HG 26 priority stack; or still applying L107 article cites inside HG 517 after this remap.

> [!danger] HANRE 168 inside pt. 3
> Amended HG 26 text still cites **HANRE 168/2019** (abrogated). For live connection procedure use [[HANRE 311-2026 — racordarea la retelele electrice (notă)|311/2026]].

---

## 1. Amendment map

### A. HG 1037/2023 (Vulcănești–Chișinău expropriation)
* Public-land partial expropriation registration via APP-approved geometric plan (pt. 4 rewrite).
* Cadastral/destination/owner errors: continue on Land Register data **without new HG** (pt. 4¹).
* Multiple annex 1/2/4 parcel corrections (Iujnoe, Băcioi, Cimișlia corridor, Găgăuzia, etc.).

### B. HG 517/2024 (plant construction/reconstruction regulation)
* Enabling clause → **L164/2025** art. 4(1)(g).
* Threshold language: „capacitate adițională” → **„putere instalată cumulată”**; >20 MW install / uprate under L164 arts. 4 & 106.
* Exceptions: competitive/auction winners under L164; large eligible producers >20 MW under L10 auctions.
* New file item: **Consiliul pentru Examinarea Investițiilor de Importanță pentru Securitatea Statului** pre-approval (pt. 28.6 + application checklist).
* Decision timelines run from **MO publication**.

### C. HG 26/2025 PSO — balancing-capacity winners
* Title + scope: fixed-price large eligible producers **and** balancing-capacity auction winners.
* Enabling → L164 art. 4(1)(e), art. 17(2)–(3).
* Priority wait-list, first-come among priority cohort, SO cooperation / available-capacity info — same mechanics extended to balancing winners (storage/plants).

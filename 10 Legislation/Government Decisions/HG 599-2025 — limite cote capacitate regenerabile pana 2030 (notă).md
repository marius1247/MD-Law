# HG nr. 599/2025 — RES capacity limits & net-billing ceilings to 2030 — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HG 599-2025 — limite cote capacitate regenerabile pana 2030 (text)]] ⚠️ *annexes 1–2 missing* · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · [[Concept — Facturare netă]] · [[Concept — Producător eligibil]] · **Abrogates:** HG 401/2021 (incl. patches such as [[HG 329-2025 — modificare HG 401-2021 cote capacitate regenerabile (notă)|HG 329/2025]])

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Sets RES **support-scheme capacity limits/quotas** (an.1) and **net-billing individual ceilings** (an.2) valid to **31.12.2030**; operational reporting via DSOs/suppliers to **CNED**; multi-site rules (household formula / non-household sum, **≤ 200 kW**); prosumers may recalculate ceilings per annex-2 formula.
> **Primary business risk:** Quoting MW caps from HG 401/329 — **abrogated** on IF of this HG (19.09.2025). Actual numbers are in the **missing annexes**.

> [!danger] Annex gap
> Without annexes 1–2, do not advise auction quotas or net-billing kW ceilings from the vault. Fetch from legis.md / MO.

## 1. Dispositif mechanics

| Point | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Approves an.1 (support limits/quotas) + an.2 (net-billing ceilings) to **31.12.2030** |
| 2 | DSO publishes annual avg consumption/kW contracted; monthly reporting of new avisuri & net-billing contracts to CNED; remuneration only within individual ceiling if oversized; CNED monthly publication |
| 3–4 | Multi-site household / non-household off-site plants (L10 39¹/39²) — ceiling rules, **≤ 200 kW** |
| 5 | Recalculation uplift of individual ceiling per annex-2 formula |
| 6 | **Abrogates HG 401/2021** (as amended) |
| 7–8 | ME control; IF on publication |

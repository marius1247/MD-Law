# HG nr. 620/2025 — FEE asset handover to beneficiaries — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HG 620-2025 — transmitere bunuri Fond eficienta energetica (text)]] ⚠️ *forms only* · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Authority:** [[CNED]] · **Parent:** [[Legea 139-2018 — eficienta energetica (notă)|L139/2018]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Operationalises **gratuitous transfer** to project beneficiaries of goods/works/services procured from the **Energy Efficiency Fund** and other CNED-managed sources — via a standard **handover act**, asset list, and value documentation pack.
> **Primary business risk:** Accounting / ownership disputes if the handover act is signed without the financing-committee decision + financing contract + director order chain (those live in the missing regulation body).

## Mechanics visible from the forms

1. **IP CNED → Beneficiary** free-of-charge transfer of listed assets/services.
2. Beneficiary must **book the value** under [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)|L287/2017]] categories.
3. From signature, **risk of fortuitous loss** and duty of care pass to the beneficiary.
4. Document retention **≥ 6 years** for CNED / control authorities.

# HG nr. 620/2025 — transfer of FEE-financed assets via CNED — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HG 620-2025 — transmitere bunuri Fond eficienta energetica CNED (text)]] ⚠️ *form annexes missing* · **CNED:** [[HG 1060-2023 — organizare functionare CNED (notă)|HG 1060]] · [[CNED]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Approves the regulation for **free-of-charge transfer** to project beneficiaries of goods/works/services procured from the **Energy Efficiency Fund** (and other CNED sources); empowers CNED to sign special primary documents and handover acts.
> **Primary business risk:** Treating FEE assets as remaining CNED property after handover, or missing beneficiary lock-up / maintenance commitments in the handover act.

---

## 1. Mechanics

Transfer stack (pt. 3): Financing & Risk Committee decision → financing contract → Director order → handover act (+ fiscal invoice).

Regulation body (pts 1–17): reception of constructions; handover extinguishes CNED administration rights; mandatory beneficiary commitments (integrity, maintenance, use restrictions, risk of fortuitous loss); refusal to sign → escalation; remediation / recovery if commitments breached; public procurement for third-party benefit under Civil Code arts. 1096–1099; **6-year** document retention by beneficiaries.

**IF:** one month after MO publication.

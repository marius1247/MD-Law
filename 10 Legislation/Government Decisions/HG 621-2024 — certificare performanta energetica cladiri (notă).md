# HG nr. 621/2024 — certificare performanță energetică clădiri — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HG 621-2024 — certificare performanta energetica cladiri (text)]] ✅ *full regulation (~76 pts)* · **Pair:** [[HG 622-2024 — calificare evaluatori energetici (notă)|622/2024]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **EE law:** [[Legea 139-2018 — eficienta energetica (notă)|L139/2018]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Approves the **Regulation on energy-performance certification (EPC)** of buildings and building units — certificate content, electronic register, buyer/tenant information duties. Replaces HG 896/2016. Parent: **L282/2023** *(not yet in vault)*.
> **Primary business risk:** Real-estate transactions or public-building compliance advice without checking EPC validity against this regulation + qualified evaluator under [[HG 622-2024 — calificare evaluatori energetici (notă)|622/2024]].

> Dispositif pt. 6: regulation IF on publication **except Cap. VI** → IF after **6 months**.

# HG nr. 622/2024 — calificare evaluatori energetici — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HG 622-2024 — calificare evaluatori energetici (text)]] ✅ *full regulation (~59 pts)* · **Pair:** [[HG 621-2024 — certificare performanta energetica cladiri (notă)|621/2024]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Regulation on **qualification and registration** of energy performance assessors and inspectors (heating, ventilation/AC systems). Operational gate for who may issue EPCs under [[HG 621-2024 — certificare performanta energetica cladiri (notă)|621/2024]].
> **Primary business risk:** Invalid EPC if issued by non-registered evaluator — check register requirements in this act before relying on a certificate.

# HG nr. 668/2022 — gas security stocks (Energocom) — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HG 668-2022 — stocuri securitate gaze naturale (text)]] ✅ consolidated · **Patches:** [[HG 364-2024 — modificare stocuri securitate gaze (notă)|364/2024]] → [[HG 302-2025 — modificare HG 668-2022 stocuri securitate gaze (notă)|302/2025]] → **HG 299/2026** *(amend not ingested)* · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Designates SPA „Energocom” as the entity to **create and maintain gas security stocks**; retail suppliers fund via ANRE-set contributions; stocks released on CSE/emergency decisions.
> **Primary business risk:** Citing **50 mcm** (HG 302) as current. Consolidated pt. 6 is **56.3 milioane m³** via **HG 299/2026** (IF 12.06.2026).

> [!warning] Currency — volume ladder
> 47.1 mcm (364) → 50 mcm (302, by 1.10.2025) → **56.3 mcm (299/2026)**. HG 299 text not yet in vault; figure comes from this consolidation.

> Pt. 8 still names **Comisia pentru Situații Excepționale**. Architecture repealed/replaced by [[HG 346-2026 — Comisia Nationala Management Crize (notă)|HG 346/2026]] (CNMC). Remap drawdown authority in practice to CNMC / L248 stack.

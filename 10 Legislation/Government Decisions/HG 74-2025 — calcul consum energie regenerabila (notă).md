# HG nr. 74/2025 — calcul consum energie regenerabilă — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HG 74-2025 — calcul consum energie regenerabila (text)]] ✅ *regulation* · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Pair:** [[HG 53-2025 — durabilitate biocarburanti emisii GES (notă)|53/2025]] · **PNIEC frame:** [[HG 86-2025 — PNIEC 2025-2030 (notă)|HG 86/2025]] · **Authority:** [[CNED]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** **Regulation on calculation of renewable energy consumption** — transparent rules for RES shares in gross final energy consumption overall and by sector (**SER-E**, **SER-T**, **SER-Î&R**). Partial RED II / RED III transposition layer + RFNBO production methodology (Delegated Reg. 2023/1184). ME + CNED + BNS cooperation. Abrogates HG 270/2020. Transport provisions IF **1 Jul 2027** subject to EnC notification (per dispositif).
> **Primary business risk:** Mis-stating national/sectoral RES percentages; ignoring RFNBO temporal-correlation rules in the regulation annexes.

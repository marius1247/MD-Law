# HG nr. 801/2025 — extend / remap electricity security PSO — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HG 801-2025 — modificare HG 1059-2023 PSO securitate EE (text)]] ✅ · **Parent:** [[HG 1059-2023 — PSO securitate aprovizionare energie electrica (notă)|HG 1059/2023]] · **Prior extend:** [[HG 156-2025 — modificare HG 1059-2023 PSO securitate EE (notă)|HG 156/2025]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** (1) Remaps HG 1059 enabling cite from repealed L107 to **L164/2025** art. 4(1)(e) + 17(3); (2) extends PSO end date from **31.12.2025** to **31.12.2026**.
> **Primary business risk:** Treating HG 156 as the last word on the PSO calendar — **801** (IF publication **26.12.2025**) supersedes that sunset.

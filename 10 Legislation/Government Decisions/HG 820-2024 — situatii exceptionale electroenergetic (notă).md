# HG nr. 820/2024 — exceptional situations in the electricity sector — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HG 820-2024 — situatii exceptionale electroenergetic (text)]] ⚠️ *dispositif only — Annexes 1–2 missing* · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Synthesis:** [[Energetică — synthesis]] · **Control:** Ministry of Energy

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Approves the **Regulation on exceptional situations in the electroenergetic sector** (Annex 1) and the **Action plan** (Annex 2), transposing **Regulation (EU) 2019/941** (risk preparedness) as adapted by EnC Decision **2021/13/MC-EnC**. Abrogates HG 149/2019.
> **Primary business risk:** The binding operational rules — crisis scenarios, roles of TSO/DSO/suppliers, load-shedding, information duties — sit in the **missing annexes**. Do not advise on concrete crisis obligations from the dispositif alone.

## 1. Statutory hierarchy & legal foundation

* **Government decision** approving secondary regulation + action plan.
* **Stated enabling basis in text:** art. 4(1)(m) of [[Legea 107-2016 — energia electrica (text)|L107/2016]].
* **Currency problem:** L107/2016 was **repealed 19.08.2025** by [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (text)|L164/2025]]. HG 820 remains in force as a Government act, but its enabling citation is stale. Locate the L164 equivalent competence (security of supply / exceptional measures / Government powers) before litigating validity or amendment procedure.
* **EU acquis:** Reg. **(EU) 2019/941** on electricity risk preparedness (repeals Dir. 2005/89/EC), EnC-adapted.
* **Crisis neighbours:** [[Legea 101-2026 — consolidarea mecanismelor de interventie in situatii de criza (notă)]] (petroleum / procurement / L164 auction transition — different instrument); Law 248/2025 crisis management *(not ingested)*.

---

## 2. What the dispositif actually decides

From the approving decision only:

1. Approves **Annex 1** — Regulation on exceptional situations in the electroenergetic sector.
2. Approves **Annex 2** — Action plan for exceptional situations in the electroenergetic sector.
3. **Abrogates HG 149/2019** (previous frame).
4. **Ministry of Energy** controls execution.
5. In force on MO publication (17.12.2024, MO 525–528 art. 954).

Everything else — definitions of exceptional situations, regional cooperation, risk assessments, crisis protocols, priority customers, manual load shedding, TSO instructions, reporting to the Energy Community — is **annex territory** and not in vault.

## 3. How to use this act until the annex arrives

| Safe to say from vault text | Not safe without annex |
|---|---|
| HG 820 is the current Government risk-preparedness vehicle for electricity | Who must do what in a named crisis scenario |
| It replaces HG 149/2019 | Exact load-shedding hierarchy / protected customers |
| It claims to transpose Reg. 2019/941 (EnC-adapted) | Cross-border assistance procedures |
| Ministry of Energy owns execution oversight | Penalties / specific information deadlines |
| Enabling cite was L107 art. 4(1)(m) — needs L164 remap | Interaction details with market suspension / balancing |

**Working method:** treat [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)|L164/2025]] security-of-supply and Government/ANRE emergency powers as the primary statute layer; use HG 820 as the designated implementing regulation **once Annex 1 is ingested**; use [[Legea 101-2026 — consolidarea mecanismelor de interventie in situatii de criza (notă)|L101/2026]] for petroleum and procurement crisis patches, not for electricity load protocols.

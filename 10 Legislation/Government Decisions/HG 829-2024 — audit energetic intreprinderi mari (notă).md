# HG nr. 829/2024 — audit energetic întreprinderi mari — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HG 829-2024 — audit energetic intreprinderi mari (text)]] ✅ *full regulation (~31 pts)* · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **EE law:** [[Legea 139-2018 — eficienta energetica (notă)|L139/2018]] · **CNED:** [[CNED]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Approves the **Regulation on energy audits for large enterprises** under L139/2018 — audit cycle, auditor requirements, reporting, follow-up measures. CNED must notify first large enterprises within one month of IF.
> **Primary business risk:** Large industrial clients missing mandatory audit deadlines or using non-compliant auditors — operative detail in regulation pts. 1–31.

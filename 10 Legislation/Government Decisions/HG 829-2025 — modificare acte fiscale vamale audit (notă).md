# HG nr. 829/2025 — fiscal / customs / internal-audit GD package — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HG 829-2025 — modificare acte fiscale vamale audit (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Fiscalitate & Contabilitate]] · **Primary target:** [[HG 93-2013 — restituirea TVA (notă)]] · **IF:** **1 January 2026**

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Government implementing package for late-2025 fiscal amendments — expands the VAT-refund regulation for energy reverse charge and a gas transitional path, inserts a **stock-option plan** regulation under HG 693/2018, tweaks payroll benefit valuation, customs cash-register annex, customs implementing rules, and internal-audit staffing criteria.
> **Primary business risk:** Energy suppliers and gas traders who keep filing under the pre-2026 refund chapters miss XI²/XI³ maths and the staged payout; employers launching ESOP-style plans without the new annex’s 3-year vesting / 25% cap / tax timing rules create unexpected payroll and corporate-tax exposure.

## 2. Practical legal mechanics

### A. VAT refund — energy reverse charge & gas transition (pct. 1)

* Adds CF art. **101(5¹)** and Law 1417 art. **4(20⁹)** to the refund catalogue (pct. 2 of HG93).
* General refund term restated at **45 days**; art. 4(20⁹) path: **22-day** control/decision then **4 × ≤25%** annual tranches.
* New chapters **XI²** (art. 101(5¹) calculation vs art. 101⁷ energy supplies) and **XI³** (Dec-2025 gas traders, box 23 > MDL 10m → lower of declared/verified).
* Extra documents for XI²: energy supply contract + invoices marked «Taxare inversă».

→ Work from the consolidated [[HG 93-2013 — restituirea TVA (text)]].

### B. Payroll in-kind benefits (pct. 2)

* Employer cost for employee use of employer-owned assets: **0.0373% of tax unamortised value** per asset per day, frozen for the whole use period (replaces “base value” drafting).
* Annual taxable-income test must include Ch. 10² / 10³ special regimes (Titlul II CF).

### C. Stock-option plan — Annex 2¹ to HG 693/2018 (pct. 3)

| Rule | Content |
|---|---|
| Approval | Company (or interdependent company) GMS regulation + individual contracts |
| Cap | Participation titles ≤ **25%** of share capital (all participants) |
| Eligibility | Resident employee/administrator; ≥ **3 years** tenure; performance objectives; no conflict |
| Vesting | Minimum **3 years** from grant to exercise |
| Price | Preferential or free grant allowed |
| Tax at grant | Participation rights **non-taxable** for income tax & social contributions at grant |
| Tax at exercise / sale | Ordinary regime thereafter; base value = acquisition price paid by beneficiary (0 if free) |
| Company deduction | **No** deduction of buy-back / cancellation costs of options |
| Transfer | Rights non-transferable before exercise (per annex text) |

### D. Customs / cash registers / internal audit (pct. 4–7)

* Cash-register annex and Customs Code implementing pct. 93¹ aligned with the fiscal package.
* HG 655/2023: internal-audit staffing threshold adjusted (**25** posts; ≥ **80%** occupancy condition inserted).

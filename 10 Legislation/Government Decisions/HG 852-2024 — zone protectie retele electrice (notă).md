# HG nr. 852/2024 — electricity network protection zones — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HG 852-2024 — zone protectie retele electrice (text)]] ⚠️ body ✅ · form annexes ❌ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Connection:** [[HANRE 311-2026 — racordarea la retelele electrice (notă)|HANRE 311/2026]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Approves the **Regulation on protection zones** for electricity networks (LEA/LEC, stations); binds OS / authorities / designers; repeals legacy protection / consumption HGs (incl. HG 514/2002).
> **Primary business risk:** Building or excavating in protection zones without OS **agreement**; or relying on repealed HG 514/2002 rules after IF (publication 27.12.2024).

> [!tip] Currency
> Enabling clause remapped to **L164** by HG 248/2026 (also patches pts 7 and 40). Form models (Regulation annexes 1–2) missing from PDF.

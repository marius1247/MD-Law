# Passenger Road Transport Regulation — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HG 854-2006 — transporturi auto calatori (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Drept comercial]] · **Framework:** [[Legea 1194-1997 — transporturi (notă)]] · **Challenge:** [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Government *Regulament* on passenger/baggage road transport (scheduled, occasional, own-account, taxi): licensing prerequisites, **route activity permits**, route allocation/contest, tariffs, passenger rights, baggage, control and claims.
> **Primary business risk:** Public passenger ops with licence but **without route permit / allocated route** (pts. 6, III) → loss of route rights, licence interaction, vehicle stop; tariff and ticket-refund breaches → passenger claims and admin sanctions.

> [!warning] Enabling cite in text
> The Regulament preamble cites **art. 2 of the Road Transport Code (Law 116-XIV/1998)**, not L1194. L1194 remains the general transport framework; treat HG 854 as road-passenger special regulation under the auto code + L1194 system.

## 2. Practical legal mechanics

### Scope & service typology (Section I)
* Binding on licensed carriers and beneficiaries for domestic and international passenger/baggage road transport.
* Carriers need **public passenger road licence** + prove technical base, professional competence, financial capacity (docs in pt. 6).
* Categories: national / international; types: public (scheduled; occasional — on-demand / tourist); taxi; own-account; regimes ordinary / rapid / express.
* Ban on converted general-purpose vans for public passenger use (phased historic exclusions).
* Vehicles operate on a **permis de activitate pe rută** per vehicle — dossier: licence, conformity certificate, star classification (where required), driver/manager training certificates (every 3 years).
* International scheduled/occasional, national >100 km scheduled/occasional, and tourist services: coaches ≥17 seats, star-classified (own-account occasional exempt from star rule).

### Passenger rights & duties (Section II)
* Child fares (free under 7 without seat; 50% rules); guide dog free access for persons with disabilities (HG592/2017).
* Refund / reissue rules: delay >1h or wrong/lower class seat → full fare; miss bus ≤3h (72h if illness/accident) → reissue +25% or refund −25%; return ticket ≥2h before departure → full fare minus advance-sale commission; 15 min–2h → −15% + commission.
* Baggage for tariff; declared value; compensation right; free timely information.
* Duties: buy/keep ticket; public-order rules; bans (distract driver, dangerous goods, etc.). Carrier/crew duties and prohibitions follow in same section.

### Scheduled routes (Section III)
* Routes based on socio-economic links; buses run to published timetable; new routes after joint road/police/carrier safety check → route passport.
* Right-to-serve applications examined in **30 days**; competing applications → contest under Annex 3 rules.
* Refusal if contrary to Regulament or existing carrier already fully meets demand.
* After award: start service ≤ **30 days** or lose right; routes **non-transferable**.
* Withdrawal grounds: own request; licence withdrawal; >10% trips suspended over three consecutive months (weather/road exceptions); uncured prior breaches; unsuitable vehicles; breach of pt. 21(1).
* On withdrawal, route re-contested; incumbent may continue pending new award; challenge withdrawal within **30 days**.
* Temporary suspension for weather/road: bus-station head + road police, with notification duties.

### Occasional, own-account, taxi (Sections IV–VI)
* Occasional: licence + pt. 6 docs; fares must not undercut approved ceiling tariffs for scheduled passenger-km.
* Own-account: no public fare; specific conditions.
* Taxi: licence (patent transitional language in older text); professional certificates; visible tariffs; taximeter modes; shortest reasonable route duty.

### Baggage, control, claims (Sections VII–IX)
* Hand baggage / hold baggage rules and storage.
* Control of passenger road activity by competent bodies.
* Claims, actions, refunds, damage recovery procedures.

## 3. Risk matrix

| Provision | Flaw / ambiguity | Practical risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pts. 6 / route permit** | Licence ≠ right to serve route | Ops stopped / route lost | Permit per vehicle + route award before first trip |
| **Section III contest** | Multi-applicant opacity | Lost route / litigation | File complete passport + contest pack in 30-day clock |
| **Withdrawal (10% trips)** | Weather exceptions poorly evidenced | Route stripped | Document suspensions with police/road acts |
| **Tariffs** | Occasional undercutting ceilings | Admin fine | Price ≥ approved passenger-km ceiling |
| **Passenger refunds** | Staff unaware of 2h/15min/1h grids | Compensation leakage | Publish refund matrix at stations/vehicles |
| **Text quality** | Dash points; `##` splits mid-numbers in vault text | Wrong point cites | Cite section + paraphrase; verify against MO |

## 4. Client checklist

- [ ] Hold public passenger licence + pt. 6 capacity file.
- [ ] Obtain **permis de activitate pe rută** for each vehicle.
- [ ] For scheduled routes: passport, 30-day filing, contest readiness (Annex 3).
- [ ] Start awarded route within 30 days; never transfer route rights.
- [ ] Display approved tariffs; taxi taximeter + door tariffs.
- [ ] Implement passenger refund/reissue grid and child/disability rules.
- [ ] Keep training certificates current (3-year cycle).
- [ ] Challenge route-withdrawal within 30 days via [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]].

# HG nr. 86/2025 — National Energy and Climate Plan 2025–2030 — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HG 86-2025 — PNIEC 2025-2030 (text) — Index]] ✅ *split A / B / Anexe* · **Governance:** [[HG 10-2024 — guvernanta energetica si actiuni climatice (notă)|HG 10/2024]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · [[Energetică — synthesis]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Moldova’s **Integrated National Energy and Climate Plan (PNIEC) 2025–2030** — strategic planning document covering the five Energy Union dimensions (decarbonisation, energy efficiency, security of supply, internal energy market, R&I/competitiveness), with 2030 targets and policy/measure fiches, plus analytical baselines and projections to 2050.
> **Primary business risk:** Treating PNIEC adoption as creating immediately enforceable tariff or licensing rights — the plan itself states adoption does **not** by itself change fiscal policy, budgets, or the regulatory framework described; it steers subsequent legislation and investment programming.

## 1. What it is (and is not)

* **Is:** EnC/EU governance-aligned planning act; investment & policy roadmap; NDC-linked decarbonisation narrative; RES / EE / interconnection ambition frame.
* **Is not:** a substitute for [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)|L164]], [[Legea 10-2016 — surse regenerabile (notă)|L10]], [[Legea 139-2018 — eficienta energetica (notă)|L139]], or ANRE methodologies.

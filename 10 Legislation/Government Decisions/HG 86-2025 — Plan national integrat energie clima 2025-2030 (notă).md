# HG nr. 86/2025 — National Energy & Climate Plan (PNIEC) 2025–2030 — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HG 86-2025 — Plan national integrat energie clima 2025-2030 (text)]] ⚠️ *dispositif only* · **Governance parent:** [[HG 10-2024 — guvernanta energetica si actiuni climatice (notă)|HG 10/2024]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Formal Government approval of Moldova’s **PNIEC 2025–2030** — the strategic planning document required by the energy-climate governance mechanism (EnC-adapted Governance Regulation).
> **Primary business risk:** Treating the approving HG as the plan itself. The PDF contains only the **two-point dispositif**; the annexed PNIEC text is **not in the vault**. Policy targets, measures and investment signals live in the missing annex / published plan package.

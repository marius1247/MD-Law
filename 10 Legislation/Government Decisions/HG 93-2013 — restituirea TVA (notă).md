# HG nr. 93/2013 — VAT refund regulation — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[HG 93-2013 — restituirea TVA (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Fiscalitate & Contabilitate]] · **Parent:** [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 (notă)]] Titlul III · **Latest amend:** [[HG 829-2025 — modificare acte fiscale vamale audit (notă)]] (IF 1 Jan 2026)

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Implements the procedural path for recovering excess VAT from the State Tax Service (SFS) under Fiscal Code arts. 101 / 101¹ / 101³ / 101⁴ / 101⁶ and related transitional bases in Law 1417/1997.
> **Primary business risk:** Filing without the correct legal base, documents, or declaration alignment → request ignored or refused; energy reverse-charge refunds under art. 101(5¹) and the Dec-2025 gas transitional path have dedicated calculation chapters (XI² / XI³) added by HG829 — miss them and cash timing collapses.

## 2. Practical legal mechanics

### A. When refund is available (Reg. pct. 2)

Refund sits only on the listed CF / Law 1417 bases, including (post-HG829):

* CF art. 101(3), (5), **(5¹)** (energy reverse-charge excess), (6) leasing;
* CF arts. 101¹ (capital investments — successive redactions), 101⁴, 101⁶;
* **Law 1417/1997 art. 4(20⁹)** — transitional gas-trader path.

Non-registered VAT payers: only arts. 101³ / 101⁴ (pct. 3).

### B. How to file (Ch. II–III)

* Mandatory application (annex 1 model); must state amount + allocation to tax extinguishment / bank account (pct. 7). No amount → **not examined**.
* Amount must match the VAT return, with stated exceptions (pct. 8).
* SFS examines only if corresponding VAT returns were filed (pct. 11), subject to CF art. 266 prescription (pct. 5).

### C. Documentary matrix & calculation chapters (Ch. IV–XI³)

Chapters VI–XI prescribe base-specific computation. After HG829:

| Chapter | Base | Practice note |
|---|---|---|
| **XI²** (pct. 47⁴) | CF art. 101(5¹) | Net refund capped by VAT on energy supplies under simplification art. **101⁷**; need energy contract + reverse-charge invoices (pct. 32(24)) |
| **XI³** (pct. 47⁵) | Law 1417 art. 4(20⁹) | Dec-2025 gas traders with box-23 carry-forward **> MDL 10m** — take the lower of declared vs verified excess |

### D. Deadlines & SFS liability (Ch. XII + HG829 overlays)

* General refund clock: **45 days** from application (HG829 rewrite of pct. 19).
* Law 1417 art. 4(20⁹) path: **22 days** to control/decision, then payout in **4 annual tranches ≤ 25%** each (HG829).
* Late refund → contraventional exposure for SFS officers (pct. 48–50) and taxpayer interest under CF art. 176(3) (pct. 51).

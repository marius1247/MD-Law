# Non-Bank Credit Organizations Law — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 1-2018 — organizatiile de creditare (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Drept comercial]] · **Supervisor:** [[Legea 548-1995 — Banca Nationala a Moldovei (notă)|BNM]] · **AML:** [[Legea 308-2017 — prevenirea spalarii banilor (notă)]] · **Payments overlay:** [[Legea 114-2012 — servicii de plata (notă)]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Dual gate for non-bank credit organisations (OCN): (1) state registration with **NBM opinion** (arts. 13–15) and (2) entry in the **Register of authorised OCNs** (arts. 18–19). Core licensed activities are **non-bank credit** and **financial leasing** (art. 8); deposit-taking is banned (art. 9).
> **Primary business risk:** Conducting art. 8(2) activities without Register entry → unlawful lending; deposit-taking or other art. 9 breaches → suspension / strike-off and fines up to **10% of prior-year operating income** (art. 24).

## 2. Practical legal mechanics

### Perimeter (arts. 1, 3, 8–9)
* OCN may professionally carry out **only** non-bank credit activities as defined: **(a) non-bank loans** and **(b) financial leasing** (art. 8(2)).
* Ancillary: factoring, bancassurance agency, payment services / e-money under L114/2012, other leasing forms, connected activities (art. 8(4)).
* **Hard bans (art. 9):** attracting deposits or other repayable funds from the public; certain self-dealing capital operations; tying credit to purchase of the OCN’s other services; outsourcing credit/leasing origination except to licensed banks or registered OCNs.
* NBM alone decides whether an entity’s activity is professional non-bank credit (art. 8(3)).

### Market entry (arts. 10–19)
* Corporate formation + state registration requires **NBM aviz** (arts. 13–15); refusal grounds in art. 15.
* Capital rules in art. **17**; fitness of administrators art. **12**.
* Authorisation completes with **Register** entry (arts. 18–19). Operating without Register status is the core illegal-lending fact pattern.

### Suspension / strike-off (art. 20)
* Suspension up to **2 years**: own request; uncured remediation orders (incl. consumer-credit breaches referred by competent authority); false data used for aviz/Register; art. 9(1)(a) deposit breach; obstructing supervision; fictitious operations.
* During suspension: still under the law; may only **manage** credits/leasing granted *before* suspension — no new art. 8(2) origination.
* Strike-off: own request / insolvency / liquidation; uncured suspension grounds; no resume request after self-requested suspension expires. Strike-off for uncured suspension (art. 20(2)(b)) **bars re-registration**.

### Supervision & sanctions (arts. 23–24)
* NBM regulation, reporting, on-site powers (art. 23).
* Sanctions ladder (art. 24): written warning; fine ≤ **10%** prior-year operating income (floor **10,000 MDL**); administrator fines (1–10 average salaries); ownership-transparency fines ≤ **50%** of holding; suspension; Register strike-off. Remediation measures (prescriptions, activity limits, branch bans, admin replacement) may run with or without fines.

### Accounting & audit (arts. 21–22)
* Specific reporting to NBM; **mandatory audit** under art. 22.

## 3. Risk matrix

| Provision | Flaw / ambiguity | Practical risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Art. 8 perimeter** | “Connected/auxiliary” activities vs banking | Scope creep into deposit-like products | Map every product to art. 8(2)/(4); escalate edge cases to NBM |
| **Art. 9(a) deposits** | Crowdfunding / “investment” labels | Suspension + strike-off | No repayable funds from public; counsel funding stack |
| **Arts. 13–19 dual gate** | Aviz without Register (or reverse) | Unlawful lending | Checklist: aviz → state reg → Register before first disbursement |
| **Art. 20(2)(b)** | Uncured suspension → permanent bar | Irreversible market exit | Treat remediation deadlines as hard; escalate early |
| **Art. 24 fines** | 10% operating-income base | Material P&L hit | Pre-inspection file on reporting, KYC, consumer-credit compliance |
| **AML / L308** | Weak CDD on high-volume microcredit | Parallel AML file + licence risk | Align KYC with [[Legea 308-2017 — prevenirea spalarii banilor (notă)]] |

## 4. Client checklist

- [ ] Confirm activity is art. 8(2)/(4) only — no deposits (art. 9).
- [ ] Obtain NBM aviz before state registration; complete Register entry before lending.
- [ ] Fit-and-proper administrators (art. 12) + capital (art. 17).
- [ ] Separate consumer vs corporate books; consumer-credit rules referral path.
- [ ] Mandatory audit + NBM reporting calendar (arts. 21–22).
- [ ] AML programme under L308; ownership transparency file.
- [ ] If adding payments/e-money → separate L114 authorisation track.

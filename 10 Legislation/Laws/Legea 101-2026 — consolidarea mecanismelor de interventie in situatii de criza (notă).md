# Legea nr. 101/2026 — crisis intervention amending package — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 101-2026 — consolidarea mecanismelor de interventie in situatii de criza (text)]] ✅ *complete amending act* · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Also:** [[MOC — Achiziții publice & Statul]] · **Synthesis:** [[Energetică — synthesis]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** A short organic **amending law** (in force **26.06.2026**) that hardens crisis tools across petroleum, ANRE contraventions, public procurement price adjustment, and electricity eligible-producer / balancing auctions under [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (text)|L164/2025]].
> **Primary business risk:** Misreading it as a standalone “crisis code”. It is a **surgical patch** — the operative regime lives in the amended parent acts. For electricity auctions already running when art. 72(10) L164 was repealed, the transitional art. 150(21¹) can decide whether an old capacity figure still binds.

---

## 1. Statutory hierarchy & legal foundation

* Amending organic law; enters into force on **publication** (Art. V) — MO 280–283/26.06.2026.
* Does **not** create a new crisis framework of its own. It modifies:
  1. **Law No. 461/2001** — petroleum products market *(not yet ingested as a vault pair)*
  2. **Contraventional Code No. 218/2008** art. 41¹ — ANRE sanctioning competence
  3. **Law No. 131/2015** — public procurement art. 76 — price-adjustment clause
  4. **[[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (text)]]** — arts. 72 & 150
* Crisis-management overlay referenced: **Law No. 248/2025** on crisis management (alert / emergency states) — parent still not in vault; implementing [[HG 346-2026 — Comisia Nationala Management Crize (notă)|HG 346/2026]] (CNMC) and amending [[Legea 150-2026 — modificare management situatii de criza (notă)|L150/2026]] now ingested.
* Related security instrument: [[HG 820-2024 — situatii exceptionale electroenergetic (notă)]] (electricity risk preparedness; annex still missing).

## 2. Practical legal mechanics — article by article

### Art. I — Petroleum (Law 461/2001)

| Change | What it does |
|---|---|
| Redefines **„situație de criză în domeniul petrolier”** | Replaces the old “situație excepțională” notion: emergency / crisis / major crisis causing sharp rise in consumption of main petroleum products and/or sharp drop in imports from external market disruption or other external factors. → [[Concept — Situație de criză în domeniul petrolier]] |
| Art. 4(2) rewrite | When standard-type main-product stocks are exhausted, retail sale of **other** main-product types is allowed **only** at a price not exceeding the max price set for the standard type. |
| New art. 6(7) | During **alert or emergency** under Law 248/2025, the National Crisis Management Commission may grant **temporary derogations** from art. 4 and from the general max-price calculation norms (Annex 2), to keep supply and critical infrastructure running. |
| Art. 18(1) rewrite | Import only through designated customs crossing points; Government sets points and transport modality; after release for free circulation, movement to depots/stations under accompanying-document and accounting rules. |

**Commercial read:** price-cap architecture stays, but crisis can both **widen** the products that may be sold and **authorise temporary derogations** from the cap methodology. Anyone holding petroleum stocks or retail licences needs a playbook for Commission derogation decisions — they will move markets overnight.

### Art. II — ANRE contraventional competence (Code 218/2008)

Art. 41¹(2) rewritten: employees empowered by ANRE’s Director General may **ascertain contraventions, examine cases, and apply sanctions**. Art. 41¹(3) **abrogated**.

**Commercial read:** clearer in-house ANRE contravention pipeline — fewer procedural footholds to argue that only a narrow subset of staff could sanction. Pair with sectoral laws and [[ANRE]] for the offence catalogue; this amendment is about **who inside ANRE** may act.

### Art. III — Public procurement price adjustment (Law 131/2015 art. 76)

New alin. (4¹):

* Contracting authorities **may** set price-adjustment clauses — including in **contracts already in execution** — up **and** down, **only** for the unperformed residual part (no reopening of accepted performances).
* For contracts **under one year**, adjustment of cost components follows the Government regulation on periodic adjustment of continuous contracts >1 year, **and**:
  * only for **construction materials and/or fuel** with ≥ **±15%** price variation vs contract date, per National Bureau of Statistics data;
  * contracting authority must have **sufficient funds**.

**Commercial read:** this is the statutory hook contractors have been asking for in a high-inflation / fuel-shock environment — but it is **permissive**, not automatic. Without a clause (or an amendment inserting one) and without budget headroom, the ±15% movement does nothing. Sectoral procurement under [[Legea 74-2020 — achizitii sectoriale (notă)]] remains a separate track — do not assume art. 76(4¹) migrates automatically.

### Art. IV — Electricity (L164/2025)

1. **Art. 72(10) abrogated** — the vault’s L164 text is already consolidated to this repeal (see [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)]]).
2. **New art. 150(21¹)** — transitional rule for **unfinished** auctions for **large eligible-producer** status or **balancing-capacity construction** that were initiated before the repeal of art. 72(10): the principle in the repealed norm **continues to apply** to the plant capacity stated in that participant’s bid. Auctions are “finished” only on MO publication of the Government decision granting large eligible-producer status, or on signing of balancing-capacity supply contracts.

**Commercial read:** if you are mid-auction, your **bid capacity figure** may still be governed by the old art. 72(10) principle even though the article is gone. If you are designing a **new** auction after 26.06.2026, do not rely on 72(10) — read the live L164 auction / eligible-producer provisions and Government tender rules. → [[Concept — Producător eligibil]]

### Art. V — Entry into force

Publication day. No vacatio.

## 3. Legal ambiguities, vulnerabilities & risk matrix

| Provision | Identified flaw / ambiguity | Practical risk | Recommended strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Art. I / Law 248/2025** | Derogation power sits with Crisis Commission under a statute not in vault | Unclear process, duration, publication of derogations | Require MO / official channel monitoring; contract force-majeure & change-in-law clauses |
| **Art. I price rules** | “Other types” vs “standard type” max price | Retailers undersell / oversell into administrative offence | SKU mapping against the max-price decision before crisis retail |
| **Art. III ±15%** | Only materials & fuel; only residual performance; needs funds | Contractors assume automatic indexation | Draft express clause; define BNS series; escrow budget confirmation |
| **Art. III vs L74/2020** | General procurement patch; sectoral utilities unclear | Energy SO contracts left without relief | Check sectoral law + contract terms separately |
| **Art. IV 150(21¹)** | “Initiated and unfinished” — edge cases (relaunched rounds, capacity changes) | Disputes over which capacity figure locks | Freeze bid data room; get written ANRE/Ministry position on auction status |
| **Parents not ingested** | L461/2001, L248/2025, Contraventional Code absent as pairs | Incomplete petroleum / crisis advice from this note alone | Ingest parents before courtroom use |

## 5. Client action checklist / compliance roadmap

- [ ] **Petroleum:** map products to “standard type” max prices; draft crisis retail & Commission-derogation SOP.
- [ ] **ANRE-regulated persons:** assume broader ANRE staff may run contravention files; refresh internal response protocol.
- [ ] **Public contractors:** seek art. 76(4¹)-style clauses; for live contracts, negotiate amendments before claiming ±15%.
- [ ] **Auction participants (large eligible / balancing):** classify auction as finished or unfinished under art. 150(21¹); preserve bid capacity evidence.
- [ ] **New auction designers:** do not cite repealed art. 72(10); use current L164 + Government decisions.

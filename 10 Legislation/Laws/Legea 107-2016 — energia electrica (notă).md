## What it was
Third-package electricity organic law (Dir. 2009/72/EC). Deliberately a **delegation statute**: objectives + ANRE competences, with operational detail in HANRE acts. That cascade still explains why so many pre-2025 ANRE acts cite L107 `temei legal` — and why they now need L164 remapping.

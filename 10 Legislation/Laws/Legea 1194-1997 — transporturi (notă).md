# Transport Law — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 1194-1997 — transporturi (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Drept comercial]] · **Road passengers:** [[HG 854-2006 — transporturi auto calatori (notă)]] · **Air:** [[Codul aerian 301-2017 (notă)]] · **Rail:** [[Codul transportului feroviar 19-2022 (notă)]] · **Challenge:** [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Short framework law (**24 articles**) on the legal/economic organisation of the national transport system. It points liability, licensing detail, and operational rules to **sectoral codes** and special acts.
> **Primary business risk:** Pleading L1194 as the complete liability/licence statute — courts and authorities will apply the modal code (rail/air/road) first; L1194 mainly supplies system architecture, duties, and residual liability heads (arts. 2, 9–10).

---

## 1. Statutory hierarchy & legal foundation

* Framework + international treaties prevail if conflicting — [[Legea 1194-1997 — transporturi (text)#Articolul 2. Legislaţia cu privire la transporturi]].
* Modal detail: railway, road, air, naval, urban electric, pipeline codes / acts — arts. 15–21; post-1997 codes include [[Codul aerian 301-2017 (notă)]] and [[Codul transportului feroviar 19-2022 (notă)]].
* Passenger road ops detailed in [[HG 854-2006 — transporturi auto calatori (notă)]] (issued under the Road Transport Code 116/1998, not under L1194 alone).
* Contracts / tort overlays — [[Codul civil 1107-2002 (notă)]]; administrative challenges — [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]].
* State administration: Ministry of Infrastructure and Regional Development (+ APL) — art. 3 (name updated by LP156/2022).

## 2. Practical legal mechanics

### System administration (arts. 1–4)
* Transport as economic branch meeting freight/passenger needs — art. 1.
* MIDR / APL ensure needs satisfaction, competition among carriers, defence of carrier and user interests, defence/civil-protection transport, statistics — art. 3.
* APL coordination commissions; no interference in carriers’ economic activity except where law allows — art. 4.

### Undertakings, land, tariffs (arts. 5–8)
* All ownership forms protected equally; **road** activity licences issued under applicable legislation — [[Legea 1194-1997 — transporturi (text)#Articolul 5. Întreprinderile de transport]].
* Transport land regime + protection zones — art. 6.
* Market contracts/orders as economic base — art. 7.
* Tariffs and road-fund charges set by normative acts; foreign-vehicle / transit fees by Government per treaties — art. 8.

### Duties, liability, security (arts. 9–14)
* Carrier duties: timely quality carriage, state defence orders, accident prevention, environment, vehicle/infrastructure standards — art. 9.
* **Liability** for passenger/cargo/baggage non-performance is set by **each mode’s code** and other acts — [[Legea 1194-1997 — transporturi (text)#Articolul 10. Răspunderea întreprinderilor de transport]]. Residual statutory heads: personal injury while using transport; loss/shortage/damage of goods/baggage and damage to roads/rail/nav aids; environmental harm (art. 10(2)).
* Custody of goods from receipt to delivery; dangerous/valuable goods escorted by shipper/consignee; MAI route coordination — art. 11.
* Emergency functioning / intentional blocking of ways — art. 12.
* Safety: staff fitness/medical exams; high-risk zones; dangerous-goods transit needs Government decision — art. 13.
* Labour / insurance of staff, passengers, baggage, goods — art. 14.

### Modal perimeter (arts. 15–21) & control (art. 22)
* Single system = rail, road, air, naval, urban electric, pipeline — art. 15.
* Descriptive definitions of each subsystem — arts. 16–21 (not licensing rules).
* Compliance control by competent central/local authorities — art. 22.
* Entry into force / Government alignment — arts. 23–24.

## 3. Risk matrix

| Provision | Flaw / ambiguity | Practical risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Art. 2 + modal codes** | Framework vs specialis conflict | Wrong liability cap / forum | Identify modal code first (air/rail/road) |
| **Art. 5(3)** | Licence “per legislation” only | Operating without mode licence | Road: licence + [[HG 854-2006 — transporturi auto calatori (notă)]] route permit |
| **Art. 10** | Liability deferred to codes | Uninsured exposure if only L1194 read | Match insurance to modal code + art. 14 |
| **Art. 11 / 13** | Dangerous-goods special regime | Criminal/admin liability | MAI-coordinated routes; Government transit decisions |
| **Arts. 16–21** | Descriptive lists look like operative rules | False completeness | Use for taxonomy only |

## 4. Client checklist

- [ ] Select governing **modal code** before drafting carriage contracts.
- [ ] Confirm transport licence category under special legislation (art. 5(3)).
- [ ] For scheduled/occasional passenger road → [[HG 854-2006 — transporturi auto calatori (notă)]].
- [ ] For rail access/operators → [[Codul transportului feroviar 19-2022 (notă)]].
- [ ] For AOC / aircraft registry → [[Codul aerian 301-2017 (notă)]].
- [ ] Map liability & insurance to art. 10 + modal rules + art. 14.
- [ ] Dangerous goods: escort, MAI route, Government transit if required (arts. 11, 13).

# Legea nr. 125/2024 — omnibus (IT Park, ANRE suspension, customs) — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 125-2024 — modificare parc IT si tranzitii vamale (text)]] ✅ · **Primary IT Park parent:** [[Legea 77-2016 — parcuri tehnologia informatiei (notă)]] · **Customs:** [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (notă)]] · **Admin procedure:** [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]] · **Energy bridge:** [[MOC — Energetică]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Omnibus organic law (IF mostly **31.05.2024**). For this vault the high-value pieces are (i) expanding [[Legea 77-2016 — parcuri tehnologia informatiei (text)|L77/2016]] art. 8 eligible activities, and (ii) carving out more **ANRE decisions from court suspension** under Codul administrativ art. 214.
> **Primary business risk:** Missing the energy-procedure bite — licence-withdrawal / continuity ANRE acts in gas (and certain electricity/gas crisis powers) remain non-suspendable.

---

## 1. Foundation

* Law 30.05.2024; MO 236–237 art. 338 / 31.05.2024.
* IF on publication, **except** Art. IV pts. 3–4 (in the part completing art. 427(10)) IF **1 July 2024** (Art. VI).

## 2. What each article does

| Art. | Target | Substance |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | L77/2016 art. 8 | CSPM rev. **2 → 2.1**; rewrites lit. **q)** export-only contact centres (82.20) incl. freight logistics customer relations; lit. **r)** export-only listed HR-supply services (78.30.11–.12 + freight logistics/customer-relations staff) |
| **II** | Codul administrativ art. **214** | Rewrites (9): ANRE decisions **not suspendable** — (a) gas licence withdrawal for unbundling/independence/certification failures + gas-transport continuity decisions; (b) L107 art. 81¹(1) electricity acts; (c) L108 art. 108¹(5) gas acts. Abrogates former (10) |
| **III** | L151/2018 | Special onerous transfer of Chișinău immovable to **USA** for embassy construction (negotiable price ≥ cadastral tax value) |
| **IV** | Codul vamal 95/2021 | Customs-activity definition; restore preferential tariff treatment after international admin cooperation; fixed-installation customs declarations timing; confiscated-goods handoff to SFS until 01.01.2025; extended transitional rules for old customs regimes / authorisations (active processing guarantees, validity to 31.12.2027 in places); declaration invalidation/rectification rules across the 31.12.2023 cut |
| **V** | L407/2023 (alcohol) | Label/packaging sell-through to **31.12.2025**; product sale until stock exhaustion but ≤ **31.08.2027**; abrogates prior (3) |
| **VI** | IF rules | See above |

> [!warning] Energy enabling citations in Art. II
> Suspension carve-outs still cite **L107/2016** art. 81¹ and **L108** art. 108¹. L107 is abrogated by [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)|L164/2025]] — remap the electricity limb to the L164 successor provision before arguing ultra vires / wrong article in 2026 litigation.

## 3. Risk matrix

| Issue | Risk | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| IT Park onboarding on old art. 8 | Wrong eligibility for contact-centre / HR-export models | Use post-LP125 art. 8 + [[Concept — Parc IT]] |
| Seeking suspension of carved-out ANRE acts | Motion rejected; continuity decisions stick | Check art. 214(9) list before filing |
| Customs transitional authorisations | Wrong guarantee / validity assumption | Read Art. IV against operative Codul vamal consolidation |
| Embassy land article | Irrelevant to most files | Ignore unless USG real-estate mandate |

## 4. Client checklist

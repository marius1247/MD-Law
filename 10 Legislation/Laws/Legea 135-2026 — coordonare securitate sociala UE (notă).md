# Legea nr. 135/2026 — EU social-security coordination — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 135-2026 — coordonare securitate sociala UE (text)]] ✅ · **Target:** [[Legea 489-1999 — sistemul public de asigurari sociale (text)]] · **IF:** **EU accession treaty** (Art. III)

> [!warning] Not yet in force
> Art. III(1): enters into force only when Moldova’s **EU accession treaty** enters into force. Government must build institutional/technical EESSI capacity beforehand (Art. III(2)).

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Creates the domestic legal shell for applying Reg. (EC) 883/2004 and 987/2009 — CNAS as liaison body (except medical insurance) and EESSI as the exchange channel; aligns data-interoperability law for EU/EEA/CH/UK cross-border exchange.
> **Primary business risk:** Advising free movement / posted-worker social-security coordination as if live today — it is **accession-gated**. Useful now for readiness projects (EESSI, CNAS roles).

## 1. Statutory hierarchy & legal foundation

* **EU:** Reg. (EC) 883/2004 (as last amended by Reg. (EU) 2019/1149); Reg. (EC) 987/2009 (as last amended by Reg. (EU) 2017/492).
* **Publication:** MO 330-333/23.07.2026, art. 337.
* **Amends:** L489/1999 (Art. I); Law 142/2018 on data exchange & interoperability (Art. II).

---

## 2. Practical legal mechanics

### Art. I — L489/1999

* New [[Legea 489-1999 — sistemul public de asigurari sociale (text)#Articolul 9¹. Coordonarea sistemelor de securitate|art. 9¹]]: **Casa Națională** = liaison body for Reg. 883/2004 art. 3(1) branches **except medical insurance**; information exchange with EU/EEA/CH/UK competent institutions via **EESSI**; Government sets institutional responsibilities.
* Art. 49(1): CNAS implements international social-security agreements (lit. m) and applies Regs. 883/2004 & 987/2009 as competent institution within legal powers (lit. m¹).

### Art. II — Law 142/2018

* Cross-border data exchange: (a) with EU/EEA/CH/UK via the national interoperability platform holder as technical access point (unless special law says otherwise); (b) other states via international treaties.

### Art. III — IF & readiness

* IF = accession treaty IF.
* Pre-IF duty: Government ensures institutional/technical framework for EU coordination rules + EESSI.

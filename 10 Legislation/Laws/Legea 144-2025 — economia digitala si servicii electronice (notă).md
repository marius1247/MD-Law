# Legea nr. 144/2025 — digital economy & electronic services package — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 144-2025 — economia digitala si servicii electronice (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Drept comercial]] · **Touches:** [[Legea 845-1992 — antreprenoriat si intreprinderi (notă)]] · [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (notă)]] · [[Legea 308-2017 — prevenirea spalarii banilor (notă)]] · [[Legea 202-2017 — activitatea bancilor (notă)]] · [[Legea 114-2012 — servicii de plata (notă)]] · [[Legea 62-2008 — reglementarea valutara (notă)]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Organic omnibus (Arts. I–XVIII) to support digital economy: merchant cash-out for payment-service users, **digital-nomad** residence, online company registration, e-receipts, crowdfunding platform duties, bank “adjacent services”, and a staged opening of **remote AML identification** for MD/EU citizens via qualified certificates / HG 977/2023 trust providers.
> **Primary business risk:** Treating Art. XII remote CDD as live for all clients — it waits on technical conditions (Art. XVIII(1)+(4)); merchants exceeding the **MDL 1,000 / txn** and **MDL 100,000 / month** cash-out caps under Art. I create payment-law and L845 exposure.

---

## 1. Statutory hierarchy & legal foundation

* **Publication:** MO 328/20.06.2025, art. 374.
* **Default IF (Art. XVIII(1)):** **3 months after publication** ≈ **20 September 2025**.
* **Exceptions:**
  * **Art. XV** (crowdfunding L181/2023) — IF on publication.
  * **Art. XII** (AML remote ID rewrite of L308 art. 5¹) — IF when technical conditions in Art. XVIII(4) exist (EU trust-service path under HG 977/2023).
* **Compliance clock:** payment / platform providers have **12 months from IF** to align and report to the supervisor (Art. XVIII(2)); Government **3 months** from publication to amend secondary acts (Art. XVIII(3)).

## 2. Practical legal mechanics

### A. Merchant cash-out for payment users (Art. I → L845 art. 6)

Enterprises may release cash to a payment-service user (L114/2012 art. 2(2)(5)) immediately before a goods/services payment, limited to cash in the ECC drawer / dedicated store, **≤ MDL 1,000 per transaction** and **≤ MDL 100,000 cumulative per month**. Already reflected in consolidated [[Legea 845-1992 — antreprenoriat si intreprinderi (text)]].

### B. Digital nomads (Arts. II–III, V, X)

* New definition in foreigners law **L200/2010**: remote work/services for a foreign employer/company, or managing/associating in a foreign legal person, via ICT.
* Temporary residence ground **art. 37²** (income, insurance, accommodation conditions — apply full Art. X text).
* Health / compulsory medical insurance carve-outs extended to “nomazi digitali”.

### C. Online state registration (Art. VII → L220/2007)

* New notions: *înregistrare de stat online*, *decizia registratorului*, *formular-tip*; constitutive-act definition aligned to CC art. 178.
* Practical: ASP digital channel becomes a first-class path — pair with [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (notă)]].

### D. Consumer e-receipts (Art. VI → L105/2003)

Cash receipt may be paper **or** electronic (phone / email / platform) if consumer data provided before payment — Government regulation to detail.

### E. AML remote ID (Art. XII → L308 art. 5¹) — staged

Remote electronic identification/verification allowed for:

* natural persons — MD or **EU** citizens holding a **qualified public-key certificate** from an EU trust service provider;
* resident legal persons whose reps/founders/admins/UBOs are MD/EU citizens or hold such certificates.

IF tied to Art. XVIII(4) technical readiness. Until then, keep prior L308 art. 5¹ practice.

### F. Banks & crowdfunding (Arts. XIII, XV)

* **L202/2017** art. 14(1¹): banks may offer non-core but banking-compatible services to own clients under NBM authorisation (reputation / depositor-protection filter).
* **L181/2023** crowdfunding: developer definition tightened to MD citizens in key roles; provider platform/tech/continuity/backup duties (live from publication).

### G. Other hooks

* Art. IV — repatriation law: non-repatriated debts of a non-resident up to **MDL 100,000** cumulative carve-out.
* Art. VIII — [[Legea 62-2008 — reglementarea valutara (notă)|L62/2008]] FX touch.
* Arts. XVI–XVII — electronic communications / roaming laws.

## 3. Risk matrix

| Provision | Flaw / ambiguity | Practical risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Art. I caps** | Per-txn / monthly cash-out limits | ECC / payment-law breach | Soft-block at POS; monthly ledger |
| **Art. XII / XVIII** | Remote AML ID not yet universally live | Onboarding EU clients on wrong ID method | Gate on Art. XVIII(4) readiness + SPCSB/NBM guidance |
| **Art. X nomad** | Income/insurance evidence standards | Residence refusal / overstay | Checklist against full art. 37² before filing |
| **Art. XIII** | “Prelungire a operațiunilor bancare” open-textured | Unauthorised non-core services | NBM pre-clearance for each product |

## 4. Relevant practice

* Already visible in consolidations of [[Legea 308-2017 — prevenirea spalarii banilor (text)]] and [[Legea 202-2017 — activitatea bancilor (text)]] (MODIFICAT LP144).
* Pair digital-nomad work with immigration counsel; tax residence still turns on [[Concept — Rezident fiscal]] / CF rules — L144 does not create a tax holiday.

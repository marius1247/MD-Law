# Legea nr. 150/2026 — amending crisis-management Law 248/2025 — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 150-2026 — modificare management situatii de criza (text)]] ✅ · **Parent:** Legea 248/2025 *(still not in vault)* · **Implementing GD:** [[HG 346-2026 — Comisia Nationala Management Crize (notă)]] · **Energy neighbours:** [[Legea 101-2026 — consolidarea mecanismelor de interventie in situatii de criza (notă)]] · [[HG 820-2024 — situatii exceptionale electroenergetic (notă)]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Organic amendment of L248/2025 — rewrites core definitions (*criză*, *dezastru*, crisis-management plan), adds **contingency plan**, **essential services**, **refuge** (cultural-property), and adjusts institutional/planning/service duties across multiple articles.
> **Primary business risk:** Operators drafting “crisis / contingency plans” under the old vocabulary; economic operators named in L248 planning duties must align to the new plan/contingency distinction. Full duty map still requires ingesting **L248 itself**.

## 2. Practical legal mechanics (Art. I highlights)

### Definitions (art. 2)

| Term | Practical note |
|---|---|
| **criză** | Scale beyond the lead institution’s resources → needs national inter-institutional coordination |
| **dezastru** | Serious effects on persons, environment, property incl. cultural heritage |
| **plan de management al situațiilor de criză** | Incidents/emergencies/crises — decision, coordination, communication, resource priority |
| **plan de contingență** *(new 14¹)* | Scenario-based measures/responsibilities/procedures/resources to respond or prevent |
| **funcții vitale ale statului** | Constitutional order, national security, life/health, economy/finance, rule of law, social stability |
| **servicii esențiale** *(new)* | Indispensable for vital state functions / vital economic activities / public health & safety / environment |
| **refugiu** *(new)* | Government-designated secure storage for threatened cultural goods (Culture Ministry list) |

### Institutional / planning limbs (arts. 9, 12, 16, 18–19, 23–24, 27–27³, 29, 32¹, 34–35, 41¹…)

Art. I walks numerous L248 articles — planning duties, CNMC/centre competences, service obligations, contingency tools, and operational rules (including service animals / use rules in later limbs). **Apply against full L248 text once ingested**; this note flags the definitional reset and the need to re-map “who must file which plan.”

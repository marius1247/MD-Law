# Legea nr. 1593/2002 — AOAM premiums (size, method, deadlines) — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 1593-2002 — prime asigurare medicala obligatorie (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Fiscalitate & Contabilitate]] · **Payroll pair:** [[Legea 489-1999 — sistemul public de asigurari sociale (notă)|L489/1999 CNAS]] · **Administrator (funds):** [[CNAM]] · **Collection/control (percentage limb):** [[SFS]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Operational statute for **compulsory health-insurance premiums (AOAM)** — who pays, percentage vs fixed sum, payment clocks, reporting, penalties — under the umbrella of Law 1585/1998 (CNAM funds).
> **Primary business risk:** Missing the **25th-of-following-month** payroll transfer/report (arts. 12, 20, 26) or misrouting **IT Park / single-tax** health components → SFS Titlul V liability + daily penalties (art. 30).

## 2. Practical legal mechanics

### A. Scope & definitions (arts. 1–3)

* Art. 1 — purpose: size / method / deadlines for premiums into CNAM funds.
* Art. 2 — personal scope: public bodies, employers, self-employed, liberal professions, employees, specified foreigners.
* Art. 3 — key definitions: *primă AOAM*, *asigurat*, *plătitor*, *unitate*.

### B. Two payment tracks (arts. 4–7 + annexes)

| Track | Who | How | Anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Percentage of salary / other rewards** | Annex nr. 1 payers (employers/employees etc.) | % of gross before tax withholdings | [[Legea 1593-2002 — prime asigurare medicala obligatorie (text)#Articolul 4.|art. 4(1)]], [[Legea 1593-2002 — prime asigurare medicala obligatorie (text)#Articolul 6.|art. 6]] |
| **Fixed sum (individual)** | Annex nr. 2 (farmers, patent holders, liberal professions, residual residents, temporary-stay foreigners, etc.) | Absolute amount under L1585 art. 17(4) + annual funds law | [[Legea 1593-2002 — prime asigurare medicala obligatorie (text)#Articolul 7.|art. 7]], [[Legea 1593-2002 — prime asigurare medicala obligatorie (text)#Articolul 16.|art. 16]], arts. 22–23 |

Headline commercial rate used in vault syntheses: **~9% employee-side AOAM** — always re-verify in the current funds law / consolidation before quoting.

**IT Park carve-out (art. 4(1¹)):** park residents and their employees do **not** pay percentage AOAM on employment income under labour law; cover comes from the **single tax** split (L77). Other income from park residents is taxed in the ordinary way. → [[Legea 77-2016 — parcuri tehnologia informatiei (notă)]] · [[Concept — Parc IT]]

### C. Clocks that bite

| Obligation | Deadline | Anchor |
|---|---|---|
| Transfer percentage premiums | **25th** of month after payment month | arts. 12, 20 |
| Employer IPC-style return (PIT + AOAM + CNAS) to SFS | **25th** of following month | art. 26 |
| Fixed-sum annual pay (persons in annex 2 at 1 Jan) | by **31 March** | art. 22 |
| Mid-year entry into annex 2 | **30 calendar days** from inclusion (pro-rata) | art. 23 |
| Late fixed-sum → insured status | from payment date + penalties | art. 23¹ |

### D. Enforcement

* SFS collects percentage / single-tax AOAM limbs like tax debt (art. 17).
* False / missing returns → Fiscal Code Titlul V (art. 29).
* Late payment → daily penalty under art. 30.
* IT Park residents: liability under Fiscal Code Titlul V by derogation (art. 29(4)).
* Prescription for AOAM debts/penalties/overpayments: **3 years** (art. 17(4)), suspended during deferral/instalments.

### E. Foreigners (art. 10)

* Employed foreigners / permanent residents / international-protection beneficiaries → same calculation as citizens unless treaty says otherwise.
* Temporary stay (family reunification, studies, humanitarian/volunteer/religious, digital nomads) → treated like individual (fixed-sum) citizens unless treaty says otherwise.

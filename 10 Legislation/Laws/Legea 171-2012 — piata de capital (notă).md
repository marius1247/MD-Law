# Capital Market Law — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 171-2012 — piata de capital (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Drept comercial]] · **Banks (investment services):** [[Legea 202-2017 — activitatea bancilor (notă)]] · **Central bank / FMI:** [[Legea 548-1995 — Banca Nationala a Moldovei (notă)]] · **Challenge:** [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** MiFID-style framework for financial instruments, public offers/prospectuses, takeovers, investment firms (A/B/C licences), market infrastructure, investor compensation fund, and market-abuse rules. Consolidated text still designates **CNPF** as competent authority (art. 1).
> **Primary business risk:** Investment services without correct licence category (arts. 33–35) or public offer without approved prospectus (arts. 13–19) → nullity / sanctions; insider dealing & manipulation (arts. 127–129) → CNPF enforcement and trading disruption.

---

## 1. Statutory hierarchy & legal foundation

* Civil-law overlays for securities ownership / contracts — [[Codul civil 1107-2002 (notă)]].
* Competent authority in this consolidated text: **Comisia Națională a Pieței Financiare** under Law 192/1998 — [[Legea 171-2012 — piata de capital (text)#Articolul 1. Domeniul de reglementare]].
* **CNPF → NBM transition caveat:** [[Legea 548-1995 — Banca Nationala a Moldovei (notă)]] art. 5(1)(p) moved **insurance/OCN** etc. to NBM; L171’s operative articles still say “Comisia Națională”. Do not rewrite supervisor for investment firms/markets from L548 alone — verify current institutional assignment before filing.
* Banks performing investment services: dual stack L171 + [[Legea 202-2017 — activitatea bancilor (notă)]] (L202 art. 14(2) maps to L171 arts. 4 & 33). Clearing/settlement of money-market/G-bonds outside RM/MTF and NBM-authorised bank systems carved out — art. 2(4).
* Challenges → [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]].

## 2. Practical legal mechanics

### Instruments & scope (arts. 1–4¹)
* Scope: investment firms, UCITS/collective schemes, Investor Compensation Fund, public offers, takeovers, market infrastructure, investor protection, market abuse, disclosure — art. 1.
* Instrument classes — [[Legea 171-2012 — piata de capital (text)#Articolul 4. Categorii de instrumente financiare]]: securities; UCITS units; money-market instruments; options/futures/swaps/forwards and other derivatives; credit derivatives; CFDs; other instruments admitted on RM/MTF. Securities defined art. 4(3) (shares, bonds, convertibles/warrants).
* Hard product bans — art. 4¹: marketing/sale/distribution of **binary options**; further restrictions on certain speculative derivatives to retail (read full art. 4¹ before structuring retail FX/CFD offers).

### Issuers & public offers (arts. 7–32)
* Securities issuance / bond specifics / admission to trading — arts. 7–9.
* Rights registry / registrar / CSD interfaces — arts. 10–11.
* Public offer prospectus: content, CNPF approval, publication, advertising, supplements — arts. 13–19. Offer **without** CNPF-approved prospectus prohibited (art. 16(6) area).
* Takeovers: mandatory/competitive offers, fair price, squeeze-out / sell-out — arts. 20–31.

### Investment firms & conduct (arts. 33+)
* Exclusive list of investment services/activities and ancillary services — [[Legea 171-2012 — piata de capital (text)#Articolul 33. Tipuri de servicii şi activităţi de investiţii]] (reception/transmission, execution, dealing on own account, portfolio management, investment advice, underwriting firm/soft, MTF operation).
* Only licensed investment firms or CNPF-accepted persons may perform them (art. 33(3)); exclusive corporate purpose except commercial banks (art. 33(4)).
* Licence categories — [[Legea 171-2012 — piata de capital (text)#Articolul 35. Licenţa de societate de investiţii]]:
  * **A** — reception/transmission, execution, portfolio mgmt, advice (+ limited ancillary); **no** proprietary trading / firm underwriting.
  * **B** — A-set + soft placement + more ancillary; still no proprietary trading / firm commitment underwriting.
  * **C** — full art. 33 menu.
* Licence indefinite, non-transferable (later chapter rules ~art. 5326 area in text). Organisational, capital, fit-and-proper, client-asset and best-execution duties developed in subsequent sections.

### Market abuse (arts. 127–129)
* Insider information: bans on dealing, tipping, recommending; issuer disclosure duty with limited delay; insider lists — art. 127.
* Market manipulation prohibited — [[Legea 171-2012 — piata de capital (text)#Articolul 128. Manipulările pe piaţă]] (false signals, artificial prices, fictitious trades/orders, false information dissemination).
* Licensed persons must notify CNPF of suspected insider/manipulation trades — art. 129.

### Investor Compensation Fund (arts. 130+)
* Fund constituted and managed by CNPF for retail clients of investment firms (and legacy fiduciary administrators) — art. 130. Contribution and payout mechanics arts. 131–132+.

## 3. Risk matrix

| Provision | Flaw / ambiguity | Practical risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Art. 1 vs L548** | Supervisor naming lag | Filing to wrong authority | Confirm live competent authority before licence/prospectus |
| **Arts. 33–35** | Category A/B/C mismatch | Unlawful dealing / underwriting | Map every product to licence letter |
| **Arts. 13–19** | Prospectus exemptions misread | Void offer + sanctions | CNPF approval before marketing |
| **Art. 4¹** | Binary/CFD retail bans | Immediate breach | Product governance screen |
| **Arts. 127–129** | Delayed disclosure / weak insider lists | Manipulation file | Disclosure calendar + MAR-style wall-crossing |
| **Banks** | L202 art. 14 vs L171 licence | Dual non-compliance | Align bank investment-services permissions with CNPF/NBM rules |

---

## 4. Client checklist

- [ ] Classify instruments under art. 4; screen art. 4¹ bans.
- [ ] Match activity to licence **A / B / C** (or bank dual authorisation).
- [ ] Public offer: CNPF-approved prospectus + publication path (arts. 14–16).
- [ ] Takeover thresholds: mandatory offer / squeeze-out calendar (arts. 21–31).
- [ ] Market-abuse policy, insider lists, suspicious-transaction reporting (arts. 127–129).
- [ ] Confirm Investor Compensation Fund contribution status (arts. 130+).
- [ ] Verify whether filings go to **CNPF** or successor — do not rely on L548 alone for capital-market transfers.

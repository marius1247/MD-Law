# Legea nr. 187/2025 — omnibus (NBM reserves + fiscal/customs package) — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 187-2025 — modificare BNM (text)]] ✅ · **Hubs:** [[MOC — Drept comercial]] · [[MOC — Fiscalitate & Contabilitate]] · **PDF mirror:** legis.md doc_id `149593` · **NBM parent:** [[Legea 548-1995 — Banca Nationala a Moldovei (notă)]] · **Also cited by:** [[HG 829-2025 — modificare acte fiscale vamale audit (notă)]] (enabling chain)

> [!warning] Filename undersells the act
> Vault file still says “modificare BNM”, but LP187 is a **26-article omnibus**: Art. I is NBM reserves; Art. II is a large [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 (notă)|Fiscal Code]] rewrite; Arts. III–XXV hit social insurance, Civil Code, CPC, Enforcement Code, trade, road transport, education, customs, SMEs, gambling, Administrative Code, state border, etc. Always open the `(text)` before advising.

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Multi-speed amending law — NBM reserve-deficit interest formula; broad 2025–27 fiscal/customs/SME/gambling measures; transfer-pricing 2024 transitional relief.
> **Primary business risk:** Applying a 2026 or 2027 limb too early (or too late). **Art. XXVI** stages IF across publication / +30 days / 1 Jan 2026 / **1 Jan 2027**.

## 2. Practical legal mechanics

### A. NBM mandatory reserves (Art. I → L548 art. 17)

* Drops “cash retained in banks’ tills” from the reserve-holding description.
* Deficit interest = **2 ×** weighted average overnight credit-facility rate × deficit × days; collected incontestably from the bank’s NBM account into the NBM budget.

### B. Fiscal Code package (Art. II — bulk)

Headline commercial themes (verify exact pct. in `(text)` against the IF map):

* Benefit-in-kind valuation → **tax unamortised value** (aligns with later [[HG 829-2025 — modificare acte fiscale vamale audit (notă)|HG 829/2025]] payroll tweak).
* Childcare alternative-services payments up to **MDL 2,500 / child / month** non-taxable design (art. 20 area).
* Employer deduction / compensation limbs (rural tourism, public-worker housing/transport, etc.).
* VAT / related pts staged 2025–2026.
* Transfer-pricing / affiliated-person single-tax reporting transitions in Art. XXVI(3)–(4): 2024 TP file on SFS request in **120 days**; 2024 single-tax return for affiliated adjustments due **25 Dec 2025**.

# Banking Activity Law — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 202-2017 — activitatea bancilor (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Drept comercial]] · **Supervisor:** [[Legea 548-1995 — Banca Nationala a Moldovei (notă)|BNM]] · **NBFI contrast:** [[Legea 1-2018 — organizatiile de creditare (notă)]] · **AML:** [[Legea 308-2017 — prevenirea spalarii banilor (notă)]] · **Challenge:** [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** CRD IV / CRR-aligned banking statute: exclusive NBM licensing, deposit monopoly, prudential capital/liquidity/governance, related-party limits, supervisory measures and sanctions (replaces Law 550/1995).
> **Primary business risk:** Operating without licence / deposit-taking by non-banks (arts. 6, 8) → enforcement and licence withdrawal; related-party and capital breaches (arts. 60, 80–81, 139–141) → Pillar 2 add-ons, dividend blocks, fines up to **5% own funds** or **10% prior-year turnover**, and licence withdrawal (art. 22).

---

## 1. Statutory hierarchy & legal foundation

* Sector statute under NBM competence — [[Legea 548-1995 — Banca Nationala a Moldovei (notă)]] (arts. 4–5 L202 cross-cite L548).
* Non-bank credit is a **different** gate: [[Legea 1-2018 — organizatiile de creditare (notă)]] (no deposits).
* AML overlay: [[Legea 308-2017 — prevenirea spalarii banilor (notă)]] — licence withdrawal ground art. 22(1)(k).
* Resolution / early intervention: Law 232/2016 (cited in arts. 4, 22) — separate from day-to-day L202 supervision.
* Challenges to NBM individual acts → [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]] with L202/L548 special rules (art. 144 L202).

## 2. Practical legal mechanics

### Deposit monopoly & name protection (arts. 6–7)
* Only banks may attract deposits / repayable funds from the public — [[Legea 202-2017 — activitatea bancilor (text)#Articolul 6. Interdicția de a desfășura activitatea]].
* Foreign banks need an NBM-licensed **branch** for art. 14(1)(a) activities; representative offices limited to information/liaison.
* Use of “bancă” / derivatives restricted to licensed banks/branches — [[Legea 202-2017 — activitatea bancilor (text)#Articolul 7. Denumirea băncii]].

### Licensing gate (arts. 8–22)
* Exclusive NBM licence before activity — [[Legea 202-2017 — activitatea bancilor (text)#Articolul 8. Licența și competența de acordare]].
* Initial capital ≥ **100 million MDL** (NBM may set higher) — [[Legea 202-2017 — activitatea bancilor (text)#Articolul 9. Capitalul inițial]]; cash (or G-bonds for bridge bank / systemic crisis).
* ≥ **3** natural persons running day-to-day business; fit-and-proper via art. 43 — arts. 11, 43.
* Qualified holders / top-20 shareholders transparency; no close links blocking supervision — [[Legea 202-2017 — activitatea bancilor (text)#Articolul 12. Acționari]].
* Business plan + governance framework (art. 38) — art. 13.
* Permitted activities catalogue — [[Legea 202-2017 — activitatea bancilor (text)#Articolul 14. Activitățile permise băncilor]] (deposits, credit, leasing, payments/e-money under L114, guarantees, investment services overlapping L171 art. 33, etc.). Licence must list activities; art. 15 bans off-licence work and certain treasury-share / affiliate-security lending.
* Two-stage process — [[Legea 202-2017 — activitatea bancilor (text)#Articolul 17. Procesul de licențiere]]: prior approval of incorporation (5 months) → ASP registration → final licence (≤ 2 months). Licence indefinite, non-transferable; fee **50,000 MDL** (art. 20).
* Refusal grounds — [[Legea 202-2017 — activitatea bancilor (text)#Articolul 19. Motivele de respingere a cererii]] (incomplete file, capital, governance, fit-and-proper, close links, premature publicity). NBM does **not** assess economic need for the market (art. 19(2)).
* Withdrawal — [[Legea 202-2017 — activitatea bancilor (text)#Articolul 22. Retragerea licenței]]: inactivity 1 year / cease >6 months; false data; prudential failure; insolvency thresholds (payment incapacity, over-indebtedness, own funds <¼ minimum, capital adequacy <¼); AML; shareholder quality; etc. Forced liquidation path when insolvency + no resolution trigger (art. 22(2)).

### Capital, liquidity, governance (Titlul III)
* Own funds / minimum levels — art. 60; capital buffers art. 63; large exposures art. 74; liquidity art. 76; leverage art. 77; ICAAP arts. 78–79.
* Governance framework — arts. 36–44 (board, executive, specialised committees, remuneration).
* Qualifying holdings approvals — arts. 45–58 (thresholds 1%/5%/10%/20%/33%/50%).

### Related-party & outsourcing (arts. 80–82)
* No preferential terms vs non-affiliates; prior board majority approval; NBM may **presume** affiliation (15 WD rebuttal) — [[Legea 202-2017 — activitatea bancilor (text)#Articolul 80. Tranzacții cu persoanele afiliate băncii]].
* Hard bans on tying credit to affiliate products, financing affiliate debt via third parties, etc. — art. 81.
* Material outsourcing needs **prior NBM approval** (60 WD + 30 WD extension); deposit/credit outsourcing only to licensed banks — art. 82.

### Supervision & enforcement (arts. 99–147)
* Supervisory review / SREP toolkit — arts. 99–106; consolidated supervision arts. 107+.
* Early remedial measures — [[Legea 202-2017 — activitatea bancilor (text)#Articolul 139. Măsuri de supraveghere]]: higher own funds, activity limits, dividend/AT1 coupon blocks, related-party excess deductions, extra reporting/liquidity, IT risk cuts.
* Sanctionable facts — art. 140; sanctions ladder — [[Legea 202-2017 — activitatea bancilor (text)#Articolul 141. Sancțiunile și măsurile sancționatoare]]: warning; bank fine ≤ **5% own funds**; legal-person fine ≤ **10%** prior-year net turnover; management fines 1–100 average salaries; natural-person fines 5,000–100m MDL; withdraw approvals; up to double benefit; temporary ban; licence withdrawal; suspend voting rights. Retracted approvals → **10-year** ban from financial sector (art. 141(4)).
* Prescription **3 years** (art. 142(3)). Challenge: art. 144 + L548 arts. 11/11¹; for insolvency withdrawal, standing limited to ≥25% voting shareholders / ≥¼ depositors / ≥¼ other creditors (art. 144(2)). CA arts. 144(6)/146(3) do **not** apply to banking licence withdrawal (art. 143(5)).

## 3. Risk matrix

| Provision | Flaw / ambiguity | Practical risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Arts. 6, 8** | Deposit-like products labelled “investment” | Unlawful banking / criminal referral | Map funding stack; OCN path if non-deposit ([[Legea 1-2018 — organizatiile de creditare (notă)]]) |
| **Art. 17 dual stage** | Prior approval expires if final pack late (5 months) | Restart full application | Diary art. 17(7)–(8) deadlines before ASP/ops spend |
| **Arts. 12, 45–52** | UBO / concert party opacity | Licence refusal or voting freeze | Pre-clear holders; 1–50% threshold matrix |
| **Arts. 80–81** | NBM affiliation presumption | Retroactive breach + reserves | Related-party register; arm’s-length file; board minutes |
| **Arts. 60, 63, 139** | Pillar 2 / buffer stacking | Dividend block / capital raise | Living ICAAP; stress vs art. 139(3)(a)/(k) |
| **Art. 22(1)(k) / L308** | AML programme gaps | Licence withdrawal | Align with [[Legea 308-2017 — prevenirea spalarii banilor (notă)]] |
| **Art. 144** | Narrow standing on insolvency withdrawal | Contestation barred | Coordinate shareholder/depositor thresholds early |

## 4. Client checklist

- [ ] Confirm activity needs **bank** licence (deposits) vs OCN Register (L1/2018).
- [ ] Capital ≥ 100m MDL cash; ≥3 day-to-day managers fit-and-proper (arts. 9, 11, 43).
- [ ] Complete art. 17 prior approval → ASP → final licence **before** first deposit.
- [ ] Licence schedule matches art. 14 activities; separate L114 / L171 authorisations where required.
- [ ] Related-party policy + board pre-approvals (arts. 80–81); outsourcing pre-clearance (art. 82).
- [ ] ICAAP, liquidity, large-exposure and buffer calendar (arts. 60–77).
- [ ] AML programme under L308; ownership transparency continuous file.
- [ ] Challenge path: L548 + [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]] / art. 144 standing rules.

# Legea nr. 227/2025 — permissive-acts optimisation (energy slices) — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 227-2025 — optimizarea actelor permisive (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · [[MOC — Drept comercial]] · **Authorisation:** [[Legea 160-2011 — reglementarea prin autorizare (notă)|L160/2011]] · [[Concept — Autorizarea activității de întreprinzător]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Large organic omnibus converting many permits into **notifications / interoperability checks**, cutting paper evidence where MConnect can pull registry data. Energy-relevant: diesel-import authorisation abolished (L461); CHP/thermal and gas **licence evidence** rewritten around declarations + interoperability; RES biocomponent production moves to **ANRE notification**; L160 portal / tacit-approval rules updated.
> **Primary business risk:** Using pre-30.12.2025 licence checklists for petroleum / thermal / gas / biofuel filings — consolidated parents already reflect LP227 (IF **30.12.2025**, with Art. XLII exceptions as patched by LP317/2025).

> [!tip] Energy focus of this note
> Full act amends ~40 instruments (health, wildlife, fiscal, advertising, urbanism…). Below: **only energy / adjacent slices**. Non-energy articles stay in the `(text)` file.

## 1. Energy amendments map

| Art. | Parent | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| **VI** | [[Legea 461-2001 — piata produselor petroliere (notă)\|L461/2001]] | Deletes **autorizație de import al motorinei** throughout; licence fee → L160 annex 2; arts. **19–21 abrogated**; licence file = L160 docs + ANRE affidavit (registration via interoperability + financial capacity + technical/staff evidence) |
| **XXIV** | [[Legea 92-2014 — energia termica si cogenerarea (notă)\|L92/2014]] art. 20(2)–(3) | Licence conditions rewritten: registry check via interoperability; ownership/use of CHP/thermal/RES plant or heat networks; financial affidavit; qualified staff; administrator residency / degree / criminal-record affidavit (MAI check via interoperability) |
| **XXVIII** | [[Legea 10-2016 — surse regenerabile (notă)\|L10/2016]] | New art. 20(2¹) — competent authority uses interoperability, no redundant docs; art. 25(3) — biofuel / blend **production notified to ANRE** (registry only, not a start-condition) |
| **XXX** | [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)\|L108/2016]] art. 14 | Licence registration prongs (2)/(2¹)/(3) lit. a) → interoperability check, no paper extract |
| **XX** | [[Legea 160-2011 — reglementarea prin autorizare (notă)\|L160]] | Broader authorisation reform (portal, notifications, tacit approval) — applies across energy permits that remain under L160 |

**IF:** Art. XLII — default **30.12.2025** (LP317 tweak); transitional preservation of converted permits until expiry; some L160 portal rules phased.

## 2. Risk matrix (energy practice)

| Provision | Flaw / ambiguity | Practical risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **L461 diesel auth repealed** | Legacy contracts may still require “autorizație motorină” | Over-documenting / wrong refusal | Update import/licence checklists to post-LP227 L461 |
| **Affidavit + interoperability** | ANRE still may ask residual paper | Incomplete file / delay | Confirm live ANRE declaration models |
| **Biofuel = notification** | “Notification ≠ licence” misread as free-for-all | Compliance gap vs petroleum buyers | Track ANRE biofuel registries (art. 14 L10) |

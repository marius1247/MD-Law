# Legea nr. 234/2022 — Energocom gas-security project — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 234-2022 — Energocom securitate furnizare gaze (text)]] ✅ · **Amended by:** [[Legea 20-2024 — modificare Legea 234-2022 Energocom (notă)|20/2024]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Gas law:** [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)|L108/2016]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Special organic law for the **„Securitatea furnizării gazelor naturale”** EBRD loan/grant project — (i) releases **S.A. Energocom** from state-guarantee obligation on the recredit line, and (ii) grants broad **VAT/customs/excise/environmental-fee exemptions** on imports and domestic supplies for project goods/services.
> **Primary business risk:** Assuming normal fiscal treatment on Energocom project procurement; counterparties must confirm goods/services fall within the named EBRD agreements and Government implementing rules (art. 2 last sentence).

## 1. Statutory hierarchy

* **Project-specific derogation law** — not a general gas-market act.
* **Art. 1:** Exception to [[Legea 419-2006 — datoria sectorului public (text)|L419/2006]] art. 29(4) on state guarantees for Energocom's EBRD recredit.
* **Art. 2:** Exceptions to Cod fiscal, Cod vamal (1149/2000 and 95/2021), customs tariff law, combined nomenclature, pollution-payment law — exemptions on import and on supplies **to** Energocom for the project. **Modality fixed by Government.**
* **Art. 3:** Immediate IF on publication (derogation from L100/2017 art. 56(2) 90-day rule).
* **Consolidated references:** Updated by [[Legea 20-2024 — modificare Legea 234-2022 Energocom (notă)|LP20/2024]] to include Amendment Agreement No. 1 (13 Oct 2023) and Grant Agreement (13 Oct 2023).

## 2. Client checklist

- [ ] Confirm transaction is covered by the listed EBRD loan/modification/grant agreements.
- [ ] Obtain Government rules on applying fiscal/customs exemptions (art. 2).
- [ ] Do not treat as precedent for non-Energocom gas infrastructure — scope is project-specific.

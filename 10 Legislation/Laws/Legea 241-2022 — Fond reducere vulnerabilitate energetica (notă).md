# Legea nr. 241/2022 — Fond de reducere a vulnerabilității energetice — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 241-2022 — Fond reducere vulnerabilitate energetica (text)]] ✅ · **Amended by:** [[Legea 255-2024 — modificare Legea 241-2022 Fond vulnerabilitate energetica (notă)|255/2024]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Creates the **Energy Vulnerability Reduction Fund** — legal framework for compensations, financial aid programmes, and the „Vulnerabilitatea energetică” information system for households facing energy poverty.
> **Primary business risk:** Advising on compensation eligibility without consolidating **L241 + LP255/2024** and checking current Government implementing acts.

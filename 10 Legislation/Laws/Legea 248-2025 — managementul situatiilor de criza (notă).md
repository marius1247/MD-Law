# Legea nr. 248/2025 — crisis management — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 248-2025 — managementul situatiilor de criza (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · [[MOC — Achiziții publice & Statul]] · **Implementing:** [[HG 346-2026 — Comisia Nationala Management Crize (notă)|HG 346/2026]] (+ [[HG 379-2026 — modificare HG 346-2026 crize (notă)|HG 379/2026]]) · **Amending:** [[Legea 150-2026 — modificare management situatii de criza (notă)|L150/2026]] · **Sector patches:** [[Legea 101-2026 — consolidarea mecanismelor de interventie in situatii de criza (notă)|L101/2026]] · [[Legea 461-2001 — piata produselor petroliere (notă)|L461]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Organic framework for **national crisis management** — prevention/preparedness, response, recovery; **alert** and **emergency** states with restriction catalogues; **Comisia Națională de Management al Crizelor (CNMC)** and the National Crisis Management Centre; duties of public bodies and **economic operators**.
> **Primary business risk:** Ignoring operator duties (art. 13) and restriction catalogues during alert/emergency — especially where sector laws (petroleum art. 6(7), electricity, procurement) expressly hook into L248 states.

> [!info] Closes a named parent gap
> CNMC HGs and L101/L150 pointed at **L248/2025** as the missing parent — now ingested from legis.md PDF (`155526`).

## 1. Statutory hierarchy

* Organic law; MO 437-440/19.08.2025, art. 600; MODIFICAT LP150/24.07.2026.
* Partial alignment to EU civil-protection Decision **1313/2013/EU**.
* Does **not** apply to crises that may lead to siege/war (art. 1(2)).
* Institutional flesh: [[HG 346-2026 — Comisia Nationala Management Crize (notă)|HG 346/2026]].

## 3. Practical legal mechanics

### A. Escalation ladder (art. 2)
**Incident → urgență → situație de criză** (plus major crisis notions in the definitions block). Sector laws may define domain-specific crises (e.g. petroleum) that sit under this ladder.

### B. Alert vs emergency
* **Alert (art. 23):** declared by Government up to **30 days**, renewable; longer extension needs Parliament within 3 days (as amended by L150).
* **Emergency (art. 29 ff.):** heavier restriction catalogue (art. 32 area) and CNMC leadership (arts. 34–38).
* Restriction proportionality / human-rights floor — arts. 25 / 32 area.

### C. Economic operators (art. 13)
Operators must cooperate on preparedness, information, and response measures — the hook that makes L248 relevant to energy, logistics and critical-supply clients even when they are private.

### D. Institutional stack
CNMC (arts. 34–38) · regional/territorial & local commissions · **Centrul Național de Management al Crizelor** (arts. 39–46) · National Response Platform (art. 44).

## 4. Risk matrix

| Provision | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Art. 13 operator duties | Non-cooperation findings | Maintain crisis contact / stock / reporting file |
| Alert/emergency IF | Sudden price / movement / supply constraints | Map sector hooks (L461 art. 6(7), L164, L131) |
| Restriction catalogues | Over-broad commercial freeze | Challenge proportionality; track CNMC acts |
| Interaction with HG 346 | Confusing who decides what | Read L248 + HG 346 together |

# Legea nr. 271/2017 — audit of financial statements — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 271-2017 — auditul situatiilor financiare (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Fiscalitate & Contabilitate]] · **Who-must-be-audited (size):** [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (notă)|L287 art. 32]] + [[Legea 86-2026 — modificare Legea contabilitatii (notă)|L86/2026]] · **Synthesis:** [[Contabilitate & raportare financiară — synthesis]] · **Concept:** [[Concept — Audit statutar]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Organic statute for the **statutory audit profession** — qualification, registers, ethics/independence, audit performance & reporting, public oversight (**Consiliul de supraveghere publică a auditului**), PIE audit committees, and liability. Transposes the EU Audit Directive / PIE Regulation model in part.
> **Primary business risk:** Treating L287 size-driven audit as the whole story — **PIEs, large entities and sector rules** add L271 obligations (insurance, transparency report, audit committee, tighter quality control). From **2027**, L86 may drop many entities out of L287 art. 32 audit; L271 still governs whoever remains audited.

> [!info] Completes a named horizon gap
> [[Fiscalitate & Contabilitate — horizon and gaps]] listed “Statutory audit law” as **P2** — this ingest closes the primary act. Secondary CSPA regulations and professional-body delegations remain open.

## 1. Statutory hierarchy & legal foundation

* **Type:** organic law; MO 7-17/12.01.2018, art. 48.
* **EU:** partial Dir. 2006/43/EC (as amended through Dir. (EU) 2023/2864); partial Reg. (EU) 537/2014 (as amended through Reg. (EU) 2023/2869); Rec. 2008/473/EC on auditor liability.
* **Last header MODIFICAT in dump:** LP327/29.12.2025 (IF 31.12.2025).
* **Interaction with accounting law:** L287 decides *whether* an entity’s statements must be audited; **L271** decides *who may audit*, *how*, and *under what oversight*.
* **Oversight body:** Consiliul de supraveghere publică a auditului (successor to the former Consiliul de supraveghere a activității de audit — art. 50 transitional).

## 3. Practical legal mechanics

### A. Who may audit

* **Auditor** — natural person with qualification certificate, registered (art. 2, arts. 6–7).
* **Audit entity** — civil-law entity registered under arts. 8–10.
* **Internship** ≥ 2 years under an auditor (art. 3); exam before the Certification Commission (arts. 4–5).
* Registers are public (auditor register art. 7; entity register art. 10).

### B. Independence, quality, insurance (Ch. IV)

* Ethics Code (IESBA) accepted for MD (art. 20); independence guarantees (art. 21); confidentiality (art. 22).
* Quality management standards (art. 24); audit file retention (art. 25).
* **Audit risk insurance** — stricter for **PIE** audits than for non-PIE (art. 26(3)–(4)).

### C. PIE / large / medium overlays (Ch. V–IX)

* PIE and **large** entity audits: additional performance conditions (art. 27(5) area).
* Auditor’s report content scaled for PIE / large / medium (art. 28).
* PIE auditors must file/publish a **transparency report** (art. 29).
* **PIEs must constitute an audit committee** (art. 44) with independence from executive/supervisory bodies; committee monitors internal control, auditor independence, and receives the **additional report** (arts. 45–46).

### D. Oversight & discipline (Ch. VIII)

* Council: legal status, functions, organisational structure, funding (arts. 37–39).
* External quality control: every audit entity at least once every **6 years**; tighter cycle for PIE auditors (art. 41 area).
* Disciplinary measures and appeal/publication (arts. 42–43).
* Council may delegate some external QC to professional organisations (art. 41 area).

### E. Liability (Ch. X)

* Auditor / audit entity liability (art. 47); audited entity duties/liability (art. 48) — including for withholding documents/explanations.

## 4. Risk matrix

| Provision | Flaw / ambiguity | Practical risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **L287 art. 32 × L271** | Size drop under L86 vs sector/PIE mandates | Wrongly cancelling audit engagement for 2027 | Model L86 **and** sector lists before terminating auditor |
| **Art. 21 independence** | Long cool-down / financial-interest rules | Engagement invalid / QC finding | Independence checklist before acceptance |
| **Arts. 44–46 PIE committee** | Committee missing or conflicted | Governance defect + regulator exposure | Constitute committee before PIE audit cycle |
| **Art. 26 insurance** | PIE vs non-PIE cover levels | Uninsured claim | Match policy to engagement mix |

## 5. Client action checklist

- [ ] Map entity: micro/small/medium/large/PIE under L287 (+ L86 from 2027).
- [ ] If audit required: engage only a registered auditor/entity; obtain independence confirmation.
- [ ] PIE: verify audit committee, transparency-report expectation, insurance level.
- [ ] Diary external QC cycle and certificate/registration status of the firm.
- [ ] Keep complete audit-file access for the statutory retention period.

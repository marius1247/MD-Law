# Legea nr. 287/2017 contabilității și raportării financiare — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)]] ✅ *complete* · **Hub:** [[MOC — Fiscalitate & Contabilitate]] · **Synthesis:** [[Contabilitate & raportare financiară — synthesis]] · **Horizon:** [[Fiscalitate & Contabilitate — horizon and gaps]] · **Tax bridge:** [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 (notă)]]

> [!summary] Accounting law is the legal base under tax and company law
> This act decides what financial information exists, who signs it, what standard applies, when statements are filed, who is audited and which groups consolidate. Those outputs feed [[Concept — Impozit pe venit]], profit distributions, net-assets tests, insolvency analysis and lender due diligence.

> [!important] 2027 category reset
> The text is already consolidated with [[Legea 86-2026 — modificare Legea contabilitatii (text)]], in force **1 January 2027**. The new micro/small/medium thresholds are materially higher. Many entities will drop a reporting category. See [[Legea 86-2026 — modificare Legea contabilitatii (notă)]].

## 1. EU model and scope

The preamble states partial transposition of Directive 2013/34/EU on annual financial statements, consolidated statements and related reports, as later amended by Delegated Directive (EU) 2023/2775 on size criteria. This is why the statute uses the EU pattern: categories by size, proportional reporting and special treatment for public-interest entities.

[[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 1. Obiectul de reglementare|Art. 1]] establishes the basic normative framework, principles, general requirements and regulatory mechanism for accounting and financial reporting in Moldova.

[[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 2. Domeniul de aplicare|Art. 2]] applies to Moldovan-registered entities, public institutions where relevant, non-commercial organisations, permanent establishments / representations of non-residents and professionals indicated by the statute. Always check the specific entity type before applying company-style rules.

## 2. Definitions that drive the regime

[[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 3. Noțiuni de bază|Art. 3]] is the central glossary. The working concepts:

| Concept | Function |
|---|---|
| **contabilitate în partidă dublă** | Double-entry bookkeeping; the ordinary business standard. |
| **contabilitate în partidă simplă** | Cash-basis/simple records for specified small physical-person activities. |
| **document primar** | Evidential basis for economic facts. |
| **Depozitarul public al situațiilor financiare** | The state collection, storage and publication system for financial statements. |
| **entitate de interes public** | Listed entities, banks, insurers, UCITS with legal personality, and large state-controlled entities. |
| **situații financiare** | Reports on financial position, performance and other activity information. → [[Concept — Situații financiare]] |
| **IFRS** | International Financial Reporting Standards accepted for Moldova. |
| **SNC** | National Accounting Standards approved by the Ministry of Finance. |

Public-interest status overrides size proportionality. A public-interest entity reports under IFRS and is audited even if its balance sheet or revenue would otherwise place it lower.

## 3. Entity and group categories

[[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 4. Categoriile entităților și grupurilor|Art. 4]] classifies entities and groups by three criteria: total assets, sales revenue and average employees. An entity fits a category when, at reporting date, it does not exceed two of the three criteria, with a two-period movement rule in [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 4. Categoriile entităților și grupurilor|art. 4(8)]].

From 1 January 2027, the consolidated text shows:

| Category | Total assets | Sales revenue | Employees |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Micro | MDL 8,500,000 | MDL 17,000,000 | 10 |
| Small | MDL 95,000,000 | MDL 190,000,000 | 50 |
| Medium | MDL 480,000,000 | MDL 960,000,000 | 250 |
| Large | exceeds two medium criteria | exceeds two medium criteria | >250 |

Group thresholds are also reset: small group thresholds are MDL 115,000,000 assets / MDL 230,000,000 revenue / 50 employees; medium group thresholds are MDL 575,000,000 assets / MDL 1,150,000,000 revenue / 250 employees.

The category criteria apply for accounting and financial-reporting purposes and do not apply to public-interest entities. For threshold transition, see [[Legea 86-2026 — modificare Legea contabilitatii (notă)]].

## 4. Standards and bookkeeping by tier

[[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 5. Modul de ținere a contabilității|Art. 5]] determines the bookkeeping/reporting set:

- micro-entities and certain physical-person entrepreneurs after VAT registration keep double-entry accounts and prepare abridged statements under SNC;
- small entities keep double-entry accounts and prepare simplified statements under SNC;
- medium and large entities keep double-entry accounts and prepare complete statements under SNC;
- public-interest entities keep double-entry accounts and prepare statements under IFRS;
- specified pre-VAT physical-person entrepreneurs and professionals may keep simple cash-basis records without preparing financial statements.

Entities may voluntarily move upward: a simple-accounting entity may use double-entry, abridged statements may be replaced by simplified or complete, and SNC entities may elect IFRS where allowed. Moving between statement sets applies from the next reporting period.

## 5. Accounting principles

[[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 6. Principii generale|Art. 6]] is the legal statement of accounting principles:

- going concern;
- accrual accounting;
- consistency of methods;
- prudence;
- opening balance continuity;
- separation of entity assets and liabilities from owners and others;
- no offsetting except where allowed;
- substance over form;
- measurement at entry cost unless standards provide otherwise;
- materiality.

"Substance over form" is legally significant. A transaction that is formally documented one way may be accounted for according to economic substance, which then affects tax, distributable profit and insolvency analysis.

[[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 7. Limba şi moneda în care|Art. 7]] requires Romanian language and national currency. Foreign-currency transactions are recorded in national and foreign currency according to standards.

## 6. Regulators and standards

[[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 8. Organele de reglementare în domeniul|Art. 8]] assigns the Ministry of Finance the central role: accepting and publishing IFRS, approving SNC, methodological guidance, the general chart of accounts, primary-document forms and instructions.

The National Bureau of Statistics manages the public depository and the submission/publication infrastructure. Sector regulators may adopt accounting regulations for specific fields, but only in line with accounting standards and with Ministry of Finance approval.

The practical map is:

1. L287 sets the legal hierarchy and duties;
2. SNC / IFRS supplies measurement and presentation;
3. Ministry orders and instructions supply forms and methods;
4. sector regulators add specific rules where authorised.

## 7. The accounting cycle and primary documents

[[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 10. Ciclul contabil|Art. 10]] defines the accounting cycle: document economic facts, recognise and measure elements, reflect them in accounts, prepare registers, inventory, and prepare financial statements.

[[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 11. Documentele primare|Art. 11]] is one of the most important provisions for tax disputes. Economic facts are recorded based on primary documents; holding assets, recording sources and economic facts without documentation and accounting reflection is prohibited.

Primary documents must generally be prepared during the transaction or immediately after, and paper and electronic primary documents have the same legal force. Mandatory elements include document name/number, date, issuer, recipient, content of economic facts, quantitative and value units, and responsible persons.

Electronic primary documents are easier after amendments: where non-special-regime primary documents are electronic, signature is not mandatory if internal procedures identify the persons initiating, ordering or approving the economic fact.

[[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 12. Documente primare cu regim special|Art. 12]] keeps special-regime primary documents for asset transfers, services, asset transport without transfer and leasing/rent/usufruct situations. International commercial documents may serve as primary documents for international operations.

## 8. Registers, inventory and retention

[[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 15. Registrele contabile|Art. 15]] requires accounting registers, on paper or electronically. [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 16. Inventarierea|Art. 16]] requires general inventory of assets, equity and liabilities under Ministry of Finance rules.

[[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 17. Păstrarea documentelor contabile|Art. 17]] is a litigation and audit provision. Accounting documents include primary documents, registers, financial statements and other accounting-organisation documents. They may be kept on paper or electronically, must be protected against unauthorised changes, and if lost, stolen or destroyed must be restored within 3 months.

In practice, failed tax deductions and VAT deductions often turn on [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 11. Documentele primare|art. 11]] and [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 17. Păstrarea documentelor contabile|art. 17]], not on the substantive commercial transaction.

## 9. Management responsibility

[[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 18. Obligațiile și drepturile entităţii|Art. 18]] places responsibility for accounting and financial reporting on the manager/administrator/director/founder/professional indicated by the statute.

The responsible person must ensure continuous accounting from registration to liquidation, accounting policies, working chart of accounts, internal document-flow rules, completeness and integrity of documents, internal control, documentation of economic facts and preparation/submission/publication of financial statements.

Delegating bookkeeping to a chief accountant or external accounting firm does not transfer statutory responsibility. Management may appoint or contract accounting staff, but [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 18. Obligațiile și drepturile entităţii|art. 18(2)-(3)]] keeps the legal duty with management.

The article also connects accounts to corporate governance: after annual statements are approved, the entity records profit distribution under the decision of the general meeting or authorised body. That links accounting directly to dividends and net-asset analysis.

## 10. Financial statements

[[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 20. Dispoziții generale|Art. 20]] requires statements to give a true and fair view of financial position, performance and other activity information. It lists qualitative characteristics: relevance and faithful representation, enhanced by comparability, verifiability, timeliness and understandability.

The true-and-fair override in [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 20. Dispoziții generale|art. 20(7)]] allows derogation from a legal or standard provision only in exceptional cases where needed for a true and fair view, with reasons and effects disclosed in the notes. Abridged-statement entities cannot use that override.

[[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 21. Conținutul situațiilor financiare individuale|Art. 21]] sets the statement sets:

- abridged: abridged balance sheet, abridged profit and loss, explanatory note;
- simplified: balance sheet, profit and loss, explanatory note;
- complete: balance sheet, profit and loss, statement of changes in equity, cash-flow statement, explanatory note;
- IFRS entities prepare according to IFRS.

## 11. Notes and management report

[[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 22. Nota explicativă la situațiile financiare|Art. 22]] is detailed. It covers accounting policies, revaluations, fair values, contingent liabilities, loans to management, extraordinary income/expense, long-term debts, related-party and affiliate information, events after reporting date, proposed profit distribution and other disclosures.

[[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 23. Raportul conducerii|Art. 23]] requires medium, large and public-interest entities to file a management report. It must present the entity's position, development and performance and include financial and non-financial performance indicators, prospects, R&D, own shares, branches, risks, environment and employee matters.

Public-interest entities meeting large/employee thresholds include a non-financial statement. Large extractive/forestry entities report payments to the state. Very large groups/entities may have income-tax reporting chapters. This creates a bridge between accounting transparency, tax and ESG-like disclosures.

## 12. Consolidation

[[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 27. Obligația întocmirii situațiilor financiare|Art. 27]] requires parent entities to prepare consolidated financial statements and consolidated management reports in addition to individual statements.

The key exemption: small and medium groups are exempt from consolidated statements and consolidated management reports unless one affiliated entity is a public-interest entity. Group classification therefore affects real compliance costs.

[[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 28. Conținutul situațiilor financiare consolidate|Art. 28]] and [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 29. Nota explicativă la situațiile financiare|art. 29]] govern consolidated content and notes. [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 30. Raportul consolidat al conducerii|Art. 30]] governs the consolidated management report, including group-level governance, non-financial and state-payment reporting.

## 13. Signature, audit, filing and publication

[[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 31. Semnarea situațiilor financiare|Art. 31]] requires financial statements to be signed by the responsible persons before submission and publication.

[[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 32. Auditul situațiilor financiare|Art. 32]] requires mandatory audit for individual statements of medium and large entities, public-interest entities and other entities under law, and for consolidated statements of groups under [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 27. Obligația întocmirii situațiilor financiare|art. 27]].

[[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 33. Prezentarea și publicarea situațiilor financiare|Art. 33]] sets filing deadlines:

- public-interest entities: 120 days after the last day of the reporting period;
- other entities: 150 days after the last day, for individual statements and related reports where applicable;
- consolidated statements/reports: 180 days.

Audited statements, management reports and audit reports must be placed on the entity's website and kept available for at least 5 years. Entities with suspended activity are exempt from presentation/publication during suspension.

## 14. Liability

[[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 34. Răspunderea pentru încălcarea|Art. 34]] states that entities and persons violating the law bear disciplinary, civil, contraventional and/or criminal liability under applicable law.

This is a gateway provision: actual sanctions may be in the Contravention Code, Criminal Code, tax law or company law, depending on the misconduct. In disputes, link the accounting breach to the downstream legal consequence: non-deductibility, unreliable accounts, unlawful dividend, insolvency delay or director liability.

## 15. Practical checklist

1. Identify entity type and whether it is public-interest.
2. Classify under [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 4. Categoriile entităților și grupurilor|art. 4]], applying two-of-three criteria and the two-year movement rule.
3. Determine standard under [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 5. Modul de ținere a contabilității|art. 5]]: SNC / IFRS, simple / double-entry, abridged / simplified / complete.
4. Check primary-document support under [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 11. Documentele primare|art. 11]] before taking a tax position.
5. Verify retention under [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 17. Păstrarea documentelor contabile|art. 17]].
6. Identify management sign-off and responsibility under [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 18. Obligațiile și drepturile entităţii|art. 18]] and [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 31. Semnarea situațiilor financiare|art. 31]].
7. Test audit and consolidation under [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 27. Obligația întocmirii situațiilor financiare|art. 27]] and [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 32. Auditul situațiilor financiare|art. 32]].
8. Diary filing/publication under [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 33. Prezentarea și publicarea situațiilor financiare|art. 33]].

# Legea nr. 308/2017 — AML/CFT — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 308-2017 — prevenirea spalarii banilor (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Drept comercial]] · **PDF mirror:** legis.md doc_id `151444` · **Remote-ID amend:** [[Legea 144-2025 — economia digitala si servicii electronice (notă)|L144/2025]] Art. XII (staged IF) · **Supervisors:** NBM · CNPF · SPCSB (*Serviciul*)

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Organic AML/CFT statute transposing 4AMLD/5AMLD-style rules and FATF standards: reporting entities, risk-based CDD (standard / simplified / enhanced), STRs, beneficial-ownership transparency, SPCSB organisation, freezing/sanctions, and administrative sanctions.
> **Primary business risk:** Onboarding without adequate CDD/UBO (arts. 5–8, 14) or failing to file STRs (art. 11) → art. 35 sanctions ladder and possible criminal referral; do **not** treat L144 remote e-ID for EU clients as freely live until Art. XII technical IF triggers.

## 2. Practical legal mechanics

### A. Who must comply (arts. 2–4)

Reporting entities (*entități raportoare*) — financial institutions and designated non-financial businesses/professions as listed in [[Legea 308-2017 — prevenirea spalarii banilor (text)#Articolul 4. Entitățile raportoare|art. 4]]. Scope and exclusions in arts. 2–3 definitions.

### B. CDD ladder (arts. 5–10)

| Level | Anchor | When |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | [[Legea 308-2017 — prevenirea spalarii banilor (text)#Articolul 5. Măsurile standarde de precauție\|art. 5]] | Default identification/verification, purpose, ongoing monitoring |
| Remote / electronic | art. 5¹ (L144 redraft) | MD/EU natural persons with qualified cert; resident legal persons with MD/EU key persons — **staged IF** via [[Legea 144-2025 — economia digitala si servicii electronice (notă)\|L144]] |
| Risk assessment | art. 6 | Entity-level and business-relationship risk |
| Simplified | art. 7 | Low-risk cases only |
| Enhanced | art. 8 (+ bis limbs) | PEPs, high-risk countries, complex/unusual patterns, etc. |
| Record-keeping | art. 9 | Retention duties |
| Third-party reliance | art. 10 | Conditional; responsibility retained |

### C. Reporting & tipping-off (arts. 11–12)

* STR / activity reports to the Service — art. 11.
* Tipping-off ban — art. 12.

### D. Internal controls & transparency (arts. 13–14)

* Policies, controls, compliance function — art. 13.
* Beneficial ownership / transparency rules — art. 14 (register interaction; keep UBO current).

### E. Supervision & SPCSB (arts. 15–32)

* Supervisory authorities — art. 15 (NBM/CNPF/others by sector).
* Limits on protected secrets vs AML — art. 16.
* Domestic/international cooperation — art. 17.
* Service organisation, powers, staff, funding — arts. 18–32.

### F. Freezing, sanctions, data (arts. 33–37)

* Protective measures — art. 33.
* Targeted financial sanctions implementation — art. 34.
* Administrative sanctions ladder — [[Legea 308-2017 — prevenirea spalarii banilor (text)#Articolul 35. Sancțiuni|art. 35]].
* Personal data — art. 36; final/transitional — art. 37.

## 3. Risk matrix

| Provision | Flaw / ambiguity | Practical risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Arts. 5–8** | Risk rating on paper only | Supervisory finding / fine | Documented RBA + PEP/sanctions screening |
| **Art. 5¹ / L144** | Remote EU e-ID not yet generally live | Invalid onboarding | Gate on L144 Art. XVIII(4); keep face-to-face/qualified MD path |

| **Art. 14** | Stale UBO data | Register mismatch | Periodic UBO refresh tied to corporate events |
| **Art. 35** | Quantum depends on breach class | Undercounting group exposure | Read sanction grid before settlement talks |

# Legea nr. 317/2025 — permissive-acts omnibus patch — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 317-2025 — modificare acte permisive (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Parents:** [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)|L164/2025]] · [[Legea 227-2025 — optimizarea actelor permisive (notă)|LP227/2025]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** End-2025 omnibus cleaning many permissive-act / migration statutes. **Energy-relevant:** Art. **XVIII** rewrites L164 art. 20 licence registration prongs to **interoperability registry checks** (no paper extract); Art. **XIX** patches LP227 (licence-term tables + Art. XLII IF wording).
> **Primary business risk:** Using pre-LP317 L164 art. 20 checklists that still demand a registry extract; or mis-stating LP227 IF after the XLII tweak.

> [!tip] Energy focus
> Full act patches many non-energy instruments. Below: energy / adjacent only.

---

## 1. Energy amendments

| Art. | Parent | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| **XVIII** | [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)\|L164]] art. 20(2)–(6) lit. a) | Registration in RM verified by ANRE via **interoperability platform** (L142/2018) — **no confirmatory document** requested |
| **XIX** | [[Legea 227-2025 — optimizarea actelor permisive (notă)\|LP227]] | Tweaks Art. XX licence-validity tables; cleans Art. XXV; Art. XLII(1) chapeau → IF **30.12.2025** with listed exceptions |

**IF (this law):** Art. XX — **31.12.2025**; limited-term permits whose new rules are unlimited auto-extend without renewal.

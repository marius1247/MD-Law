# Legea nr. 318/2025 — annual fiscal-policy package — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 318-2025 — modificare acte fiscale (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Fiscalitate & Contabilitate]] · **CF master:** [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 (notă)]] · **Later patch:** [[Legea 41-2026 — suport desfasurare afaceri (notă)|L41/2026]] (amends Art. XIII(3) temporary-admission limb) · **Horizon:** [[Fiscalitate & Contabilitate — horizon and gaps]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** The **2026 fiscal-policy organic law** — large CF rewrite (Art. I) plus social-insurance, AOAM, registration, customs, agri-VAT and cleanup of prior amending laws (Arts. II–XII), with staged IF and a temporary-admission vehicle regularisation window (Art. XIII).
> **Primary business risk:** Using the wrong IF limb. Default **1 Jan 2026**; selected Art. I points + Arts. II–III wait until **1 July 2026**; Arts. VIII/XI/XII hit **31 Dec 2025**. VAT threshold path: L318 → **1.5 MDL m**, then [[Legea 41-2026 — suport desfasurare afaceri (notă)|L41]] → **1.7 MDL m**.

> [!info] Closes a named P0 gap
> [[Fiscalitate & Contabilitate — horizon and gaps]] listed annual fiscal-policy laws (esp. **318/2025**) as **P0** — this ingest closes that primary act.

## 3. High-signal Art. I mechanics

* **Art. 87(11) deferral** — window through **2026**; eligibility rewritten to ≤249 staff + ≤100 MDL m turnover **or** assets; new exclusion for larger section-G traders (>49 staff and >50 MDL m).
* **Energy bridge** — deductible free transfer of connection assets to the SO under L164 (new art. 24(14¹)); L107 cites replaced by L164.
* **VAT** — registration threshold figures in arts. 54¹ / 112 moved to **1.5** (superseded to **1.7** by L41); taxable value reduced by discounts at supply; gas distribution equalisation adjustment via accounting note.
* **Promo / cashback** — new art. 5(38) definition of *campanie promoțională*.

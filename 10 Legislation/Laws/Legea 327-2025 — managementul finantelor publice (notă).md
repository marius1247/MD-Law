# Legea nr. 327/2025 — public-finance management reform — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 327-2025 — managementul finantelor publice (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Achiziții publice & Statul]] · **Also:** [[MOC — Fiscalitate & Contabilitate]] · **Touches:** [[Legea 845-1992 — antreprenoriat si intreprinderi (text)]] · [[Legea 62-2008 — reglementarea valutara (text)]] · [[Legea 100-2017 — actele normative (text)]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Organic reform package that renames/centralises state financial inspection as **Inspectoratul Control Financiar de Stat**, cleans out the “public authority/institution on self-management” category, and deep-amends Law 181/2014 (public finance & fiscal responsibility) plus local public finance and internal public financial control.
> **Primary business risk:** SOEs, municipal enterprises and counterparties of “self-managed” public bodies face a **split IF calendar** — many structural pieces only hit on **1 January 2027**, while the rename and several control hooks are live from **31 December 2025**. Wrong inspector name on filings / wrong budget assumptions for 2026 vs 2027 = procedural defect.

---

## 1. Statutory hierarchy & legal foundation

* **Type:** organic law (Arts. I–XVIII).
* **Publication:** MO 665/31.12.2025, art. 814.
* **Staged IF (Art. XVIII):**
  * **Default:** on publication (31 Dec 2025).
  * **Art. XIV** (Customs Service law tweak): waits for publication of the *Law on public institutions*.
  * **Large 2027 block** (Art. XVIII(1)(b)): Arts. II, parts of IV/V/VIII/IX, X, XV–XVII → **1 January 2027**.
  * **Budget process:** public institutions financed from budget revenues apply L181 rules starting with the **2027** budget cycle (Art. XVIII(2)).
  * **Bridge:** until 1 Jan 2027, financial inspection of self-managed public bodies continues on the Art. IX pct. 33 / art. 78(3)(b) aspects (Art. XVIII(3)).
  * Government has **6 months** to amend / issue implementing acts (Art. XVIII(4)).

---

## 2. Practical legal mechanics

### A. Rename — Inspectoratul Control Financiar de Stat (Arts. I, III, VII…)

Legacy “Inspecția financiară” / “autoritatea administrativă de inspectare financiară din subordinea Ministerului Finanțelor” → **Inspectoratul Control Financiar de Stat** across L845, philanthropy law, Contraventional Code art. 402, etc.

### B. Abolition of “autoritate/instituție publică la autogestiune” (Arts. II, IX…)

* Social-insurance law and, from 2027, the public-finance architecture stop using the self-management public-body category.
* Expect reclassification / budget absorption of entities that lived in that bucket — commercially relevant for contracts, guarantees and payment discipline.

### C. Deep amend of Law 181/2014 (Art. IX — bulk of the act)

Art. IX rewrites definitions (treasury single account, public institution, financial monitoring…), blocking of allocations, expenditure categories (personnel incl. social contributions; energy/utilities; ICT), monitoring of public institutions / municipal enterprises / companies with public ownership, and financial-inspection perimeter. Read the full Art. IX block in the `(text)` before advising on 2027 budget behaviour.

## 3. Risk matrix

| Provision | Flaw / ambiguity | Practical risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Art. XVIII** | Multi-speed IF | Citing 2027 rules in 2026 (or vice versa) | Use the Art. XVIII map as a checklist per article |
| **Art. IX definitions** | “Instituție publică” redraw | Contract party mis-identified | Re-KYC public counterparties before 2027 budget year |
| **Inspector rename** | Old forms / powers of attorney | Invalid filings | Replace party name from 31 Dec 2025 |
| **Art. XIV** | Tied to unpublished public-institutions law | Dangling IF | Track that law’s publication before relying on Art. XIV |

## 4. Relevant practice

* Public-sector and PPP desks should update inspection / audit contact points immediately; budget and monitoring changes can wait for the 2027 modelling cycle.
* Cross-check municipal enterprises under L845 + local public-finance amendments.

# Legea nr. 330/2025 — trade-secrets vocabulary sweep — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 330-2025 — modificare secret comercial (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Drept comercial]] · **Anchor statute:** Legea nr. 384/2023 (*not yet ingested*) · **Also amends:** [[Legea 845-1992 — antreprenoriat si intreprinderi (text)]] · [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)]] · [[Legea 183-2012 — concurenta (text)]] · [[Legea 149-2012 — insolvabilitate (text)]] · [[Legea 74-2020 — achizitii sectoriale (text)]] · [[Codul de procedura civila 225-2003 (text)]] · [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (text)]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Organic omnibus that rewires dozens of statutes from legacy “secret / taină comercială / păstrare” language to the **Legea 384/2023** protection vocabulary — so confidentiality exceptions, court closures, inspections, procurement, competition and insolvency all point to one regime.
> **Primary business risk:** Relying on old “commercial secret list” practices without checking L384 definitions and lawful-acquisition / exception rules; also note **Art. XLI** quietly postpones the inheritance-reform IF in Law 251/2025 from 1 Jan 2026 to **1 April 2026**.

---

## 1. Statutory hierarchy & legal foundation

* **Type:** organic law (Arts. I–XLII).
* **IF:** Art. XLII — **on publication** (MO 662-664/31.12.2025, art. 810) → **31 Dec 2025**.
* **Pivot act:** Legea nr. 384/2023 privind protecția secretelor comerciale (Arts. I, XXXIV and cross-references throughout).
* **Technique:** mostly terminological substitution (*păstrare → protecție*, *taină comercială → secret comercial*, *dezvălui → divulga*), plus a few substantive redirects to L384.

---

## 2. Practical legal mechanics

### A. Entrepreneurship & companies (Arts. I, X)

* [[Legea 845-1992 — antreprenoriat si intreprinderi (text)#Articolul 12. Controlul asupra activităţii de antreprenoriat|L845 art. 12(3)]] rewritten: enterprise may withhold commercial-secret data from public authorities **except** law-enforcement and control bodies acting within their powers; the enterprise still draws the list, but **subject to L384**.
* L135/2007 SRL art. 44(4) aligned to the same vocabulary (Art. X).

### B. Procedure & enforcement (Arts. VI–VII, XIII–XIV)

* CPC [[Codul de procedura civila 225-2003 (notă)|arts. 23 / 134]] — confidentiality of commercial secrets / closed hearings language updated.
* Criminal procedure art. 214, Contraventional Code, judicial officers, special couriers — same sweep.

### C. Market & regulated sectors (selected)

| Article | Act in vault / note | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **XVIII** | [[Legea 183-2012 — concurenta (notă)]] | Competition Act confidentiality / secret vocabulary → L384 |
| **XVII** | [[Legea 149-2012 — insolvabilitate (notă)]] | Art. 87 wording |
| **XXIII / XXX** | L131/2015 (*pending*) · [[Legea 74-2020 — achizitii sectoriale (notă)]] | Procurement / sectoral procurement secrecy exceptions |
| **XXXII** | [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (notă)]] | Art. 12(13)(k) |
| **XII** | [[Legea 179-2008 — parteneriat public-privat (notă)]] | PPP confidentiality limb |
| **XXXIV** | L384/2023 itself | Minor *protejării → protecției* clean-up |

### D. Non-secrets rider (Art. XLI)

* Law 251/2025 (inheritance regime revisions): IF date **1 Jan 2026 → 1 Apr 2026**. Easy to miss in a “trade secrets” act — flag for civil-law desks.

## 3. Risk matrix

| Provision | Flaw / ambiguity | Practical risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Art. I / L845 art. 12(3)** | “Control bodies” still broad | Over-disclosure or unlawful refusal | Map requesting authority’s statutory powers + L384 exceptions |
| **Omnibus vocabulary** | Substantive duties unchanged in many acts | False comfort that L384 doesn’t apply | Treat L384 as the governing definition everywhere “secret comercial” appears |
| **Art. XLI** | Hidden IF shift | Wrong inheritance-law start date | Diary **1 April 2026** for L251/2025 |
| **L384 not in vault** | Parent act missing | Incomplete advice | Ingest Legea 384/2023 next |

## 4. Relevant practice

* Competition / dawn-raid confidentiality claims should cite L384 + L183 as amended, not the repealed “legea secretului comercial” references.
* Litigation: update template motions for closed hearings under CPC art. 23 to the new phrasing.

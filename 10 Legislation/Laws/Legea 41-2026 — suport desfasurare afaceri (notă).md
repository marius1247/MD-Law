# Legea nr. 41/2026 — business-support omnibus — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 41-2026 — suport desfasurare afaceri (text)]] ✅ · **Hubs:** [[MOC — Fiscalitate & Contabilitate]] · [[MOC — Societăți & Guvernanță corporativă]] · [[MOC — Achiziții publice & Statul]] · **CF master:** [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 (notă)]] · **SRL parent:** [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (notă)]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Short organic omnibus (Arts. I–X) easing business friction — VAT registration threshold bump, SME deferral/window tweaks, enforcement shield when the State owes you money, freer performance-bond forms in public procurement, SRL withdrawal presumption, free-zone excise exit, agricultural VAT refund redesign, repeal of the section-I special services tax.
> **Primary business risk:** Applying limbs on the wrong IF date. **Default IF = publication (27.03.2026)**; **Art. I pct. 4** waits until **1 May 2026**; Art. I pcts. 3 & 7 apply only while taxpayers use CF art. 87(11).

## 1. Statutory hierarchy & legal foundation

* **Type:** organic amending law; MO 143-144/27.03.2026, art. 114.
* **IF (Art. X):** on publication, derogating from L100/2017 art. 56(2); exception Art. I pct. 4 → **1 May 2026**.
* **Art. I pcts. 3 & 7:** apply for fiscal periods in which taxpayers apply CF art. 87(11) (Art. X(2)).
* Government: **5 months** to align secondary acts (Art. X(3)).

## 3. Practical legal mechanics (high-signal)

### A. Fiscal Code — Art. I

1. **VAT registration threshold** arts. 54¹(3)(a) & (3¹): **1.7 million** lei (was 1.5). Remodel SME VAT-registration and 4% turnover-CIT interaction → [[Fiscalitate — synthesis]] · [[Concept — TVA]].
2. **Art. 84(6)** time window extended through **2026**.
3. **Art. 87(11)** — removes the sentence that ignored late corrected returns aimed at changing the deferral option (applies while 87(11) is used — Art. X(2)).
4. **Art. 96(b)** — drops the “medical devices in the State Register” limb (**IF 1 May 2026**).
5. **Arts. 197(4) & 229(9)** — no forced collection / no account suspension for the slice of taxpayer arrears that does not exceed what budget-financed institutions owe that taxpayer (within approved allocations). Classic B2G cash-trap relief.
6. **Art. 260(6)** — percentage fine on undeclared taxable income replaced by **fixed MDL 1,000–10,000** (scoped by Art. X(2) to 87(11) periods).

### B. SRL withdrawal — Art. III

Closes a litigation game: challengers of associate status after the withdrawal notice can no longer freeze the company’s art. 47¹(3)/(5) duties. Radiation at [[ASP]] is the effectiveness moment. → [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (notă)]] · [[Concept — Retragere asociat SRL]]

### C. Procurement performance bond — Art. V

Stops authorities from mandating a single bond form. Operator picks among five statutory forms at contract signature. Wire into [[MOC — Achiziții publice & Statul]] even while **L131/2015** full text is still pending.

### D. Agri VAT refund — Art. VII

Programme recalibrated around **2025 activity mix** and a **40% / 12% cap** formula. Do not quote old L337 percentages after this act.

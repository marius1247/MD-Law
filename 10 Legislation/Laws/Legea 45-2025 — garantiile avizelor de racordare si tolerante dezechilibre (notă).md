# Legea nr. 45/2025 — connection guarantees & imbalance tolerances — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 45-2025 — garantiile avizelor de racordare si tolerante dezechilibre (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · [[MOC — Racordare și acces la rețele]] · **Concepts:** [[Concept — Garanție de bună execuție a avizului de racordare]] · [[Concept — Aviz de racordare]] · [[Concept — Producător eligibil]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Three-track organic amending law — (1) **performance guarantees** for connection notices >200 kW; (2) **early recognition of national-interest TSO CAPEX** in the transport tariff; (3) **wider imbalance deadbands** for eligible wind (±8%) and PV (±4%) inside the central buyer’s balancing group.
> **Primary business risk:** Holding a pre-LP45 >200 kW *aviz* past 30 June 2025 without either extending (and posting the guarantee) or voluntarily surrendering it — then facing the **50–70% nevalorificare tax** at expiry.

> [!danger] L107 currency
> Art. I amends **L107/2016**, repealed 19.08.2025 by [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (text)|L164/2025]]. The guarantee / cancellation mechanics now live operationally in [[HANRE 311-2026 — racordarea la retelele electrice (text)|HANRE 311/2026]] (and the ANRE instruction on guarantees). Remap before 2026 litigation; keep LP45 for transitional Art. III deadlines and the L10 Art. II imbalance rewrite.

## 2. Practical legal mechanics & key provisions

### A. Connection performance guarantees (Art. I §1 → L107 art. 47(9)–(13))

| Rule | Content |
|---|---|
| Threshold | Power plants **> 200 kW** |
| Obligation | Post **garanție de bună execuție** with the system operator after obtaining the *aviz* |
| Quantum / terms | Set by **ANRE** (not in this law) |
| Sanction | SO **cancels** the *aviz* if guarantee not posted |
| Exemptions | **Eligible producers** + **renewable energy communities** |
| Refund | Guarantee posted before eligible-producer status is **returned on request** once status is granted |

→ [[Concept — Garanție de bună execuție a avizului de racordare]] · [[Legea 45-2025 — garantiile avizelor de racordare si tolerante dezechilibre (text)#Art. I.]]

### B. Transitional squeeze on legacy avisuri (Art. III(2)–(5))

1. **Existing** >200 kW avisuri valid at IF → guarantee only if holder **requests extension**; deposit clock runs from extension approval.
2. **Voluntary surrender window** until **30 June 2025** (SO notification).
3. After that window: if *aviz* cannot be extended / expires → **taxă de nevalorificare = 50–70%** of the ANRE guarantee amount → state budget; ANRE decision transmitted to **SFS** for monitoring / forced execution.

### C. TSO tariff — project value of national-interest works (Art. I §2 → L107 art. 88(2))

Derogation from art. 87(3)(d): for transport-tariff setting, ANRE takes into account the **project value** of transmission network works declared **lucrări de utilitate publică de interes național** from the moment funds are disbursed; true-up on commissioning. Relevant for large interconnectors / backbone CAPEX timing in [[MOC — Tarife și metodologii ANRE]].

### D. Eligible-producer imbalance deadbands (Art. II → L10 art. 37(7))

Inside the central buyer’s balancing group:

| Technology | Deadband (measured vs contractual net position) | Outside band |
|---|---|---|
| Wind | **± 8%** | Imbalance only on the **excess** beyond the band |
| PV | **± 4%** | Same — excess only |

Central buyer does **not** calculate imbalance inside the band. → [[Concept — Producător eligibil]] · [[Concept — Parte responsabilă de echilibrare]]

| **Art. I cites L107** | Parent repealed Aug 2025 | Wrong article cite in pleadings | Remap to L164 + HANRE 311 |

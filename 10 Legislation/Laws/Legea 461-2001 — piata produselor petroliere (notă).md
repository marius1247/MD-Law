# Legea nr. 461/2001 — petroleum products market — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 461-2001 — piata produselor petroliere (text)]] ✅ *body complete; annex pricing formula OCR-noisy* · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Crisis patch:** [[Legea 101-2026 — consolidarea mecanismelor de interventie in situatii de criza (notă)|L101/2026]] · **Crisis parent:** [[Legea 248-2025 — managementul situatiilor de criza (notă)|L248/2025]] · **Concept:** [[Concept — Situație de criză în domeniul petrolier]] · **Regulator:** [[ANRE]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Organic statute for Moldova’s **petroleum products market** — ANRE price-cap powers for standard main products, licensing of market activities, import/storage/wholesale/retail rules, consumer protection, and (after L101) crisis-mode CNMC derogations.
> **Primary business risk:** Selling retail above the **daily ANRE max price** (art. 4) or holding the wrong licence class (Ch. III). In alert/emergency under L248, CNMC may temporarily derogate from art. 4 / Annex 2 price norms (art. 6(7)).

## 3. Practical legal mechanics

### A. Max retail prices (art. 4) — the commercial core

* ANRE sets **daily** max retail prices for **standard-type main petroleum products** under its methodology; valid from **00:01** next day(s); publishes PL×CV indicators, VAT, excise, specific margin, density.
* Ban on retail above the max price (art. 4(4)).
* **L101 rewrite of art. 4(2):** if standard stocks are exhausted, other main-product types may be sold retail **only** at ≤ the standard-type max price.
* Crisis definition retitled **„situație de criză în domeniul petrolier”** (art. 2 via L101) → [[Concept — Situație de criză în domeniul petrolier]].

### B. CNMC temporary derogations (art. 6(7) — L101)

During **alert or emergency** under L248, CNMC may temporarily derogate from art. 4 and Annex 2 calculation norms to keep supply and critical infrastructure running.

### C. Licensing & logistics

* Ch. III — activities requiring a licence; special conditions; suspension/withdrawal.
* Import through designated customs posts (art. 18 as rewritten by L101).
* Storage / wholesale / retail duties (Ch. V–VI); quality compliance & consumer rights (art. 5).

# Legea nr. 489/1999 — public social insurance system — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 489-1999 — sistemul public de asigurari sociale (text)]] ⚠️ *annex likely truncated* · **Hub:** [[MOC — Fiscalitate & Contabilitate]] *(payroll wedge)* · **EU coordination (future):** [[Legea 135-2026 — coordonare securitate sociala UE (notă)]] · **Also amended by:** [[Legea 327-2025 — managementul finantelor publice (notă)|L327/2025]] · [[Legea 187-2025 — modificare BNM (notă)|L187/2025]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Organic statute for Moldova’s public social-insurance system — insured persons, CNAS budget, contribution payers/base/deadlines, benefits architecture, individual records, enforcement.
> **Primary business risk:** Misclassifying the contribution base (arts. 22–23 + annex exclusions) or missing registration/payment clocks (arts. 20, 26, 28) → surcharges and forced execution; EU coordination art. 9¹ is in the consolidation but **not live** until accession ([[Legea 135-2026 — coordonare securitate sociala UE (notă)|L135/2026]]).

> PDF dump ends mid-annex without the promulgation block. Article body through art. 57 appears present; **verify annex tables at legis.md** before relying on exclusion lists.

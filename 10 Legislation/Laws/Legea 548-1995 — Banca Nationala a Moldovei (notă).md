# National Bank of Moldova Law — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 548-1995 — Banca Nationala a Moldovei (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Drept comercial]] · **Banks:** [[Legea 202-2017 — activitatea bancilor (notă)]] · **OCN:** [[Legea 1-2018 — organizatiile de creditare (notă)]] · **Insurance:** [[Legea 92-2022 — activitatea de asigurare (notă)]] · **AML:** [[Legea 308-2017 — prevenirea spalarii banilor (notă)]] · **Challenge:** [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Organic law of the autonomous central bank: price stability primary objective; monetary/FX policy; exclusive currency issue; licensing/supervision of banks **and** (post-reform) insurers, OCNs, savings & loan associations, credit bureaux; payment-system oversight; resolution authority; macroprudential policy.
> **Primary business risk:** Underestimating NBM’s expanded perimeter (art. 5(1)(p)–(q)) — insurance/OCN/AML files treated as “CNPF-era” → wrong supervisor, missed reporting, and sanctions under sector statutes + L548 act regime.

---

## 1. Statutory hierarchy & legal foundation

* Constitutional autonomy / accountability to Parliament — art. 1; primary objective price stability — [[Legea 548-1995 — Banca Nationala a Moldovei (text)#Articolul 4. Obiectivul fundamental]].
* Sector statutes implement supervisory tools: [[Legea 202-2017 — activitatea bancilor (notă)]], [[Legea 1-2018 — organizatiile de creditare (notă)]], [[Legea 92-2022 — activitatea de asigurare (notă)]], [[Legea 114-2012 — servicii de plata (notă)]], [[Legea 308-2017 — prevenirea spalarii banilor (notă)]].
* Capital-market statute [[Legea 171-2012 — piata de capital (notă)]] still names **CNPF** as competent authority in the consolidated text — do not assume automatic NBM transfer for securities firms from L548 alone.
* Licensing/control laws L160/2011, L131/2012, L235/2006 **do not apply** to NBM’s exercise of L548 powers (art. 5(6)).
* Individual NBM acts → [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]] as specialised by arts. 11 / 11¹ L548 and sector challenge clauses (e.g. L202 art. 144).

## 2. Practical legal mechanics

### Status & objective (arts. 1–4)
* Public autonomous legal person, accountable to Parliament; not registered in enterprise registers — [[Legea 548-1995 — Banca Nationala a Moldovei (text)#Articolul 1. Statutul juridic al Băncii Naţionale]].
* Without prejudice to price stability, contributes to financial-system stability and general economic policy — art. 4(2).

### Core attributions (art. 5)
Key practitioner map — [[Legea 548-1995 — Banca Nationala a Moldovei (text)#Articolul 5. Atribuţiile de bază]]:
* **(a)–(c), (g)–(l)** — monetary/FX policy, state banker/agent, analyses, sole currency issuer, exchange-regime, international reserves, BOP/IIP/external debt stats, FX regulation.
* **(d)** — license, regulate, supervise banks (solo/consolidated) → operationalised in L202.
* **(e)** — bank credit including emergency liquidity assistance.
* **(f)** — financial-market infrastructures, payment schemes/arrangements/instruments (license, regulate, monitor).
* **(m)** — payment services & e-money.
* **(o)** — bank resolution authority (Law 232/2016).
* **(p)** — license/authorise/opinion for state registration, regulate and supervise **insurers, reinsurers, insurance intermediaries, BNAA, savings & loan associations, credit bureaux, OCNs**.
* **(q)** — supervisory authority for reporting entities under L308 AML.
* **(r)–(s)** — macroprudential policy; financial education/inclusion policy documents.
* Systemic-crisis stabilisation tools (suspend/limit payment or delivery obligations of supervised entities up to **6 months**, in consultation with Government) — art. 5(5).

### Macroprudential toolkit (arts. 5¹–5²)
* Strategy approved via National Financial Stability Committee (Law 209/2018) — art. 5¹.
* May tighten vs L202: own funds (L202 arts. 60–62), large exposures (74), liquidity (76), disclosure (91–94); set buffers (L202 art. 63); leverage, sectoral capital, LTV/DTI/DSTI, funding limits, CCP/derivative margins, intra-financial exposures — art. 5². Acts publish in Monitorul Oficial.

### Governance (arts. 22–28)
* Dual board: **Supervisory Council** (oversight) + **Executive Committee** (executive management / core attributions) — [[Legea 548-1995 — Banca Nationala a Moldovei (text)#Articolul 22. Organele de conducere ale Băncii]].
* Council: Governor (chair) + First Deputy + five non-employee members (post-LP45/2026 composition) — art. 23.
* Executive Committee: Governor + First Deputy + three Deputy Governors — art. 23(2).
* Parliamentary appointment; incompatibilities art. 25; Council powers art. 26; Executive Committee art. 27; Audit Committee art. 28.

### Acts, cooperation, monetary tools
* NBM acts (regulations, ordinances, recommendations, individual acts) — art. 11 and follow-ons; professional secrecy and notification rules interact with sector challenge paths.
* Cooperation with state bodies without prejudice to independence — art. 6; international cooperation art. 7.
* Monetary policy instruments: open market, FX ops, reserve requirements, bank credit — arts. 14–18.

### Interaction with supervised markets
| Perimeter | Organic hook (L548) | Operational statute |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Banks | art. 5(1)(d),(o) | [[Legea 202-2017 — activitatea bancilor (notă)]] |
| OCN / AEÎ / credit bureaux | art. 5(1)(p) | [[Legea 1-2018 — organizatiile de creditare (notă)]] (+ sector AEÎ rules) |
| Insurance | art. 5(1)(p) | [[Legea 92-2022 — activitatea de asigurare (notă)]] (“autoritate de supraveghere = BNM”) |
| Payments / e-money / FMI | art. 5(1)(f),(m) | [[Legea 114-2012 — servicii de plata (notă)]] |
| AML reporting entities | art. 5(1)(q) | [[Legea 308-2017 — prevenirea spalarii banilor (notă)]] |
| Capital market (securities firms, markets) | clearing/settlement carve-outs only in L171 | [[Legea 171-2012 — piata de capital (notă)]] — text still centres **CNPF** |

## 3. Risk matrix

| Provision | Flaw / ambiguity | Practical risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Art. 5(1)(p)** | Legacy “CNPF” habits for insurance/OCN | Wrong filings / invalid applications | Treat NBM as supervisor; map L92 / L1 gates |
| **Art. 5(5)** | Broad crisis suspension powers | Contractual payment freezes | Force-majeure / regulatory-change clauses in FS contracts |
| **Art. 5(6)** | Carve-out from L160/L131 | Ordinary licensing playbooks fail | Use sector statutes + NBM regulations only |
| **Art. 5²** | Macro overlays on L202 minima | Sudden buffer / LTV shocks | Monitor MO publications; stress capital plans |
| **Arts. 22–23** | Dual-board decision attribution | Wrong addressee for challenge | Identify act issuer (Executive Committee vs Governor) |
| **L171 vs L548** | Capital-market text still says CNPF | Misdirected licensing | Read L171 arts. 1, 33–35 as written until amended |

## 4. Client checklist

- [ ] Identify whether the entity sits in art. 5(1)(d)/(p)/(m)/(q) perimeter.
- [ ] For banks → L202 licence; for OCN → L1 aviz + Register; for insurers → L92 NBM licence.
- [ ] Track macroprudential acts (art. 5²) separately from microprudential L202/L92 rules.
- [ ] Payment institutions / e-money → art. 5(1)(m) + L114 track.
- [ ] Challenge NBM individual acts via [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]] + arts. 11/11¹ L548 (+ sector standing rules).
- [ ] Watch LP187/2025 and LP45/2026 amendments (reserves, Council composition).

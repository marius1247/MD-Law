# Legea nr. 74/2024 — climate actions — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 74-2024 — actiuni climatice (text)]] ✅ (+ annexes) · **Amended by:** [[Legea 53-2026 — reforma autoritatilor Ministerul Mediului (notă)|LP53/2026]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · [[HG 86-2025 — Plan national integrat energie clima 2025-2030 (notă)|PNIEC / HG 86]] · [[CNED]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Organic climate statute — national GHG targets, adaptation, **NDC**, policy documents, MRV / ETS-style operator duties, and cross-ministry competences. Energy slice: **Ministry of Energy** duties (art. 9) including energy-union/climate governance, low-carbon tech, electricity/energy MAFC, **CNED** information channel, and **PNIEC** update.
> **Primary business risk:** Advising energy/industrial clients without checking whether they are **GES operators** under this law’s annex activity list; or treating PNIEC as free-standing from this statute.

> [!tip] Energy focus
> Full act is climate/environment-wide. Below: energy-adjacent hooks. Distinct from [[Legea 74-2025 — Curtea Constitutionala (notă)|LP74/2025 Constitutional Court]] (different year/subject).

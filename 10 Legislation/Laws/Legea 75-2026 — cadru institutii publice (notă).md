# Legea nr. 75/2026 — public institutions framework — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 75-2026 — cadru institutii publice (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** peripheral to [[MOC — Energetică]] · possible governance overlay for [[CNED]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Organic rules for **public institutions** founded by Government / local / special-level authorities (excluding education, research, health, social, culture, youth/sport). Performance evaluation, founder control, tariff methodology approved by Government for chargeable services.
> **Primary business risk for energy practice:** Low direct. Watch whether energy public institutions (e.g. **CNED**) fall inside scope once IF hits — governance / performance / tariff methodology may shift.

> [!note] Peripheral ingest
> Not a substitute for L164 / L108 / ANRE acts. IF **1.01.2027** (art. 13(9) → **1.01.2029**).

---

## 1. Energy-adjacent watchpoints

* Scope exclusions are sector-based (education/health/…) — **energy not excluded by name**.
* Founder-approved statutes; Government methodology for service tariffs where charges apply.
* Cross-check [[HG 1060-2023 — organizare functionare CNED (notă)|HG 1060]] / [[Legea 139-2018 — eficienta energetica (notă)|L139]] once transitional mapping appears.

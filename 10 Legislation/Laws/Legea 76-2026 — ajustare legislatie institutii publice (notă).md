# Legea nr. 76/2026 — aligning legislation to the public-institutions framework — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 76-2026 — ajustare legislatie institutii publice (text)]] ✅ · **Hubs:** [[MOC — Drept civil]] · [[MOC — Societăți & Guvernanță corporativă]] · [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Civil Code:** [[Codul civil 1107-2002 (notă)]] · **Registration:** [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (notă)]] · **CNED hook:** [[Legea 139-2018 — eficienta energetica (notă)|L139]] · [[CNED]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Short organic companion that retunes Civil Code art. 307, state registration, civil-service, central-administration and SME/CNED methodology hooks to the new **public-institution categories** law (**L75/2026**, not yet in vault).
> **Primary business risk:** Missing the split IF — **Art. II (registration of public legal persons) is live on publication (23.07.2026)**; the rest waits until **1 January 2027**.

---

## 1. Statutory hierarchy & IF

* Organic law; MO 330-333/23.07.2026, art. 333.
* Companion to **Law 75/2026** (regulatory framework for categories of public institutions) — *ingest next*.
* **Art. VII:** IF **1 Jan 2027**, except **Art. II** (on publication). Government must align secondary acts before the general IF.

---

## 2. Article map

| Art. | Target | Bite |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | [[Codul civil 1107-2002 (text) — Index\|CC]] art. 307 | Redefines *instituție publică* as a non-commercial public legal person created by public-authority act; **repeals** art. 307(3)–(5) |
| **II** | [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (text)\|L220]] art. 3 | Public legal persons use a Government-set **take-on-register (evidență)** procedure — **IF on publication** |
| **III** | L158/2008 civil service | Board-membership activity carve-in for public-institution councils |
| **IV** | L98/2012 central specialised administration | Multi-point rewrite incl. new art. 32 on public institutions |
| **V** | L179/2016 SMEs | Support-instrument amounts set on a **founder-approved methodology** |
| **VI** | [[Legea 139-2018 — eficienta energetica (text)\|L139/2018]] art. 11(3) | CNED-related methodology approved by **CNED’s founder** |
| **VII** | IF / transition | See §1 |

---

## 3. Practical points

* **Civil Code cleanup:** art. 307 no longer carries the old detailed public-institution sub-rules — those migrate to L75/2026 + L98 art. 32.
* **ASP practice:** from publication, public legal persons are not ordinary company registrations; watch the forthcoming Government procedure under Art. II.
* **Energy/efficiency:** Art. VI is a small but real [[CNED]] governance hook — methodologies for certain L139 instruments need founder approval language.

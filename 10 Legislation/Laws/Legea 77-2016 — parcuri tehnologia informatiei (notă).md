# Legea nr. 77/2016 — IT Parks — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 77-2016 — parcuri tehnologia informatiei (text)]] ✅ *(consolidation incl. LP125/2024)* · **Fiscal Code:** [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 (notă)|Titlul X]] · **Amending:** [[Legea 125-2024 — modificare parc IT si tranzitii vamale (notă)]] · **Concept:** [[Concept — Parc IT]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Fiscalitate & Contabilitate]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Creates and governs Moldova’s **IT Parks** — residency, eligible CAEM/CSPM activities, Administration, and the flagship **7% single tax on sales revenue** (with a per-employee monthly floor).
> **Primary business risk:** Assuming Titlul X alone is enough. **Admission, activity list, annual audit verification, and withdrawal** live here. Wrong CAEM codes or failed annual indicators → recalculation of all taxes in ordinary form from the breach period (art. 15(5)).

## 3. Practical mechanics

### A. Becoming a resident
* Registration by the Park Administration under a **Government-approved** resident-registration regulation (exhaustive procedure + model contract) — art. 7(1).
* Contract ≥ **4 years**, cannot exceed park life; entered in the resident register on signing.
* Resident must have Moldova seat and carry on **one or more art. 8 activities as principal activity**, including via remote employees in/out of Moldova — art. 7(4).
* Accounting / financial statements / tax returns still under ordinary L287 + CF rules — art. 7(5).

### B. Eligible activities (art. 8 — post-LP125)
Core software / IT services CAEM codes (62.01, 58.21, 58.29, 62.02–62.03, 62.09, 63.11–63.12, limited education/R&D, etc.), plus LP125 expansions for **export-only contact centres (82.20)** and **export-only specified HR supply (78.30.xx)**. Always verify the current art. 8 list before onboarding a client.

### C. The 7% single tax (art. 15)
* **7% of sales revenue (*venit din vânzări*)**, but not below the monthly minimum in art. 15(2): **30% of the forecast national average monthly wage per employee**.
* Bundles into one payment: CIT, payroll income tax, employer+employee social contributions, employer+employee health premiums, local taxes, real-estate tax, and road tax for vehicles registered in MD — art. 15(1)(a), (1¹).
* **Other taxes** (VAT, etc.) remain under the ordinary regime.
* Paid **monthly**.
* **Stability clause** through **2035** if later acts change rate/composition/cancel the single tax — residents may keep the prior regime (art. 15(4)); composition adjusts if bundled taxes are renamed/replaced, without changing the 7% rate.
* Breach of facility conditions → recalculation under ordinary rules from the period of breach (art. 15(5)).

### D. Employees (art. 16)
* Full social-benefit types available, but **insured monthly income = 68% of forecast average wage** (not actual salary) — pension/benefit base effect clients must disclose pre-hire (art. 16(3)).
* Health-insurance insured status via the ordinary payroll return information flow.

### E. Annual verification & withdrawal (arts. 18–19)
* Mandatory annual audit verification of indicators for anyone who used the special regime ≥1 month in the calendar year.
* Withdrawal via contract resolution — including failed annual audit indicators (art. 19(1)(e)), AML/court judgments, contract breach uncured in 30 days.

### F. Budget split (art. 14)
Single-tax receipts are trezorerial-split across state budget, CNAS, CNAM, and local budgets at fixed percentages (orientation for cash-flow / authority mapping — not a client election).

## 4. Risk matrix

| Issue | Risk | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| Activity mix drifts outside art. 8 | Loss of regime + ordinary-tax recalculation | Annual CAEM/CSPM mapping; contract activity list hygiene |
| Revenue too low vs headcount | Minimum tax (30% avg wage × employees) dominates | Model floor before hiring plans |
| Employees unaware of 68% insured base | Labour / reputation disputes | Written pre-hire disclosure (art. 16(3)) |
| Titlul X cited without L77 | Incomplete admission/withdrawal advice | Always pair CF Titlul X + this law + HG resident regulation |
| HG registration regulation not in vault | Procedure detail incomplete | Pull current HG for onboarding checklists |

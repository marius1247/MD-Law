# Legea nr. 845/1992 — entrepreneurship & enterprises — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 845-1992 — antreprenoriat si intreprinderi (text)]] ✅ · **Hubs:** [[MOC — Drept comercial]] · [[MOC — Societăți & Guvernanță corporativă]] · **Synthesis:** [[Drept comercial — synthesis]] · **Latest amends on this dump:** [[Legea 327-2025 — managementul finantelor publice (notă)|L327/2025]] · [[Legea 330-2025 — modificare secret comercial (notă)|L330/2025]] (both IF 31 Dec 2025)

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Legacy framework statute defining entrepreneurship, enterprise forms, registration principles, state–enterprise relations, social entrepreneurship and fiscal-monopoly status.
> **Primary business risk:** Treating L845 as the *primary* company or licensing statute. Formative company law now lives in [[Codul civil 1107-2002 (notă)|Civil Code]] + [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (notă)|L135]] / [[Legea 1134-1997 — societati pe actiuni (notă)|L1134]]; permits in [[Legea 160-2011 — reglementarea prin autorizare (notă)|L160/2011]]. L845 still bites on **cash-payment duties**, **price/markup fines**, **commercial-secret refusals**, **social enterprise / fiscal monopoly** chapters, and residual form rules — until the Government’s replacement law (project 345/MDED/2025) repeals it after a two-year transition.

> [!warning] Legacy — check displacement first
> Before citing any L845 article, ask whether the Civil Code, L135/L1134, L220/2007, L160/2011 or L235/2006 already occupies the field. Prefer the modern act.

## 2. Practical legal mechanics

### A. Definitions & scope (Ch. I, arts. 1–5)

* **Entrepreneurship** — independent, own-name, own-risk activity aimed at a permanent income source (art. 1); **social entrepreneurship** carved in art. 1¹ / Ch. VI¹.
* **Enterprise** — organisational form of entrepreneurship; may be legal or natural person (art. 3).
* Public-authority managers generally barred from entrepreneurship (art. 2(2)).

### B. Rights, duties, state relations (Ch. II, arts. 6–12) — still live commercially

| Topic | Anchor | Practice |
|---|---|---|
| Rights catalogue | art. 6 | Broad; many limbs superseded in practice by CC / special laws |
| Cash acceptance / card | [[Legea 845-1992 — antreprenoriat si intreprinderi (text)#Articolul 7¹. Obligația privind acceptarea plăților\|art. 7¹]] | Mandatory acceptance rules; fine 4k–6k MDL/case via SFS (art. 10(5¹)) |
| Discount duty | art. 7² | Operational retail constraint |
| Permits | art. 10(2) | Points to **L160/2011** (or special financial laws) |
| State-only activities | art. 10(3) | Closed list (military, certain medical, state securities printing, etc.) |
| Price / social-product fines | art. 10(6), (8) | 10–20% (and higher for social-product markups); collector now **Inspectoratul Control Financiar de Stat** / SFS / Court of Accounts — per [[Legea 327-2025 — managementul finantelor publice (notă)\|L327]] |
| Commercial secrets vs authorities | [[Legea 845-1992 — antreprenoriat si intreprinderi (text)#Articolul 12. Controlul asupra activităţii de antreprenoriat\|art. 12(3)]] | Withhold from public authorities except law-enforcement/control bodies within powers; list must respect **L384/2023** — [[Legea 330-2025 — modificare secret comercial (notă)\|L330]] |
| Challenging controls | art. 12(4¹) | L131/2012 state-control law; SFS/Customs → CF / Customs Code |

### C. Forms (Ch. III, arts. 13–22) — mostly historical

Individual enterprise, SNC, SCS, SA/SRL pointers, cooperatives, lease enterprises, state/municipal enterprises, unions. **Do not** incorporate from these articles alone — use CC + L135/L1134/L220.

### D. Firm name & registration principles (Ch. IV–V)

Arts. 23–30 still supply residual firm-name and suspension/resume rules; constitutive registration is [[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (notă)|L220/2007]].

### E. Reorganisation / liquidation (Ch. VI)

Arts. 32–34; insolvency overlay [[Legea 149-2012 — insolvabilitate (notă)]]. Arts. 35–36 abrogated.

### F. Social entrepreneurship (Ch. VI¹, arts. 36¹–36⁵)

Status, National Commission for Social Entrepreneurship, support measures — still the positive-law home until repeal/replacement.

### G. Fiscal monopoly (Ch. VI², arts. 36⁶–36⁸)

Government may grant **fiscal-monopoly** status to majority/wholly state-owned entities for exclusive economic activities aimed at state revenue, on proposal of the National Commission for Fiscal Monopoly (MDED chair, MF, Competition Council, 2 civil-society members). Status falls if state ownership becomes minority. Permits under L160 still required (art. 36⁷(4)). Competition / state-aid analysis mandatory before relying on exclusivity — [[Concept — Ajutor de stat]] · [[Legea 183-2012 — concurenta (notă)]].

## 3. Risk matrix

| Provision | Flaw / ambiguity | Practical risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Whole act** | Parallel / contradictory architecture vs CC | Wrong governing law | Start from CC + special company laws; use L845 only where still unique |

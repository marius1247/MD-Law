# Legea nr. 86/2026 — accounting threshold reset from 2027

**Raw text:** [[Legea 86-2026 — modificare Legea contabilitatii (text)]] ✅ *complete* · **Amends:** [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)]] · **Main note:** [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (notă)]] · **Synthesis:** [[Contabilitate & raportare financiară — synthesis]] · **Horizon:** [[Fiscalitate & Contabilitate — horizon and gaps]]

> [!danger] Effective 1 January 2027
> This amendment materially raises the size criteria in [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 4. Categoriile entităților și grupurilor|L287 art. 4]]. The practical effect is downward reclassification: many entities that are currently small become micro; many medium become small; some large become medium. Audit, reporting and consolidation obligations may fall away.

## 1. What the act is

[[Legea 86-2026 — modificare Legea contabilitatii (text)]] is a short organic law amending [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)]]. Its purpose is not to rewrite accounting law. It updates the category thresholds to match the EU inflation adjustment made by Delegated Directive (EU) 2023/2775.

The preamble states transposition of Delegated Directive (EU) 2023/2775 amending Directive 2013/34/EU as regards size criteria for micro, small, medium and large undertakings or groups.

## 2. The legal technique

The amendment changes the transposition note in L287 and then amends [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 4. Categoriile entităților și grupurilor|art. 4]] by substituting higher MDL amounts.

No employee thresholds are changed in the amendment text. The employee tests remain:

- micro: 10;
- small: 50;
- medium: 250;
- large: above 250 where the large criteria are exceeded;
- small group: 50;
- medium group: 250;
- large group: above 250 where large group criteria are exceeded.

The movement rule in [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 4. Categoriile entităților și grupurilor|L287 art. 4(8)]] remains: an entity or group moves category if, at reporting date, for the last two reporting periods, it exceeds or ceases to exceed the limits of two criteria of its category. But L86 adds a specific transition for 2027.

## 3. New entity thresholds

[[Legea 86-2026 — modificare Legea contabilitatii (text)#2. Articolul 4:|Art. I pct. 2]] substitutes the key amounts:

| Entity category under L287 art. 4 | Old assets | New assets from 2027 | Old sales revenue | New sales revenue from 2027 | Employees |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Micro | MDL 5,600,000 | **MDL 8,500,000** | MDL 11,200,000 | **MDL 17,000,000** | 10 |
| Small | MDL 63,600,000 | **MDL 95,000,000** | MDL 127,200,000 | **MDL 190,000,000** | 50 |
| Medium | MDL 318,000,000 | **MDL 480,000,000** | MDL 636,000,000 | **MDL 960,000,000** | 250 |
| Large | above medium | above **MDL 480,000,000** | above medium | above **MDL 960,000,000** | >250 |

Because the category test is two out of three, the employee number can keep some entities in their old category even if assets and revenue fall below the new line, but for many ordinary companies assets/revenue will drive the result.

## 4. New group thresholds

The same amendment raises group thresholds:

| Group category under L287 art. 4 | Old assets | New assets from 2027 | Old sales revenue | New sales revenue from 2027 | Employees |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Small group | MDL 80,300,000 | **MDL 115,000,000** | MDL 160,600,000 | **MDL 230,000,000** | 50 |
| Medium group | MDL 401,500,000 | **MDL 575,000,000** | MDL 803,100,000 | **MDL 1,150,000,000** | 250 |
| Large group | above medium group | above **MDL 575,000,000** | above medium group | above **MDL 1,150,000,000** | >250 |

This is commercially important because [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 27. Obligația întocmirii situațiilor financiare|L287 art. 27(4)]] exempts small and medium groups from consolidated financial statements and consolidated management reports unless a member is a public-interest entity.

## 5. Entry into force and transitional measurement

[[Legea 86-2026 — modificare Legea contabilitatii (text)#Art. II. – (1) Prezenta lege intră în vigoare la data de 1 ianuarie 2027.|Art. II(1)]] sets entry into force at **1 January 2027**.

[[Legea 86-2026 — modificare Legea contabilitatii (text)#Art. II. – (1) Prezenta lege intră în vigoare la data de 1 ianuarie 2027.|Art. II(2)]] gives the transition:

- entities or groups using the calendar year determine their category at entry into force based on the criteria in L287 art. 4(1)-(7), according to the situation at **31 December 2026**;
- entities or groups using a non-calendar reporting period determine their category according to the situation at the first reporting date after entry into force.

This is not merely prospective classification after two future years. The transition tells entities to classify at the threshold-reset date using the 31 December 2026 position.

## 6. Why many entities drop a category

The thresholds rise by roughly one third to one half depending on category. A company with MDL 80 million sales revenue and MDL 40 million assets was not small under the old small thresholds if combined with employee count or other criteria; under the new small thresholds it is much more likely to be small, and if lower it may be micro.

The categories drive obligations under [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 5. Modul de ținere a contabilității|L287 art. 5]]:

- micro → abridged statements under SNC;
- small → simplified statements under SNC;
- medium/large → complete statements under SNC;
- public-interest entity → IFRS regardless of size.

They also drive management-report and audit exposure under [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 23. Raportul conducerii|L287 art. 23]] and [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 32. Auditul situațiilor financiare|art. 32]].

## 7. Audit consequences

[[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 32. Auditul situațiilor financiare|L287 art. 32]] requires mandatory audit for individual statements of medium and large entities, public-interest entities and other entities under law.

If an entity drops from medium to small from 2027, the statutory audit requirement tied to medium status may disappear, unless another law or sector rule independently requires audit. This can materially reduce cost and timing pressure.

Do not assume "small" means no audit in every case. Banks, insurers, other regulated entities, public-interest entities and entities subject to special legislation may still be audited.

## 8. Reporting package consequences

Under [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 21. Conținutul situațiilor financiare individuale|L287 art. 21]]:

- micro category usually means abridged balance sheet, abridged profit and loss and explanatory note;
- small means balance sheet, profit and loss and explanatory note;
- medium/large means complete package including changes in equity and cash-flow statement.

So the threshold reset affects not only audit but the statement set itself. For management, lenders and investors, losing a cash-flow statement or full notes may reduce available information, even if legal compliance is easier.

## 9. Management report and non-financial reporting

[[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 23. Raportul conducerii|L287 art. 23]] requires medium, large and public-interest entities to prepare a management report. Dropping from medium to small may remove that obligation.

Public-interest entities and large entities with special characteristics may still have non-financial, governance, extractive/forestry state-payment or income-tax disclosure chapters. Those duties must be tested separately and not assumed away solely because thresholds rose.

## 10. Consolidation consequences

For groups, the effect may be even larger. [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 27. Obligația întocmirii situațiilor financiare|L287 art. 27(4)]] exempts small and medium groups from consolidated statements and consolidated management reports unless one affiliate is a public-interest entity.

The 2027 group thresholds therefore may move a group below large-group status and remove consolidation. But a public-interest entity in the group overrides the exemption.

In transaction work, this affects due diligence: historical consolidated accounts may exist, but future consolidated reporting may stop. Deal documents should specify which accounts are required post-closing, not assume statutory consolidation continues.

## 11. Tax consequences are indirect but real

L86 does not amend the Fiscal Code. It does not directly change [[Concept — Impozit pe venit]], [[Concept — TVA]] or [[SFS]] procedure.

But tax effects are indirect:

- the accounting base may be less detailed;
- audit evidence may disappear for entities dropping below mandatory-audit status;
- primary-document and bookkeeping duties remain under [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 11. Documentele primare|L287 art. 11]] and [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 18. Obligațiile și drepturile entităţii|art. 18]];
- [[SFS]] may still require documents and explanations during fiscal control under [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 — text — Titlul V]].

So "lower category" is not "lower tax obligation." It is lower accounting/reporting burden.

## 12. Client action list for 2026

Before 1 January 2027:

1. calculate 2026 assets, sales revenue and average employee count;
2. apply the new L287 art. 4 thresholds as amended by L86;
3. test whether public-interest status overrides the category;
4. test group status and public-interest members;
5. identify changes to statement package under [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 21. Conținutul situațiilor financiare individuale|L287 art. 21]];
6. identify audit changes under [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 32. Auditul situațiilor financiare|L287 art. 32]];
7. review loan covenants, shareholder agreements and grant contracts that require audited or complete statements independently of statute;
8. adjust filing and publication calendar under [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 33. Prezentarea și publicarea situațiilor financiare|L287 art. 33]];
9. document the board / administrator decision on category and reporting package.

## 13. Drafting point for contracts

Contracts often say "audited annual financial statements as required by law." After L86, that wording may stop requiring audit for entities that drop below medium. If audit is commercially required, say so expressly:

- "audited annual financial statements, whether or not statutory audit is mandatory"; or
- "financial statements prepared as a complete SNC package"; or
- "management accounts plus statutory financial statements."

Do not rely on category status where the counterparty is near a threshold.

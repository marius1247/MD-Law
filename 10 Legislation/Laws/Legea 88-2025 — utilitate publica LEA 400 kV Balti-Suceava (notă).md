# Legea nr. 88/2025 — Bălți–Suceava 400 kV public-utility declaration — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 88-2025 — utilitate publica LEA 400 kV Balti-Suceava (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Twin pattern:** Legea 120/2022 (Vulcănești–Chișinău) · **TSO:** Moldelectrica

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> **Core purpose:** Declares **public utility of national interest** for the **Bălți–Suceava 400 kV** interconnection (Phase II MD–RO), modernisation of Bălți 330 kV and construction of Bălți 400 kV — with a **special expropriation procedure** (annex), planning/building-permit derogations, and Moldelectrica as free-of-charge holder of the resulting rights.
> **Primary business risk:** Treating ordinary Cod urbanism / Cod funciar / L107 connection-expropriation sequencing as controlling for parcels on the corridor — this organic law **derogates** across those regimes.

> [!danger] L107 cite in Art. 7
> Art. 7 derogates from **L107/2016 art. 61(2)** (repealed). For 2026 advice remap the underlying expropriation/connection logic to [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)|L164/2025]], but keep LP88 as the special-procedure lex specialis for this Project.

---

## 1. Project frame

* **Works (Art. 3):** LEA 400 kV Bălți–Suceava; modernise Bălți 330 kV; build Bălți 400 kV.
* **Finance:** BEI FI 88495 + EBRD loan 54567 (L104/2024) + EBRD grant 54567 (06.02.2025); ESAP / LARAP under EBRD PRs.
* **Corridor LGAs (Art. 6):** Glodeni / Fălești / Rîșcani communes listed in the act.
* **IF:** publication (15.05.2025).

## 2. Key derogations & mechanics

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Expropriation | Special procedure in **annex** (Art. 11) — L488/1999 largely displaced except arts. 11, 12, 12¹, 14, 15 |
| Sequencing | Works may **precede / run parallel** to expropriation (Art. 12) |
| Planning / build | Urbanism certificate & building permit **without owner consent** (Arts. 8–9); no preliminary EPC where L282/2023 would require it |
| Land | Withdrawal from agricultural/forestry circuit by force of law (Art. 13) — permanent vs temporary |
| Cadastre | IPOT forms parcels without right-holder consent (Art. 14); APP administration after expropriation act |
| Compensation | State budget via energy project PIU (Arts. 16–17); court challenge on quantum within **2 years** (annex) |
| Holder | Moldelectrica — free title for operation (Art. 18+) |
| Freeze | From IF: ban on destination/ownership changes that frustrate the Project; certain prior avisuri/agreements null insofar as conflicting (Arts. 22–23) |

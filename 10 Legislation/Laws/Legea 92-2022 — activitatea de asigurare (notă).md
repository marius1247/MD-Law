# Insurance Activity Law — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 92-2022 — activitatea de asigurare (text)]] ✅ · **Hub:** [[MOC — Drept comercial]] · **Supervisor:** [[Legea 548-1995 — Banca Nationala a Moldovei (notă)|BNM]] · **Banks / bancassurance:** [[Legea 202-2017 — activitatea bancilor (notă)]] · **Challenge:** [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary & commercial impact
> **Core purpose:** Solvency-style insurance/reinsurance statute: NBM licensing, governance (incl. actuarial & outsourcing), technical provisions and eligible assets, MCR/solvency ratio, early intervention, intermediaries, group supervision.
> **Primary business risk:** Underwriting without NBM licence (arts. 9–16) or falling below solvency / MCR (arts. 72–74, 77–79) → suspension/withdrawal and forced liquidation; weak governance/outsourcing (arts. 34–43) → key-function bans and fines under art. 122.

---

## 1. Statutory hierarchy & legal foundation

* **Supervisor = Banca Națională** — express definition in art. 4 (“autoritate de supraveghere”) and organic hook [[Legea 548-1995 — Banca Nationala a Moldovei (notă)]] art. 5(1)(p).
* Civil-law insurance contracts — [[Codul civil 1107-2002 (notă)]]; L160/2011 licensing law does **not** apply to L92 authorisation processes (art. 2(2)).
* Compulsory classes (e.g. MTPL) sit on special statutes; L92 supplies prudential shell (art. 5).
* Challenges to NBM acts → [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]].

## 2. Practical legal mechanics

### Licensing (arts. 9–18)
* General access rules + business plan — arts. 9–11.
* Licensing procedure — [[Legea 92-2022 — activitatea de asigurare (text)#Articolul 10. Procedura de licențiere]]: NBM opinion / licence path with hard clocks; refusal if eligible own funds insufficient for MCR among other grounds (art. 12).
* Suspension — art. 15 (includes solvency-ratio breach below **100%** among grounds); withdrawal — art. 16 (false data, insolvency situations, uncured suspension, etc.); forced liquidation when insolvency tests met — art. 16(13) area.
* Professional participants register — art. 18. Third-country branches — arts. 19–27 (endowment capital, local management ≥3 persons, quality assessment).

### Ownership & perimeter (arts. 29–33)
* Founders/shareholders fitness; qualifying holdings 10%/20%/33%/50% with prior NBM assessment — arts. 29–31.
* Activity limits / naming / seat — arts. 32–33.

### Governance, actuarial, outsourcing (arts. 34–43)
* Solid governance system: clear responsibilities, key functions, internal policies — [[Legea 92-2022 — activitatea de asigurare (text)#Articolul 34. Sistemul de guvernanță]]. Annual review; affiliated-party transactions not on preferential terms (art. 34(8)).
* Board + executive; prior NBM approval of appointments; one professional participant only (with group exception) — art. 35.
* Key functions (risk, compliance, actuarial, internal audit) — art. 36; audit committee art. 37; risk management art. 38; **ORSA** art. 39 (at least annually / on material change); internal control art. 40; internal audit art. 41.
* Actuarial function — [[Legea 92-2022 — activitatea de asigurare (text)#Articolul 42. Funcția actuarială]]: technical provisions, solvency/MCR calculations, underwriting/reinsurance opinions; NBM keeps public actuarial register (art. 42(6)).
* Outsourcing — [[Legea 92-2022 — activitatea de asigurare (text)#Articolul 43. Funcțiile și activitățile externalizate]]: written contracts, ongoing responsibility of insurer, NBM may order termination; material/key-function outsourcing under NBM norms.

### Solvency & assets (arts. 57–79)
* Market-consistent asset/liability valuation — arts. 57–58.
* Eligible assets for technical provisions & MCR — arts. 59–64 (G-bonds, rated foreign sovereigns, listed equities/bonds with concentration caps, bank deposits with NBM-licensed banks, etc.). Assets covering TP and MCR must stay unencumbered (art. 58(5)).
* Technical provisions calculation — arts. 65–70.
* Own funds — art. 71; solvency & financial stability — art. 72; calculation frequency — art. 73; **MCR** — art. 74.
* Deterioration identification, enhanced supervision, recovery/finance plans — arts. 77–79.

### Intermediation (arts. 106–113)
* Agents, bancassurance agents (banks/OCN/AEÎ under mandate), brokers, brokerage assistants — arts. 106–109.
* Broker licensing, suspension/withdrawal — arts. 110–111.

### Supervision & sanctions (arts. 114–122)
* NBM competences and supervisory methods — arts. 115–118.
* Liability and sanctions ladder — art. 122 (warnings, fines, activity restrictions, key-person measures, licence tools — apply text quantum when advising).

## 3. Risk matrix

| Provision | Flaw / ambiguity | Practical risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Art. 4 / L548** | Legacy CNPF forms | Invalid applications | File to **NBM** only |
| **Arts. 10–16** | Dual aviz/licence clocks | Licence lapse / suspension | Project plan against art. 10 deadlines |
| **Arts. 34–36, 42** | Key functions on paper only | Fit-and-proper removal | Staffed actuarial + ORSA evidence |
| **Art. 43** | Cloud/claims outsourcing without NBM path | Ordered contract termination | Pre-notify material outsourcing |
| **Arts. 59–60, 72–74** | Ineligible / concentrated assets | Solvency breach → art. 15/16 | Asset eligibility matrix + concentration caps |
| **Arts. 77–79** | Late deterioration notice | Recovery plan under duress | Early-warning triggers in ALM |
| **Arts. 106–111** | Unlicensed intermediation | Commission clawback / nullity | Verify broker/agent registration |

## 4. Client checklist

- [ ] Confirm NBM is supervisor (art. 4); discard CNPF-era templates.
- [ ] Licence + Register entry before underwriting; capital/MCR file ready (arts. 10–12, 72–74).
- [ ] Prior NBM approval of board/executive and key functions (arts. 35–36).
- [ ] Actuarial function + annual ORSA (arts. 39, 42).
- [ ] Outsourcing register and contracts meet art. 43.
- [ ] Eligible-asset inventory vs arts. 59–60; unencumbered TP/MCR cover.
- [ ] Intermediaries licensed/registered (arts. 106–111); bancassurance mandates documented.
- [ ] Challenge path: [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]] + L548 act rules.

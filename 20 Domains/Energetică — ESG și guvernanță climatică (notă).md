# ESG & climate governance — cross-cutting layer

Step 7 layer C. Architecture: [[Energetică — architecture map]] §4.4. Sits **above** market trading rules: planning, reporting, efficiency obligations, GHG counting, disclosure bridges.

> [!abstract] Job of this note
> Show where **climate / ESG duties** bind energy clients without pretending PNIEC is a licence condition or that HG 10 replaces HANRE. Separate **governance**, **sectoral compliance**, and **corporate disclosure**.

## 2. Governance spine (Plane A)

| Act | Role |
|---|---|
| **HG 10/2024** | Energy & climate **governance mechanism** — PNIEC elaboration/update/evaluation, progress reporting, public participation; five Energy Union dimensions; EnC-adapted Governance Reg. 2018/1999 path |
| **PNIEC (HG 86/2025)** | Integrated targets & measures 2025–2030 (RES shares, EE, decarbonisation measures incl. biofuels, geothermal PM_DC25, H₂ watch, etc.) |
| **L174** | Institutional framework; ANRE independence; policy hooks |
| **L117 / EnC** | Treaty obligation — adapted acquis is the transposition target |

**Advice:** for investment memos and quota fights, pull **current PNIEC measure codes** and HG 10 reporting cycle — then drop into the sectoral act that actually binds (L10 / L139 / L164…).

## 4. Disclosure bridge (Plane C)

Energy clients increasingly need a **story that matches the compliance file**:

- PNIEC / HG 10 targets → strategy and risk sections of annual reports.
- L139 audits, obligated-party payments, EPC savings → operational KPIs.
- Biofuel sustainability certificates / mass balance → supply-chain ESG claims ([[Concept — Criterii de durabilitate biocarburanți]] — counting gate discipline applies to public claims too).
- Accounting non-financial / sustainability reporting: route through [[Contabilitate & raportare financiară — synthesis]] and company-law notes; **vault depth is thinner than energy sectoral law** — do not invent CSRD-equivalent duties from PNIEC alone.

## 5. Risk matrix

| Trap | Advice rule |
|---|---|
| Cite PNIEC as if it were a HANRE | Policy/governance ≠ market rule |
| ESG brochure claims without HG 53 / mass balance | Greenwashing vs counting-gate failure |

| Treat vulnerability fund as climate governance | Social overlay (L241) — adjacent, different statute |

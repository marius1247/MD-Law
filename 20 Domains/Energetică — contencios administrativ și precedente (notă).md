# Energy — administrative litigation, precedents & CSE (Batch 6 dossier)

Cross-act synthesis for challenging ANRE decisions, judicial precedents, and emergency energy decrees. Procedural foundation: [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]] · Hub: [[MOC — Energetică]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> Actions against ANRE fall into two tracks: **individual administrative acts** (mandatory prior complaint, 30-day window) and **normative administrative acts** (direct court filing). Chișinău Court of Appeal holds exclusive first-instance jurisdiction. Art. 214 CA suspension is often more valuable than eventual annulment. During states of emergency, CSE dispositions can override primary energy laws.

---

## 1. Dual administrative remedy pathway

```
                    JUDICIAL REVIEW PATHWAYS
                              │
         ┌────────────────────┴────────────────────┐
         ▼                                         ▼
INDIVIDUAL ADMINISTRATIVE ACTS              NORMATIVE ADMINISTRATIVE ACTS
(licences, tariffs, sanctions, BRP disputes)  (market rules, methodologies)
• Mandatory cerere prealabilă (30 days)       • NO prior complaint (CA art. 163)
• Filed with ANRE Administration Board        • Direct filing: CA Chișinău
• ANRE has 30 days to reply                   • Review of legality / proportionality
```

### 1.1 Prior complaint procedure (*cerere prealabilă*)

| Element | Rule | Citation |
|---|---|---|
| Filing window | 30 calendar days from notification/publication | CA art. 164–165 |
| Competent body | ANRE Administration Board | — |
| Automatic suspension | **No** — unless ANRE orders stay or court grants art. 214 | CA art. 162 |
| ANRE response | 30 days; silence = right to sue | CA art. 167–168 |
| Court deadline | 30 days from rejection or expiry of admin window | CA art. 209 |

### 1.2 Court jurisdiction & suspension

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| First instance | **Chișinău Court of Appeal** — exclusive for ANRE disputes (CA art. 191) |
| Suspension standard | Imminent irreparable harm **or** serious doubt on legality (CA art. 214) |
| Expedited hearing | Often ex-parte or within 5 days |
| Effect of stay | Freezes enforcement including bank guarantee drawdowns and fine collection |

## 2. Judicial precedents matrix

*Note: case references below reflect established doctrinal trends cited in Law House analysis. Populate with specific docket numbers as cases are identified in the vault.*

| Category | Contested action | Primary finding | Precedential value |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tariff indexation** | ANRE delays annual recalculation despite >5% cost fluctuation | ANRE obligated to adjust within statutory timelines; failure = illegal inaction | **Binding benchmark** |
| **Financial deviations** | ANRE refuses to incorporate accumulated FX/commodity losses | Cost-reflectivity principle (L174 art. 3) binds ANRE; arbitrary deferral violates art. 46 Constitution | **Binding benchmark** |
| **ANRE sanctions** | Maximum turnover fines (5–10%) for reporting errors | Proportionality (CA arts. 21–29) requires proof of necessity; courts reduce to minimum or void | **High persuasive** |
| **BRP imbalance billing** | TSO charges based on unverified telemetry | TSO bears burden of proof; unverified SCADA logs insufficient | **High persuasive** |
| **Grid access denial** | DSO refuses connection citing congestion | DSO must offer conditional access or detail expansion plans; blanket refusal illegal | **High persuasive** |
| **Bank guarantee execution** | ANRE executes guarantee during force majeure / grid delay | Stay granted under art. 214 when delay stems from third-party authority | **Critical litigation tool** |
| **License revocation** | ANRE revokes without court order | Revocation requires judicial proceedings; ANRE cannot execute unilaterally (L174 art. 15) | **Binding benchmark** |
| **Prior complaint forfeiture** | Plaintiff misses 30-day window | CSJ consistently dismisses actions without timely cerere prealabilă | **Binding benchmark** |

## 3. CSE emergency energy decrees

**Statutory basis:** Law 212/2004 (state of emergency) · CSE Dispositions (*Dispozițiile CSE*)

### 3.1 Emergency hierarchy

```
REGULAR REGIME                          EMERGENCY CSE REGIME
• L164, L108, L174                      • CSE Dispositions
• ANRE HANRE acts                       • Prevail over conflicting laws
• Public consultations (15–30 days)     • Effective immediately
• Full judicial appeals                 • Ultra-fast execution via ANRE/Ministry
```

### 3.2 Core emergency measures

| Measure | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Centralised procurement** | Energocom designated sole state trader; mandatory procurement through emergency contracts |
| **Fast-track tariffs** | ANRE adjusts retail tariffs within 2–5 business days; overrides public hearing rules |
| **Customs/tax derogations** | VAT deductions, duty exemptions, expedited cross-border transit for strategic storage |
| **Price caps** | Emergency margin compression on suppliers and traders |

### 3.3 CSE litigation strategy

| Risk | Strategy |
|---|---|
| Contract breach driven by CSE orders | Force majeure / hardship (*impreviziune*) under Civil Code art. 904 |
| Unlawful margin compression | Build audit files for post-emergency damages claims against State |
| Judicial review limits | Direct challenge at CA Chișinău (proportionality under L212/2004) or constitutional exception (*excepția de neconstituționalitate*) |

## 4. Risk matrix

| Risk | Basis | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Missed 30-day prior complaint deadline | CA arts. 164, 209 | **CRITICAL** — forfeiture of judicial review | Log receipt/publication dates; calendar alerts |
| Fine enforcement during lawsuit | L174 art. 29; CA art. 214 | Immediate financial harm | File suspension motion simultaneously with main claim |
| Dismissal for lack of standing | CA art. 17; L174 | Case inadmissible | Demonstrate direct impairment of statutory rights |
| Following ANRE regulation deadline over CA | Conflicting appeal periods in HANRE acts | Loss of rights | Always apply 30-day CA art. 162 rule as overriding |
| CSE procurement overriding contracts | CSE Dispositions | Supply chain disruption | Formal force majeure notice; document unrecovered costs |

## 5. Court filing sequence (practitioner playbook)

```
[STEP 1] Receipt of contested ANRE decision
  └─ Audit for formal invalidity (reasoning, hearing, competence)

[STEP 2] Cerere prealabilă to ANRE (within 30 days)
  └─ Detail procedural and substantive violations
  └─ Request voluntary administrative stay

[STEP 3] Urgent art. 214 CA suspension motion
  └─ File at Chișinău Court of Appeal with financial harm evidence
  └─ Obtain încheiere staying execution

[STEP 4] Main action in contentious administrative matters
  └─ Request annulment + cost recovery
  └─ 30-day deadline from admin rejection/expiry
```

*Skeleton for adaptation — not legal advice.*

```
CĂTRE CURTEA DE APEL CHIȘINĂU
Secția Contencios Administrativ și Fiscal

RECLAMANT: [Denumirea Societății], IDNO [...], sediu [...]
PÂRÂT: AGENȚIA NAȚIONALĂ PENTRU REGLEMENTARE ÎN ENERGETICĂ (ANRE)

CERERE DE SUSPENDARE A EXECUTĂRII ACTULUI ADMINISTRATIV INDIVIDUAL
(în temeiul Art. 214 din Codul Administrativ nr. 116/2018)

I. OBIECTUL CERERII:
Suspendarea executării Hotărârii Consiliului de Administrație al ANRE nr. [...]
din [Data], prin care s-a dispus [sancțiune / retragere licență / tarif], până la
soluționarea definitivă a cauzei pe fond.

II. MOTIVARE:
• Îndoiala serioasă privind legalitatea: încălcarea Art. 29 L174/2017 și
  principiului proporționalității (CA art. 21–29)...
• Paguba iminentă și ireparabilă: executarea imediată conduce la executare silită
  a conturilor bancare în sumă de [...] MDL, generând insolvabilitate imediată...

III. DISPOZITIV SOLICITAT:
Admiterea cererii și suspendarea executării Hotărârii ANRE nr. [...] până la
pronunțarea unei hotărâri judecătorești irevocabile.
```

## 8. Client action checklist

- [ ] Log exact receipt/publication dates of all ANRE decisions for 30-day deadline calculation.
- [ ] File cerere prealabilă within 30 days for every individual administrative act challenge.
- [ ] Simultaneously prepare art. 214 suspension motion with financial audit evidence.
- [ ] Request and analyse the full administrative file before drafting court pleadings.
- [ ] For tariff disputes: plead cost-reflectivity under L174 art. 3, not merely commercial preference.
- [ ] During CSE emergency: serve formal force majeure notices; document all unrecovered costs for post-emergency claims.
- [ ] Use qualified advanced electronic signature for all ANRE electronic petitions.

# Coal — regulatory gap stub

Step 4 companion to [[MOC — Piața produselor petroliere]]. Honest map of **where coal sits** in Moldovan energy law — and where it does **not**.

> [!abstract] Verdict
> Moldova does **not** have a dedicated ANRE-regulated **coal market statute** analogous to [[Legea 461-2001 — piata produselor petroliere (notă)|L461]] (petroleum), [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)|L108]] (gas) or [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)|L164]] (electricity). Coal appears as a **minor imported solid fuel** in energy balances and as a **state-reserve / environmental / customs** object — not as a licensed wholesale/retail energy market with ANRE price caps or network codes.

| Evidence | Reading |
|---|---|

| No `Legea … cărbune` / HANRE coal market rules in vault | No primary “coal market” act ingested; none flagged in [[MOC — Energetică]] tier-1 list |
| [[HG 677-2024 — plan sezon incalzire 2024-2025 (notă)\|HG 677]] planning annex | Mentions completing **state reserves** of păcură **and coal** — material-reserves logic, not ANRE market regulation |
| L174 / L164 / L108 / L461 | Organise electricity, gas, petroleum, heat — **not** a coal commodity market |

## 2. Likely legal homes (outside energy-market cascade)

If a coal matter arises, route it here first — then confirm at source:

| Issue | Probable regime |
|---|---|
| Import / customs classification | [[Codul vamal 95-2021 (notă)\|Customs Code]] + fiscal ([[Concept — Accize]] if applicable) |
| Industrial use / emissions / waste | Environmental law *(thin in this vault)* |
| State strategic stocks | Material reserves / MAI instruments (HG 677-type) |
| Heating fuel in buildings | Building / local heat practice; district heat remains [[Legea 92-2014 — energia termica si cogenerarea (notă)\|L92]] |
| Competition / trade | General commercial / competition law |

Do **not** advise clients from L461 or L108 by analogy without an explicit statutory hook.

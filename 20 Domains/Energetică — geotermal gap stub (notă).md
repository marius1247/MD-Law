# Geothermal — regulatory gap stub

Step 5 companion to [[MOC — Piața energiei termice]]. Honest classification of **where geothermal sits** in Moldovan energy law — and where a dedicated regime is still missing.

> [!abstract] Verdict
> There is **no standalone geothermal market law** and **no ANRE geothermal wholesale/retail market**. Geothermal energy is defined and counted as **RES** under [[Legea 10-2016 — surse regenerabile (notă)|L10/2016]]. If heat is commercialised through a **SACET**, [[Legea 92-2014 — energia termica si cogenerarea (notă)|L92/2014]] applies. Deep / dedicated normative framework is an explicit **PNIEC watch item** (PM_DC25) — not yet a filled market hub.

## 1. Classification map

| Situation | Legal home | Not |
|---|---|---|
| Definition + RES statistics (incl. HP geothermal heat when efficiency test met) | **L10** art. 2 (*energie geotermală*) · art. 3(6) · [[HG 74-2025 — calcul consum energie regenerabila (notă)\|HG 74/2025]] | A commodity market statute |
| Shallow geothermal / heat-pump **installer** qualification & safety supervision | L10 arts. 13, 23 · ANRE as energy-supervision body for install rules | L92 licensing of “geothermal traders” |
| Heat sold into / through **centralised district heat** | **L92** (SACET production/distribution/supply licences + tariffs); RES integration art. 15¹ | A separate geothermal tariff code |
| Building-level geothermal HP outside SACET | Building / installer / general commercial practice + L10 RES accounting | Fake “geothermal market segments” |
| Policy gap / future framework | [[HG 86-2025 — PNIEC 2025-2030 (notă)\|PNIEC]] **PM_DC25** — assess potential + **define normative framework** (2025–2030) | Treat PM_DC25 as already-transposed law |

## 2. What the corpus shows

| Evidence | Reading |
|---|---|
| L10 defines *energie geotermală* as heat stored under the solid surface | RES taxonomy, not market design |
| L10 installer register includes **sisteme geotermale de mică adâncime** and heat pumps | Skills / safety gate |
| L92 art. 15¹ promotes RES heat into SACET; efficient-system criteria ≥20 MW | District-heat pathway if connected |
| PNIEC PM_DC25 | Explicit admission that **normative framework for valorisation** still needs defining; MD geothermal resources small, mainly building heat |
| Related PNIEC measure on HP integration into SACET | Technology pathway (geothermal / water-water / waste heat) under heat policy, not a new market law |
| No `Legea … geotermal` / HANRE geothermal market rules in vault | Silence = stub, not “regulated like gas” |

## 3. Practitioner routing

| Client question | First stop |
|---|---|
| Is my heat pump “renewable” for targets / reporting? | L10 + HG 74 efficiency test |
| Who may install shallow geothermal / HP? | L10 installer regulation + ANRE supervision |
| Can I inject geothermal / RES heat into district heat? | L92 art. 15¹ + SACET licence-holder *aviz de racordare* |
| Is there a geothermal licence / feed-in tariff? | **Not evidenced** as a dedicated regime — do not invent one from L10 definitions alone |
| Deep geothermal project / mining-style rights? | Outside current energy-market cascade — watch PM_DC25; check environmental / subsurface regimes at source |

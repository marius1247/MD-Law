# Hydrogen — regulatory gap stub

Step 6 companion to [[MOC — Biocarburanți și combustibili din biomasă]]. Honest map of **where hydrogen sits** in Moldovan energy law — and where a dedicated regime is still missing.

> [!abstract] Verdict
> Moldova has **no dedicated hydrogen market statute**, no ANRE hydrogen network code, and no licensed “hydrogen TSO/DSO” catalogue analogous to L164/L108. Hydrogen appears as: (1) a **RES accounting / GO** object under [[Legea 10-2016 — surse regenerabile (notă)|L10]]; (2) an **RFNBO GHG floor** under [[HG 53-2025 — durabilitate biocarburanti emisii GES (notă)|HG 53]] pct. 2; (3) **infrastructure project definitions** in [[Legea 174-2017 — energetica (notă)|L174]] (CE interest); (4) a **PNIEC / strategy watch-list** (green H₂, sector coupling). Treat as **watch-list vector** — not a filled market hub.

## 1. Classification map

| Situation | Legal home | Not |
|---|---|---|
| Renewable H₂ / RFNBO in **transport GHG** | HG 53 **pct. 2** (≥ **70%** GHG reduction from **1 Jan 2026**); Annex 1 energy content | A biofuel feedstock rule |
| Double-counting avoidance in RES share | L10 art. 7(2) — renewable H₂ counted **once** | Separate H₂ quota statute |
| Guarantees of origin (gas incl. hydrogen) | L10 GO provisions (art. area 37+; GO may refer to electricity, gas **including hydrogen**, or heating/cooling) | Live H₂ GO market operational depth TBD |
| Projects of energy-community interest (H₂ pipes, storage, reception, end-use) | L174 definitions for hydrogen infrastructure in CE-project vocabulary | Domestic H₂ market design |
| E-mobility fuel-cell vehicles | [[Legea 139-2018 — eficienta energetica (notă)\|L139]] e-mobility definition | H₂ refuelling market law |
| Policy ambition (green H₂, sector coupling, GO extension) | [[HG 86-2025 — PNIEC 2025-2030 (notă)\|PNIEC]] · Energy Strategy 2050 concept references | Transposed market package |

## 2. What the corpus shows

| Evidence | Reading |
|---|---|
| No `Legea … hidrogen` / HANRE hydrogen market rules in vault | Primary **silence** on market organisation |
| L10 + HG 53 RFNBO floor | Compliance/accounting hooks arrive **before** market design |
| L174 H₂ infrastructure definitions | Forward-looking CE/PCI-style vocabulary — not a domestic licensing catalogue |
| PNIEC | Explicit future: green H₂ as gas alternative; “rules for sector coupling and hydrogen market integration”; GO framework to extend to gas/biogas **including hydrogen** |
| EnC / EU | EU hydrogen & decarbonised-gas package + RED RFNBO rules create **acquis pressure**; MD has started with RFNBO GHG and definitions, not with unbundling/TPA for H₂ networks |

## 3. Practitioner routing

| Client question | First stop |
|---|---|
| Can my RFNBO / renewable H₂ count in transport? | HG 53 pct. 2 (≥70% from 1 Jan 2026) + methodology path; do not use biofuel Annex 2 feedstocks by analogy |
| Is there a hydrogen supply / network licence? | **Not evidenced** — do not invent from L108/L164 by analogy |
| GO for hydrogen? | L10 GO vocabulary contemplates gas incl. H₂ — check current GO regulation scope before promising tradable H₂ GOs |
| Blending H₂ into natural gas network? | Gas L108 + network code first; no dedicated H₂ blending statute identified in vault |
| Project finance / CE interest label | L174 CE-project hydrogen definitions + EnC/EU PCI pathways |
| Strategy / subsidy narrative | PNIEC + state-aid (L139/2012) — policy, not market rules |

# Incentives & support schemes — cross-cutting layer

Step 7 layer B. Architecture: [[Energetică — architecture map]] §4.4. Does **not** replace market hubs — support changes **who gets paid what**, not which wholesale segment exists.

> [!abstract] Job of this note
> Map the **revenue / cost overlays** that sit on top of electricity, heat, petroleum, and efficiency: RES support, FCEE, network-access PSO, efficiency obligation / FEE, vulnerability fund, biofuel ceiling prices.

## 2. RES support — practitioner rules

1. **Status first:** without [[Concept — Producător eligibil|eligible-producer]] status, no fixed tariff / auction support — plant is an ordinary market participant (possibly active consumer / prosumer instead).
2. **Quota is the scarce asset:** HG 599 (and predecessors) — timing and queue beat “merit” narratives.
3. **Two tracks:** small → administrative fixed tariff (375); large → competitive tender against ceiling price. Thresholds change — pull current HG.
4. **FCEE is the commercial hinge:** art. 87(2) mandatory buy/resell + supplier purchase by retail share. RES path sunsets on **CfD** migration (art. 150(3)). Urban CHP path needs ANRE **exit calendar** (art. 150(4)) — calendar ≠ automatic cut-off.
5. **Do not conflate** net billing, eligible-producer support, and active-consumer market sales — [[Energetică — prosumer vs consumator activ (notă)]].
6. **Connection / curtailment** often destroy more value than tariff level — [[Concept — Racordare la rețea]] · flexible connection · redispach rules in L164.

## 4. Risk matrix

| Trap | Advice rule |
|---|---|
| Support opinion from L10 alone | Always add L164 market / BRP / FCEE / connection |
| Perpetual FCEE RES offtake | Art. 150(3) CfD sunset |
| Invent urban-CHP FCEE end date | Only ANRE calendar under art. 150(4) |
| Net billing = eligible producer | Different quotas and economics |
| Cite repealed HANRE 24 for support procurement | Market-based purchase + [[Legea 74-2020 — achizitii sectoriale (notă)\|L74]] |
| Vulnerability advice from L241 text alone | Check LP255 + live Government programmes |
| Biofuel *preț plafon* = pump max price | Ceiling is local-biofuel purchase economics; pump max is L461 art. 4 |

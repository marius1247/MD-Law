# Energy — licensing, cross-border capacity & REMIT (Batch 5 dossier)

Cross-act synthesis for market entry, cross-border interconnection, and wholesale market integrity. Hub: [[MOC — Energetică]] · Concept: [[Concept — Licență în energetică]]

> [!abstract] Executive summary
> No commercial energy activity in Moldova may proceed without an [[ANRE]] licence. Cross-border electricity capacity is allocated through coordinated auctions with Romania and Ukraine. REMIT (transposed via L164/2025 and L108/2016) imposes registration, inside-information publication, and market-manipulation prohibitions on all wholesale participants.

---

## 1. ANRE licensing framework

**Statutory basis:** [[Legea 174-2017 — energetica (notă)]] arts. 12–20 · [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)]] arts. 12–20 · [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)]] arts. 12–18

### 1.1 Mandatory licence categories

```
              ANRE MANDATORY ENERGY LICENSES
                            │
     ┌──────────────────────┼──────────────────────┐
     ▼                      ▼                      ▼
ELECTRICITY              NATURAL GAS            RENEWABLES
• Generation (≥1 MW)     • Transmission (TSO)   • Generation (25-yr)
• Transmission (TSO)     • Distribution (DSO)   • Biogas to grid
• Distribution (DSO)     • Storage (SSO)        • Thermal RES
• Supply / Trading       • Wholesale supply
• Storage                • CNG stations
• Aggregation (L164)
```

### 1.2 Qualification prerequisites

1. **Legal entity** registered in Moldova
2. **Financial soundness** — audited statements or liquidity proof
3. **Technical capacity** — infrastructure ownership/control; certified personnel
4. **Reputational integrity** — directors/UBOs clear of financial/economic convictions

### 1.3 Modification, suspension, revocation

| Action | Trigger | Maximum duration / effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Modification** (*reperfectare*) | Name, address, asset list change | 10 business days to file |
| **Suspension** | Non-compliance, fee non-payment, reporting failure | 2–6 months to cure |
| **Revocation** | Fraud, repeated suspension, insolvency, grid instability | Judicial proceedings required (L174 art. 15) |

### 1.4 Licensing timeline

| Phase | Duration | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Preparation | Day 1 | Compile declaration, financials, technical specs, UBO clearance |
| Completeness check | Days 2–15 | ANRE verifies; 10-day cure if incomplete |
| Assessment | Days 16–30 | Financial, technical, UBO review; site inspection |
| Issuance | Days 30–45 | Board resolution; licence upon fee payment |

## 2. Cross-border capacity allocation

**Legal basis:** HANRE 424/2019 (not yet ingested as text — summary from Law House dossier) · [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)]] art. 52

### 2.1 Border architecture

| Border | Platform | Timeframes | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MD–RO** | Transelectrica S.A. | Annual, monthly, daily, intraday | Explicit coordinated auctions |
| **MD–UA** | NPC Ukrenergo | Annual, monthly, daily | Explicit coordinated auctions |

### 2.2 Capacity products

| Product | Principle | Effect of non-nomination |
|---|---|---|
| **Long-term** (annual/monthly) | UIOLI | Capacity returned to daily pool without compensation |
| **Short-term** (daily/intraday) | UIOSI | Unnominated PTRs monetised at daily clearing price |

### 2.3 Firmness & curtailment compensation

- Allocated cross-border capacity is **legally firm**
- If TSO curtails before D-1 nomination deadline: reimbursement = auction clearing price per MW/h curtailed
- Emergency curtailment without compensation is challengeable — verify formal State of Emergency declaration

## 3. REMIT framework

**Statutory basis:** L108/2016 arts. 94¹–94⁶ · L164/2025 (REMIT transposition) · HANRE 26/2023 (wholesale participant registry) · EU Regulation 1227/2011

### 3.1 Three pillars

```
                    REMIT REGULATORY PILLARS
                              │
       ┌──────────────────────┼──────────────────────┐
       ▼                      ▼                      ▼
MANDATORY REGISTRATION   PROHIBITION OF ABUSE    INSIDE INFORMATION
• ANRE registry          • Insider trading        • UMM publication
• Prerequisite for       • Market manipulation    • Before trading
  wholesale trading        • Attempted manipulation • ACER-compliant venues
```

### 3.2 Inside information (UMM)

- **Definition:** Non-public, precise information that could significantly affect wholesale energy product prices if published
- **Mandatory content:** Planned/unplanned outages of generation, storage, interconnectors, demand shifts
- **Timeline:** Publish **before** engaging in wholesale transactions
- **Venues:** Inside Information Platforms (IIPs) or compliant public website portals

### 3.3 Penalty matrix

| Violation | Basis | Penalty |
|---|---|---|
| Failure to register | HANRE 26/2023; L108 art. 94² | Suspension from trading; fines up to 5% turnover |
| Failure to publish UMM | L108 art. 94³; REMIT art. 4 | Fines up to 10% turnover; director sanctions |
| Market manipulation / insider trading | L108 art. 94⁴; Criminal Code | Up to 10% turnover; licence revocation; criminal referral |

## 4. Risk matrix

| Risk | Basis | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unlicensed commercial operations | L174 art. 29; Criminal Code art. 241 | **CRITICAL** — criminal exposure | Audit all delivery points against licence perimeter |
| UBO change without ANRE notification | L164/L108 licensing articles | Automatic licence voidance risk | Prior ANRE approval for >10% ownership changes |
| UIOLI capacity loss | HANRE 424/2019 | Lost allocation rights without compensation | Automate Physical Notification submittals before cut-off |
| REMIT registration gap | HANRE 26/2023 | Immediate trading suspension | Register before any commercial transaction notification |
| Emergency TSO curtailment | HANRE 424/2019 art. 45 | Uncompensated capacity loss | Challenge; verify Force Majeure / CSE declaration validity |

## 6. Client action checklist

- [ ] Map all commercial activities to required licence categories (including new L164 activities: storage, aggregation, trading).
- [ ] Register in ANRE wholesale participant registry under HANRE 26/2023 before any wholesale transaction.
- [ ] Implement UMM protocol: designate compliance officer; automate SCADA outage → public portal alerts.
- [ ] For cross-border traders: maintain LC buffers on Transelectrica and Ukrenergo portals.
- [ ] Establish trade surveillance policies and information barriers between trading and asset management desks.
- [ ] On licence suspension: file urgent art. 214 CA suspension motion to maintain operations during court review.

# Prosumers & active consumers — cross-cutting layer

Step 7 layer A. **Boundary taxonomy (do not re-derive):** [[Energetică — prosumer vs consumator activ (notă)]]. Electricity hub: [[MOC — Piața de energie electrică]] · Architecture: [[Energetică — architecture map]] §4.4.

> [!abstract] Job of this note
> Operational map of **who may self-generate, store, share, sell, or net-bill** — and which procedure stack applies. The boundary note answers “which status?”; this note answers “which acts and which traps?”

---

## 1. Status boxes (quick map)

| Status | Home | Revenue / settlement | Typical gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Consumator activ** | L164 arts. 122–123 | Market / bilaterals / flexibility / sharing | Not main business; BRP; ANRE capacity cap by technology |
| **Prosumer (facturare netă)** | L10 + [[Concept — Facturare netă]] | Monetary netting vs bill | HG quota + individual ceiling · connection · supplier annex |
| **CEC member / CEC** | L164 arts. 124–126 · [[Concept — Comunitate de energie a cetățenilor]] | Collective self-consume / share / sell | ANRE register; non-profit-primary |
| **Eligible producer** | L10 · [[Concept — Producător eligibil]] | Support price / FCEE (→ CfD) | Quota / auction — **not** the prosumer track |
| **Storage operator** | L164 · [[Concept — Stocare a energiei]] | Market + services | Licence if ≥ **1 MW** autonomous injection |

Art. **122(6) lock-in:** entering L10 net billing **narrows** the active-consumer toolbox. Decision tree → boundary note §3.

## 2. Procedure stacks

### 2.1 Active consumer (no net billing)

1. Confirm purpose filter (not main commercial activity) — L164 art. 122.
2. Connection under [[HANRE 311-2026 — racordarea la retelele electrice (notă)|HANRE 311]] (legacy designs may still sit under pre-19.08.2025 rules — architecture §1.3).
3. Check ANRE technology capacity cap (art. 122(5)).
4. Arrange **PRE** or delegation (art. 122(3)) — self-consumption does not erase imbalance duty.
5. If storage ≥1 MW autonomous injection → storage licence.
6. Energy sharing (art. 123) = **not supply**, no supply licence — but ANRE sharing regulation still expected.
7. Flexible connection option → [[Concept — Racordare la rețea]] (curtailment priced in the deal).

### 2.2 Prosumer / facturare netă

1. Confirm aggregate quota + **individual ceiling** for the connection date: HG 401 (to 31.12.2025) → [[HG 599-2025 — limite capacitate regenerabile 2030 (notă)|HG 599]] Anexa 2 (to 2030).
2. Connection (HANRE 311) within ceilings.
3. Supplier–prosumer contractual annex: [[HANRE 169-2019 — furnizarea energiei electrice (notă)|HANRE 169]] Anexa 5 via [[HANRE 833-2023 — modificarea unor hotarari ANRE regenerabile (notă)|833/2023]].
4. Expect BRP inside **supplier** perimeter (HANRE 853 / market rules) — not a free active-consumer trading desk.
5. Warn on art. 122(6): other active-consumer activities constrained once on net billing.

### 2.3 CEC

1. Legal form + non-profit-primary filter (L164).
2. **ANRE register** (art. 125) — without registration, CEC rights do not attach.
3. Licences when catalogue thresholds hit (generation / storage / supply / aggregation).
4. Members may individually be active consumers or prosumers — status stacks per person/site.

## 4. Risk matrix

| Trap | Advice rule |
|---|---|
| “Every rooftop is a prosumer” | Need L10 net-billing enrolment + quota room |
| Eligible-producer advice for net-billing clients | Wrong track — different quota, offtake, balancing |
| Ignore BRP on self-consumption | Art. 122(3) still assigns imbalance |
| Cite HG 401 after its envelope | Check HG 599 for connection date |
| CEC as branding without register | No register → no CEC rights |
| HANRE 169 FUO 90 days | Prefer L164 art. 115 ≥6 months (architecture §1.3) |
| Sharing / CEC / aggregation regs missing | Statute-first; do not invent secondary procedure |

**Incomplete / pending secondary:** energy-sharing regulation (art. 123); CEC register detail (art. 125); aggregation regulation (art. 120(9)); supply-reg full L164 re-adoption; HG 599 annex completeness flags in concept note.

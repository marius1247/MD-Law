## Key provisions & 2026 updates

### A. RES integration & prosumers (HANRE 311/2026)
- Streamlined connection for PV installations (net billing under [[Legea 10-2016 — surse regenerabile (notă)]]).
- Connection permit within **10 calendar days** for small installations (<50 kW).
- Energy storage systems regulated at consumption/generation point.

### B. Balancing responsibility (HANRE 283/2020 + 383/2026)
- Every market participant financially responsible for deviations → [[Concept — Parte responsabilă de echilibrare]].
- HANRE 383/2026 refines BRP establishment, imbalance settlement, and cross-border compensation.

### C. Quality & disconnection (HANRE 169/2019)
- Non-payment disconnection only after **10 calendar days** advance notice.
- Supplier must pay automatic compensation for unplanned outages exceeding SAIDI/SAIFI thresholds.

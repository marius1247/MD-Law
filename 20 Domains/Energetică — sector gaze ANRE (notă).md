> [!abstract] Executive summary
> The gas regulatory framework transposes the EU Third Energy Package: consumer protection, TSO/DSO unbundling, and non-discriminatory third-party access. Three operational layers: **connection** (112), **supply** (113), **network operation & balancing** (420 + 2025/2026 amendments).

## Key legal mechanisms

### A. Connection rights (HANRE 112/2019)
- DSO/TSO must issue connection permit within **15 calendar days** of complete application (10 working days for final consumers; 30 calendar days for producers/storage).
- Applicant pays only ANRE-approved connection tariff; unjustified additional works prohibited.
- Demarcation point: typically at safety valve outlet or regulation-metering station (SRM).

### B. Consumer protection & supplier switching (HANRE 113/2019)
- Supplier switching is **free** and must complete within **21 calendar days** (3 weeks).
- Supplier of Last Resort (SoLR): automatic takeover for up to **3 months** when primary supplier fails.
- Disconnection prohibited on Fridays, weekends, public holidays, or day before; requires **5 working days** written notice.

### C. Capacity & balancing (HANRE 420/2019 + 328/2025 + 310/2026)
- Nomination/renomination regime aligned with ENTSOG standards.
- 2025/2026 amendments optimise daily imbalance calculation and penalty billing.

> [!danger] Legal warning
> Contractual clauses limiting the consumer's right to terminate for supplier switching are **absolutely void** under art. 61 [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (text)]] and HANRE 113/2019.

## Risk matrix

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| SoLR tariff spike on supplier failure | Critical cost surge | Monitor contract expiry 60 days ahead |
| Connection cost shifting | Unlawful capex on applicant | Challenge via [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]] |
| Imbalance cash-out spread | Trading margin erosion | Real-time flow monitoring per [[HANRE 534-2019 — Regulile pietei gazelor naturale (notă)]] |
| PSO withdrawal for large industrials (2026) | Market tariff exposure | Review supply contracts before 01.04.2026 |

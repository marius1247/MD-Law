# Thermal / CHP market segments — regulatory briefs

Step 5 for [[MOC — Piața energiei termice]]. Frame: [[Legea 92-2014 — energia termica si cogenerarea (notă)|L92/2014]] · Supply: [[HANRE 23-2017 — furnizarea energiei termice (notă)|HANRE 23/2017]] · HE quantities: [[HG 197-2025 — metodologie cogenerare inalta eficienta (notă)|HG 197/2025]].

---

## A. Segment briefs

### 1. Heat production

| | |
|---|---|
| **What** | Commercial production of thermal energy into a centralised system (boiler / CHP / RES heat plant) |
| **Who** | Licensed **producător** / unitate termoenergetică |
| **Key rules** | Arts. 18–19(1)(a), 34; licence needs plant in ownership/use for term (art. 20(2)(b)) |
| **Practice** | Production is a **licensed public-service activity**, not a free commodity desk. CHP plants also face electricity-side duties under L164. |

### 2. Heat distribution (network monopoly)

| | |
|---|---|
| **What** | Operation of heat networks; losses; physical delivery to delimitation |
| **Who** | Licensed **distribuitor** |
| **Key rules** | Arts. 19(1)(b), 36, 38; Cap. VI protection zones / land use; tariff recognition of network losses (art. 45) |
| **Practice** | Local natural-monopoly economics. Do not import electricity TPA vocabulary without an L92 hook. |

### 3. Heat supply (retail / public service)

| | |
|---|---|
| **What** | Supply at the **delimitation point** under regulated contract |
| **Who** | Licensed **furnizor**; consumer or condominium association as intermediary |
| **Key rules** | Arts. 19(1)(c), 39–44 · [[HANRE 23-2017 — furnizarea energiei termice (notă)\|HANRE 23]] Annex 1 |
| **Practice** | Continuity, metering, disconnection notice, uneconomic withdrawal (≥6 months before 15 Oct). **Regulation body missing** in vault — do not advise season rules from the contract annex alone. |

### 4. Regulated tariffs

| | |
|---|---|
| **What** | ANRE-approved prices/tariffs for heat production, distribution, supply (to delimitation) and regulated electricity production prices from CHP |
| **Who** | ANRE · licensed undertakings |
| **Key rules** | Art. 45 · [[Concept — Tarif reglementat]]; optional capacity + energy components (art. 45(5)); supernormal losses out |
| **Practice** | Public-law price. Disputes are administrative / contencios, not ordinary commercial price fights. |

### 5. High-efficiency cogeneration + guarantees of origin

| | |
|---|---|
| **What** | Classification of CHP as **înaltă eficiență** and certification of GO-eligible electricity quantities |
| **Who** | CHP producers · ANRE · GO procedure (HANRE 201/2017 pending ingest) |
| **Key rules** | L92 Cap. IV · annexes 1–2 · art. 14 · [[HG 197-2025 — metodologie cogenerare inalta eficienta (notă)\|HG 197]] · [[Concept — Cogenerare de înaltă eficiență]] |
| **Practice** | HE status is a **measurement + threshold** problem first; GO filing second. Verify formulas against official PDF (upload placeholders). |

### 6. CHP electricity → electricity market

| | |
|---|---|
| **What** | Electricity half of CHP output sold / scheduled on the power market |
| **Who** | CHP producer · OST/OSD · (transitional) [[Concept — Furnizor central de energie electrică\|FCEE]] for urban district-heating CHP |
| **Key rules** | L92 art. 15 (network access; still cites L107 — read via L164) · L164 FCEE transitional exit · [[MOC — Piața de energie electrică]] |
| **Practice** | Heat continuity can constrain plant dispatch; electricity settlement follows L164/HANRE 283 — **two statutes, one plant**. L92 art. 15 still cites L107 — **apply L164**. Redispach: L164 prefers avoiding downward redispach of **urban CHP** in the cold season where heat continuity would be affected. FCEE offtake for urban CHP is transitional — art. **150(4)** calendar. HE status for GOs ≠ FCEE offtake right. |

### 7. Efficient SACET / RES / storage integration

| | |
|---|---|
| **What** | Duty path for **efficient** centralised heat/cooling systems ≥ **20 MW** thermal, RES heat uptake, thermal storage, waste-to-energy heat |
| **Who** | Unități termoenergetice · ANRE list of efficient systems (art. 15¹(6)) |
| **Key rules** | Arts. 15¹–15³; criteria to **31 Dec 2029** / from **1 Jan 2030**; condominium RES heat right + *aviz de racordare* |
| **Practice** | Investment planning + tariff recognition (storage / WtE after cost-benefit demonstrated to ANRE). Environment Agency authorisation for incineration (L209/2016). Condominium RES heat integration is a **heat-network aviz**, not an electricity connection under HANRE 311. |

### 8. Outside the commercial SACET perimeter

| | |
|---|---|
| **What** | Individual / building-level heat not commercialised through a centralised system |
| **Who** | Building owners, installers |
| **Practice** | L92 licensing targets commercial production/distribution/supply in SACET. Shallow geothermal / heat pumps as **RES** → L10 + installer rules; if later tied into SACET, L92 art. 15¹ applies. See [[Energetică — geotermal gap stub (notă)]]. |

## E. Professional deepening — Cap. VI / Cap. VIII / dual statute

### E.1 Cap. VI — land, networks, protection zones

District heat is physically embedded in streets and buildings. Cap. VI (arts. 28–32): land-use rights, restriction of activities affecting heat infrastructure, expropriation, **protection zones**, restructuring/merger. A consumer or works dispute often has **both** contract and real-estate dimensions — route accordingly (ANRE for supply/tariff; property/local procedures for zones).

### E.2 Cap. VIII + HANRE 23 — consumer operations

Heat-supply contract, interruption/limitation, metering, disputes (arts. 39–44). Annex 1 contract is reliable for: parties (incl. condominium intermediary), delimitation point, disconnection notice patterns, uneconomic withdrawal (≥6 months before **15 Oct**), ANRE dispute channel. **Missing regulation body** blocks advice on season start/stop, connection procedure, flat-rate recalculation — flag the gap rather than inventing.

### E.3 Dual-statute CHP checklist

1. Heat activity licensed under L92 art. 19?
2. Electricity activity / market participation under L164?
3. HE status claimed? → annexes + HG 197 measurement pack
4. Urban CHP still on FCEE offtake? → art. 87(2) + ANRE art. 150(4) calendar
5. Cold-season redispach / heat-continuity constraint in play?
6. Which HANRE are you citing — 23 (heat) vs 283 (electricity)?

## 1. The one thing to understand first

Moldovan energy law is a **delegation system**, not a single rulebook. Organic / sectoral laws create [[ANRE]], name objectives and licensing catalogues, and enumerate what must be regulated. The binding operational answer (tariff, connection clock, imbalance cash-out, supply contract clause) almost always lives in a **HANRE** — or, for policy quotas and methodologies of government, in an **HG**.

**Reading rule:** (1) name the vector and the **gate** (licence ≠ connection ≠ support ≠ REMIT); (2) cite the lowest tier that binds; (3) currency-check *temei* and statute conflicts; (4) check whether a **crisis overlay** is live. Full conflict doctrines: architecture **§1.3**.

## 2. The cascade (and the map)

```
Energy Community Treaty (L117) → adapted acquis
        ↓
Parliament — sectoral / framework law
        ↓  temei legal
Government — HG (quotas, SoS, methodologies of policy)
        ↓
ANRE — HANRE (market rules, codes, connection, supply, tariffs)
        ↓
ANRE — individual decisions (licences, specific tariffs)  ← not ingested
```

Walk *up* from any HANRE via `Temei legal` for *ultra vires* challenges. Horizontal bridges (L164↔L10, L92↔FCEE, L10↔L461 biofuels, crisis splits) are tabulated in the architecture map — do not advise from one silo.

## 3. Four regulatory problems (still the deep structure)

Everything in the corpus answers one of these. Market hubs show *where* each problem lands per vector.

### 3.1 Natural monopoly → regulated tariffs
Networks cannot be duplicated → methodology (normative) + individual tariff decision. → [[Concept — Tarif reglementat]] · [[MOC — Tarife și metodologii ANRE]] · [[Energetică — metodologii tarifare (notă)]].

### 3.2 Vertical integration → unbundling
Separate network from supply/generation. Gas is where this still bites hardest (certification / Vestmoldtransgaz perimeter). → [[Concept — Unbundling]] · gas hub §8.

### 3.3 Entry → licensing and connection (two gates)
Licence = activity permission ([[Concept — Licență în energetică]]). Connection = attach a site ([[Concept — Racordare la rețea]] · [[MOC — Racordare și acces la rețele]] · HANRE 311). Above **200 kW**: financial guarantee. Flexible connection = non-firm injection — price curtailment.

### 3.4 Socially exposed consumers → PSO
Universal service vs last resort are **separated** on L164 (arts. 114–115). FUO duration: statute **≥6 months** beats HANRE 169’s 90-day text. Gas industrial PSO withdrawal targeted **1 Apr 2026** — confirm live instruments on ANRE (hub open thread).

## 5. Renewables & support (revenue overlays)

[[Legea 10-2016 — surse regenerabile (notă)|L10]] two-track support: fixed tariff (small) vs auction/ceiling (large); gate = [[Concept — Producător eligibil]]; scarcity = Government quota ([[HG 599-2025 — limite capacitate regenerabile 2030 (notă)|HG 599]]). Prosumers = [[Concept — Facturare netă]], not eligible-producer track. Commercial hinge = [[Concept — Furnizor central de energie electrică|FCEE]] (art. 87(2); RES→CfD sunset art. 150(3); urban CHP exit **calendar** art. 150(4)).

Binding constraint is usually **connection, curtailment, balancing** — not the tariff number. Full overlay map: [[Energetică — incentives și scheme de sprijin (notă)]].

## 7. Where the risk sits (mid-2026)

1. **Version risk dominates** — re-check [ANRE Hotărâri](https://anre.md/acte-normative-3-18) before advising from any HANRE snapshot.
2. **Statute vs stale HANRE** — exemplars in architecture §1.3 (FUO 6m vs 90d; L107 *temei* cluster 283/169/316).
3. **Four gates** — never collapse licence / connection / support / REMIT.
4. **Crisis instruments are vector-specific** — HG 820 ≠ L101/L248/CNMC ≠ gas SoS storage HGs.
5. **Live / just-passed transitions to confirm at source:** gas PSO after **1 Apr 2026**; FCEE urban-CHP calendar (duty by 1 Jan 2026); HANRE **853** / **383** IF **1 Jul 2026**; HG **53** IF ≈**11.02.2026**; RFNBO 70% from **1 Jan 2026**.
6. **Efficiency is hard compliance** — L139 obligated parties, audits, EPC/FEE via [[CNED]]; DR hooks citing L107 → route via L164.
7. **Truncation / missing annexes** still block some advice — see audit note; do not infer absence from silence.

## Regulated revenue formula

$$\text{Regulated Revenue} = \text{OPEX} + \text{Depreciation} + (\text{RAB} \times \text{WACC})$$

| Component | Meaning |
|---|---|
| OPEX | Strictly necessary operating and maintenance costs |
| RAB | Regulated asset base — net value of assets used in regulated service |
| WACC | Weighted average cost of capital set by ANRE per sector |

## Sectoral breakdown

### A. Renewable support tariffs (HANRE 375/2017)
- Fixed tariffs (*feed-in*) and auction price ceilings for eligible producers.
- **15-year** tariff guarantee from commissioning date.
- Annual indexation tied to USD/MDL exchange rate and inflation.
- Cross-link: [[Legea 10-2016 — surse regenerabile (notă)]] · [[Concept — Producător eligibil]]

### B. Water & sewerage (HANRE 169/2025)
- Methodology for including capital investment in public water/sewerage tariffs.
- Water losses exceeding ANRE-approved normative thresholds **cannot be recovered via tariff** — absorbed from operator profit.
- Cross-link: [[Legea 303-2013 — serviciul public apa (notă)]]

### C. Electricity transmission (HANRE 261/2026)
- Updates Moldelectrica transmission tariff for technological losses at new balancing energy prices.
- Factors in cross-border interconnection capacity allocation revenues.
- Cross-link: [[HANRE 626-2023 — Metodologie tarife transport EE (notă)]]

# Energetică — Stage 1 topic catalogue for deep legal analysis

**Purpose:** Structured inventory of **what should be analysed in depth** for the Moldovan energy market corpus — especially market design, operators, market types, and petroleum — with a coverage audit against the vault as of 2026-08-07.

**This note is Stage 1 only** (topic generation + gap flagging). Stage 2+ will produce the Law House deep analyses themselves.

**Hubs:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · [[Energetică — synthesis]] · [[MOC — Piața de energie electrică]] · [[Status ingestie — Energetica]] · Pattern sibling: [[Fiscalitate & Contabilitate — horizon and gaps]]

**Coverage verdict (Energy overall):** Strong **statutory ingestion** and a usable **cascade mental model** ([[Energetică — synthesis]]). Weak on **big-picture market architecture**, **operator institutional maps**, **market-segment-by-segment doctrine**, and **petroleum as a full market** (beyond crisis + price-cap). Several electricity MOCs are still **stale** (cite L107 as live).

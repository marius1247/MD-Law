# Public procurement — synthesis

The reasoning layer for **general** and **state** procurement. Act inventory: [[MOC — Achiziții publice & Statul]].

> [!important] Two procurement codes — always classify first
> **General public procurement** → [[Legea 325-2025 — achizitii publice (notă)|L325/2025]] (from **01.01.2027**, replaces L131/2015).
> **Utilities sectoral procurement** (energy, water, transport, postal) → [[Legea 74-2020 — achizitii sectoriale (notă)|L74/2020]].
> A network operator may be regulated under **L74**, not L325, even when it looks like a “company tender”.

| Layer | Instrument | Role |

| **Performance** | [[Codul civil 1107-2002 (notă)]] | Contract claims after award |

## 2. The L325/2025 transition (live from 2026)

- **Published:** 12.02.2026 (MO 76–79 art. 39).
- **In force:** **01.01.2027**.
- **Abrogates:** Legea nr. 131/2015 (art. 90 alin. (4)).
- **Grandfathering:** procedures started and executing contracts at entry → prior law (art. 91).
- **Government duty:** align secondary legislation before entry (art. 90 alin. (3)).

## 3. Threshold logic (L325 — verify annually)

Art. 1 splits **standard thresholds** (alin. (1)) and **higher particularities** (alin. (2)). Wrong band → wrong publication duties (national vs EU OJEU) and wrong procedure set.

**Commercial checkpoints:**
- Is the buyer a **central** vs **local** authority vs **public-law entity**?
- Is the object **works / goods / services / social services** (annex 2)?
- Is defence annex-1 goods involved?
- Above art. 1(2) → EU publication mandatory where stated (e.g. arts. 49–50).

Sectoral thresholds under L74 differ and LP102/2026 changes apply from **01.01.2027** — mirror check for energy mandates.

## 4. Procedure map

1. **Planning** — annual plan, market consultation, justification for negotiated procedures.
2. **Publication** — notice type per value band (annex forms).
3. **Qualification & award** — MEAT / price / cost — document in **dosar achiziție**.
4. **Standstill & award notice** — ANSC window.
5. **Contract** — subcontracting disclosure, modification rules (art. 74).
6. **Remedy** — ANSC → court.

→ [[Concept — Procedură de achiziție]] · [[Concept — Documentația de atribuire]] · [[Concept — Contestare (ANSC)]]

## 5. Energy bridge

Electricity/gas/heat network activities can trigger **L74**, not L325:
- [[Legea 74-2020 — achizitii sectoriale (notă)]] arts. 9–10.
- Licence-holder procurement rules (**HANRE 24/2017** — not ingested) may add sector duties.
- Market-based procurement including losses (2026 policy) changes compliant supply contracts → [[MOC — Energetică]].

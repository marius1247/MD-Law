## 1. Structure of the Civil Code

**[[Codul civil 1107-2002 (text) — Index|Codul civil nr. 1107/2002]]** — split by **Carte** in the vault:

| Carte | Subject | Commercial weight |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | Dispoziții generale — persons, legal acts, **prescription** | High — capacity, representation, nullity |
| **II** | Drepturi reale — property, possession, usufruct | Medium — asset security |
| **III** | **Obligații** — contracts, torts, remedies | **Highest** — all commercial contracts |
| **IV** | Succession | Medium — family holdings |
| **V** | PIL | Cross-border deals |

## 2. Why civil law sits under everything else

Moldova is **monist** — no separate commercial code. Company law ([[MOC — Societăți & Guvernanță corporativă]]) and commercial activity ([[Drept comercial — sinteza]]) both lean on Cartea III:

- **Contract formation & interpretation** — freedom of contract, good faith, standard terms
- **Remedies** — specific performance, termination, damages — primary commercial dispute toolkit
- **Torts** — delictual liability alongside contract
- **Property security** — Cartea II for mortgages, usufruct, possession

## 4. Interface with public law

- **Administrative contracts** — CA 116/2018 + Cartea III
- **Procurement contracts** — [[Legea 325-2025 — achizitii publice (notă)]] performance + civil remedies
- **Energy supply** — regulated tariffs + civil contract terms
- **Consumer rules** — where professional vs consumer asymmetry applies (verify special laws)

Administrative litigation does **not** replace civil claims for contractual performance — route depends on object.

# Procedure — synthesis

How **disputes** and **state decisions** are made and challenged. Act inventory: [[MOC — Proceduri]].

---

## 1. Two procedural worlds

| Track | Code | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| **Administrative** | [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]] | Licences, permits, sanctions, individual acts, ANRE decisions, many procurement steps |
| **Civil litigation** | [[Codul de procedura civila 225-2003 (notă)]] | Private disputes, contract claims, some public-law monetary claims |
| **Enforcement** | [[Codul de executare 443-2004 (notă)]] | Executing judgments |
| **Arbitral** | [[Legea 23-2008 — arbitraj (notă)]] | Commercial arbitration (optional) |
| **Constitutional** | [[Legea 74-2025 — Curtea Constitutionala (notă)]] / [[Curtea Constituțională]] | Constitutionality — separate from ordinary appeals |

Most cross-domain mandates (energy, tax, procurement) require **administrative route first**, then court.

---

## 2. Administrative procedure spine

From [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]]:

1. **Individual administrative act** → [[Concept — Act administrativ individual]]
2. **Prior complaint** (where required) to issuer or hierarchical superior
3. **Administrative litigation** in court → [[Concept — Contencios administrativ]]
4. **Suspension** pending review — strategically critical in tariffs and licences
5. **Appeal / recourse** → [[Concept — Căi de atac]]

**Meta-law:** normative act drafting hierarchy → [[Legea 100-2017 — actele normative (notă)]].

## 4. Time limits — prescription

**Prescripție** cuts both substantive and procedural rights:
- Civil: [[Codul civil 1107-2002 — text — Cartea I]] + [[Concept — Prescripție]]
- Administrative: CA 116/2018 limitation rules for litigation
- Procurement: ANSC-specific windows — miss → inadmissible

Always calendar from **service of act** or **publication**, not from internal knowledge.

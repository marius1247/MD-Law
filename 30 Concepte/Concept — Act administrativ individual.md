# Concept — Act administrativ individual

**RO:** act administrativ individual · **EN:** individual administrative act

## Definition

A **unilateral decision** by a public authority that creates, modifies, or extinguishes rights/obligations for **identified persons** — licences, permits, tariff approvals, sanctions, refusals.

Defined and governed in [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)|Codul administrativ nr. 116/2018]].

## Why it is the gateway concept

Most sector regulators (ANRE, SFS, local authorities) operate through individual acts. Remedies depend on:
- **lawful form** and service
- **prior complaint** requirements
- **limitation** periods

## Not the same as

- **Normative acts** — general rules → [[Legea 100-2017 — actele normative (notă)]]
- **Administrative contracts** — bilateral → civil + CA rules
- **Procurement contest** — ANSC route for procedure breaches

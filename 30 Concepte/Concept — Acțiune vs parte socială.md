# Concept — Acțiune vs parte socială

**RO:** acțiune / parte socială · **EN:** share (security) / participation interest

## Why the distinction matters
Both represent ownership in a company. They are **different kinds of legal object**, and the difference drives transfer mechanics, regulation, pledging, and where the definitive record of ownership sits.

| | **Acțiune** (SA) | **Parte socială** (SRL) |
|---|---|---|
| Legal nature | A **security** (*valoare mobiliară*) | A contractual participation / bundle of rights |
| Where ownership is recorded | **Securities register**, kept by the registrar under [[CNPF]] supervision | **State Register of Legal Entities** at [[ASP]] + the constitutive act |
| Transfer | Effected through the registrar; free in principle | Notarised transfer + registration; **pre-emption rights** for other members by default |
| Divisibility | Freely divisible into units | One member holds **one** participation, of a given size |
| Regulatory overlay | Securities-market law; disclosure; [[CNPF]] | None beyond company law |
| Can be publicly offered | Yes | **No** |
| Pledge | Pledge over securities, registered with the registrar | Pledge over the participation, registered |

## The consequences in practice
- **Due diligence looks in different places.** For an SA, the securities register is the source of truth on ownership; for an SRL, the ASP register and the constitutive act. Checking the wrong register is a real and common error.
- **Transfer risk differs.** In an SRL, a share purchase agreement can be defeated by an unwaived pre-emption right — waivers must be collected *before* signing, not after.
- **An SRL cannot raise public equity.** If the plan involves outside investors at scale or an eventual listing, the form has to change; transformation SRL → SA is available but is a full reorganisation.
- **Classes.** An SA can issue **ordinary and preference shares** with differentiated rights. SRL participations are more uniform, and economic differentiation has to be engineered through the constitutive act and members' agreements — less robust.

## Rights attaching to both
Voting, dividend, information, liquidation surplus, and the right to challenge resolutions. The *content* is broadly similar; the *machinery* for exercising them is not — notably, SA shareholder rights are reinforced by the transposition of **Directive 2007/36/EC**.

## Notes / conclusions
> Shorthand: *acțiune* = a security, regulated, transferable, publicly offerable. *Parte socială* = a contractual stake in a closed company, transferable only through the door the other members hold open.

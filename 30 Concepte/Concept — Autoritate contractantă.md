# Concept — Autoritate contractantă

**RO:** autoritate contractantă · **EN:** contracting authority

## Definition

A **public entity** (or category expressly listed in law) that **awards public procurement contracts** or organises design contests under [[Legea 325-2025 — achizitii publice (notă)|L325/2025]] — not the same as every body that spends public money.

## Why it matters

Classification determines:
- applicable **thresholds** (central vs local vs public-law entity — art. 1)
- **publication** duties (national / EU)
- **grup de lucru** and **certification** duties (arts. 87–88)
- whether **L325** or **sectoral L74** applies instead

## Distinction

| Term | Law | Catch |
|---|---|---|
| **Autoritate contractantă** | L325/2025 | Classic budget spenders |
| **Entitate contractantă** | L74/2020 | Utility undertakings by **activity** |

A state-owned energy company may be an **entitate contractantă** under L74 without being a classic autoritate contractantă.

# Concept — Contencios administrativ

**RO:** contencios administrativ · **EN:** administrative litigation

## Definition

**Judicial review** of administrative activity — chiefly **individual administrative acts** — under [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)|CA 116/2018]] and [[Codul de procedura civila 225-2003 (notă)|CPC]] where referred.

## Typical sequence

1. **Individual act** issued
2. **Prior complaint** (if required)
3. **Application** to administrative court
4. Optional **suspension** pending review
5. **Judgment** — annulment, obligation to act, damages where available

## Strategic notes

- **Suspension** can preserve tariff or licence position during review — energy cases often fought here.
- Procurement **procedure** breaches may require **ANSC** first → [[Concept — Contestare (ANSC)]].

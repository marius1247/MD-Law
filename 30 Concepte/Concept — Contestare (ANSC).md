# Concept — Contestare (ANSC)

**RO:** contestație la ANSC · **EN:** procurement challenge before ANSC

## Definition

A **specialised remedy** against breaches in a procurement **procedure** — filed with [[ANSC]] under [[Legea 325-2025 — achizitii publice (notă)|L325/2025]] arts. 82–84 (from **01.01.2027**).

## How it differs from administrative litigation

| ANSC contest | Administrative court |
|---|---|
| Procurement-specific standing & deadlines | General individual acts |
| Often **before** contract signature | After administrative act service |
| Sector expertise | General judge |

Court review of ANSC decisions follows [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]] — not a substitute for filing in time at ANSC.

## Strategic use

- **Standstill** period coordination
- Challenge **specifications** that exclude competition
- Challenge **evaluation** and **award** before contract makes remedy harder

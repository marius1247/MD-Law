# Concept — Căi de atac

**RO:** căi de atac · **EN:** appeals / recourse routes

## Definition

The **hierarchy of remedies** against a decision — prior complaint, special body (ANSC), first-instance court, appeal, recourse, constitutional review.

There is no single “appeal” — route is **act-specific**.

## Map (simplified)

| Decision type | First remedy | Next |
|---|---|---|
| Procurement procedure | [[ANSC]] | Administrative court |
| ANRE licence/tariff act | Prior complaint / ANRE | Administrative court |
| Tax assessment | SFS complaint | Court |
| Civil judgment | Appeal | Recourse (ÎCCJ) |
| Constitutionality | — | [[Curtea Constituțională]] |

# Concept — Documentația de atribuire

**RO:** documentația de atribuire · **EN:** procurement documents / tender documentation

## Definition

The **complete package** issued to candidates — specifications, criteria, contract terms, forms, deadlines — that defines what can be bid and how offers are evaluated.

## Legal role

- Must be **consistent** with the notice and law — post-publication changes restricted
- **Evaluation criteria** must be disclosed before bids — MEAT / price rules
- **Technical vs financial** envelopes — integrity protections (see L325 confidentiality rules)
- Annex **formulare** from legis.md pack — upload PDF omitted annexes 1–14

## Risk

Authorities that “negotiate” substantive terms after publication without lawful procedure amendment lose contests at **ANSC**.

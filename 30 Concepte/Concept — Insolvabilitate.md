# Concept — Insolvabilitate

**RO:** insolvabilitate · **EN:** insolvency

## Definition
The state in which a debtor is **unable to meet its obligations as they fall due** (cash-flow insolvency) or its liabilities exceed its assets (balance-sheet insolvency), opening a **collective procedure** under **Legea insolvabilității nr. 149/2012**.

## Why it is a *collective* procedure — the whole logic
Outside insolvency, creditors enforce individually and the fastest one wins. As a debtor nears failure this becomes destructive: a race to seize assets dismembers a business that might be worth more intact, and rewards speed rather than priority. Insolvency law replaces the race with a **collective process**: individual enforcement is stayed, claims are proved and ranked, and the estate is realised once, for everyone, in order of priority.

Everything else in the statute follows from that: the stay, the administrator, the claims table, the ranking, the avoidance powers.

## The two routes
| | **General procedure → liquidation** | **Restructuring** |
|---|---|---|
| Premise | The business is worth more broken up | The business is worth more alive |
| Mechanism | Realise assets, distribute by rank | Court-supervised plan, creditor classes vote, court confirms |
| Outcome | Debtor is dissolved | Debtor continues under the plan |

Restructuring is the alternative to liquidation, and its success depends almost entirely on **timing** — a plan proposed when the company is already an empty shell has nothing to restructure.

## Key machinery
- **Opening** — on the debtor's petition (with a **duty to file** once insolvency exists) or a creditor's
- **Stay** of individual enforcement
- **Administrator/liquidator** — displaces or supervises management; investigates the pre-insolvency period
- **Claims proof and ranking** — secured creditors, then statutory priorities, then unsecured; equity last
- **Suspect period** — transactions before opening can be **avoided**: preferences to favoured creditors, transfers at undervalue, and asset-stripping. This is the estate's principal recovery tool.
- **Director liability** — for late filing and for suspect-period conduct

## The two dates that decide everything
1. The date the debtor **became insolvent in fact**
2. The date the petition was **filed**

The interval between them defines the suspect period, sets the scope of avoidance, and measures directors' personal exposure. Establishing date (1) is the central forensic question in almost every case, and it is answered from the accounting records. → [[Concept — Situații financiare]] · [[Concept — Administrator]]

## Notes / conclusions
> Limited liability is not free — it is paid for with the creditor-protection corridor: capital maintenance, the net-assets duty, the filing duty, and avoidance. Insolvency is where that corridor is enforced, retrospectively and personally.

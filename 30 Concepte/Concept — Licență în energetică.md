# Concept — Licență în energetică

**RO:** licență · **EN:** licence / sectoral authorisation

> [!danger] Base act replaced — 19 August 2025
> References below to **Legea 107/2016** are to a **repealed** statute. It was abrogated in full by [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)|Legea nr. 164/2025]], which transposes the EU **fourth package** (Dir. 2019/944, Reg. 2019/943), **REMIT** and **CACM**. Article numbering is entirely different, and new regulated activities — **storage, aggregation, trading** — were added. Verify every L107 citation against L164/2025 before use.

## Definition
An administrative act by which [[ANRE]] authorises a person to **carry on a regulated activity** in the energy sector. It attaches to the *activity*, not to a site or an installation — that is the job of [[Concept — Racordare la rețea|connection]].

## Licensable activities
Broadly, for electricity: generation, transmission, distribution, supply, and market-operator functions; for gas: transport, distribution, storage, supply. The definitive list is in the sectoral law, activity by activity — check it before assuming an activity is licence-free, because trading and aggregation sit awkwardly at the edges.

## How it works
- **Grant** — conditions and procedure are set by the law and detailed by ANRE; the general authorisation framework of Legea nr. 160/2011 (`reglementarea prin autorizare a activităţii de întreprinzător`) applies in the background, notably for *reperfectare* (amendment/reissue).
- **Content** — the licence carries **conditions**, and those conditions are enforceable in their own right. Breach of a licence condition is a distinct ground of action from breach of the law.
- **Scope and outsourcing** — a licence-holder may outsource parts of the activity within limits set by the law; the holder remains responsible. → [[Legea 107-2016 — energia electrica (text)#Articolul 13. Sfera de acţiune a licenţelor şi externalizarea|L107 art. 13]]
- **Suspension** — ANRE may suspend at the holder's own request within 3 working days; otherwise suspension generally requires a court decision. **Exception:** where failure has interrupted supply beyond the permitted period and endangered national security, life or health, or where the holder's acts breach public order and demand immediate remedy, ANRE may act directly. → [[Legea 107-2016 — energia electrica (text)#Articolul 16. Reperfectarea, suspendarea şi reluarea valabilităţii|L107 art. 16]]
- **Withdrawal** — the terminal sanction; triggers the [[Concept — Furnizor de ultimă opțiune|supplier-of-last-resort]] machinery if a supplier is the one that fails.

## Why it is the first question in any energy mandate
The licence is the **entry gate and the leash simultaneously**. It determines whether the client can operate at all, and it is the instrument through which every downstream obligation — tariff, network code, market rules, public service obligation — is made enforceable against them. A licensing defect contaminates everything built on top of it.

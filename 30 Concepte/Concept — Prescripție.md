# Concept — Prescripție

**RO:** prescripție · **EN:** limitation / prescription

## Definition

Time limits after which a **right of action** or **substantive claim** expires if not exercised — civil **prescripție extinctivă** in [[Codul civil 1107-2002 — text — Cartea I]] and procedural limitation in [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)|CA 116/2018]].

## Dual layer

| Layer | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Substantive** (civil) | Claim dies — court may dismiss on merits |
| **Procedural** (administrative litigation) | Application inadmissible |

Special laws add **short windows** — procurement ANSC deadlines are procedural extinction risks, not classical civil prescription.

## Practice

- Calendar from **service** or **publication**, not internal awareness
- **LP76/2026** may adjust civil prescription from 01.01.2027 — re-check Cartea I consolidation

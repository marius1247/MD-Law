# Concept — Procedură de achiziție

**RO:** procedură de achiziție publică · **EN:** procurement procedure

## Definition

The **regulated sequence** from planning through publication, qualification, evaluation, award, and standstill — ending in contract signature — governed by [[Legea 325-2025 — achizitii publice (notă)|L325/2025]] or [[Legea 74-2020 — achizitii sectoriale (notă)|L74/2020]].

## Main procedure types (L325)

- **Licitație deschisă** (open)
- **Licitație restrânsă** (restricted)
- **Negociere competitivă** / **dialog competitiv**
- **Negociere fără publicare** — requires justification + audit trail
- **Concurs de soluții** — design contests (cap. XII)

Choice depends on **value band**, object, and justification duties.

## Failure points

- Wrong procedure for threshold → **ANSC** annulment
- Missing **dosar achiziție** documentation (art. 84)
- **Modification** after award outside art. 74 → unenforceable spend

# Concept — Producător eligibil

**RO:** producător eligibil · **EN:** eligible producer

> [!danger] Base act replaced — 19 August 2025
> References below to **Legea 107/2016** are to a **repealed** statute. It was abrogated in full by [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)|Legea nr. 164/2025]], which transposes the EU **fourth package** (Dir. 2019/944, Reg. 2019/943), **REMIT** and **CACM**. Article numbering is entirely different, and new regulated activities — **storage, aggregation, trading** — were added. Verify every L107 citation against L164/2025 before use.

## Definition
A producer of energy from renewable sources that has been **granted the status** entitling it to the statutory support scheme under [[Legea 10-2016 — surse regenerabile (text)|Legea nr. 10/2016]]. Status is the gate: without it, a renewable plant is simply a market participant with no support.

## Why status is scarce
Support costs money that ultimately reaches consumers, so the legislator caps it. The cap operates through a **capacity quota** set by Government per technology and category. Once the quota is exhausted, further projects get no support regardless of merit. Eligible-producer status is therefore a **rivalrous, quantity-limited entitlement** — which is what makes timing and queue position commercially decisive.

## The two tracks
| | Small capacity | Large capacity |
|---|---|---|
| Allocation | Administrative — status granted against the quota | **Competitive tender / auction** |
| Price | **Fixed tariff** set by ANRE under [[HANRE 375-2017 — Metodologie tarife regenerabile (text)|methodology 375/2017]] | Bid price, capped by a **ceiling price** |
| Logic | Low transaction cost for small projects | Price discovery where the volume justifies it |

The capacity threshold separating the tracks, and the quota, are set administratively and change — **always check the current values**, never rely on a figure in a note.

## Attached rights
- **Guaranteed purchase / offtake** for the support period, discharged through a designated central buyer or obligated supplier
- **Priority dispatch**, subject to system security — the point where support policy collides with system operation, and the origin of **curtailment** disputes
- **Guarantees of origin**, tracking the renewable attribute separately from the energy itself

## Prosumers — a different animal
Small self-consumers with generation are handled by **net metering / net billing** rather than the eligible-producer track: surplus injected is set off against consumption over a settlement period. Lower administrative burden, no quota competition, but no tariff support either.

## Governing provisions
- [[Legea 10-2016 — surse regenerabile (text)]] — the support scheme, eligibility, quotas, net metering *(truncated at art. 34 — later provisions verify at source)*
- [[HANRE 375-2017 — Metodologie tarife regenerabile (text)]] — how the fixed tariff and ceiling price are computed *(annex missing)*
- [[Legea 107-2016 — energia electrica (text)|L107/2016]] — market participation, dispatch, connection

## Notes / conclusions
> Status secures the *price*. It does not secure the *route to market*: connection capacity, curtailment risk and balancing exposure are all outside it, and in a small system they dominate the project economics. Do not let a client treat eligible-producer status as the end of the risk analysis.

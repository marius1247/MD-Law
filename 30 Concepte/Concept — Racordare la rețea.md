# Concept — Racordare la rețea

**RO:** racordare la rețea · **EN:** network connection

> [!danger] Base act replaced — 19 August 2025
> References below to **Legea 107/2016** are to a **repealed** statute. It was abrogated in full by [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)|Legea nr. 164/2025]], which transposes the EU **fourth package** (Dir. 2019/944, Reg. 2019/943), **REMIT** and **CACM**. Article numbering is entirely different, and new regulated activities — **storage, aggregation, trading** — were added. Verify every L107 citation against L164/2025 before use.

## Definition
The regulated procedure by which a specific installation — a consumer's premises, a generating plant — is **physically and contractually attached** to an electricity or gas network. Granted by the **network operator**, under a procedure ANRE prescribes; it is not an ANRE decision, which is why the appeal route runs operator → ANRE → court.

## The stages
1. **Application** and preliminary information from the operator
2. **Connection permit / avizul de racordare** — states the technical conditions and the connection point
3. **Design and construction** of the connection works
4. **Connection contract** and commissioning
5. **Energisation** and, for a producer, entry into the market arrangements

Each stage carries a **regulated maximum time limit**. Missing them is the operator's breach, and the time limits are the most practically useful thing in the whole regulation.

## The three fights
- **Cost allocation** — how much of the reinforcement needed to accommodate the new user falls on that user versus the general tariff base. Deep vs. shallow connection charging; this is where the money is.
- **Capacity and queue** — where network capacity is scarce, position in the queue is the asset. Speculative applications that hold capacity without building are the systemic pathology.
- **Refusal** — an operator may refuse only on grounds the regulation allows, and must reason the refusal. Unreasoned or capacity-based refusals are the standard subject of complaint to ANRE.

> [!important] 2025 amendment — financial guarantee above 200 kW
> Applicants for and **holders of** connection permits for plants above **200 kW** must lodge a **financial guarantee**, in an amount set by ANRE. Aimed squarely at the queue-blocking problem: make holding capacity cost something. Check the transitional rules — the reform was drafted to reach existing holders, not only new applicants. *(Beyond the truncation point in the vault text — verify at source.)*

## Notes / conclusions
> Renewables projects in Moldova fail at connection far more often than at permitting or finance. If you only audit one thing in a project file, audit the connection permit: its capacity, its expiry, its conditions, and now its guarantee.

# Concept — TVA

**RO:** taxa pe valoarea adăugată (TVA) · **EN:** value added tax

## Definition
A general, indirect consumption tax on the supply of goods and services and on imports, governed by **Titlul III of the Codul fiscal**. Built on the EU VAT model: **output tax on sales, minus input tax on purchases**, with the difference remitted; the burden lands on the final consumer, and registered businesses are collectors rather than bearers.

## Rates *(verified July 2026 — the reduced-rate list is amended almost annually)*
| Rate | Applies to |
|---|---|
| **20%** | Standard |
| **8%** | Bread and bakery products; milk and dairy; **transport of natural gas**; certain agricultural products and inputs |
| **0%** | Exports and assimilated supplies — with **full input tax recovery** |
| Exempt | Specified supplies — **without** input tax recovery |

> The 8% rate on **natural gas transport** is a live cross-domain point: VAT treatment feeds directly into regulated tariff construction. → [[Concept — Tarif reglementat]] · [[Energetică — sinteza sectorului]]

## Zero-rated vs exempt — the distinction that costs money
Both mean no VAT is charged on the sale. They are opposite in effect:

- **Zero-rated (0%)** — a taxable supply at a nil rate. Input VAT **is recoverable**. The supplier is made whole.
- **Exempt** — outside the charge. Input VAT is **not recoverable** and becomes a real cost embedded in the price.

An exempt business is therefore a *worse* position than a zero-rated one, and exemption can make a business structurally uncompetitive against a taxable competitor. Clients routinely treat the two as equivalent. They are not.

## Registration — a genuine strategic decision
Registration is **threshold-based** on taxable turnover, with **voluntary registration** available below the threshold.

Registering is not automatically good:
- **Register** if customers are VAT-registered businesses (they recover the VAT, so the price is neutral to them) and if there is meaningful input VAT to recover.
- **Stay unregistered** if customers are final consumers — VAT then raises the effective price by 20% against unregistered competitors.

Crucially, the **4% small business income tax regime requires being outside VAT registration**. The two decisions are linked and must be modelled together, not sequentially. → [[Concept — Impozit pe venit]]

## Mechanics
- **Chargeable event and time of supply** — determines the period in which the tax is accounted for
- **Tax invoice** (*factura fiscală*) — the document that carries the right to deduct. **No valid invoice, no deduction**: most disputed VAT assessments are evidential, not substantive
- **Reverse charge** on specified cross-border services
- **Import VAT** at the border, administered by the Serviciul Vamal, then deductible
- **Refunds** — export-heavy and investment-heavy businesses run structural credit positions; refund procedure and timing are a real cash-flow issue
- **Partial exemption** — mixed taxable and exempt activity requires an apportionment of input tax

## Notes / conclusions
> VAT is administratively the heaviest tax for a Moldovan business and the most audited. The two recurring failure modes are **defective invoices** destroying otherwise valid deductions, and **slow refunds** creating a financing cost that never appears in anyone's model.

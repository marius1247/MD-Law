# Concept — Accize

**RO:** accize · **EN:** excise duties

## Definition
A **selective** indirect tax on specified goods, governed by **Titlul IV of the Codul fiscal**. Unlike [[Concept — TVA|VAT]], which is general and proportional to value, excise targets particular products and is usually charged **per physical unit**.

## Why excise exists — three overlapping rationales
1. **Corrective** — internalising external costs: health costs of alcohol and tobacco, environmental and congestion costs of fuel and vehicles.
2. **Revenue** — the goods are price-inelastic and consumption is concentrated, so the tax raises reliable revenue with limited behavioural loss.
3. **EU harmonisation** — the excise structure and the minimum-rate trajectory follow the EU acquis, which is why Moldovan rates rise on a scheduled, multi-year path rather than opportunistically.

## Scope
- **Alcohol** — beer, wine, spirits
- **Tobacco** — cigarettes and other products, typically on a mixed specific + ad valorem basis
- **Petroleum products** — petrol, diesel, LPG
- **Vehicles**, and specified other goods (the list is set out in the annex to Titlu IV)

## Mechanics
- **Chargeable event** — production or importation, not retail sale. The tax is levied high in the chain and passed forward in the price.
- **Specific rates** are the norm — an amount per litre, per 1,000 cigarettes, per unit — and are **indexed annually**. Ad valorem elements appear mainly in tobacco.
- **Excise warehouse / suspension** — goods may be held and moved under duty suspension, with the charge crystallising on release for consumption. This is what makes production and export logistics workable.
- **Export relief** — excise is a domestic consumption tax; exports are relieved.
- **Marking** — excise stamps on alcohol and tobacco, an anti-fraud control that is itself a compliance obligation.
- **Interaction with VAT** — excise forms part of the **VAT base**. Tax on tax, deliberately.

## Where it matters beyond alcohol and tobacco
**Fuel excise** flows through the entire transport cost base and into energy prices — it is an input to the cost stack that regulated tariffs are built from. → [[Concept — Tarif reglementat]] · [[Energetică — synthesis]]

**Smuggling and illicit trade** are the structural constraint on rate increases. Where a rate rises faster than in neighbouring markets, the differential creates a cross-border arbitrage that erodes the base — which is why the EU alignment path is scheduled rather than immediate.

- **Codul vamal** — import excise and suspension arrangements

## Notes / conclusions
> Rates are indexed every year and the schedule is published in advance. For any excise-exposed business, the multi-year rate path is a planning input, not a compliance detail — model it forward rather than checking it annually.

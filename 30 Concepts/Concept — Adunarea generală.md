# Concept — Adunarea generală

**RO:** adunarea generală a acționarilor / asociaților · **EN:** general meeting

## Definition
The **supreme organ** of the company: the assembly through which owners exercise the decisions the law and the constitutive act reserve to them. It is not a management body — it acts occasionally, on reserved matters, and by resolution.

## Reserved competence — the core
Typically exclusive to the general meeting and incapable of delegation:

- amendment of the **constitutive act**
- **increase and reduction of share capital**
- approval of **annual financial statements** and the **distribution of profit** → [[Concept — Situații financiare]]
- **appointment and removal** of the board, the executive, and the auditor/censor
- **reorganisation** (merger, division, transformation) and **dissolution**
- major transactions and **related-party transactions** above the statutory thresholds

The competence list is the primary instrument of owner control. Everything not reserved falls to the board or the administrator.

## Mechanics that decide outcomes
- **Convening** — who may call it (board, administrator, members holding a statutory minimum), notice period, and agenda. **Defects in convening are the most common ground for annulling a resolution** — attack the procedure before the substance.
- **Agenda binding** — a meeting generally cannot validly resolve on matters not on the notified agenda. This protects absent members and is routinely breached.
- **Quorum and majorities** — ordinary matters by simple majority; **structural matters by qualified majority** (constitutive act, capital, reorganisation, dissolution). The qualified majority is what gives a blocking minority its value: control at 50%+1 is very different from control at the qualified threshold.
- **Ordinary vs extraordinary** — the annual meeting approving accounts and profit distribution, versus meetings convened as needed.
- **Challenge** — a member may seek annulment of a resolution taken in breach of law or of the constitutive act, within a **short limitation period**. Diarise it immediately; the period is the practical constraint, not the merits.
- **SA specifics** — reinforced convening, participation and voting rights following the transposition of **Directive 2007/36/EC**.

## Where the real leverage is
Not in the vote itself but in **who controls the agenda and the convening process**, and in the **qualified-majority list**. When drafting a shareholders' agreement or constitutive act, the reserved-matters list and the majority thresholds are the two provisions worth spending time on.

## Notes / conclusions
> Resolutions fall on procedure far more often than on substance. When reviewing a contested decision, work the sequence: was it properly convened → was the item on the agenda → was quorum met → was the correct majority applied → is the challenge still in time.

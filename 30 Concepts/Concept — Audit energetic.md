# Concept — Audit energetic

**RO:** audit energetic · **EN:** energy audit

> Defined in [[Legea 139-2018 — eficienta energetica (text)#Articolul 3]] — systematic procedure to obtain information on the energy-consumption profile of a building, process, installation or service, identify and quantify cost-effective saving opportunities, and report results. Operative regime: arts. 19–21, 28–30, Annex 5. → [[Legea 139-2018 — eficienta energetica (notă)]]

## How it works
- **Who must:** large enterprises outside the SME Law 179/2016 perimeter, on [[CNED]] notification (art. 19(1)).
- **Exemption:** energy / environmental management system that already includes an audit meeting Annex 5 minimum criteria (art. 19(2)).
- **Who may perform:** only qualified auditors registered in CNED’s electronic register (arts. 20–21).
- **Cadence:** first audit within **1 year** of notification; then at least every **4 years** (art. 30(14)).
- **Project audits:** EE projects financed from state/local budgets or CNED also require an audit, except building projects ≤ **250 m²** useful area (art. 19(3)).

## Enforcement
Failure: **0.1%** of annual turnover; repeated refusal: **0.5%** (art. 28(3)–(4)) — National Inspectorate for Technical Supervision; contest under [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]].

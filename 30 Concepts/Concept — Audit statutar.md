# Concept — Audit statutar

**RO:** auditul situațiilor financiare (obligatoriu) · **EN:** statutory audit of financial statements

## Definition
The mandatory audit of individual or consolidated financial statements required by legislation (or performed at shareholders’ request), carried out only by a registered **auditor** / **audit entity** under [[Legea 271-2017 — auditul situatiilor financiare (notă)|Legea nr. 271/2017]], to international audit standards accepted in Moldova.

> Defined in [[Legea 271-2017 — auditul situatiilor financiare (text)#Articolul 2. Noţiuni|L271 art. 2]]; *whether* audit is mandatory for size categories sits in [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 32. Auditul situațiilor financiare|L287 art. 32]].

## How it works
1. **Trigger** — L287 category (medium/large), public-interest entity (PIE), or special sector law; from **1 Jan 2027** recalculate under [[Legea 86-2026 — modificare Legea contabilitatii (notă)|L86]].
2. **Capacity** — only persons/entities on the public registers (L271 arts. 6–10), with valid certificate and insurance.
3. **Independence & ethics** — IESBA Code + L271 art. 21 guarantees; PIE engagements carry heavier insurance and transparency duties.
4. **Output** — auditor’s report (art. 28); for PIEs also transparency report (art. 29) and additional report to the **audit committee** (arts. 44–46).
5. **Oversight** — Consiliul de supraveghere publică a auditului (external quality control, discipline).

## Governing provisions
- [[Legea 271-2017 — auditul situatiilor financiare (text)#Articolul 1. Obiectul de reglementare|L271]] — profession, performance, oversight
- [[Legea 287-2017 — contabilitate (text)#Articolul 32. Auditul situațiilor financiare|L287 art. 32]] — who must be audited by size/PIE
- [[Legea 86-2026 — modificare Legea contabilitatii (notă)|L86/2026]] — 2027 threshold reset (often *reduces* audit exposure)

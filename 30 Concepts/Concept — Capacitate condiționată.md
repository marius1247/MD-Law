# Concept — Capacitate condiționată

**RO:** capacitate condiționată · **EN:** conditional capacity

> Introduced into the Gas Network Code by [[HANRE 328-2025 — modificare Codul retelelor de gaze naturale (notă)|HANRE 328/2025]]; **recast** by [[HANRE 310-2026 — modificare Codul retelelor de gaze naturale (notă)|HANRE 310/2026]]. Parent: [[HANRE 420-2019 — Codul retelelor de gaze naturale (notă)]].

## Definition
A special gas transmission capacity product enabling use of Moldova’s network as part of the **Trans-Balkan corridor**, subject to cumulative route, matching, customs-transit and (where applicable) PVT-access conditions. Not ordinary firm IP capacity.

## How it works
- Products: day-ahead / monthly / quarterly / annual at IPs with ANRE-approved tariffs (methodology HANRE 535/2019, as amended by [[HANRE 329-2025 — modificare Metodologie tarife transport gaze (notă)|HANRE 329/2025]] — new Section 7² for conditional-capacity tariffs; parent 535 text still missing).
- Quantities sit **outside** the shipper’s balancing portfolio and daily imbalance calculation.
- Current path logic centres on **Căușeni** (UA→MD) and **Grebenyky** (MD→UA); PVT access via Căușeni capped operationally at **6 million m³/day**; above that, no-PVT capacity requires matched exit reservation.
- Priority: firm > interruptible > conditional. OST may reduce mismatched nominations to the lower quantity and interrupt for breach.

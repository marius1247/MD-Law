# Concept — Capital social

**RO:** capital social · **EN:** share capital / registered capital

## Definition
The aggregate value of members' contributions, expressed in lei, recorded in the constitutive act and in the **State Register of Legal Entities**. Legally it is not a pot of money — it is a **figure on the liabilities side** representing the minimum net asset value the company undertakes to maintain against distribution to its owners.

## What it actually does
Two jobs, and only one of them works well:

1. **Creditor protection (weak).** The capital-maintenance idea is that a fixed floor of assets is fenced off from shareholders. In practice a nominal figure is a poor proxy for solvency, which is why the EU and most modern systems have retreated from minimum-capital requirements toward **solvency tests and directors' duties**. Moldova has followed: the SRL minimum was abolished.
2. **Allocation of rights (strong).** Capital determines each member's share of votes, profits and liquidation surplus. This is the function that genuinely matters day to day.

## The numbers *(verified July 2026 — re-check)*
| Form | Minimum |
|---|---|
| **SRL** (Legea 135/2007) | **None.** The historic MDL 5,400 floor was repealed; founders set the figure freely |
| **SA** (Legea 1134/1997, art. 38(2)) | **MDL 600,000** |

> [!warning] Legacy SAs — check this first
> Law nr. 18/2020 gave existing joint-stock companies until **1 January 2024** to reach the MDL 600,000 minimum. A company that neither complied nor resolved to reorganise or dissolve can be **dissolved by court order** on application by a shareholder or by [[CNPF]].

Regulated sectors (banking, insurance, payment institutions) impose far higher sector-specific capital requirements that displace the general rules.

## Mechanics
- **Contributions** may be in cash or in kind; **in-kind contributions require valuation**, and over-valuation is a classic route to director and founder liability.
- **Increase** — new contributions or capitalisation of reserves/profits. Existing members typically hold **pre-emption rights**; their disapplication is a minority-protection flashpoint.
- **Reduction** — creditor-protective procedure with notice and objection rights, because reduction directly shrinks the fenced-off amount.
- **Net assets below share capital** — triggers a duty to act: recapitalise, reduce capital, or reorganise/dissolve. This is the practical trigger that makes the figure legally live, and it is where the concept connects to [[Concept — Insolvabilitate|insolvency]].

## Notes / conclusions
> Do not read the capital figure as a measure of substance — a company with MDL 600,000 of registered capital may have no assets. Read it as (a) a rights-allocation key and (b) a trigger level for the net-assets duty. For creditworthiness, go to the financial statements. → [[Concept — Situații financiare]]

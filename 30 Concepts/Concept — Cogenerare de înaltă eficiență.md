# Concept — Cogenerare de înaltă eficiență

**RO:** cogenerare de înaltă eficiență · **EN:** high-efficiency cogeneration (HE CHP)

## Definition
Simultaneous production of heat and electricity (or mechanical energy) in one process that meets the **efficiency / primary-energy-savings criteria** in [[Legea 92-2014 — energia termica si cogenerarea (notă)|L92/2014]] **annexes 1–2**. Only electricity attributable to that HE process is eligible for **guarantees of origin** under the L92 Cap. IV stack.
> L92 art. 5 (definitions) · arts. 12–14 · annexes 1–2.

## How it works
1. **Classify** the Configuration’s technology (CCGT with heat recovery, back-pressure / extraction steam, GT+HR, ICE, microturbines, Stirling, fuel cells, steam engines, ORC, other CHP — HG 197 list).
2. **Measure** actual operating data for the GO period; keep records **≥ 5 years**.
3. Apply [[HG 197-2025 — metodologie cogenerare inalta eficienta (notă)|HG 197/2025]] five-stage method: global efficiency → CHP electricity (with / without high global-efficiency path) → default power/heat ratio → **HE CHP electricity + PES**.
4. GO-eligible quantity is capped by electricity **actually delivered/sold** from the Configuration (methodology carve-outs for non-eligible own use).
5. **GOs** issued under HANRE **201/2017** *(procedure not yet ingested)* — cannot be claimed for HE CHP electricity produced **outside** Moldova (L92 art. 14(3)).

## Notes / conclusions
> HE CHP is a **qualification + metering doctrine**, not a market segment. Heat stays under L92 public-service tariffs; electricity follows L164. Do not treat “cogeneration plant” as automatically GO-eligible without annex thresholds and HG 197 math.

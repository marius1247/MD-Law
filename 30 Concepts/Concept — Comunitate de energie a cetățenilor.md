# Concept — Comunitate de energie a cetățenilor

**RO:** comunitate de energie a cetățenilor (CEC) · **EN:** citizen energy community

## Definition
A legal person that cumulatively: (a) is based on **voluntary, open participation** and is **effectively controlled** by natural persons, first-level local authorities, and/or micro and small companies; (b) has as **primary purpose** environmental, economic or social benefits for members/associates or local areas **rather than financial profit**; (c) may engage in generation (incl. RES), distribution, supply, consumption, aggregation, storage, efficiency, EV charging or other energy services for members.
> Defined in [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (text)#Articolul 2. Noțiuni|L164 art. 2 pt. 21]] · regime [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (text)#Articolul 125. Comunitățile de energie ale cetățenilor|arts. 125–126]].

## How it works
**Registration:** CEC status is obtained by entry in ANRE’s **Register of citizen energy communities**, under an ANRE regulation (art. 125(9)). Without registration, do not treat a co-op or SPV as a CEC for L164 rights.

**Control / purpose filters:** micro/small companies may participate only if CEC membership is **not** their main business (art. 125(2)). For-profit legal forms must state the non-profit-primary purpose expressly in constitutive documents (art. 125(3)).

**Activities (art. 125(12)–(14)):** production (incl. RES), energy sharing, storage, distribution, supply, aggregation, flexibility/balancing services, efficiency, EV charging, other energy services to members — plus optional RES-community activities under L10 art. 39⁵.

**Licensing interaction (art. 20(9) area):** if the CEC wants to **supply or aggregate**, or holds generation ≥ **5 MW**, storage ≥ **1 MW** injection, or integrated plant+storage ≥ 5 MW injection, it must obtain the corresponding licence — with a statutory carve-out from one ordinary licensing condition (capital/form — verify exact alin. at source).

**Member rights:** members may still choose an external supplier; if the CEC operates a distribution network, it must recalculate the tariff to network costs only for that member and must not obstruct switching (art. 126).

## Notes / conclusions
> The **“not primarily for profit” + member-control** filters are the anti-abuse core. Ordinary commercial generation wearing a co-op label fails the definition. ANRE’s implementing regulation and register are the operational gate — statute alone is not enough to claim CEC status.

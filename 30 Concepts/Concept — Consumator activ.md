# Concept — Consumator activ

**RO:** consumator activ · **EN:** active customer / active consumer

## Definition
A final customer (or jointly acting group) that **consumes or stores** self-generated or shared electricity, **sells** self-generated electricity, or **participates in flexibility / energy-efficiency programmes**, provided those activities are **not its main commercial or professional activity**.
> Defined in [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (text)#Articolul 2. Noțiuni|L164 art. 2 pt. 27]] · rights regime [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (text)#Articolul 122. Consumatorii activi|art. 122]].

## How it works
**Rights (art. 122(2)):** operate directly or through aggregation; sell self-produced electricity including via bilaterals; join flexibility and efficiency mechanisms; delegate plant management to a third party (that third party is **not** itself an active consumer); pay cost-reflective T/D tariffs that **separately account** injection and withdrawal.

**Balance responsibility (art. 122(3)):** active consumers are financially responsible for imbalances — they act as [[Concept — Parte responsabilă de echilibrare|PRE]] or delegate to one.

**Storage (art. 122(4)):** connection rights under arts. 71–73 / connection regulation; **no double network charges** for stored energy remaining on site or when providing flexibility; may provide multiple services if technically feasible. Storage ≥ **1 MW** injection may still need a storage licence → [[Concept — Stocare a energiei]].

**Capacity cap (art. 122(5)):** ANRE sets, by decision, the **maximum generation capacity by technology** final customers may install as active consumers.

**Energy sharing (art. 123):** households, micro/SMEs and public authorities may share RES electricity within a DSO area (or other ANRE-set geography). Sharing is **not supply** and needs **no supply licence**.

**Bridge to prosumers (art. 122(6)):** an active consumer may become a RES **prosumer** under [[Legea 10-2016 — surse regenerabile (notă)|L10/2016]] art. 39¹ and then **may not** engage in activities outside that L10 regime. → [[Concept — Facturare netă]]

## Notes / conclusions
> Do not collapse **consumator activ** into **prosumer**. Active consumer is the L164 market-participation status; prosumer/net billing is the L10 support overlay. A client can be one, both (if L10 conditions met), or move between them — the licence and balancing consequences differ.

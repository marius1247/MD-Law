# Concept — Contract de performanță energetică

**RO:** contract de performanță energetică · **EN:** energy performance contract (EPC)

> Defined in [[Legea 139-2018 — eficienta energetica (text)#Articolul 3]]; mandatory content and public/regulated-undertaking rules in [[Legea 139-2018 — eficienta energetica (text)#Articolul 23]]. Market access: art. 22. → [[Legea 139-2018 — eficienta energetica (notă)]]

## How it works
An EPC pays the provider wholly or partly from the **agreed energy-efficiency improvement / financial savings** achieved. Art. 23(5) requires, among other things: measures, **guaranteed savings**, measurement & verification methods, baseline, price/payment terms, monitoring, **penalties**, change/termination rules, subcontracting and dispute resolution.

**Public clients** must budget next-year financial obligations under EPCs (art. 23(6)). **Regulated energy undertakings** may enter EPCs; [[ANRE]] reviews related investment plans; tariff recognition is capped to **monetary savings confirmed by [[CNED]]** (art. 23(7)–(12)).

[[CNED]] publishes model EPCs, an EPC guide, and a public ESCO list (art. 22(4)).

## Hard edges
- Obstruction of the energy-services market by network operators/suppliers is a competition issue (art. 22(3), art. 28(7)) → [[Consiliul Concurenței]] · [[Concept — Poziție dominantă]].
- Building-level EPCs also depend on Law 282/2023 (performance of buildings) — not yet in vault.

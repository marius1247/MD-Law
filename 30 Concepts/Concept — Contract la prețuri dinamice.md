# Concept — Contract la prețuri dinamice

**RO:** contract de furnizare a energiei electrice la prețuri dinamice · **EN:** dynamic electricity price supply contract

## Definition
A supply contract between a supplier and a final customer whose price **reflects short-term wholesale market variation** — including day-ahead and intraday — at intervals **at least equal to the market settlement intervals**.
> Defined in [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (text)#Articolul 2. Noțiuni|L164 art. 2 pt. 33]] · rights/duties [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (text)#Articolul 119. Dreptul la un contract de furnizare|art. 119]].

Paired statutory product (same article): **fixed-term fixed-price contract** (art. 2 pt. 34) — terms including price stay unchanged for the term (≥ **1 year** entitlement); may include a flexible peak/off-peak element; bill changes only from non-supplier elements (taxes/charges).

## How it works
**Who may request dynamic prices (art. 119(1)):** final customers with a **smart metering system** installed.

**Supplier offer duties:**
- Large suppliers (**> 200 000** final customers) must offer fixed-term fixed-price contracts on request; publish general T&Cs by end of December and June each year and file them with ANRE (art. 119(2)).
- ANRE may exempt such a supplier if it offers **only** dynamic contracts, competition is not harmed, and enough fixed-price offers remain on the market (art. 119(3)).

**Consumer protection:**
- Pre-contract **summary** of main terms in clear language (total price & components; whether fixed/variable/dynamic; contact; one-offs/promos) — art. 119(4).
- **Express consent** required before concluding a dynamic-price contract (art. 119(7)).
- Suppliers must explain advantages, costs, risks, and metering needs; **no unilateral amendment or early termination** of fixed-term fixed-price contracts by the supplier (art. 119(6)).
- Fixed-price customers retain rights to energy sharing, demand response and flexibility participation (art. 119(5)).

**Secondary act:** principles/procedure sit in the electricity **supply regulation** (art. 119(8)) — currently [[HANRE 169-2019 — furnizarea energiei electrice (notă)|HANRE 169/2019]], with L164 currency risk until re-adoption.

**Supervision:** ANRE monitors abusive practices and early-termination fees; for ≥ **10 years** after dynamic contracts become available, publishes an annual report on offers, bill impact and volatility (art. 119(9)). Comparison tools must cover dynamic offers for households and micro/small companies below 100 MWh/year (art. 113 area).

## Notes / conclusions
> Dynamic price = retail product that **passes through** PZU/PI volatility. Advise only where smart metering exists and the client accepts imbalance/timing risk; otherwise steer to the fixed-term fixed-price entitlement. Do not treat HANRE 169 silence as absence of the right — L164 art. 119 is already in force.

# Concept — Criterii de durabilitate biocarburanți

**RO:** criterii de durabilitate și de reducere a emisiilor de gaze cu efect de seră (biocarburanți, biolichide, combustibili din biomasă) · **EN:** sustainability and GHG-emission-saving criteria for biofuels, bioliquids and biomass fuels

## Definition
The set of **land-use / biodiversity / forestry** conditions and **minimum GHG emission savings** that biofuels, bioliquids and biomass fuels must meet so that their energy may be **taken into account** for (a) national RES targets, (b) RES consumption obligations, and (c) eligibility for financial support — under [[Legea 10-2016 — surse regenerabile (notă)|L10/2016]] art. **26¹(2)**.
> Operationalised by [[HG 53-2025 — durabilitate biocarburanti emisii GES (notă)|HG 53/2025]] (regulation + GHG methodology; RED II / EnC 2021/14/MC-EnC + Delegated Reg. 2019/807).

## How it works
1. **Produce / import** the fuel under ordinary commercial rules (petroleum channel often via [[Legea 461-2001 — piata produselor petroliere (notă)|L461]] importers).
2. Run a **mass-balance** system so batch sustainability/GHG attributes survive mixing (art. 26²(3)).
3. Prove compliance via **voluntary / recognised certification schemes** and file information with the **Environment Agency**; arrange **annual independent assessment** (art. 26²(4)–(7)).
4. Classify feedstock: HG 53 Annex 2 **§1 advanced** (double-count path) vs **§2 capped** (UCO / specified animal fats) vs Annex 3 **high-ILUC** (excluded unless low-ILUC-risk certified).
5. Apply GHG methodology (HG 53 annex methodology) — typical vs actual values; energy content factors (Annex 1) align with [[HG 74-2025 — calcul consum energie regenerabila (notă)|HG 74]] statistics practice.
6. Only then claim the volume toward targets / importer obligation fulfilment / support eligibility.

## Governing provisions
- [[Legea 10-2016 — surse regenerabile (notă)|L10]] arts. **26¹–26²**, 25, 26⁷ · enabling for HG: arts. 10(k2), 12(a), 33(2)
- [[HG 53-2025 — durabilitate biocarburanti emisii GES (notă)|HG 53/2025]] — IF ≈ **11.02.2026** (12 months after MO 11.02.2025); abrogates HG 107/2019
- HG 53 **pct. 2** (RFNBO ≥70% GHG cut from **1 Jan 2026**) — adjacent, not a biofuel feedstock rule → [[Energetică — hidrogen gap stub (notă)]]

## Notes / conclusions
> **Counting gate, not a sales ban.** Non-compliant volumes can still move commercially; they simply fail L10 art. 26¹(2) purposes. The recurring malpractice is claiming advanced contribution from capped or high-ILUC streams without Annex 2/3 matching.

# Concept — Eficiența energetică înainte de toate

**RO:** eficiența energetică înainte de toate · **EN:** energy efficiency first

> Anchored in [[Legea 139-2018 — eficienta energetica (text)#Articolul 1]] as part of the law’s purpose — creating the legal frame for promoting the principle. Broader policy architecture: arts. 4–7¹. → [[Legea 139-2018 — eficienta energetica (notă)]]

## How it works
In the EU / Energy Community EED vocabulary, “energy efficiency first” means considering cost-effective EE solutions **before** investing in new supply-side capacity. Moldova’s L139 states the principle expressly but does **not** enact a self-contained administrative test that every infrastructure or licensing decision must document an EE alternative.

**Practical use today:** interpretive principle + NECP / programme design driver (arts. 5–7¹), not a freestanding injunction. Harden it only where a secondary act, tender rule, or financing condition makes EE assessment mandatory.

# Concept — Facturare netă

**RO:** facturare netă · **EN:** net billing

## Definition
Prosumer settlement under [[Legea 10-2016 — surse regenerabile (notă)|L10/2016]] where surplus electricity injected to the grid is valued/credited against consumption over a settlement period — distinct from the **eligible-producer** support track. Capacity available for the mechanism is capped by Government quotas (HG 401/2021 as patched).

## How it works
* Government sets **capacity quotas / individual ceilings** (HG 401/2021; [[HG 329-2025 — modificare HG 401-2021 cote capacitate regenerabile (notă)|HG 329/2025]] refreshes annex 2 and multi-site rules).
* Hard practical cap for multi-site / off-site configurations under HG 329: **≤ 200 kW**.
* Connection / metering: [[HANRE 311-2026 — racordarea la retelele electrice (notă)|HANRE 311/2026]] (historic patches in [[HANRE 833-2023 — modificarea unor hotarari ANRE regenerabile (notă)|833/2023]]).
* CNED-type body monitors commissioning and alerts ME at **90%** of the net-billing quota (HG 329 pt. 1.1).

# Concept — Facturare netă (net billing)

**RO:** facturare netă · **EN:** net billing

## Legal home
Primary rules in [[Legea 10-2016 — surse regenerabile (notă)|L10/2016]] (prosumer / net-billing mechanism). Government sets aggregate quotas and individual capacity ceilings:
* Legacy envelope to **31.12.2025:** [[HG 401-2021 — cote capacitate facturare neta (notă)|HG 401/2021]] annex 2 (**100 MW** aggregate; household ≤10 kW or consumption formula).
* 2030 package: [[HG 599-2025 — limite capacitate regenerabile 2030 (notă)|HG 599/2025]] (annex 1 support quotas in vault; net-billing annex 2 still missing).

Connection / contractual interface: [[HANRE 311-2026 — racordarea la retelele electrice (notă)|HANRE 311/2026]]; supply-side patches historically via [[HANRE 833-2023 — modificarea unor hotarari ANRE regenerabile (notă)|833/2023]].

## Definition
Monetary netting for **prosumers**: electricity injected to the DSO network is valued and credited against electricity consumed, within an **individual capacity ceiling** and the Government’s aggregate quota. Distinct from **eligible-producer** support ([[Concept — Producător eligibil]]) — no fixed tariff / fixed price support, lower administrative burden, quota-limited.

## Sizing formulas (HG 401/2021 annex 2 — through 2025)
Households choose ≤10 kW **or** `P = E_max / k`; budget entities use max(peak 3-year consumption, contracted power × OSD category average) / `k`, with **k** = 1200 (PV), 1600 (wind), 6100 (biogas) kWh/kW·year.

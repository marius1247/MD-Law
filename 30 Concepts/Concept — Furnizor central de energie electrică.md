# Concept — Furnizor central de energie electrică

**RO:** furnizor central de energie electrică (FCEE) · **EN:** central electricity supplier / central buyer

## Definition
A Government-designated entity that **buys** electricity from **eligible RES producers** (and, for a defined period, from **urban district-heating CHP** plants) and **resells** it on the electricity market at **ANRE-approved regulated prices**.
> Defined in [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (text)#Articolul 2. Noțiuni|L164 art. 2 pt. 55]]. Support gate: [[Concept — Producător eligibil]] under [[Legea 10-2016 — surse regenerabile (notă)|L10/2016]].

## How it works
**Buy side:** offtake from eligible RES (and transitional urban CHP) under the support scheme — not a free commercial PPA choice for those volumes.

**Sell side (art. 87(2)):** FCEE buys from eligible RES plants and from **urban district-heating CHP** plants and **resells to suppliers** under an ANRE-pre-set algorithm, at ANRE-approved regulated prices (L164 + L10 + market rules). **Suppliers must buy** the ANRE-allocated quantities according to their **retail market shares**, at those regulated prices. This is a **mandatory offtake / mandatory purchase** couple — not a voluntary wholesale product.

**Balancing:** under [[HANRE 283-2020 — Regulile pietei energiei electrice (notă)|HANRE 283]], FCEE acts as balance-responsible party for the balancing group of eligible RES producers that transfer balancing responsibility to it (intraday-market dependent rules in the consolidation).

**Licence / PSO continuity:** FCEE is a critical designation — licence suspension/withdrawal triggers ANRE designation of a replacement under public-service obligations (arts. 21–23). Government may institute an undertaking to perform the FCEE function on ANRE’s referral (art. 4(1)(f)).

## Sunset / transition (resolved from L164 art. 150)

| Track | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Eligible RES → CfD** | Art. 9(1)(l) FCEE regulated-price approval + FCEE buy/resell duty + suppliers’ correlative purchase duty under art. 87(2) **cease** once eligible RES plants move to **contracts for difference** under L10 | Art. **150(3)** |
| **Urban CHP exit calendar** | By **1 January 2026**, ANRE must adopt a **calendar** excluding urban district-heating CHP from the support mechanism in arts. 9(1)(l), 87(2) and 129(2)(a),(g) — including FCEE buy/resell and suppliers’ correlative purchase of that CHP electricity | Art. **150(4)** |

> [!warning] Calendar ≠ automatic exit
> Art. 150(4) is a **duty on ANRE to set a phasing calendar**, not an automatic statutory cut-off of urban CHP offtake on 1 Jan 2026. Advise from the **published ANRE calendar** (if adopted) plus art. 150(4); do not invent an end date that the statute does not fix.

## Notes / conclusions
> FCEE is the **commercial hinge** of the support scheme: bankability = FCEE credit + regulated resale economics + BRP allocation + connection queue + **supplier purchase obligation**. When CfDs replace FCEE offtake for RES (art. 150(3)), re-paper every eligible-producer structure. For urban CHP, track the **ANRE exit calendar** under art. 150(4) and the heat-continuity constraints in L92 / L164 redispach rules — do not treat FCEE offtake as perpetual.

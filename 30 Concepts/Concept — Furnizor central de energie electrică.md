# Concept — Furnizor central de energie electrică

**RO:** furnizor central de energie electrică (FCEE) · **EN:** central electricity supplier / central buyer

## Definition
A Government-designated entity that **buys** electricity from **eligible RES producers** (and, for a defined period, from **urban district-heating CHP** plants) and **resells** it on the electricity market at **ANRE-approved regulated prices**.
> Defined in [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (text)#Articolul 2. Noțiuni|L164 art. 2 pt. 55]]. Support gate: [[Concept — Producător eligibil]] under [[Legea 10-2016 — surse regenerabile (notă)|L10/2016]].

## How it works
**Buy side:** offtake from eligible RES (and transitional urban CHP) under the support scheme — not a free commercial PPA choice for those volumes.

**Sell side:** resale into the market (bilaterals / organised markets) at regulated prices; suppliers have correlated purchase obligations for allocated shares under market rules / L164 art. 87(2) path (verify operative text when advising).

**Balancing:** under [[HANRE 283-2020 — Regulile pietei energiei electrice (notă)|HANRE 283]], FCEE acts as balance-responsible party for the balancing group of eligible RES producers that transfer balancing responsibility to it (intraday-market dependent rules in the consolidation).

**Licence / PSO continuity:** FCEE is a critical designation — licence suspension/withdrawal triggers ANRE designation of a replacement under public-service obligations (L164 arts. 22–23 area). Government may institute an undertaking to perform the FCEE function on ANRE’s referral (art. 4 path).

**Sunset / transition (L164 final provisions):**
- FCEE regulated-price approval and the duty to buy/resell **eligible RES** cease once those plants move to **contracts for difference** under L10 (art. transitional — verify current alin.).
- By **1 Jan 2026** ANRE was to set a calendar phasing **urban CHP** out of the FCEE support mechanism.

## Notes / conclusions
> FCEE is the **commercial hinge** of the support scheme: bankability = FCEE credit + regulated resale economics + BRP allocation + connection queue. When CfDs replace FCEE offtake for RES, re-paper every eligible-producer structure — do not assume perpetual central-buyer offtake.

# Concept — Furnizor de servicii de echilibrare

**RO:** furnizor de servicii de echilibrare (FSE) · **EN:** balancing service provider (BSP)

## Definition
A market participant **technically qualified** and contracted to offer **balancing energy and/or balancing capacity** to the TSO (OST) on the balancing energy market (PEE), under [[HANRE 853-2025 — clauze echilibrare FSE si PRE (notă)|HANRE 853/2025]] and [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)|L164/2025]].

## How it works
1. **Technical qualification** with the OST (FSE regulation Ch. III).
2. Optional **balancing-capacity** contract (Ch. VII) — OST procures reserves.
3. **Bid** balancing energy on the PEE for each product / ISP.
4. **Activation / settlement / invoicing** under Ch. IV–V; post **payment guarantees**.
5. Special track for **frequency-containment reserve (RSF)** (Ch. IX).

Distinct from [[Concept — Parte responsabilă de echilibrare|PRE]]: the FSE *sells* balancing products; the PRE *pays* for imbalances in its perimeter. The same entity may hold both roles.

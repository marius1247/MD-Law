# Concept — Furnizor de ultimă opțiune

**RO:** furnizor de ultimă opțiune · **EN:** supplier of last resort

> Defined in [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (text)|L164/2025]] art. 2 pt. 57; operative regime art. **115** (and retail procedure in the supply regulation). Gas parallel under [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)|L108/2016]] + [[HANRE 113-2019 — furnizarea gazelor naturale (notă)|HANRE 113/2019]] / [[HANRE 177-2026 — modificarea unor hotarari ANRE (notă)|177/2026]].

> [!warning] Do not use L107 for live advice
> [[Legea 107-2016 — energia electrica (text)|L107/2016]] is **abrogated**. Old 90-day last-resort wording in [[HANRE 169-2019 — furnizarea energiei electrice (notă)|HANRE 169/2019]] is **stale** — L164 art. 115 gives consumers last-resort supply for **at least 6 months**.

## Definition
A supplier **designated to supply final consumers of a supplier that has ceased activity** (licence suspension/withdrawal, insolvency, inability to supply). Contingency mechanism that makes liberalisation of an essential service safe.

## Distinguish from *universal service*
| | Universal service (art. 114) | Last resort (art. 115) |
|---|---|---|
| Trigger | Standing entitlement | Supplier failure / inability |
| Who | Households + **micro and small** companies | Final consumers who lost their supplier |
| Character | Ongoing PSO | Bridge — temporary |

Often the same undertaking holds both designations; the obligations remain distinct.

## How it works (electricity — L164)
- Designation / territory by [[ANRE]]; US and LR suppliers must contract on request with eligible consumers (art. 108(7)).
- Procurement: bilaterals + organised markets (PZU/PI/POCB) under the market rules; US/LR must buy at lowest transparent cost (art. 108(5)–(6)) → [[HANRE 283-2020 — Regulile pietei energiei electrice (notă)]] · [[Concept — Piața organizată a contractelor bilaterale]].
- On licence suspension/withdrawal of US/LR/central buyer/TSO/DSO/OPEE, ANRE designates a replacement under PSO (arts. 22–23 machinery).
- Retail mechanics (deemed contract, notices, billing) still detailed in HANRE 169 — apply **only where consistent with L164**.

## Gas note
PSO withdrawal for large industrials (target **1 Apr 2026**) narrows public-service protection; mid/large non-households are pushed to competitive supply under [[HANRE 177-2026 — modificarea unor hotarari ANRE (notă)|177/2026]].

Regulated **FUO / PSO supply prices**: [[HANRE 355-2021 — preturi reglementate furnizare gaze (notă)|HANRE 355/2021]] (+ [[HANRE 540-2024 — modificare Metodologie preturi furnizare gaze (notă)|540/2024]]). FUO prices approved monthly (by the 25th; IF on the 1st).

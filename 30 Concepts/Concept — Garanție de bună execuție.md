# Concept — Garanție de bună execuție

**RO:** garanție de bună execuție · **EN:** performance guarantee / performance bond

## Definition
Security that the economic operator must furnish at signature of a public-procurement contract to cover proper performance. Under L131/2015 art. 68 as amended by [[Legea 41-2026 — suport desfasurare afaceri (notă)|L41/2026 Art. V]], the contracting authority sets **issuer requirements, amount and basic terms**, but **may not impose the form**.

## How it works
The operator chooses one of (art. 68(9¹)):

1. Bank or insurance **letter of guarantee**
2. **Transfer** to the authority’s account
3. **Pledge (cauțiune)**
4. Successive retentions to a **special bank account** agreed by both parties
5. Successive retentions **directly** from invoice payments

Unlawful qualification/selection criteria that block access to award procedures are separately sanctioned under Contravențional art. 327¹(4) as rewritten by L41 Art. IV (fine 60–200 c.u.).

## Notes / conclusions
> If a tender dossier mandates a single bond form, raise a clarification citing art. 68(9)/(9¹). Form choice is a statutory right of the operator, not a courtesy of the authority.

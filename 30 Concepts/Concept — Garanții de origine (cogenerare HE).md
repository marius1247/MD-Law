# Concept — Garanții de origine (cogenerare de înaltă eficiență)

**RO:** garanții de origine pentru energia electrică din cogenerare de înaltă eficiență · **EN:** guarantees of origin for high-efficiency CHP electricity

## Definition
Electronic certificates tracking the **high-efficiency cogeneration** attribute of electricity, distinct from (but parallel to) renewable GOs under [[Legea 10-2016 — surse regenerabile (notă)|L10/2016]]. Quantity of HE-CHP electricity that may be certified is set by Government methodology under [[Legea 92-2014 — energia termica si cogenerarea (notă)|L92/2014]].

## How it works
1. Plant produces combined heat & power; only the **HE share** of electricity qualifies.
2. HE share is computed against **harmonised efficiency reference values** (HG 297/2016 as updated) and the quantification methodology.
3. [[HG 197-2025 — metodologie cogenerare inalta eficienta garantii origine (notă)|HG 197/2025]] approves that methodology (annex still missing in vault) and updates reference-value tables per Delegated Reg. (EU) **2023/2104**.
4. ANRE must align secondary acts for certification (HG 197 pt. 4).

## Notes / conclusions
> Do not equate RES GOs with HE-CHP GOs. Construction-year band (pre-2016 / 2016–2023 / from 2024) can change the reference efficiency and thus the certifiable volume.

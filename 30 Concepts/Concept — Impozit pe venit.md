# Concept — Impozit pe venit

**RO:** impozit pe venit · **EN:** income tax (corporate and personal)

## Definition
The tax on income, governed by **Titlul II of the Codul fiscal nr. 1163/1997**. Moldova uses **one Titlu for both** corporate and personal income tax, with common rules on source, residence and deductions and separate rate schedules.

## Rates *(verified July / Aug 2026 — re-verify, these change annually)*

**Companies**
| Regime | Rate | Base |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | **12%** | Taxable profit |
| Agricultural | **7%** | Taxable profit of farming enterprises |
| **Small business regime** | **4%** | **Turnover**, for qualifying SMEs **not registered for VAT** |
| **IT Park resident** | **7%** | **Turnover** — a single tax replacing most others |

**Individuals**
- **12%** on taxable income, after personal exemptions
- Plus the payroll wedge: **CNAS 24%** (employer) and **AOAM 9%** (employee)
- **Independent entrepreneurs (cap. 10⁴):** 15% up to 1.2 MDL m; **35%** on the excess

**Dividends: 6% withholding**, residents and non-residents alike; treaty relief available for non-residents on production of a residency certificate.

> [!danger] 2027 draft is NOT live
> The Government **politica fiscală 2027** project proposes an **Estonian-style** corporate tax on **distributed profit** (indicative draft rates ~15% CIT / progressive PIT 7%+15%). **Do not advise as enacted.** Contingency modelling only — see [[Fiscalitate & Contabilitate — horizon and gaps]].

## From accounting profit to taxable profit
Taxable profit is **not** accounting profit. It is accounting profit adjusted, principally for:

- **non-deductible expenses** — expenses not incurred in the business, and categories capped or disallowed by the Code
- **depreciation** — fiscal depreciation follows its own rules and diverges from book depreciation
- **provisions and reserves** — generally not deductible until realised
- **related-party transactions** — arm's-length adjustment
- **loss carry-forward** — available over a statutory number of years
- **art. 87(11) deferral** — timing of *payment* (not computation) until distribution, for qualifying SMEs through 2026

Keep the reconciliation. It is the first document requested in a fiscal control. → [[Contabilitate & raportare financiară — synthesis]] · [[Concept — Situații financiare]]

## 4% SME / VAT interaction
The **4% turnover regime** requires staying **outside VAT registration**. The 2026 VAT threshold is **1.5 MDL m**. Crossing the VAT line usually kills the 4% regime — model both decisions together, never sequentially. → [[Concept — TVA]]

## The structuring question this concept exists to answer
How should an owner-manager extract value from the company?

| Route | Approximate burden |
|---|---|
| **Salary** | 12% PIT + 9% AOAM (employee) + **24% CNAS on top** (employer) |
| **Dividend** | **12% CIT** on profit, then **6%** on the distribution → **~17.3%** combined |
| **Deferred CIT + later dividend** | Art. 87(11) defers CIT cash until distribution (eligibility constraints) |
| **Small business regime** | **4% of turnover**, then 6% on distribution — transformative for low-margin, non-VAT businesses |
| **IT Park** | **7% of turnover**, single tax |
| **Independent entrepreneur** | 15%/35% single tax — micro-service channel; recharacterisation risk |

Dividends are materially cheaper than salary in almost every configuration. The constraints are non-tax: distributable profit must exist, social contributions build pension entitlement, and the substance of the arrangement has to hold up. **Model the whole chain, never a single rate.**

## Withholding on outbound payments
Payments to non-residents — dividends, interest, royalties, certain services — carry withholding at domestic rates unless reduced by a **double tax treaty**. Two practical rules: obtain the **residency certificate before payment**, not after; and check beneficial-ownership and anti-abuse conditions in the treaty.

## Notes / conclusions
> The headline 12% is not the number that matters. What matters is the **combination**: which regime applies, what the base is (profit or turnover), whether art. 87(11) deferral is available, and what the extraction route costs on top. Never treat the 2027 Estonian draft as current law.

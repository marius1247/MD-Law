# Concept — Indicatori de calitate (SAIDI / SAIFI / CAIDI / MAIFI)

**RO:** indicatori generali / garantați de continuitate · **EN:** continuity-of-supply indices

## Legal home
Defined and applied in [[HANRE 537-2020 — calitate servicii transport distributie EE (text)|HANRE 537/2020]] (QoS regulation for electricity TSO/DSO), with compensation / tariff-reduction magnitudes in its Annex 3. Patched by [[HANRE 833-2023 — modificarea unor hotarari ANRE regenerabile (notă)|833/2023]]. Enabling historically under L107/2016 art. 54 → check [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)|L164/2025]].

## Definition
* **SAIDI** — average interruption **duration** per system user (long interruptions >3 min).
* **SAIFI** — average interruption **frequency** per system user.
* **CAIDI** — average duration of **one** interruption for an affected user (= SAIDI/SAIFI).
* **MAIFI** — average frequency of **short** interruptions (≤3 min, >1 s).

Planned and unplanned interruptions are recorded and scored **separately**. Guaranteed individual limits (max duration of a long interruption; max annual unplanned long interruptions) vary by voltage level and urban/rural zone.

## Why it matters
These indices are not statistics only — breach triggers **individual compensations** and/or **regulated tariff reductions**. Force majeure, special weather, and classified exceptional market situations carve out liability.

# Concept — Instituție publică

**RO:** instituție publică · **EN:** public institution

## Definition
A **public-law legal person** created by an act of a public authority, that does **not** carry on commercial activity and operates under the conditions set by normative acts.

> Restated in [[Legea 76-2026 — ajustare legislatie institutii publice (text)#Art. I.|L76/2026 Art. I]] rewriting Civil Code art. 307(1); detailed category framework sits in **Law 75/2026** *(not yet ingested)*.

## How it works
- **Creation** by public-authority act (not private constitutive documents).
- **Registration / evidence:** from publication of L76 Art. II, public legal persons follow a Government-set **evidență** procedure at the state-registration body — not the ordinary company path ([[Legea 220-2007 — inregistrarea de stat (notă)|L220]]).
- **Governance** hooks in L98/2012 (as rewritten) and civil-service rules for council membership (L76 Arts. III–IV).
- General IF for the Civil Code / administration rewrite: **1 Jan 2027**.

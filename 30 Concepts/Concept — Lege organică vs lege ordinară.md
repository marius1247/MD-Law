# Concept — Lege organică vs lege ordinară

**RO:** lege organică / lege ordinară · **EN:** organic law / ordinary law

## Definition
Under the [[Constituția RM (text)|Constitution]] and [[Legea 100-2017 — actele normative (text)|Legea nr. 100/2017]], Moldovan parliamentary legislation is split by subject matter and adoption majority into **organic** and **ordinary** laws. Organic laws rank above ordinary laws in the normative hierarchy.

> Hierarchy overview: [[Ierarhia actelor normative]].

## How it works
- **Organic laws** — reserved domains listed in the Constitution (e.g. electoral system, organisation of Government, organic regimes for rights); adopted by a higher majority.
- **Ordinary laws** — residual parliamentary competence; must not contradict organic law or the Constitution.

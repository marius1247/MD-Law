# Concept — Licență în energetică

**RO:** licență · **EN:** licence / sectoral authorisation

> Current electricity regime: [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)|L164/2025]] arts. **18–23** (licence types, grant, amendment, suspension, withdrawal). Gas parallel: [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)|L108/2016]]. Framework: [[Legea 174-2017 — energetica (notă)|L174/2017]] · general authorisation law [[Legea 160-2011 — reglementarea prin autorizare (notă)|L160/2011]].

> [!danger] Wrong file trap
> [[HANRE 286-2018 — licentiere energie (notă)|HANRE 286/2018]] is **not** the licensing regulation — despite its vault filename. It is the procedure for licence-holders’ **tariff/price applications**. Do not cite it for licence grant/suspension.

> [!warning] L107 citations are historical only
> [[Legea 107-2016 — energia electrica (text)|L107/2016]] arts. 12–16 are abrogated. Remap every old licence opinion to L164.

## Definition
An administrative act by which [[ANRE]] authorises a person to **carry on a regulated activity** in the energy sector. It attaches to the *activity*, not to a site — that is [[Concept — Racordare la rețea|connection]].

## Licensable activities (electricity — L164)
Art. 18 types now expressly cover activities that L107 treated poorly or not at all as licensed: among others **trading, storage, aggregation**, alongside generation, transport, distribution, supply and market operation. Thresholds/exemptions — verify operative paragraphs at source before assuming an activity is licence-free.

## How it works
- **Grant / conditions / duties** — L164 licensing chapter + ANRE secondary acts; L160/2011 background for reperfectare.
- **Suspension / withdrawal** — triggers designation of a replacement for US/LR/central buyer/TSO/DSO/OPEE under PSO (L164 arts. 22–23). → [[Concept — Furnizor de ultimă opțiune]]
- **Notice before exit** — holders of critical licences must notify ANRE ≥ **3 months** before requesting suspension/withdrawal or non-renewal (L164 art. 21(6) area — verify exact alineat).
- **REMIT / market participation** — wholesale registration is a separate compliance layer → [[Energetică — licențiere cross-border și REMIT (notă)]] · [[HANRE 283-2020 — Regulile pietei energiei electrice (notă)]].

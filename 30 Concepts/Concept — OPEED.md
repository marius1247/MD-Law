# Concept — OPEED

**RO:** operator al pieței energiei electrice desemnat (OPEED) · **EN:** nominated electricity market operator (NEMO)

## Definition
The **licensed electricity market operator that ANRE designates** to perform tasks for **single day-ahead and/or intraday market coupling** (CACM transposition).
> Defined in [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (text)#Articolul 2. Noțiuni|L164 art. 2 pt. 83]] · designation regime [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (text)#Articolul 94. Desemnarea OPEED și revocarea|art. 94]].

Distinct from the plain **operator al pieței energiei electrice (OPEE)** (art. 2 pt. 82): every OPEED is an OPEE; designation adds the **coupling** mandate.

## How it works
1. **Licence** for market operation (art. 18(1)(c)) — single national licence; Government designates the OPEE entity (art. 20 path).
2. **ANRE designation as OPEED** — application under ANRE call; decision within **2 months**; initial term **4 years**, renewable if criteria still met (art. 94(2)).
3. **Criteria** (art. 94(3)): adequate IT/financial/operational resources for joint coupling; free access to information; cost efficiency + separate accounts for coupling vs other activities; adequate separation from other market participants; non-discrimination; market surveillance; transparency/confidentiality agreements with participants and TSO; clearing/settlement capability; cross-border coordination systems.
4. **Functions** — act as market coupling operator with other NEMOs; cooperate with TSO; may develop TCMs for ANRE approval; may delegate tasks but remains responsible (art. 94(9)–(12)).
5. **Costs** — recovered through ANRE-approved market-operation tariffs (art. 94(5) → art. 93).
6. **Revocation** — if non-compliance not cured within **6 months** of ANRE notice (art. 94(7)).
7. **Monopoly review** — after 4 years, then every 2 years, ANRE assesses whether to open trading services to more than one OPEED (art. 95). Foreign-designated NEMOs may offer DA/ID delivery into MD under notification rules (art. 95(6)–(9)).

## Notes / conclusions
> Until ANRE has designated an OPEED and coupling is live, treat HANRE 283 PZU/PI as the **national organised markets**, and OPEED as the **coupling end-state** already hard-coded in L164. Do not equate “OPEE runs PZU” with “OPEED designation completed” without checking ANRE’s designation decision.

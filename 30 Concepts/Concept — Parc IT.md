# Concept — Parc IT (IT Park resident)

**RO:** parc pentru tehnologia informației / rezident al parcului · **EN:** IT Park / IT Park resident

> Status regime created by [[Legea 77-2016 — parcuri tehnologia informatiei (notă)|Legea nr. 77/2016]], with the tax outcome mirrored in [[Codul fiscal 1163-1997 (notă)|Codul fiscal Titlul X]]. Activity list updated by [[Legea 125-2024 — modificare parc IT si tranzitii vamale (notă)|LP125/2024]].

## Definition
A **resident** of a Government-created IT Park who contracts with the Park Administration and carries on one or more **art. 8 principal activities**. Residency is what unlocks the **7% single tax on sales revenue** (subject to the per-employee monthly floor) — not mere software CAEM codes outside the park.

## How it works
- **Admission:** register with Park Administration under the HG resident-registration regulation; contract ≥ 4 years.
- **Tax:** **7%** of *venit din vânzări*, ≥ **30% of forecast average monthly wage × employees** each month; bundles CIT, payroll PIT, social + health contributions (employer+employee), local taxes, real-estate tax, road tax. **VAT and other taxes** stay ordinary.
- **Employees:** insured income for social benefits = **68%** of forecast average wage — disclose in writing before hire / before residency.
- **Keep status:** annual audit verification of indicators; failure can trigger withdrawal (art. 19) and ordinary-tax recalculation (art. 15(5)).
- **Stability:** art. 15(4) protects the regime’s rate/composition logic through **2035** if later acts try to change/cancel it.

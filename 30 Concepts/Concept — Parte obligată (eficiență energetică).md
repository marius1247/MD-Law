# Concept — Parte obligată (eficiență energetică)

**RO:** parte obligată · **EN:** obligated party (energy-efficiency obligation scheme)

> Defined in [[Legea 139-2018 — eficienta energetica (text)#Articolul 3]]; designation and duties in [[Legea 139-2018 — eficienta energetica (text)#Articolul 8]] (LP111/2025). → [[Legea 139-2018 — eficienta energetica (notă)]]

## How it works
By law, **electricity DSOs, gas DSOs and petroleum-products importers** are designated obligated parties (art. 8(1)). If Government activates the EE obligation scheme, they must **contribute financially** to annual new final-consumer energy savings, calculated by reference to average annual volumes sold/distributed/imported (art. 8(2)).

[[ANRE]] maintains and **publishes quarterly** the obligated-party list and supplies volume data. Contributions transfer to Treasury accounts on a quarterly rhythm (art. 8(6)). [[CNED]] monitors payment correctness and measures/verifies savings (Annex 2 accounting — no double counting).

## Enforcement
Late transfer → sanction of **50%** of the unpaid contribution; repeated refusal → **100%** (art. 28(1)–(2)). Payment of the sanction does **not** cancel the contribution duty; sanctions are **not** included in regulated tariffs/prices (art. 28(8)–(9)).

## Hard edges
Art. 8(9) still mentions heat-supply tariff recovery while heat undertakings are no longer designated parties after LP111 — verify the live contribution methodology before advising.

# Concept — Piața organizată a contractelor bilaterale

**RO:** piața organizată a contractelor bilaterale (POCB) · **EN:** organised bilateral contracts market

> Inserted into the Electricity Market Rules by [[HANRE 383-2026 — modificare Regulile pietei energiei electrice (notă)|HANRE 383/2026]] (pts. 32¹–32⁴¹), in force **01.07.2026**. Parent rules: [[HANRE 283-2020 — Regulile pietei energiei electrice (notă)]].

## Definition
A transparent public-auction venue operated by **OPEE** for physical-delivery bilateral electricity contracts. Voluntary participation, but gated: balancing contract with OST, OPEE registration, and POCB framework contract.

## How it works
- Portfolio-level offers (individual or aggregated); delivery period ≥ one month; optional public exchange-index price formula; quantity variation band max **25%** (or **100%** for RES producers).
- Contracts cannot quietly rewrite price, period, quantity/profile, or bolt on hidden services; unilateral termination clauses prohibited.
- Distinct from OTC negotiated bilaterals — those remain possible but must still be **registered with OPEE** (5 WD / ≥3 WD before delivery).
- System operators may use POCB only for losses / technological consumption, not for profit.

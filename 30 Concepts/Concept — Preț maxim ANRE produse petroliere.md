# Concept — Preț maxim ANRE produse petroliere

**RO:** preț maxim de comercializare cu amănuntul (produse petroliere principale de tip standard) · **EN:** ANRE maximum retail price for standard main petroleum products

## Definition
The **daily ceiling** set by [[ANRE]] for retail sale of **standard-type main petroleum products**, calculated under the Agency’s methodology and the general norms in L461 Annex 2. Retail above the published max price is prohibited.
> [[Legea 461-2001 — piata produselor petroliere (text)#Articolul 4. Formarea prețurilor la produsele petroliere|L461 art. 4]].

## How it works
1. ANRE publishes the max retail price **each working day**; validity from **00:01** of the following day(s).
2. Publication includes PL×CV indicators, VAT, excise, **specific commercial margin**, and density per product.
3. When **standard-type stocks are exhausted**, other main-product types may be sold retail **only** at ≤ the standard-type max (art. 4(2) as rewritten by [[Legea 101-2026 — consolidarea mecanismelor de interventie in situatii de criza (notă)|L101/2026]]).
4. In **alert / emergency** under [[Legea 248-2025 — managementul situatiilor de criza (notă)|L248]], CNMC may grant **temporary derogations** from art. 4 and Annex 2 norms (art. 6(7)) → [[Concept — Situație de criză în domeniul petrolier]].

# Concept — Prima AOAM

**RO:** primă de asigurare obligatorie de asistență medicală · **EN:** compulsory health-insurance premium

## Definition
The sum — either a **percentage of salary and other rewards** or a **fixed annual amount** — that a payer must remit into the compulsory health-insurance funds administered by [[CNAM]].

> Defined in [[Legea 1593-2002 — prime asigurare medicala obligatorie (text)#Articolul 3.|L1593/2002 art. 3]]; operational regime in the same act; framework parent Law **1585/1998** *(not yet ingested)*.

## How it works
Two tracks under [[Legea 1593-2002 — prime asigurare medicala obligatorie (notă)|L1593/2002]]:

| Track | Payers | Mechanics |
|---|---|---|
| **Percentage** | Annex 1 (employment / rewards) | Calculated on gross before tax withholdings; transfer + SFS return by the **25th** of the following month |
| **Fixed sum** | Annex 2 (self-employed, patent, liberal professions, residual residents, some foreigners) | Absolute amount set via L1585 + annual AOAM funds law; ordinary due date **31 March** |

**IT Park:** employment income of park residents/employees is covered through the **single tax**, not the percentage limb ([[Concept — Parc IT]] · L1593 art. 4(1¹)).

Headline vault rate for the employment limb: **~9%** employee-side — verify annually.

## Notes / conclusions
> AOAM is outside the Fiscal Code but collected and enforced like a payroll tax. Model it with CNAS as one **employment cost wedge**, and always check the IT Park single-tax split before adding percentage premiums.

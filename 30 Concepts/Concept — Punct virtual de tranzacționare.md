# Concept — Punct virtual de tranzacționare

**RO:** punct virtual de tranzacționare (PVT) · **EN:** virtual trading point (VTP)

## Definition
The **national virtual hub** of Moldova’s gas entry-exit system where wholesale title to gas is transferred **without** booking physical capacity between internal points. Under the market rules, the PVT is the **delivery point** for wholesale contracts.
> Operational home: [[HANRE 534-2019 — Regulile pietei gazelor naturale (notă)|HANRE 534/2019]] · network mechanics [[HANRE 420-2019 — Codul retelelor de gaze naturale (notă)|HANRE 420/2019]]. Frame: [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)|L108/2016]].

## How it works
1. **Entry-exit architecture:** shippers book **entry** and **exit** capacity separately (interconnections, DSO offtakes, industrial exits, storage). Internal title transfer happens at PVT.
2. **Trade notifications:** system users / [[Concept — Parte responsabilă de echilibrare|PRE]] (gas) notify commercial positions (typically D-1); the balancing entity matches and confirms PVT notifications.
3. **Balancing zone:** the MD balancing zone includes all TSO/DSO networks and the PVT; balancing period = **gas day**.
4. **Secondary capacity:** unused firm capacity can be reallocated via PVT mechanisms (subject to rules / guarantees).
5. **Conditional capacity interaction:** [[Concept — Capacitate condiționată]] Trans-Balkan products may sit **outside** the ordinary balancing portfolio; PVT access via certain IPs is operationally capped (see 310/2026 path).

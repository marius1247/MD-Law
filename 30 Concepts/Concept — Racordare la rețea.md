# Concept — Racordare la rețea

**RO:** racordare la rețea · **EN:** network connection

> Electricity connection live act: [[HANRE 311-2026 — racordarea la retelele electrice (notă)|HANRE 311/2026]] under [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)|L164/2025]]. Gas: [[HANRE 112-2019 — racordarea la retelele de gaze (notă)|HANRE 112/2019]] under [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)|L108]] (amended by 328/2025).

> [!warning] L107 / HANRE 168 are historical
> [[Legea 107-2016 — energia electrica (text)|L107]] is abrogated; [[HANRE 168-2019 — racordarea la retelele electrice (notă)|HANRE 168/2019]] is abrogated by 311/2026. Use only for legacy permits.

## Definition
The regulated procedure by which a specific installation — a consumer's premises, a generating plant — is **physically and contractually attached** to an electricity or gas network. Granted by the **network operator**, under a procedure ANRE prescribes; it is not an ANRE decision, which is why the appeal route runs operator → ANRE → court.

## The stages
1. **Application** and preliminary information from the operator
2. **Connection permit / avizul de racordare** — states the technical conditions and the connection point
3. **Design and construction** of the connection works
4. **Connection contract** and commissioning
5. **Energisation** and, for a producer, entry into the market arrangements

Each stage carries a **regulated maximum time limit**. Missing them is the operator's breach, and the time limits are the most practically useful thing in the whole regulation.

## The three fights
- **Cost allocation** — how much of the reinforcement needed to accommodate the new user falls on that user versus the general tariff base. Deep vs. shallow connection charging; this is where the money is.
- **Capacity and queue** — where network capacity is scarce, position in the queue is the asset. Speculative applications that hold capacity without building are the systemic pathology.
- **Refusal** — an operator may refuse only on grounds the regulation allows, and must reason the refusal. Unreasoned or capacity-based refusals are the standard subject of complaint to ANRE.

> [!important] Financial guarantee above 200 kW
> Applicants for and **holders of** electricity connection permits for plants above **200 kW** must lodge a **financial guarantee** (amount set by ANRE) — queue-blocking remedy. Check transitional reach to pre-reform holders in L10 / L164 consolidations and [[HANRE 311-2026 — racordarea la retelele electrice (notă)]].

## Flexible connection (L164)
**acord de racordare în condiții flexibile** (L164 art. 2 pt. 2): connection on agreed terms that include **limitation and control of injection/withdrawal**. Answer to capacity scarcity from the other side of the guarantee reform — non-firm capacity with curtailment built into the contract, potentially available years earlier than firm queue position.

**Bankability rule:** price curtailment risk in the PPA / financing model; check operationalisation in [[HANRE 311-2026 — racordarea la retelele electrice (notă)|HANRE 311]]. Do not treat a flexible connection as firm injection rights. Heat/SACET *aviz de racordare* under L92 art. 15¹ is a **different** instrument — do not conflate.

# Concept — Retragere asociat SRL

**RO:** retragerea asociatului din SRL · **EN:** withdrawal of an LLC member

## Definition
The unilateral exit of a member from a limited-liability company under [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (notă)|Legea nr. 135/2007]] art. 47¹, completed by radiation from the State Register — after notice, determination of the withdrawal value, and (from March 2026) under the evidentiary presumption introduced by [[Legea 41-2026 — suport desfasurare afaceri (notă)|L41/2026 Art. III]].

## How it works
1. Member files **withdrawal notice** with the company.
2. Company issues the **withdrawal-value certificate** in the statutory term (or a court judgment stands in for it).
3. Member applies to [[ASP]] for **radiation**; withdrawal is effective on the radiation date (L135 art. 47¹(4) as rewritten by L41).
4. **Presumption (L41):** whoever was registered as associate on the notice date is presumed associate; later challenges/exclusions do **not** suspend the company’s pay-out duties under art. 47¹(3)/(5).

## Governing provisions
- [[Legea 135-2007 — SRL (text)]] art. 47¹ — base procedure
- [[Legea 41-2026 — suport desfasurare afaceri (text)#Art. III.|L41/2026 Art. III]] — presumption + radiation effectiveness
- Register practice: [[ASP]]

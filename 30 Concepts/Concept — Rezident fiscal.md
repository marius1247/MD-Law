# Concept — Rezident fiscal

**RO:** rezident fiscal · **EN:** tax resident

## Definition
The status that determines **the extent of a person's liability to Moldovan tax**, defined in **Titlul I of the Codul fiscal**. It is the first question in any cross-border matter, and it is answered before anything else.

## What turns on it
| | **Resident** | **Non-resident** |
|---|---|---|
| Scope of taxation | **Worldwide income** | **Moldovan-source income only** |
| Collection | Self-assessment and filing | Largely by **withholding at source** |
| Treaty access | Can claim Moldovan treaty benefits abroad | Claims relief from Moldovan tax under their own treaty |
| Relief for foreign tax | Foreign tax credit | Not applicable |

## The tests
**Companies** — organisation under Moldovan law, and the place of effective management and control. A company incorporated abroad but managed from Chișinău is exposed to a residence argument; conversely, a Moldovan company genuinely managed abroad creates a dual-residence problem.

**Individuals** — domicile and **physical presence** over the tax year, with the day-count test the practical one. Presence tests are mechanical, so records matter: keep the travel evidence contemporaneously, because reconstructing it years later during an audit rarely works.

## Source rules — the other half
Residence tells you *whose worldwide income* Moldova taxes. **Source rules** tell you which income is Moldovan for a non-resident: income from activity performed in Moldova, from Moldovan property, dividends and interest paid by residents, royalties for use in Moldova. Both questions must be answered; answering only one is the classic error.

## Permanent establishment
A non-resident with a **permanent establishment** in Moldova is taxed on the profits attributable to it, roughly as a resident would be on that activity. The PE threshold — fixed place of business, or a dependent agent habitually concluding contracts — is the boundary between withholding-only exposure and full filing exposure. Construction sites and prolonged service presence are the classic triggers. Domestic PE definition: Titlul I art. 5.

## Treaties override
Moldova has a **broad double tax treaty network**. Where a treaty applies it:
- **breaks residence ties** through the tie-breaker cascade (permanent home → centre of vital interests → habitual abode → nationality → mutual agreement)
- **caps withholding rates** on dividends, interest and royalties — often well below the 6% / domestic rates
- **defines permanent establishment**, usually more narrowly than domestic law

**Two operational rules.** Obtain the **certificate of residence before payment**, not afterwards — retroactive relief is a refund claim, and refund claims are slow. And check the treaty's **beneficial ownership and anti-abuse** conditions; a conduit arrangement will not survive them.

Treaty *texts* are still a vault gap (horizon P1) — do not invent treaty rates from memory.

## Notes / conclusions
> In cross-border work, run the sequence in order and never skip a step: **residence → source → permanent establishment → treaty**. Most errors come from jumping straight to the treaty rate.

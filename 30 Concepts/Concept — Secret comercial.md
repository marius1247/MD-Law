# Concept — Secret comercial

**RO:** secret comercial · **EN:** trade secret

> Vocabulary pivot: **Legea nr. 384/2023** privind protecția secretelor comerciale (not yet ingested as `(text)`/`(notă)`). Cross-statute alignment vehicle: [[Legea 330-2025 — modificare secret comercial (notă)|Legea nr. 330/2025]].

## Definition
Information that has **commercial value because it is secret**, is subject to **reasonable steps** to keep it secret, and is protected against unlawful acquisition, use or disclosure under L384/2023 (EU Directive 2016/943 alignment). Older labels — *taină comercială*, *păstrarea secretului comercial* — were swept into this vocabulary by L330/2025.

## How it works in practice
- **One regime:** confidentiality exceptions in company, competition, insolvency, procurement, customs and procedure acts now point to L384 definitions and lawful-acquisition rules — do not advise from pre-330 wording alone.
- **Entrepreneurship residual:** [[Legea 845-1992 — antreprenoriat si intreprinderi (text)#Articolul 12. Controlul asupra activităţii de antreprenoriat|L845 art. 12(3)]] lets the enterprise withhold commercial-secret data from public authorities **except** law-enforcement / control bodies within their powers; the list must still respect L384.
- **Competition limb:** unlawful obtaining/use remains a competition-law fault path under [[Legea 183-2012 — concurenta (notă)|L183/2012]], now using L384 vocabulary.
- **Court / procedure:** CPC closed-hearing and confidentiality language updated by L330 — protect the secret in litigation without assuming every “commercial secret” label still matches L384.

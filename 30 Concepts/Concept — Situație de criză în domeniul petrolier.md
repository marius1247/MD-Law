# Concept — Situație de criză în domeniul petrolier

**RO:** situație de criză în domeniul petrolier · **EN:** petroleum-sector crisis situation

> Inserted into Law No. 461/2001 art. 2 by [[Legea 101-2026 — consolidarea mecanismelor de interventie in situatii de criza (text)|Legea nr. 101/2026]] Art. I — replaces the prior “situație excepțională” wording. → [[Legea 101-2026 — consolidarea mecanismelor de interventie in situatii de criza (notă)]]

## Definition
Emergency, crisis or major crisis that causes a **sharp increase in consumption** of main petroleum products and/or a **sharp decrease in imports** of such products, from disruption on the external petroleum market or other external factors.

## How it works
Once the crisis frame is engaged (together with alert/emergency states under Law 248/2025):
- Retail of **non-standard** main-product types may be allowed when standard-type stocks are exhausted, capped at the **max price** of the standard type (L461 art. 4(2) as rewritten).
- The National Crisis Management Commission may grant **temporary derogations** from art. 4 and from the general max-price calculation norms (new art. 6(7)).

Law 461/2001 is **not yet ingested** as a vault `(text)`/`(notă)` pair — verify operative articles at source before courtroom use.

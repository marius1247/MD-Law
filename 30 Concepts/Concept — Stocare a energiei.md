# Concept — Stocare a energiei

**RO:** stocarea energiei / instalație de stocare a energiei · **EN:** energy storage / energy storage facility

## Definition
In the electro-energetic system, a facility in which **energy storage** takes place. Storage is a **regulated activity** under L164 (alongside generation, trading, aggregation).
> Facility definition: [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (text)#Articolul 2. Noțiuni|L164 art. 2 pt. 64]]. Activity list: [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (text)#Articolul 16. Activitățile desfășurate în sectorul|art. 16(1)(b)]].

## How it works
### Licensing thresholds (art. 18(3))
- Licence required for an **autonomous** storage facility with injection power **≥ 1 MW**.
- A final customer holding storage ≥ 1 MW injection **must** obtain the storage licence.
- **No** storage licence for system operators when they are allowed to own/operate storage under the statutory derogations.
- Generation licence covers an integrated plant+storage package when combined injection ≥ **5 MW** (art. 18(2)); storage licence term **25 years** (art. 18(10)).

### TSO / DSO ownership ban with narrow exceptions
TSO default: **may not** own, develop, manage or operate storage ([[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (text)#Articolul 34. Deținerea instalațiilor de stocare a energiei|art. 34]]; parallel DSO rule art. 63). Exceptions: fully integrated network components approved by ANRE, or failed open tender + necessity for non-frequency system services + ANRE approval. Periodic market test (≥ every 5 years) can force phase-out within 18 months.

### Market participation
Storage appears across wholesale products (energy, balancing, system services) and in active-consumer rights (no double network charges for on-site stored energy / flexibility — art. 122(4)). OPEED product design must allow small bids (≥ 100 kW) so storage and small RES can participate effectively (art. 94 path / art. 96 area).

### Connection
Storage operators are connection applicants under [[HANRE 311-2026 — racordarea la retelele electrice (notă)|HANRE 311/2026]] and the network code — same physical gate as generators/loads.

## Notes / conclusions
> Below 1 MW autonomous injection, storage may sit outside the storage-licence gate — but connection, metering, balancing and (if RES-linked) L10 rules still apply. Always map **size → licence class** before advising “unlicensed behind-the-meter”.

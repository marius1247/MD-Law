# Concept — TVA

**RO:** taxa pe valoarea adăugată (TVA) · **EN:** value added tax

## Definition
A general, indirect consumption tax on the supply of goods and services and on imports, governed by **Titlul III of the Codul fiscal**. Built on the EU VAT model: **output tax on sales, minus input tax on purchases**, with the difference remitted; the burden lands on the final consumer, and registered businesses are collectors rather than bearers.

| Exempt | Specified supplies — **without** input tax recovery |

> The 8% rate on **natural gas transport** and the **art. 101⁷ reverse charge** are live cross-domain points: VAT treatment feeds regulated tariff construction and energy-trading invoicing. → [[Concept — Tarif reglementat]] · [[Energetică — synthesis]]

## Zero-rated vs exempt — the distinction that costs money
Both mean no VAT is charged on the sale. They are opposite in effect:

- **Zero-rated (0%)** — a taxable supply at a nil rate. Input VAT **is recoverable**. The supplier is made whole.
- **Exempt** — outside the charge. Input VAT is **not recoverable** and becomes a real cost embedded in the price.

Clients routinely treat the two as equivalent. They are not.

## Registration — a genuine strategic decision
Registration is **threshold-based** on taxable turnover (**1.5 MDL m** from 2026 — raised from 1.2), with **voluntary registration** available below the threshold. Re-verify the figure before advising.

Registering is not automatically good:
- **Register** if customers are VAT-registered businesses (they recover the VAT) and if there is meaningful input VAT to recover.
- **Stay unregistered** if customers are final consumers — VAT then raises the effective price by 20% against unregistered competitors.

Crucially, the **4% small business income tax regime requires being outside VAT registration**. The two decisions are linked and must be modelled together. → [[Concept — Impozit pe venit]]

## 2026 mechanics that changed advice
- **Energy reverse charge (art. 101⁷):** seller invoices comerciant without VAT; comerciant self-accounts output + input. Wrong status / missing SFS declaration destroys deduction.
- **Art. 102(18) abrogated:** non-e-Factura formats are no longer an automatic deduction denial against mandatory e-Factura users — but a valid invoice and primary documents remain essential.
- **Refunds** — export-heavy and investment-heavy businesses; procedure layer is [[HG 93-2013 — restituirea TVA (notă)|HG 93/2013]], as last rewritten by [[HG 829-2025 — modificare acte fiscale vamale audit (notă)|HG 829/2025]].

## Notes / conclusions

# Concept — Tarif reglementat

**RO:** tarif reglementat · **EN:** regulated tariff

> Live electricity tariff power: [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)|L164/2025]] art. **9**(k)–(l) (methodologies + regulated tariffs/prices) and the Board examination clocks in art. **13**. Filing procedure for licence-holders: [[HANRE 286-2018 — licentiere energie (notă)|HANRE 286/2018]] *(not licensing)*. Gas parallel remains under [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)|L108]].

> [!warning] L107 citations are historical only
> [[Legea 107-2016 — energia electrica (text)|L107 art. 7]] is abrogated. Do not cite it for current ANRE tariff competence.

## Definition
A price for an energy service that is **set or approved by [[ANRE]]** rather than agreed between the parties. It applies where competition cannot discipline price — network activities (transmission, distribution), and supply segments still under public service obligation.

## The two-act structure — the thing people get wrong
A regulated tariff is produced by **two legally distinct acts**:

| | Methodology | Tariff decision |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | The **algorithm** — cost categories, regulated asset base, rate of return, efficiency factor, correction mechanism | The **number**, computed by applying the algorithm to a specific operator's audited data |
| Legal character | Normative act (general, abstract) | Individual administrative act |
| Cadence | Multi-year application period (commonly 5 years) | Periodic — annually or on defined triggers |
| In this vault | Ingested (see below) | **Not ingested** — deliberate scope decision |

This matters procedurally: challenging *the number* and challenging *the formula* are different actions, on different limitation periods, with different standing.

## How it works
1. ANRE adopts a methodology under an express delegation in the sectoral law.
2. The operator files under [[HANRE 286-2018 — licentiere energie (notă)|HANRE 286/2018]] (base costs / annual / adjustment tracks; electricity/gas annual filing by **1 February**).
3. ANRE verifies against the methodology's admissible-cost rules, applies efficiency targets, and issues the tariff.
4. Deviations between forecast and actual are trued up through a correction/regularisation mechanism — → [[Concept — Devieri financiare]].

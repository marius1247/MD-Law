# MOC — Biocarburanți și combustibili din biomasă

Regulation-oriented map of Moldova’s **biofuels / bioliquids / biomass-fuels** layer: transport RES obligations, sustainability & GHG criteria, blending duties on petroleum importers, and certification.

Architecture spine: [[Energetică — architecture map]] · **Segment briefs:** [[Energetică — segmente piață biocarburanți (notă)]] · Hydrogen gap: [[Energetică — hidrogen gap stub (notă)]] · Petroleum bridge: [[MOC — Piața produselor petroliere]] · Master inventory: [[MOC — Energetică]] · Narrative: [[Energetică — synthesis]]

> [!abstract] Not a wholesale energy market
> This is **not** PZU / PVT. It is a **compliance + obligation layer** sitting on the petroleum import/retail chain and on RES transport accounting: L10 sets quotas and importer purchase/blending duties; [[HG 53-2025 — durabilitate biocarburanti emisii GES (notă)|HG 53/2025]] supplies sustainability / GHG methodology (RED II via EnC); ANRE sets annual min quantities, ceiling prices for local biofuels, and sanctions. Do not invent an organised biofuel exchange.

## 1. Governing stack

| Tier | Act | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Framework | [[Legea 174-2017 — energetica (notă)\|L174/2017]] | ANRE institutional home; sanction procedure art. 20 |
| RES / transport | **[[Legea 10-2016 — surse regenerabile (notă)\|L10/2016]]** | Transport RES share; art. 26¹–26² sustainability; art. 26⁷ categories & measures; importer purchase duty; ANRE registers & ceiling prices |
| Sustainability / GHG | **[[HG 53-2025 — durabilitate biocarburanti emisii GES (notă)\|HG 53/2025]]** | Regulation (criteria) + methodology (GHG) — L10 arts. 10(k2), 12(a), 33(2); IF **12 months after MO publication** (MO 11.02.2025 → **≈11.02.2026**) |
| Stats / energy content | [[HG 74-2025 — calcul consum energie regenerabila (notă)\|HG 74/2025]] | Transport RES calculation; energy-content factors |
| Policy quotas | [[HG 86-2025 — PNIEC 2025-2030 (notă)\|PNIEC]] | Transport RES trajectory (e.g. **6.9%** by 2030 in PNIEC modelling); category caps |
| Petroleum market bridge | [[Legea 461-2001 — piata produselor petroliere (notă)\|L461/2001]] | Licensed importers who must blend / place biofuel mixtures |
| State aid bridge | Legea 139/2012 | Support schemes for production plants (L10 art. 26⁷(3)(d)) |
| Hydrogen watch | [[Energetică — hidrogen gap stub (notă)]] | RFNBO ≥70% GHG cut from **1 Jan 2026** (HG 53 pct. 2); no H₂ market statute |

Horizontal logic: **L10 creates the transport RES duty and importer purchase/blending obligation; ANRE operationalises annual quantities, ceiling prices, registers, sanctions; HG 53 decides what volumes *count* toward targets and support; L461 is the commercial channel through which blended fuels reach retail.**

## 2. Market / compliance segments (regulatory view)

| Segment | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Transport RES accounting | Share of RES in final transport energy (art. 8 / 26⁷; HG 74) | Brief ✅ |
| Sustainability & GHG criteria | Land / biodiversity / forest + GHG thresholds (art. 26¹ · HG 53) | Brief ✅ · [[Concept — Criterii de durabilitate biocarburanți]] |
| Mass balance & verification | Producer mass-balance system; Environment Agency filings; voluntary schemes (art. 26²) | Brief ✅ |
| Advanced / capped / high-ILUC feedstocks | Annex 2 sections + Annex 3 ILUC (HG 53) · art. 26⁷(1) categories | Brief ✅ |
| Importer blending / purchase obligation | Min annual biofuel quantities; local priority then import (arts. 14(1)(e), 29(3), 26⁷) | Brief ✅ |
| Local production & blending plants | Notification + ANRE registers (art. 25(3)); not a licence gate | Brief ✅ |
| Ceiling prices (local biofuels) | ANRE *prețuri plafon* aligned to EU markets (art. 14(1)(g¹)) | Brief ✅ |
| Biomass fuels in heat/power (≥ thresholds) | Art. 26¹(4)–(6) plant-size / HE-CHP gates for counting | Brief ✅ |
| RFNBO / renewable H₂ (transport) | HG 53 pct. 2 · Annex 1 energy content — see hydrogen stub | Bridge ✅ |

No day-ahead / balancing segments. Retail of blended petrol/diesel remains under [[MOC — Piața produselor petroliere]] (max prices, L461 licences).

## 3. Actors

| Actor | Role | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Importator de produse petroliere principale** | Must purchase/blend min biofuel shares; report to ANRE | L10 arts. 8(6), 14(1)(e), 29(3), 26⁷ |
| **Producător de biocarburant / biocombustibil** | Produce & commercialise; mass balance; sustainability proof | L10 art. 2 · 25–26² |

| **Agenția de Mediu** | Receives sustainability information; biodiversity/protected-area findings; voluntary-scheme interface | Art. 26² · HG 53 |

| Final transport consumers | Receive blended fuels at retail — price-cap still L461 | [[MOC — Piața produselor petroliere]] |

## 4. Regulated activities (what is / is not a licence)

| Activity | Gate | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Production of biofuel for MD importers or export | **Notification** to ANRE → register (art. 25(3)) | Registration is **evidentiary / monitoring**, not a condition precedent to start activity |
| Local blending installations | Same notification + register (art. 14(1)(b⁴)) | |
| Import / wholesale / retail of petroleum (incl. blends) | **L461 licences** | Biofuel duty rides on the petroleum importer |
| Sustainability counting toward targets / support | Voluntary / recognised **certification schemes** + mass balance | Art. 26²(7) · HG 53 |
| Placing non-sustainable volumes on market | Allowed commercially, but **do not count** toward L10 art. 26¹(2) purposes (targets, obligations, support eligibility) | Art. 7(3) path / art. 26¹ |

Doctrine parallel: [[Concept — Licență în energetică]] (petroleum) ≠ biofuel notification register.

## 6. Professional legal analysis (risk matrix)

| Issue | Doctrine | Advice rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Counting ≠ selling** | Non-compliant biofuels may be sold but **do not count** for targets / obligations / support (art. 26¹(2)) | Separate commercial placement from RES accounting claims |
| **Advanced vs capped feedstock** | HG 53 Annex 2 §1 (advanced / double-count) vs §2 (UCO / cat. 1–2 fats — capped) | Match consignment to section **before** claiming advanced contribution |
| **High-ILUC** | Art. 26⁷(1)(d) · HG 53 Annex 3 | High-ILUC food/feed crops excluded unless **low-ILUC-risk** certified |
| **Importer duty** | Local purchase **priority**, then import to meet ANRE annual min (art. 29(3)) | Sanction exposure up to **5%** annual main-petroleum revenue |
| **Information duties** | Art. 8(6)–(7) | Separate sanction track up to **1%** |
| **Ceiling prices** | Art. 14(1)(g¹) — ANRE *prețuri plafon* for local biofuels | Enter petroleum max-price economics via L10 art. 14(4) differential |
| **HG 53 currency** | IF ≈ **11.02.2026** (12 months after MO 11.02.2025); abrogates HG 107/2019 | Do not advise from repealed 107 methodology after IF |
| **RFNBO floor** | HG 53 **pct. 2**: from **1 Jan 2026**, renewable fuels of non-biological origin in transport need ≥ **70%** GHG reduction | Bridge to hydrogen stub — not a biofuel rule alone |
| **Mass balance** | Art. 26²(3) — batch characteristics preserved; single counting | Audit / falsification risk → annual independent assessment (art. 26²(4)) |
| **Biomass plant thresholds** | Art. 26¹(4): solid ≥20 MW / gaseous ≥2 MW must meet criteria to count; electricity further gates (5)–(6) | Heat/power clients: check size before claiming RES credit |

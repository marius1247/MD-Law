## Scope boundary — read this before filing anything here
Moldova has **no commercial code**; the Civil Code governs civil and commercial relations alike (**monist** model). So the boundary is drawn by subject matter, not by statute:

| Hub | Governs |
|---|---|
| [[MOC — Societăți & Guvernanță corporativă]] | **The entity** — formation, organs, capital, ownership, failure |
| [[MOC — Drept civil]] | **The general law of obligations** — contract, performance, remedies, property |
| **This hub** | **The activity** — right to trade, permits, market conduct, competition, state aid |

## Primary legislation
**The right to do business** *(in transition)*
- **Legea nr. 845/1992** cu privire la antreprenoriat și întreprinderi — legacy; **slated for complete repeal** by a new entrepreneurship law (Government project 345/MDED/2025), in force **two years after publication**
- **Legea nr. 235/2006** privind principiile de bază de reglementare a activității de întreprinzător — the better-regulation statute: proportionality, predictability, RIA, "silence is consent"
- **Legea nr. 160/2011** privind reglementarea prin autorizare a activității de întreprinzător — the **exhaustive permits list**; no permit may be demanded unless it is on it

**Contracts**
- **Codul civil nr. 1107/2002**, Cartea III — obligations, substantially reformed in 2018–19 on a DCFR-influenced model → [[MOC — Drept civil]]

**Market conduct**
- **Legea concurenței nr. 183/2012** — agreements, abuse of dominance, merger control, unfair competition
- The **state aid** regime — a consequence of the Association Agreement

**Exit**
- **Legea insolvabilității nr. 149/2012** — liquidation and restructuring → [[Concept — Insolvabilitate]]

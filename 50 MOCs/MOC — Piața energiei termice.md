# MOC — Piața energiei termice

Regulation-oriented map of Moldova’s **district heat / CHP** sector: public-service tariffs, licensing, consumer contracts, and the electricity-market bridge.

Architecture spine: [[Energetică — architecture map]] · **Segment briefs:** [[Energetică — segmente piață termică (notă)]] · Geothermal gap: [[Energetică — geotermal gap stub (notă)]] · Master inventory: [[MOC — Energetică]] · Narrative: [[Energetică — synthesis]] · Act note: [[Legea 92-2014 — energia termica si cogenerarea (notă)]]

> [!abstract] Different animal from electricity / gas / petroleum
> This is **not** an organised wholesale market (no PZU / PVT). District heat is a **local public service of general interest** (L92 art. 2(2)) with **ANRE-regulated tariffs**, three licence classes, and a mandatory supply-contract frame. **CHP electricity** leaves this hub and enters [[MOC — Piața de energie electrică]] (incl. transitional [[Concept — Furnizor central de energie electrică|FCEE]] offtake for urban CHP).

## 4. Regulated / licensable activities (arts. 18–19)

| Licence (art. 19(1)) | Covers |
|---|---|
| (a) | **Producerea** de energie termică |
| (b) | **Distribuția** de energie termică |
| (c) | **Furnizarea** de energie termică |

- Term: **25 years** (art. 19(1)).
- Combined activities → **one licence** covering all held activities (art. 19(2)); holder must meet duties for each.
- Commercialisation triggers licensing (art. 18); conditions art. 20 (plant/networks in ownership or use for licence term; financial capacity; qualified staff; administrator fitness — LP227/2025 rewrite IF 30.12.2025).
- Doctrine: [[Concept — Licență în energetică]] (heat has public-service flavour distinct from EE trading licences).

## 5. Monopoly / tariff layer

- Tariffs — L92 art. 45 · [[Concept — Tarif reglementat]] · [[MOC — Tarife și metodologii ANRE]]
- Two-component heat tariff possible: capacity + energy (art. 45(5))
- Regulated **electricity production prices** from CHP also under art. 45(1) (same ANRE methodology lane)
- Network losses / technological consumption — methodology incentives (art. 45(2)(e)–(f)); supernormal losses excluded (art. 45(4))
- Land / protection zones — Cap. VI (property + local-government intersection)
- Connection of RES heat into SACET — art. 15¹(4) *aviz de racordare*

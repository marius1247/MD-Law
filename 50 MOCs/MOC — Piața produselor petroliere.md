# MOC — Piața produselor petroliere

Regulation-oriented map of Moldova’s **petroleum products market**: price-cap retail, licensing, logistics, and crisis overlays.

Architecture spine: [[Energetică — architecture map]] · **Segment briefs:** [[Energetică — segmente piață petrol (notă)]] · Coal gap (separate): [[Energetică — cărbune gap stub (notă)]] · Master inventory: [[MOC — Energetică]] · Narrative: [[Energetică — synthesis]] · Act note: [[Legea 461-2001 — piata produselor petroliere (notă)]]

> [!abstract] Different animal from electricity / gas
> This is **not** an organised wholesale power/gas market. It is an **import → storage → wholesale → retail** chain with **daily ANRE max retail prices** for standard main products, ANRE licensing, and a crisis overlay via L248/CNMC. Do not import PVT / PZU vocabulary here.

## 2. Market segments (regulatory view)

| Segment | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Import | Customs entry of petroleum products; designated posts (art. 18 / L101) | Brief ✅ |
| Transport | Specialised carriers; importer may transport via separate accounting unit | Brief ✅ |
| Storage / depots | Certified depots; minimum volumes for importers (1000 m³ main / 150 m³ LPG) | Brief ✅ |
| Wholesale | Ridicata by licensed importer / participants | Brief ✅ |
| Retail (filling stations) | Amănuntul at certified stations — **ANRE max price** binds | Brief ✅ · [[Concept — Preț maxim ANRE produse petroliere]] |
| LPG track | Parallel licence class for liquefied gas at stations | Brief ✅ |
| Crisis mode | Petroleum crisis situation → stock-out sales + CNMC derogations | Brief ✅ · [[Concept — Situație de criză în domeniul petrolier]] |

No day-ahead / balancing / VTP segments. Security stocks of **păcură / coal** under state reserves are **not** this market — see coal stub and HG 677 references.

## 4. Regulated / licensable activities (art. 12)

| Licence class (art. 12) | Covers |
|---|---|
| (a) | Import + wholesale and/or retail of **petrol and diesel** at filling stations |
| (b) | Import + wholesale and/or retail of **LPG** at filling stations |

**Special importer conditions (art. 14):** own/leased depot ≥ **1000 m³** (main products) or ≥ **150 m³** (LPG); retail licence needs certified station(s); industrial-object register for safety.

Licence term **5 years** (art. 7(2)); L160 background. Annual regulatory payment on importers (art. 2 — *plată regulatorie*).

## 7. Professional legal analysis

Cross-vector rules: [[Energetică — architecture map]] §1.3. Segment detail: [[Energetică — segmente piață petrol (notă)]] §E.

| Issue | Doctrine | Advice rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Not a power/gas market** | Import→storage→wholesale→retail + daily ANRE max retail | Do not import PVT/PZU vocabulary or balancing analogies |
| **Daily max-price compliance** | Art. 4 — publication each working day; ban on over-max retail | Diary-watch problem; price-cap concept → [[Concept — Preț maxim ANRE produse petroliere]] |
| **Crisis decision tree** | Ordinary max binds → stock-out art. 4(2) allows non-standard ≤ standard max → L248 alert/emergency may unlock CNMC art. 6(7) derogations | Ask: is a crisis situation declared? Has CNMC issued a derogation? If no → ordinary art. 4 still binds |
| **Licence class trap** | Art. 12(a) petrol/diesel ≠ 12(b) LPG | Separate licence; depot minima 1000 m³ / 150 m³ are **security gates**, not working-capital options |
| **Quality / ISS (art. 5)** | Conformity controls outside ANRE price function | Retail compliance ≠ price-cap alone |
| **Crisis ≠ electro crisis** | Petroleum L101/L248/CNMC ≠ HG 820 electricity | Separate playbooks |
| **Formula source quality** | Annex 2 OCR noisy in vault | Quote legis.md / ANRE methodology PDF only — do not invent components |
| **Tax in the formula** | Excise/VAT enter max-price math | Bridge [[Concept — Accize]] · [[Concept — TVA]] |

# ANRE — Agenția Națională pentru Reglementare în Energetică

**Level:** autonomous public authority / sector regulator — category (f) in [[Ierarhia actelor normative|the hierarchy of normative acts]]
**Basis:** [[Legea 174-2017 — energetica (notă)|Legea nr. 174/2017 cu privire la energetică]], plus sectoral mandates in [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)|Legea 164/2025]] (electricity) and [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (notă)|Legea 108/2016]] (gas).

> [!danger] Do not cite L107 for live ANRE powers
> [[Legea 107-2016 — energia electrica (notă)|L107/2016]] is **abrogated** (19.08.2025). Electricity attributions now live in L164 arts. **7–15** (regulator, objectives, general/specific functions, transparency). Legacy HANRE acts still *cite* L107 as historical `temei legal` — remap before relying on ultra vires arguments.

## Mandate
Regulates electricity, natural gas, thermal energy, petroleum products, and water/sanitation. Core powers: **licensing**, **setting regulated tariffs and prices**, approving **network codes and market rules**, connection and supply rules, quality-of-service standards, market monitoring, and dispute handling.

Electricity powers (live): [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (text)#Articolul 7. Autoritatea de reglementare|L164 art. 7]] (regulator) · [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (text)#Articolul 9. Funcțiile generale ale Agenției|art. 9]] (licences, methodologies, market acts) · [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (text)#Articolul 11. Atribuțiile și competențele Agenției|art. 11]] · transparency/tariff procedure art. **13** area (publication, 15 WD comments, Board deadlines — aligns with [[HANRE 286-2018 — licentiere energie (notă)|HANRE 286/2018]] tariff-*application* procedure, which is **not** licensing).

## Governance
Run by a **Consiliu de administrație** (Director general + directors), whose decisions (`HANRE`) are registered with the **Ministry of Justice** before publication in [[Monitorul Oficial]] — visible in the registration block of every ingested HANRE act.

## Pattern worth noting
Every ANRE act cites the **exact article of the parent law** empowering it, and methodologies are adopted for a fixed **multi-year application period**. That makes the law→regulator chain traceable in both directions — and means post-L164 electricity acts must be checked for re-adoption or fresh enabling citations.

> [!warning] Suspension carve-outs
> [[Legea 125-2024 — modificare parc IT si tranzitii vamale (notă)|LP125/2024]] rewrote Codul administrativ art. **214(9)** so certain ANRE decisions (gas unbundling/continuity licence withdrawals; specified electricity/gas crisis powers) **cannot be court-suspended**. Screen before filing art. 214 motions.

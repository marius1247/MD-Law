# CNAM — Compania Națională de Asigurări în Medicină

**RO:** Compania Națională de Asigurări în Medicină · **EN:** National Health Insurance Company

## What it is
The public body that **constitutes and manages the compulsory health-insurance (AOAM) funds** and grants insured status. Premium **size, method and deadlines** are set by [[Legea 1593-2002 — prime asigurare medicala obligatorie (notă)|Legea nr. 1593/2002]]; the framework parent is Law **1585/1998** *(not yet in vault)*.

## Functions *(orientation)*
- Manage AOAM funds and insured-person status
- Receive fixed-sum premiums from Annex-2 payers; receive transfers from employers / Ministry of Finance for Government-insured categories
- Conclude deferral / instalment arrangements for fixed-sum debts (L1593 art. 17(1²))
- Coordinate data exchange with [[SFS]] (percentage limb and IT Park single-tax split)

**Collection/control of percentage premiums** sits primarily with **[[SFS]]** (L1593 art. 17), not CNAM.

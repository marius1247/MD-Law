# CNED — Centrul Național pentru Energie Durabilă

**Type/level:** public institution under the Ministry of Energy · **Established / governed by:** [[Legea 139-2018 — eficienta energetica (text)|Legea nr. 139/2018]] arts. 11–12 (as amended by LP111/2025)

## Mandate
Coordinates and organises implementation of state policy on **energy efficiency** and related sustainable-energy programmes: attracts and manages finance, runs registers and information systems, measures and verifies energy savings, and supports the Ministry on NECP / EE policy.

## Governing acts
- [[Legea 139-2018 — eficienta energetica (text)]] — organic frame; CNED competences (art. 11–12), FEE (art. 23¹–23³), audits (art. 19–21), obligation-scheme monitoring (art. 8)
- [[Legea 174-2017 — energetica (text)]] — energy-sector governance bridge
- [[Legea 10-2016 — surse regenerabile (text)]] — RES policy interface (LP111/2025 blends EE/RES finance)
- Law No. 241/2022 — Energy Vulnerability Reduction Fund (subsidisation mechanism implemented by CNED under art. 12)

## Position in the state
- Reports to / founder function exercised by: **Ministry of Energy** (on behalf of Government)
- Works with: [[ANRE]] (obligated-party lists, tariff interfaces), local authorities, National Inspectorate for Technical Supervision (sanctions under L139)
- Related: [[Guvernul RM]], [[MOC — Energetică]]

## Acts / instruments it issues or runs
- Energy Efficiency Fund (FEE) programmes — grants, guarantees, compensations
- Electronic register of energy auditors; quality checks of audit reports
- Public ESCO list; model energy performance contracts
- National EE information systems (SIMVE / SIME components under art. 13¹)
- Annual savings measurement/verification and reporting into the NECP cycle

## Notes
> After LP111/2025, CNED is the operational centre of gravity for EE *and* a finance bridge into RES / energy-poverty programmes. Policy remains with the Ministry; binding market rules and tariffs remain with [[ANRE]].

# CNED — Centrul Național pentru Energie Durabilă

**Type/level:** public institution under the Ministry of Energy · **Established / governed by:** [[Legea 139-2018 — eficienta energetica (text)|Legea nr. 139/2018]] arts. 11–12 (as amended by LP111/2025) · organisational HG: [[HG 1060-2023 — organizare functionare CNED (notă)|HG 1060/2023]] (AEE → CNED from 1.01.2024; patched HG 765/2025)

## Mandate
Coordinates and organises implementation of state policy on **energy efficiency** and related sustainable-energy programmes: attracts and manages finance, runs registers and information systems, measures and verifies energy savings, and supports the Ministry on NECP / EE policy. Also receives monthly **net-billing** commissioning data from DSOs/suppliers under [[HG 599-2025 — limite cote capacitate regenerabile pana 2030 (notă)|HG 599/2025]].

## Governing acts
- [[Legea 139-2018 — eficienta energetica (text)]] — organic frame; CNED competences (art. 11–12), FEE (art. 23¹–23³), audits (art. 19–21), obligation-scheme monitoring (art. 8)
- [[HG 1060-2023 — organizare functionare CNED (text)|HG 1060/2023]] — statute / structure / founder
- [[HG 620-2025 — transmitere bunuri Fond eficienta energetica CNED (text)|HG 620/2025]] — free transfer of FEE-financed assets to beneficiaries
- [[Legea 74-2024 — actiuni climatice (text)|LP74/2024]] art. 9 — Ministry of Energy channel: CNED provides public info on low-carbon products/technologies/services
- [[Legea 174-2017 — energetica (text)]] — energy-sector governance bridge
- [[Legea 10-2016 — surse regenerabile (text)]] — RES policy interface (LP111/2025 blends EE/RES finance)
- [[Legea 241-2022 — Fond reducere vulnerabilitate energetica (text)|L241/2022]] — Energy Vulnerability Reduction Fund interface

## Position in the state
- Reports to / founder function exercised by: **Ministry of Energy** (on behalf of Government)
- Works with: [[ANRE]] (obligated-party lists, tariff interfaces), local authorities, National Inspectorate for Technical Supervision (sanctions under L139)
- Related: [[Guvernul RM]], [[MOC — Energetică]]

## Acts / instruments it issues or runs
- Energy Efficiency Fund (FEE) programmes — grants, guarantees, compensations; asset handover under HG 620
- Electronic register of energy auditors; quality checks of audit reports
- Public ESCO list; model energy performance contracts
- National EE information systems (SIMVE / SIME components under art. 13¹)
- Annual savings measurement/verification and reporting into the NECP cycle
- Public monthly net-billing capacity uptake stats (HG 599)

## Notes
> After LP111/2025, CNED is the operational centre of gravity for EE *and* a finance bridge into RES / energy-poverty programmes. Policy remains with the Ministry; binding market rules and tariffs remain with [[ANRE]].

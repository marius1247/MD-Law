# Curtea Constituțională

**Level:** sole authority of constitutional jurisdiction (outside the ordinary court hierarchy) · **Basis:** [[Constituția RM (text)|Constitution]] **Titlul V** + Legea privind Curtea Constituțională & Codul jurisdicției constituționale.

## Mandate
Reviews the constitutionality of laws and other acts; interprets the Constitution; rules on certain disputes. Its decisions are **binding**.

## Governing provisions
- Constitution Titlul V (arts. on the Court)

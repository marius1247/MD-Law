# Guvernul Republicii Moldova

**Level:** central executive · **Basis:** [[Constituția RM (text)|Constitution]] Titlul III, Cap. VI + **Legea nr. 136/2017 cu privire la Guvern**.

## Mandate
Implements domestic & foreign policy; exercises general leadership of public administration. Issues **hotărâri** (to organise execution of laws) and **ordonanțe** (delegated legislation, on special empowerment).

## Governing provisions
- Constitution Titlul III Cap. VI (Guvernul)
- **Legea 136/2017** — organisation & functioning (also referenced by [[Legea 100-2017 — actele normative (text)#Articolul 7. Corelaţia actelor normative|L100/2017 art. 7]] for competence limits)

# Președintele Republicii Moldova

**Level:** head of state · **Basis:** [[Constituția RM (text)|Constitution]] Titlul III, Cap. V.

## Mandate
Represents the state; **promulgates laws**; issues **decrete**; roles in foreign affairs, defence, and certain appointments.

## Governing provisions
- Constitution Titlul III Cap. V (Președintele Republicii Moldova)

## Issues
Decrete (published in [[Monitorul Oficial]]).

# AAP — Agenția Achiziții Publice

**Level:** administrative authority under the **Ministerul Finanțelor** — not a contracting authority.
**Basis:** [[Legea 325-2025 — achizitii publice (notă)|Legea nr. 325/2025]] cap. XIII (arts. 85–86); previously under L131/2015 until **01.01.2027**.

## Mandate

- State policy on public procurement — legislative proposals to MF
- **Monitoring** compliance of contracting authorities and small-value procurement
- **Certification** framework for procurement specialists (arts. 87–88)
- **Analysis** of the procurement system — reports to MF/Parliament
- **EU cooperation** — information exchange (art. 89)
- Publishes **GPA/WTO thresholds** on its website (art. 1 alin. (7))

## Powers vs ANSC

| AAP | ANSC |
|---|---|
| Monitoring, reports, policy | **Contestation** decisions on procedure breaches |
| Can refer grave breaches to other authorities | Binding remedy orders in contest scope |
| Not a court | Not a policy maker |

→ [[ANSC]] · [[Concept — Contestare (ANSC)]]

## Practical notes

- Contracting authorities must send **modification reports** and certain **award documentation** to AAP (e.g. art. 74 alin. (11), art. 84).
- **Grup de lucru** certification rules tie AAP certification to design-build and delegated procurement (arts. 87–88).

# ANRE — Agenția Națională pentru Reglementare în Energetică

> [!danger] Base act replaced — 19 August 2025
> References below to **Legea 107/2016** are to a **repealed** statute. It was abrogated in full by [[Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica (notă)|Legea nr. 164/2025]], which transposes the EU **fourth package** (Dir. 2019/944, Reg. 2019/943), **REMIT** and **CACM**. Article numbering is entirely different, and new regulated activities — **storage, aggregation, trading** — were added. Verify every L107 citation against L164/2025 before use.

**Level:** autonomous public authority / sector regulator — category (f) in [[Ierarhia actelor normative|the hierarchy of normative acts]]
**Basis:** [[Legea 174-2017 — energetica (text)|Legea nr. 174/2017 cu privire la energetică]], plus sectoral mandates in [[Legea 107-2016 — energia electrica (text)|Legea 107/2016]] (electricity) and [[Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale (text)|Legea 108/2016]] (gas).

## Mandate
Regulates electricity, natural gas, thermal energy, petroleum products, and water/sanitation. Core powers: **licensing**, **setting regulated tariffs and prices**, approving **network codes and market rules**, connection and supply rules, quality-of-service standards, market monitoring, and dispute handling.

Its powers are enumerated in [[Legea 107-2016 — energia electrica (text)#Articolul 7. Atribuţiile Agenţiei|Legea 107/2016 art. 7]] (attributions) and [[Legea 107-2016 — energia electrica (text)#Articolul 8. Alte atribuții și drepturi ale Agenţiei|art. 8]]; the regulator itself is established under [[Legea 107-2016 — energia electrica (text)#Articolul 6. Autoritatea de reglementare|art. 6]]. Transparency duties: [[Legea 107-2016 — energia electrica (text)#Articolul 9. Transparenţa activităţii Agenţiei|art. 9]].

## Governance
Run by a **Consiliu de administrație** (Director general + directors), whose decisions (`HANRE`) are registered with the **Ministry of Justice** before publication in [[Monitorul Oficial]] — visible in the registration block of every ingested HANRE act.

## Pattern worth noting
Every ANRE act cites the **exact article of the parent law** empowering it, and methodologies are adopted for a fixed **five-year application period**. That makes the law→regulator chain traceable in both directions, and means tariff methodologies have predictable review cycles.

## Not covered here
Individual tariff decisions and licences — deliberately out of scope, see [[Status ingestie — Energetica]]. Current tariffs: [ANRE › Tarife în vigoare](https://anre.md/tarife-in-vigoare-3-204).

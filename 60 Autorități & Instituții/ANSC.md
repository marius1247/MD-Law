# ANSC — Agenția Națională pentru Soluționarea Contestațiilor

**Level:** specialised administrative body for **procurement remedies** — distinct from ordinary administrative litigation courts.
**Basis:** [[Legea 325-2025 — achizitii publice (notă)|Legea nr. 325/2025]] arts. 82–84 (successor regime from **01.01.2027**).

## Mandate

- **Contestation** of procurement procedure acts and omissions
- Orders remedies within statutory powers — annulment, reinstatement, fines where provided
- Works within deadlines that **preclude** parallel premature court actions where law requires exhaustion

## Procedure spine

1. **Standing** — bidder / candidate with interest in outcome
2. **Deadline** — from publication / knowledge of breach (verify current text arts. 82–83)
3. **Decision** — subject to judicial review under [[Codul administrativ 116-2018 (notă)]]
4. **Standstill** interactions — coordinate with award notice timing

## Distinction from other routes

| Route | When |
|---|---|
| **ANSC** | Procurement procedure breach |
| **ANRE** | Sector regulator individual acts (energy) |
| **SFS** | Tax assessments |
| **Administrative court** | General individual administrative acts after prior complaint |

Misrouting wastes limitation periods.

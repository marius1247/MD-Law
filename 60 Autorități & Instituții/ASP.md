# ASP — Agenția Servicii Publice

**RO:** Agenția Servicii Publice · **EN:** Public Services Agency · **Web:** [asp.gov.md](https://asp.gov.md)

## What it is
The consolidated **registry and public-services agency**, subordinated to Government. It merged a set of formerly separate registrars, and for company law its decisive function is keeping the **State Register of Legal Entities and Individual Entrepreneurs** (*Registrul de stat al persoanelor juridice și al întreprinzătorilor individuali*).

## Why it matters
Registration under **Legea nr. 220/2007** is **constitutive**: a company exists as a legal person from the moment of registration, not from signature of the constitutive act. ASP is therefore the gate through which corporate existence, corporate change, and corporate death all pass.

The register performs three distinct functions:

1. **Constitutive** — creates and terminates legal personality
2. **Publicity** — registered data is opposable to third parties; a good-faith third party is generally protected in relying on it, and the company cannot invoke unregistered facts against them
3. **Control** — a legality check at entry, limited in scope

## What is registered
- Incorporation, name, seat, object, share capital
- **The administrator and the scope of representation** — the authority a counterparty must verify → [[Concept — Administrator]]
- Members/shareholders (for SRLs; SA share ownership sits with the securities registrar under [[CNPF]]) → [[Concept — Acțiune vs parte socială]]
- Amendments to the constitutive act
- **Beneficial owners** under the AML framework
- Reorganisation, dissolution, liquidation, and insolvency entries → [[Concept — Insolvabilitate]]

## Practical notes
- **Due diligence starts here.** Pull the current extract before relying on any file document — mandates expire and removals are sometimes unregistered.
- **Timing:** corporate changes take effect toward third parties on registration, not on the resolution date. Plan closings around the registration, not the signing.
- ASP also runs civil status, identity documents, cadastre and vehicle registers — relevant when the corporate question touches real property.

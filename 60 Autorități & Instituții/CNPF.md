# CNPF — Comisia Națională a Pieței Financiare

**RO:** Comisia Națională a Pieței Financiare · **EN:** National Commission for Financial Markets · **Web:** [cnpf.md](https://www.cnpf.md)

## What it is
The **autonomous supervisor of the non-banking financial market**, accountable to Parliament. Banking supervision sits with the **Banca Națională a Moldovei**; CNPF covers everything else financial.

## Perimeter
- **Securities market** — issuers, market infrastructure, registrars, disclosure
- **Joint-stock companies (SA)** — corporate governance oversight, shareholder rights, share registers
- **Insurance and reinsurance**
- **Non-bank credit organisations, savings and loan associations, leasing**
- **Voluntary pension funds**

## Why it appears in company law
An **SA** is supervised in a way an SRL never is. CNPF is the second regulator sitting on top of [[ASP]] registration, and it is the reason the SA form is materially more expensive to operate. → [[Societăți & guvernanță — sinteza]]

Concretely, CNPF:
- supervises **share registers** and the registrars that keep them → [[Concept — Acțiune vs parte socială]]
- enforces **shareholder rights and general-meeting procedure**, following the transposition of **Directive 2007/36/EC** → [[Concept — Adunarea generală]]
- polices **disclosure** and material-transaction reporting
- has standing to seek the **forced dissolution** of an SA that has not brought its share capital to the **MDL 600,000** statutory minimum → [[Concept — Capital social]]

## Practical notes
- CNPF's public register and its notices are a real due-diligence source for any SA — check for warnings and enforcement before relying on an SA counterparty's standing.
- The EU-alignment agenda (Directive (EU) 2017/1132, Directive 2007/36/EC, and progressively the capital-markets acquis) runs largely through CNPF's rulemaking.

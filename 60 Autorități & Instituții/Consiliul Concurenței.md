# Consiliul Concurenței

**RO:** Consiliul Concurenței · **EN:** Competition Council · **Web:** [competition.md](https://competition.md)

## What it is
The **autonomous competition and state aid authority**, accountable to Parliament. Its independence is structural and deliberate: it takes decisions against state bodies and state-owned enterprises, which is not possible for an authority subordinated to Government.

## Mandate — four distinct jobs
1. **Anticompetitive agreements** — cartels, bid rigging, vertical restraints (Art. 101 TFEU analogue)
2. **Abuse of dominance** — Art. 102 analogue → [[Concept — Poziție dominantă]]
3. **Merger control** — mandatory notification above turnover thresholds, with **standstill** before clearance
4. **State aid** — notification, clearance, and **recovery of unlawful aid** → [[Concept — Ajutor de stat]]

Plus **unfair competition** — misleading practices, confusion, denigration, misuse of trade secrets — and advocacy on the competitive effects of proposed legislation.

## Powers
Investigation on complaint or of its own motion; **dawn raids** and information requests; interim measures; **commitment decisions**; and **fines calculated as a percentage of turnover**. Leniency is available for cartel participants who come forward first. Decisions are subject to judicial review under the **Codul administrativ nr. 116/2018** → [[MOC — Proceduri]].

## Where it shows up in this vault
- **Bid rigging in public procurement** — simultaneously a competition infringement and a procurement problem → [[MOC — Achiziții publice & Statul]]
- **Energy** — renewables support schemes and PSO compensation are state aid measures; dominance in network industries is structural and handled *ex ante* by [[ANRE]], with the Council as backstop → [[Energetică — sinteza sectorului]]
- **Tax incentives** — IT Park and other sectoral regimes raise selectivity questions → [[Fiscalitate — sinteza sistemului fiscal]]
- **M&A** — merger notification is a condition of closing, not an afterthought → [[Societăți & guvernanță — sinteza]]

## Practical notes
- **Standstill is the trap in M&A.** Closing before clearance is an infringement in itself, independently of whether the deal harms competition.
- The regime is transposed from EU law, so **EU case law and Commission practice are the working interpretive guide** — cite them.
- The Council publishes its decisions; the merger and state aid decisions in particular are the best available source on how thresholds and tests are actually applied.

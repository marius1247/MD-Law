# Parlamentul Republicii Moldova

**Level:** supreme representative & sole legislative body · **Basis:** [[Constituția RM — text|Constitution]] Titlul III, Cap. IV.

## Mandate
Unicameral, **101 deputați** elected for 4-year terms. Adopts **legi** (constitutional, organic, ordinary) and **hotărâri**; ratifies treaties; approves the budget; appoints/oversees certain officials.
